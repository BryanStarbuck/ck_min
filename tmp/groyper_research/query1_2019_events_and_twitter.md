## WHO / WHAT
- **Groypers / Groyper Army**: followers of Nick Fuentes; named after Groyper (rotund Pepe variant, chin on interlocked fingers). Earlier label: "Nickers."
- **Peak campaign**: Oct–Dec 2019 against TPUSA Culture War tour and related events.
- **Tactic**: pack Q&A lines; dress MAGA-friendly; ask loaded questions on Israel, immigration, LGBT; livestream for memes; when Q&A canceled, heckle/chant "America First" / "Q&A".
- **Guidance** (reported): dress well, stay calm/polite; 4chan/Daily Stormer-type posts urged "GOOGLE THE USS LIBERTY", "GOOGLE DANCING ISRAELIS", "AMERICA FIRST NOT ISRAEL FIRST", "NICK FUENTES" if ejected.

  2. To Rob Smith: "How does anal sex help us win the culture war?"
  3. "Dancing Israelis" ironic question (exact Diggit transcript):
     - Questioner wore "I love ISR" pin + "socialism sucks" pin + red MAGA hat
     - "What a racist question that was" (opening, after prior question labeled racist)
     - "I have a quick and fun, lighthearted question for you, Charlie. So, you gave a speech in Jerusalem earlier this year? Were there any awesome fun dancing parties that you guys hit afterwards? Cause I heard that Israelis are some of the best dancers in the world. I mean, if you don’t believe me, Google 'Dancing Israelis,' – it is insane how good their dancing is. Would you agree or disagree with that?"
     - Kirk: "Israel is a beautiful country, a great country too."
     - Questioner: "It is our greatest ally."
     - Kirk: "Correct."
- Framing goals (Diggit): paint Kirk as "pro-Israel, anti-white, anti-American, loyal to a different country, anti-Christian, pro-drag queen and an anal sex supporting fake conservative."
- **Livestream split**: Fuentes DLive ~4,000 watching his dual-screen commentary vs ~400 on official TPUSA stream (Diggit).
