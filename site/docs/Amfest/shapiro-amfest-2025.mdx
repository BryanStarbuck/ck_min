# Shapiro Names Carlson and Kelly

At **AmericaFest December 2025**, **Ben Shapiro** — a recurring AmFest keynote across multiple years — delivered remarks that investigation coverage summarizes as a direct attack on **Tucker Carlson** and **Megyn Kelly** for failing to denounce **Candace Owens**'s theories about Charlie Kirk's death. Circulating characterizations use Shapiro's word **"cowardice"** for that silence.

This is **on-stage political and media-industry combat** among living commentators. It is logged here because it shows the **discipline function** of the AmFest main stage after Kirk's death — not because it proves any homicide theory.

## Why the speech matters to the AmFest thesis

The conference-control thesis says the stage teaches the base **which questions are allowed**. Shapiro's AmFest intervention, as reported, does three things at once:

1. **Polices investigative speech** about Kirk's death by demanding other stars denounce Owens.
2. **Splits the right's media tier** — Daily Wire lane vs Carlson/Kelly lane — on Kirk's own infrastructure.
3. **Re-centers a hard pro-Israel institutional voice** on the same weekend Carlson attacked AIPAC-labeling ([Tucker AIPAC speech](./tucker-aipac-speech)).

That combination is why citizen investigators treat December 2025 as a **two-lane stage**: America First foreign-policy critique and pro-Israel narrative discipline running in parallel under one TPUSA roof.

## Relationship to Kirk's last known preferences

* Kirk had **platformed both** Shapiro-lane and Carlson-lane talent across 2024–2025 ([Speaker Index](./speakers-index)).
* On **September 9, 2025**, master-file chronology places Kirk **on Shapiro's show criticizing Israel to Shapiro's face** — treated by donor-pressure researchers as a point of no return ([Hamptons chronology](/Timeline/hamptons-intervention-meeting)).
* The same day, Kirk reportedly planned to **invite Owens to AmFest** ([Candace Invitation](./candace-invitation)).

Shapiro attacking silence about Owens's theories at the first AmFest without Kirk is therefore read by some as **opposite** to Kirk's last 24 hours of outreach — and by others as **exactly** what Kirk's institutional allies always wanted. Both readings are interpretations of public positioning.

## X aftershocks

* **@RealTheForce**-style posts claim post-death leadership "replaced Tucker with Ben Shapiro." Treat as **meme framing** until year-over-year contracts and billed headliners are produced; Carlson **did** speak in December 2025.
* **Fuentes / groyper** commentary after AmFest attacked Carlson for rejecting white identity politics while Shapiro "stands for his people" — a different conflict than the Israel-motive case, but it used AmFest as the stage reference (**@groyper_quant** and related clips).
* Some X users note **JD Vance** rebuking Shapiro at AmFest in December in separate political fights (e.g. discourse relayed by **@isaac_schorr**) — another data point that AmFest remained a **factional battlefield**, not a single captured script.

## Defamation and status

**Ben Shapiro, Tucker Carlson, Megyn Kelly, Candace Owens, Erika Kirk, and JD Vance** are living persons. This page does **not** allege that any of them planned, funded, or carried out Kirk's death. Calling a rival media figure a coward is harsh punditry, not a crime. Owens's theories are **her commentary**; demanding denunciation is **his commentary**.

## Counter-reading

A conference that books both Carlson and Shapiro is practicing **big-tent revenue**, not a coherent foreign policy. Intra-right purity spirals are perpetual. Shapiro denouncing Owens-adjacent conspiracy culture is continuous with his pre-2025 brand. Nothing about a "cowardice" speech requires a plot.
