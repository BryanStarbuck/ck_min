# Bannon: "We Are At War"

**Steve Bannon** has been an AmFest fixture across **2023–2025**, including a **2024** close with a **"Victory or Death!"** call to action. At **AmericaFest 2025**, coverage in this investigation file places a **December 19, 2025** appearance in which Bannon declared **"We are at war"** and described Charlie Kirk in maximal historic terms — circulating characterizations include potentially **"the greatest American produced since the Revolution."**

Exact wording should be checked against the primary livestream or TPUSA upload. What matters for this section is the **function** of the speech on the first AmFest without Kirk.

## Function on the post-death stage

Bannon's lane at TPUSA events has long been **mobilization rhetoric**: existential stakes, civilizational conflict, and cadre energy for young activists. In December 2025 that lane collided with **martyr framing**:

* **War language** turns a homicide into a **movement-wide conflict narrative** — useful for fundraising and loyalty, dangerous if it short-circuits forensic investigation.
* **Historic elevation** of Kirk freezes him as icon inside the org that still books the stage, sells the merch, and runs the chapters.
* Placement on the same multi-day program as [Carlson's AIPAC critique](./tucker-aipac-speech) and [Shapiro's discipline speech](./shapiro-amfest-2025) means attendees heard **war, lobby critique, and purity policing** as concurrent messages.

## Continuity with the August 25 flyer

Master-file commentary on the [August 25, 2025 flyer](./august-25-flyer) places Bannon **below Carlson** on the graphic Kirk's team released sixteen days before the killing. Bannon was therefore part of the **pre-death sold program**, not a post-death surprise booking. That undercuts any simple story that "only pro-Israel speakers were added after." It does not undercut stories about **which messages were amplified** by official channels after the fact — that requires a clip-boost audit (see [Open Questions](./open-questions-checklist)).

## How investigators should use war rhetoric

* **As atmosphere:** War language shows how TPUSA's largest stage metabolized the assassination into **identity and combat**.
* **As not-evidence:** A pundit saying "we are at war" is not a confession, not a foreign-intel finding, and not ballistics.
* **As comparison point:** Compare Bannon 2024 "Victory or Death!" with Bannon 2025 "We are at war" — tonal continuity may be higher than rupture.
