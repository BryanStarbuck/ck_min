# MTG, AIPAC, and the AmFest Invite Claim

In mid-September 2025, high-engagement X posts claimed that Charlie Kirk had **invited Marjorie Taylor Greene (MTG)** to speak at **AmFest** on **AIPAC** — after Greene had publicly called for AIPAC to register under **FARA** and had made other hard criticisms of Israeli government policy. This page preserves that discourse as **attributed claims**. It does not assert that Greene, AIPAC, or any living person committed a crime.

## Representative posts (as discourse)

**@AdameMedia** (September 17, 2025, post ID `1968303137124483101`, ~535 likes / ~12k views at harvest):

> "BREAKING: MAJORIE TAYLOR GREENE EXPOSES CHARLIE KIRK WANTED HER TO SPEAK ON AIPAC
> Charlie Kirk invited MTG to speak at Amfest a few weeks ago before he was assassinated.
> This was AFTER she called for AIPAC to registered under FARA, said Israel had nukes, said Israel was committing genocide, and said Israel was killing innocent children, Christians, and people."

**@Villgecrazylady** (September 18, 2025, post ID `1968684046071869674`, ~3336 likes / ~140k views at harvest) packaged a multi-step timeline:

* **August 28** (per that post): Kirk announces AmFest lineup with Tucker Carlson and Steve Bannon; invited MTG for a debate over AIPAC; receives "immense blowback" from pro-Israel accounts.
* **September 10**: Kirk murdered at UVU.
* **September 17**: Claim that Mike Johnson met AIPAC-linked figures about screening "isolationist" Republican candidates for 2026.

Date precision in viral timelines is often **off by a few days** relative to the master file's **August 25 flyer** date. Treat social dates as leads, not calendar truth.

## How this fits the AmFest booking thesis

If an MTG/AIPAC AmFest slot was real, it sits in the same family as:

* Refusing to cancel Carlson ([Tucker invitation fight](./tucker-invitation-fight))
* Planning to invite Owens ([Candace invitation](./candace-invitation))
* Staging Smith vs Hammer on Israel ([SAS 2025](./sas-2025-tampa))

All are **America First / foreign-lobby friction** signals on Kirk's infrastructure. The MTG claim is currently **weaker** in this file than the Carlson flyer (public graphic) or the Owens text (named private message claim), because this repo does not yet hold a primary invite email, contract, or Greene primary statement transcript confirming the AmFest slot.

## Related motive packaging on X

Some posts (e.g. **@FurkanGozukara** amplifying Candace Owens on Piers Morgan) restate a broader motive claim: that pressure around **AIPAC FARA registration** was part of why "Israel had the motive" — **Owens's and posters' theory**, not a court finding. Pair with [Murder Motive and AmFest on X](./murder-motive-amfest-x).

## Status

**Marjorie Taylor Greene** is alive. Appearing at or being invited to a political conference is lawful. Criticizing a lobby is lawful. **This site does not claim she was targeted or that she participated in any illegal act.** AIPAC is a living organization; lobbying activity is not homicide.

## Document asks

1. Any **primary** MTG or Kirk statement confirming an AmFest AIPAC debate invite (video with date, email, TPUSA announcement).
2. Reconciliation of **August 25 flyer** vs **August 28 announcement** social dating.
3. List of pro-Israel influencer posts constituting the alleged "immense blowback" (IDs, not vibes).
