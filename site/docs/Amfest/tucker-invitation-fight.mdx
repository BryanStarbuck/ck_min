# The Fight Over Tucker's Invitation

This is the load-bearing AmFest claim in the whole case: that **Tucker Carlson's invitation to AmericaFest became the specific thing Charlie Kirk was pressured to give up**, that he refused, and that the refusal cost him a major donor weeks before he was killed. Every element below is an **attributed allegation**, several are **denied on the record**, and none of it establishes that any person named here had any knowledge of or involvement in a crime.

## The allegation

Per reporting attributed to **Max Blumenthal's *Grayzone*** (published **September 15, 2025**, citing five sources with "intimate knowledge" plus attendees) and amplified by **Candace Owens**:

* At a **Bridgehampton** donor weekend on **August 4–5, 2025**, a gathering that began on another subject **"went off the rails"** when **Bill Ackman** confronted Kirk over his Israel criticism and over **platforming critics like Carlson**.
* One anonymous source says Kirk was **demanded to rescind Tucker Carlson's AmFest invitation**.
* An attendee described Kirk afterward as **"hammered," "horrified,"** and worried about **"blackmail."**
* Accounts describe a presented **list of perceived offenses**, pressure to take a **funding** commitment, and pressure to make an **Israel trip**.
* An **"unknown British woman"** — later identified as **Natasha Hausdorff** — allegedly **screamed** at Kirk.

## Kirk's own reported words

The strongest version of this claim does not come from a journalist. It comes from **private texts Owens publicized**, whose authenticity was discussed in mainstream commentary with TPUSA spokesman **Andrew Kolvet**. In them, Kirk reportedly wrote roughly **48 hours** before September 10 that donor pressure was **"leaving me no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause,"** and that he **had lost a major donor after refusing to cancel Tucker Carlson**.

That second clause is the entire AmFest thesis in Kirk's own reported voice: **the invitation was the sticking point, and he did not give it up.**

A separate account holds that **two days before the assassination** Kirk lost a major donor over insisting on platforming **both Carlson and Owens**.

## The denials and the counter-account

This is contested, and the contest is on the record:

* **Bill Ackman**'s later account: he hosted roughly **35 influencers** at a Bridgehampton hotel, **Kirk initiated the convening**, Ackman **missed the Israel session**, there was **"no angry confrontation,"** and **"at no time"** was Kirk threatened. Ackman has called threat claims **false**.
* **Natasha Hausdorff** **categorically denies** screaming at Kirk or any such intervention, calling the accusation **"categorically untrue."**
* **Seth Dillon** said publicly on **September 16** that there was "no angry confrontation"; under later questioning by **Carrie Prejean Boller** he is reported to have acknowledged a **"heated and at times spirited debate."** Prejean confronted him publicly — "You were at the Hamptons and you were bullying Charlie Kirk" — and Dillon disputes the bullying framing.
* **No public filing corroborates blackmail or threats.**
* Even the **date** is disputed: the master file hedges "Aug 4th (or 5th–6th)," and some notes float **September 9** as the intervention date instead.

The complete side-by-side of competing accounts is on [Hamptons Intervention Meeting](/Timeline/hamptons-intervention-meeting).

## The Netanyahu call

Attached to the same window: master-file and anonymous-donor tracks report that **Benjamin Netanyahu telephoned Kirk, offered TPUSA funding, and pressed an Israel trip**, and that Kirk **declined**. An anonymous donor account puts **$150 million** on the offer; another describes Ackman as urging Kirk to "take the cash and fall in line." In post-shooting podcast appearances Netanyahu publicly stated he had **called Kirk weeks earlier and invited him to Israel** — confirmation of **contact**, not of a funding transcript. **No readout or transcript is public and the figure remains unverified.** See Israel's Prime Minister.

## Why the flyer matters here

The single piece of documentary evidence that bears on this dispute is not testimony. It is the **[August 25, 2025 AmFest flyer](./august-25-flyer)** — released three weeks after the Hamptons weekend, showing **Carlson prominently placed**. If a demand to rescind the invitation was made in early August, the flyer is contemporaneous public evidence that it was **not honoured**. That is consistent with Kirk's reported texts and it does not depend on anyone's recollection of a hotel conversation.

## What this does and does not prove

**Does:** it establishes a coherent, dated, partially documented **motive story** — that a conference booking became a proxy for a policy fight, and that Kirk chose the booking over the money.

**Does not:** it does not identify who fired a shot. Donors conditioning support on speaker choices is **legal pressure exercised legally**, and it happens at every large political nonprofit in the country. A person can lose a donor in August and be murdered in September by someone entirely unconnected to either. Former NCTC director Joe Kent has said on the record that Kirk's disputes with pro-Israel donors were among the **unexplored** foreign-nexus lead areas his center was ordered to drop once a suspect was in custody — which is a statement that the thread was never worked, not a statement about what it contains.
