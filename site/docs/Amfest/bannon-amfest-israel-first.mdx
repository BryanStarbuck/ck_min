# Bannon at AmFest: Israel First Contention

**Steve Bannon** is not only the man who said **["We are at war"](./bannon-we-are-at-war)** at AmericaFest 2025. On the same conference track he is a multi-year fixture whose **2025 remarks about Israel First / Greater Israel and Kirk's anti-land-war policy work** became high-engagement X material. This page collects that contention layer. **Bannon is alive; no crime is alleged.**

## Booking record (documentable)

* **AmFest 2023–2025** fixture per timeline and [speaker index](./speakers-index).
* **AmFest 2024** close: **"Victory or Death!"**
* **August 25, 2025 flyer** for December AmFest: master-file graphic analysis places Bannon **below** Tucker Carlson, with Don Jr. and Posobiec lower still ([flyer page](./august-25-flyer)). Some X readers treat lower placement as donor-preferred hierarchy; that remains **amateur design reading**.
* **December 2025**: spoke; war/martyr language; War Room official account posted AmFest policy clips.

Bannon was on the **pre-death sold program**. He is a weak example of "blocked from AmFest" and a strong example of **"America First foreign-policy contention still reached the mic."**

## AmFest 2025: Israel First as the real fight

**@AFpost** (Dec 20, 2025, post `2002231207233532003`, ~2100 likes at harvest) packaged Bannon at AmFest saying that **"Greater Israel" and "Israel First"** — not free-speech/deplatforming debates — are the **central issues plaguing the conservative movement**.

That framing matters to the AmFest thesis because it treats the conference as the place where the **right's Israel hierarchy** is named out loud after Kirk's death.

## War Room #AMFEST2025: Kirk and Iran

**@Bannons_WarRoom** (Dec 20, 2025, `2002199659109101706`) attributed to Bannon on the AmFest stage/adjacent package:

* Kirk worked with people around the Vice President (Sergio Gor named in the post) to avoid a **land war / decapitation path on Iran** that would suck in U.S. troops — described as **Netanyahu's plan** in Bannon's telling.
* The **"Israel first crowd"** is destroying Israel.
* Kirk's simple through-line: **who controls America / no more foreign wars**.

These are **Bannon's political claims** about Kirk's private policy work. They are not court findings. They do feed investigator narratives that Kirk was a **blocker on Iran escalation** — a motive theory also tracked under Iran pages.

## Continuity counters on X

Accounts such as **@NJBeisner** argue AmFest 2025 under Erika still headlined **Tucker, Bannon, and Megyn**, staged an Israel-relationship debate, and even saw hard questions (e.g. USS Liberty toward Shapiro). That package is used to **rebut** "TPUSA was fully captured into pro-Israel silence." Capture theorists answer that **one weekend of optics** does not equal institutional direction. Both claims need **transcript and clip-boost audits** ([open questions](./open-questions-checklist)).

## Motive discourse (attributed only)

Some investigators fold Bannon in as:

* Evidence the **America First foreign-policy lane** Kirk protected still had a megaphone in December — so "why kill him?" must be deeper than one speech; or
* Evidence the conference became a **martyr mobilization** stage (saint/martyr language in other Bannon AmFest clips) that freezes Kirk while leadership steers elsewhere.

Neither path is proven homicide. See [Murder Motive on X](./murder-motive-amfest-x).

## Counter-reading

Bannon's brand is permanent emergency. "We are at war" and "Israel First is the problem" are continuous with War Room years before September 10. Booking him sells tickets to a faction. That is commerce and ideology, not a forensic trail.
