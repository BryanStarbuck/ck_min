# Dave Smith: Stage Booking and Donor Ire

**Dave Smith** is the libertarian commentator Charlie Kirk put on a TPUSA main stage opposite **Josh Hammer** on **July 13, 2025**. The subjects recorded in this file's video index include the **United States, Israel, Epstein, and Mossad**. Pro-Israel and America First accounts both claim victory in the room. Donor-pressure narratives treat the booking itself as the offense. **Smith and Hammer are living people; neither is accused of any crime here.**

## Event facts vs AmFest label

* **What it was:** TPUSA **Student Action Summit**, Tampa, **July 11–13, 2025** ([full summit page](./sas-2025-tampa)).
* **What X often calls it:** "AmFest," "Am Fest," or "AmFest in Florida" — **wrong venue/month**; December AmFest is Phoenix.
* **Next day:** Tucker Carlson and the [GO MAX](./go-max-tucker) track.
* **Two days later:** podcast packaging *Inside the Turning Point USA Fallout* (July 16) — evidence the summit produced **visible internal conflict**.

## Why this is a "pressured speaker" node

The AmFest control thesis does not require Smith to have been **banned from December AmFest**. It requires that **platforming him in July** was treated by some donors and influencers as **unacceptable stage policy**. That reading is supported by:

* Public **disgust-he-was-even-on-stage** posts the night of the debate.
* Later claims donors were **furious** at Kirk for the matchup.
* The same summer's private texts and Hamptons fight over **Israel-critical talent** generally.

Whether Smith was ever on a December AmFest shortlist is **not documented** in this file. The investigable fact is the **July booking Kirk controlled**.

## Motive packaging (do not promote as fact)

Some assassination commentators compress: Kirk platforms Smith → Hammer/donors humiliated → pressure campaign → Kirk killed. **That compression is a theory chain.** Missing links include a documented kill decision, a shooter link, and any court finding against donors. **Tyler Robinson is charged, not convicted.** This site rejects stating "donors killed Kirk for booking Smith" as fact.

## Counter-reading

Youth conferences stage **blood-sport debates** for clips. Booking a famous libertarian against a famous pro-Israel columnist is **content strategy**. Audience boos and cheers are normal. Reading a homicide out of a bad night for one side is post-hoc narrative.
