# X Discourse: Pro-Israel Speaker Pressure

This page is a **discourse archive** for Grok/X research query #1: public posts about AmFest and contentious pressure — especially from pro-Israel donors and influencers — over who may speak. **Posts are claims by their authors.** Engagement numbers are point-in-time at harvest. Living persons named in posts are **not** accused by this site of crimes.

## Recurring meme: Candace + Tucker = America Only pivot

**@RealTheForce** recirculated variants through 2026 (examples include post IDs `2026430508574327009` ~1391 likes / ~56k views; `2031428104938312139`; `2082203434673967173`):

> "Charlie Kirk wanted to invite Candace to AMFEST, to join Tucker Carlson.
> He wanted to transform TPUSA from a pro Israel organization, to an anti-Zionist America Only organization.
> I don't think the 'Arizonan Israel Public Affairs Committee' or donors were too happy about that"

Later variants add:

> "Erika has now replaced Tucker with Ben Shapiro."

**How to use:** documents the **dominant folk theory** of AmFest speaker politics on X. Does **not** replace the [August 25 flyer](./august-25-flyer) (Carlson was still listed) or the fact Carlson **spoke** in December 2025. Erika Kirk is living; succession booking claims require contracts, not memes.
