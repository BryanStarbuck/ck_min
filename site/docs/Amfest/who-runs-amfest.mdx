# Who Actually Runs AmFest

Speaker fights dominate AmFest commentary. Operational control is quieter and often more decisive: **who can sign a rider, kill a camera angle, approve a tent build, or schedule night crew**. After September 10, 2025, those functions continued under succession leadership. This page maps the **publicly discussed** operations layer without inventing a conspiracy from a call sheet.

## Events executive layer (TPUSA)

Master-file commentary identifies a senior TPUSA executive often referred to as **Terrell** as the **top events executive** who runs the massive events — **AmFest, SAS, the big national tours** — and who does **not** typically fly out for random campus stops. Exact legal name, title, and reporting line should be confirmed from primary TPUSA filings and staff directories; citizen posts sometimes compress titles.

Why it matters:

* Booking politics may be decided by founders and donors; **load-in, security interfaces, and AV** are decided by events staff.
* Post-death narratives that blame "the org" without naming **which function** (CEO, events, communications, legal) are unfalsifiable.
* Campus-stop vs national-conference travel patterns are used by OSINT accounts to flag anomalous staff presence — a method, not a verdict.

Related people and internal response threads live under TPUSA and key-individuals sections; this page stays on **conference machinery**.

## Mosaic Pro Events (MOSAIC)

**Mosaic Pro Events** — often stylized **MOSAIC** — has been TPUSA's primary **live-production partner** for years, handling lighting, sound, video, LED walls, and staging for:

* AmericaFest
* Student Action Summit
* Campus tours
* The **September 2025 stadium memorial** at State Farm Stadium (massive multi-truck production)

Master-file notes on **"Mosaic night staff schedule"** describe overnight/late shifts for load-in, load-out, and continuous show maintenance on multi-day events. That is ordinary large-event practice. It becomes investigable when:

* Chain-of-custody questions arise about **cameras, SD cards, or stage audio** on September 10.
* The same vendor bridges **UVU-scale** campus production, **memorial** production, and **AmFest** production — shared crews and kit create **continuity of access**, which is interesting for document requests, not proof of guilt.

No claim is made here that Mosaic or any named crew member committed a crime. Vendors execute scopes of work.

## Why operations are part of the AmFest section

1. **Lineup is policy; production is enforcement.** A speaker can be booked and still be poorly mic'd, poorly lit, or clipped out of official highlight reels.
2. **Memorial and AmFest share DNA.** The [tent replica](./murder-tent-replica), AI memorial content ([AI Voice](./ai-voice-and-memorial-tech)), and December main stage all depend on the same events/production stack.
3. **Document availability.** Crew lists, call sheets, radio logs, and vendor contracts are the kinds of records disclosure laws ([Fix](/Fix/overview)) can surface without needing a foreign-intel theory first.

## Counter-reading

Large nonprofits outsource production. Continuity of vendor is efficiency. Events executives exist so founders can fundraise and speak. None of that is sinister until a specific falsifiable allegation attaches to a specific decision with a document.
