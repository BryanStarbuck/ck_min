# "Go Max": Kirk's Instruction to Tucker

The single most-cited **pre-death stage instruction** in the AmFest / TPUSA speaker fight is short: **Charlie Kirk** told **Tucker Carlson** to **"go max"** (also reported as **"go all the way"**) before Carlson spoke at the **July 2025 TPUSA Student Action Summit in Florida**. Citizen investigators treat that phrase as permission to hit **Israel-related critique and Epstein material** without self-censoring for donors. **It is political speech between living people. It is not proof of who killed Kirk.**

## What is claimed as primary evidence

In **November 2025**, **Megyn Kelly** released discussion and video material in which she states she captured Kirk backstage telling Carlson something whose key words are **"Go max…"** — framed as what mattered to Kirk about Israel before the Turning Point event.

Master-file chronology also records **July 14, 2025**: Carlson speaks and, in circulated accounts, tells the audience to **"go all the way."** Separate accounts attribute a **private** "go max" instruction from Kirk. Both phrasings appear in the investigation file; the **Kelly capture** is the item X treats as accidental primary audio.

## Event context (not December AmFest)

This happened at the **Student Action Summit (SAS), Tampa, July 11–13, 2025**, with Carlson on **July 14** — the event constantly mislabeled **"AmFest in Florida."** Same weekend: **Dave Smith** vs **Josh Hammer** on Israel / Epstein / Mossad. Detail: [July 2025 Summit](./sas-2025-tampa).

Why AmFest still owns the page: GO MAX is the **permission structure** for the same speaker (Carlson) who later appears on the [August 25 AmFest flyer](./august-25-flyer) and whose invitation donors allegedly tried to [rescind](./tucker-invitation-fight).

## How investigators weaponize the phrase

Citizen narratives chain:

1. Kirk greenlights maximum Israel/Epstein critique on his stage (**GO MAX**).
2. Donors and pro-Israel influencers treat that as betrayal → Hamptons pressure, lost gifts, texts about leaving the pro-Israel cause.
3. AmFest December programming keeps Carlson prominent on the flyer (non-compliance).
4. Motive theories leap to homicide.

Steps 1–3 can be researched with video and documents. Step 4 remains **unproven attribution**.

## What is not established

* A court-authenticated forensic audio file with chain of custody (public viral video ≠ trial exhibit).
* That "go max" explicitly named "Jewish donors" on the tape (that is **interpreter language** on X).
* That any donor ordered a murder because of the phrase.
* That Carlson's later public remarks were dictated by Kirk rather than Carlson's own prior positions.

## Status

**Charlie Kirk** is deceased (2025). **Tucker Carlson** and **Megyn Kelly** are alive. Nothing on this page alleges either living person committed a crime. Encouraging a guest to speak freely is ordinary conference host behavior until proven otherwise.

## Counter-reading

Hosts hype speakers before they walk on. "Go max" can mean **entertain the room**, not **declare war on a lobby**. Carlson had been making Epstein and foreign-influence arguments for years. A pep talk plus a later homicide does not create a proof chain without intermediate evidence of a kill decision.
