# Topic Analyses

Cross-cutting analyses distilled from legacy documentation — timelines, facts, and disputed claims without repetitive boilerplate.

These pages extract substantive material from former `analysis_documentation` trash files (`comprehensive_examination`, `detailed_analysis`, `10_page_write_up`, `charlie_kirk_assassination_overview`, `heinous_act_description`, `shockwaves_political_landscape`) and link outward to specialized sections (Planes, FBI, UVU, Israel) instead of duplicating them.

The week before September 10 matters because alleged donor-pressure meetings, Kirk's reported policy shifts, and reported UVU warning failures all appear in the same narrow window. The day-of timeline anchors flight data and shooting timestamps that motive theories rely on. Ballistics and surveillance claims remain the technical battleground between the prosecution's rooftop narrative and independent tent, acoustic, and drone analyses.
