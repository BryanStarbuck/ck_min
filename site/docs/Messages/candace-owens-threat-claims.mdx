# Candace Owens' Threat Claims

:::danger Uncorroborated allegations by a third party
Everything on this page is **Candace Owens' own public account**, sourced to an anonymous informant she does not name. **No part of it has been corroborated by any document, agency, court, or independent journalist known to this investigation.** The named individuals and governments are living people and sovereign states; **this site does not assert that any of them planned, funded, ordered, or participated in an assassination or an assassination plot.** The claims are recorded because they were made publicly by a prominent figure and are widely circulated — not because they are established.
:::

## Why this page exists at all

Candace Owens is the single most important source in this section. She released the [September 8 donor group chat](./sept-8-donor-group-chat), whose authenticity TPUSA's spokesman confirmed. She is the source for the [September 9 "kill me" messages](./sept-9-they-are-going-to-kill-me) and for the report about [Dan Flood](./message-to-dan-flood).

She has also made a separate set of claims that are **far more serious and far less supported**. Readers assessing her documented material need to see both, because the credibility of a source is one question and the corroboration of a specific claim is another. Conflating them in either direction — dismissing the confirmed texts because of the unconfirmed plot, or accepting the plot because the texts checked out — is the error this page exists to prevent.

## What she has alleged

In public statements, Owens has said:

* She was contacted by a person she describes as a high-ranking French government employee, whose position she says she verified.
* That source claims a plot against her life was authorized and paid for, involving a small unit and, per the source, one Israeli participant.
* Payments for assassinations, per the same source, run through a French organization she names.
* The same source claims Charlie Kirk's assassin trained with a French legion brigade.
* French legionnaires were, in her characterization, involved in Kirk's assassination.
* A named journalist's life is also at risk.
* She has informed people in the federal government and the White House and offered to provide details.

She has stated she is publishing the account **"in the event that something happens"** to her.

## What supports it

Her own assessment of the source's placement, and her willingness to attach her name and reputation to the claim in public. That is not nothing — a fabricated allegation of this magnitude carries obvious legal and professional exposure for the person making it.

## What is missing

Everything else. There is:

* **No named source.** The informant is anonymous.
* **No document.** No financial record, no order, no communication has been published.
* **No independent confirmation.** No journalist, agency, or government has corroborated any element.
* **No official response documented here.** If the named parties have denied the claims — and they may well have — those denials belong on this page and should be added.

The claims also intersect with the Tyler Robinson prosecution, in which the state's case is a lone-actor account. Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, and nothing in either the state's case or Owens' account has been adjudicated.

## How this site treats it

As an **allegation of record, not a finding**. It is documented so that readers encountering it elsewhere can see exactly what it rests on. It should not be cited as evidence of anything, and it should not be used to characterize any living person or government. If corroboration emerges, this page will say so.
