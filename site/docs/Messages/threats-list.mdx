# Threats List — Candace Owens and Others

Every reported threat against a person connected to this case, one line each, dated where a date is known. Candace Owens has the longest list, so she comes first. Each entry says **who reported it**; almost none has been confirmed by police or a court, and **no living person, agency, or government is accused here of making a threat**.

People ask a consistent question about this list: **does it look like lone wolves?** Reconnaissance missions, insider warnings from inside a foreign government, payments through overseas accounts, and professional units are not what an angry individual produces. That is why so many readers ask whether these threats point to **something foreign, at the level of intelligence services**.

## Charlie Kirk (before September 10, 2025)

* Reportedly told several people in his final days that "they" were going to kill him. See [September 9 — "They Are Going To Kill Me"](/Messages/sept-9-they-are-going-to-kill-me) and the numbered KM index.
* Reportedly warned by a TPUSA donor to "back off or you won't make it to Christmas," per an unnamed donor's account. See Fear & Kill Warnings.

## Other commentators and journalists

* **Megyn Kelly** — said publicly that she and Tucker Carlson are under serious threat after being targeted over their Israel commentary.
* **Tucker Carlson** — named by Kelly; also reported as the subject of "neutralized" rhetoric before the assassination.
* **Ian Carroll** — reported by Owens as receiving threats immediately after the assassination.
* **Xavier Poussard** (French journalist) — named in Owens' November 2025 post as someone whose life, per her French source, is also at risk.
* **Ana Kasparian** — listed as "under threat" in investigation notes alongside Kelly, Carlson, and Owens; no detail recorded.
* **Benny Johnson** — reportedly met with Kash Patel and Pam Bondi over death threats, the day after a figure close to Charlie's team said there was "considerable evidence" of "state actors."

## What this list can and cannot show

It shows a **pattern of reported threats against the people who questioned the official account**, several of them describing foreign, organized, or state-level actors rather than individuals. It cannot show who is behind any of them. Most entries rest on the target's own account; the Daily Mail story is the first time a major newspaper has put a foreign reconnaissance claim about Owens in a headline. If police reports, protective orders, or official denials surface, they belong on this page.
