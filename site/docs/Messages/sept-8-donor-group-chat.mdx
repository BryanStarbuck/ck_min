# The September 8 Donor Group Chat

This is the **strongest-sourced message set in the entire investigation**. Unlike the "kill me" messages, these were released as screenshots, reported by mainstream outlets, and publicly acknowledged as genuine by TPUSA's own spokesman.

## What was released

Candace Owens published messages from a **nine-person group chat dated September 8, 2025** — two days before Charlie Kirk's death. Reporting describes the chat as running on WhatsApp and as including donors and figures in Kirk's orbit. The lines most widely quoted are:

> "Just lost another huge Jewish donor. $2 million a year because we won't cancel Tucker."

> "Jewish donors play into all the stereotypes. I cannot and will not be bullied like this. Leaving me no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause."

## Why the authentication matters

TPUSA spokesman **Andrew Kolvet** has publicly confirmed the donor-related texts as genuine. That confirmation is what separates this page from every other page in this section. It means the following are not in dispute:

* Kirk was under **financial pressure from donors** in the days before his death.
* The pressure was tied to his refusal to cancel **Tucker Carlson**.
* He described himself as being pushed toward **stepping back from the pro-Israel cause**.

What is *not* established by these messages is any threat of violence. The chat is about money and platform decisions. The escalation to "they're going to kill me" (KM-09) is reported for the **next day** and is documented separately on [September 9 — "They Are Going To Kill Me"](./sept-9-they-are-going-to-kill-me).

## What this does and does not prove

It proves motive-adjacent context: a well-funded coalition was reportedly withdrawing support, and Kirk was reportedly considering a public break. It does **not** prove that any donor, organization, or country had anything to do with his death. Donors withdrawing funding over a booking dispute is ordinary politics. No named donor is accused here of anything, and none has been charged.

The chain that investigators draw — donor pressure on September 8, fear-of-death messages on September 9, assassination on September 10 — is a **timeline**, not a causal finding. Two of its three links are far better documented than the middle one.

## Open questions

1. Have the full, unredacted screenshots of the nine-person chat been published anywhere?
2. Who were the other eight participants, and has any of them spoken publicly?
3. Which donor is referenced in the "$2 million a year" line, and has that person responded?
4. Does the same chat contain any of the fear-of-death language reported for September 9?

## The Rebuttal: Turek Says the Next Day Went the Other Way

These texts are dated **September 8**. On **September 9** — the day after, and the day before the assassination — Frank Turek says he sat in a room at TPUSA headquarters while Kirk convened a **50-minute Zoom call with two officials in Israel** to ask how he could better defend Israel publicly. Turek's stated purpose in telling it is to rebut the reading of the chat on this page.

Both accounts can be true. A man can write in anger on Monday that donor blackmail is pushing him out of a coalition and sit down on Tuesday with people from that coalition to talk it through. What Turek adds is not a contradiction of the screenshots — Kolvet authenticated those — but a **witness to what Kirk was still doing about it on his last full day alive**.
