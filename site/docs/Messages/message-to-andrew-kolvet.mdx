# The Message to Andrew Kolvet

**Andrew Kolvet** is the TPUSA spokesman and a producer on Charlie Kirk's show. He occupies an unusual position in this section: he is both a **reported recipient** of a threat message and the **official who publicly authenticated** a different set of Kirk's messages.

## The reported message

Investigation notes record the message to Kolvet as short and direct:

> "They will kill me." (KM-08)

The same notes record that Kirk sent messages of this kind through **Signal and Telegram**, not iMessage — a distinction that matters for whether any of it can ever be recovered from a phone. See [Signal and Telegram, Not iMessage](./signal-telegram-not-imessage).

## What Kolvet has confirmed on the record

Kolvet publicly acknowledged the authenticity of the **[September 8 donor texts](./sept-8-donor-group-chat)** — the "$2 million a year because we won't cancel Tucker" and "leaving me no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause" messages. That confirmation is documented in mainstream coverage and is not disputed.

Candace Owens has separately stated that Kolvet confirmed Kirk's texts about threats. **This site treats the donor-text confirmation as established and the threat-text confirmation as reported by Owens** — those are two different evidentiary standards and should not be merged.

## Why Kolvet is the pivotal witness

Kolvet is the only figure who is simultaneously (a) inside TPUSA's official communications function, (b) a named recipient in the threat-message accounts, and (c) already on the record authenticating Kirk's private messages once. If the September 9 messages exist, he is the person best positioned to produce them and the person whose confirmation would be hardest to dismiss. His account also sits directly against the denial documented on Erika Kirk's Denial.

**No accusation is made against Kolvet here.** He is a living person who has cooperated publicly with reporters. This page records what has been attributed to him and what he has said himself.

## Open questions

1. Has Kolvet ever directly confirmed receiving a "they will kill me" (KM-08) message, in his own words?
2. Does he still have access to the Signal or Telegram thread?
3. Was he interviewed by investigators, and is any statement of his in the case file?
4. Would he testify to the messages under oath if subpoenaed?
