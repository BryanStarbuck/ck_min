# Mic vs Pendant — Competing Internal-Device Theories

Citizen investigators largely agree Charlie Kirk was **not** killed by a clean .30-06 rooftop
shot. They disagree on **which internal device** delivered the fatal energy. The two leading
**chest-path** hypotheses both live under the Mic section because both reject the official bullet narrative.
Two further forks — [LiPO short / battery runaway](./lipo-battery-short-charge) (still usually a pack, but not military fill)
and [exploding earpiece (IEM)](./exploding-earpiece-iem) (head/ear path) — are tracked on their own pages so this
comparison stays focused on **lapel mic vs crucifix pendant**.

## Side-by-side comparison

| Factor | [Lapel mic theory](./rode-wireless-mic) | [Crucifix pendant theory](./exploding-pendant-shaped-charge) |

| **Anchor** | Belt clip / fabric (weaker) | Silver box chain around neck (stronger) |
| **Blast direction** | Left-side pack → upward pinch toward neck | Upward from chest toward neck |

@cletus_jethro's anchoring objection is aimed at a loosely clipped, *unanchored* lapel mic. Investigators answer that on September 10 the transmitter was reportedly [routed under the shirt](./AV_Team_Mic_Setup) and held magnetically against the chest — anchored against the body — and that either placement is an explosion at the collar, not the government's rifle. See the [exploding-mic evidence hub](./overview).

## Why both beat the rifle story (per investigators)

- [Canon XA55](./Canon_XA55_Audio): stage detonation separate from 120 m muzzle blast
- [Video](./mic-explosion-video-analysis): spherical shirt motion, not rifle vector
- Trajectory: rooftop angle mismatch to neck entry
- No catastrophic .30-06 exit / tarp blood per Proof Not Tyler

## Can both be true?

Some analysts treat mic and pendant as **redundant kill mechanisms** — either consistent with
foreign-intelligence "pager attack" style redundancy. Others look for one primary device.
Either way, investigators' conclusion is the same: the explosive was worn at the chest and neck.

No public residue test of either object has been released.

## The smoke-signature question

[@Trillion0x](https://x.com/Trillion0x/status/2070699686961520802), a defender of the rifle narrative, asked why a
**large smoke signature** like the one in a breaching demonstration is absent in UVU footage. Investigators
answer that any field device would be a **micro-EFP**, not a breaching charge — see [Demonstration of a Shaped Charge](./Demo_of_shape_charge).
