# Six Forensic Physics Phenomena

On **August 14, 2026**, @ZREIKMIESTER (Ali Zreik) posted a
six-part thread on X laying out what he calls **six visual forensic physics phenomena** that, in
his argument, show a shaped **PETN** charge was inside the [RØDE microphone](./rode-wireless-mic)
clipped to Charlie Kirk. The thread's framing is unusually direct: he
argues each observation is a physical signature that a rifle round cannot produce, so defending
the ".30-06 narrative" means arguing against physics rather than against him.

This page reproduces the thread near-verbatim, with all nine images the author attached. These
are the **author's attributed claims and his own reading of the footage**, not findings of fact.
The government's stated account remains a rooftop rifle shot; the questions its defenders raise,
and investigators' answers, are on the [shaped-charge demo](./Demo_of_shape_charge) and
[Mic vs Pendant](./Mic_vs_Pendant) pages. Nothing here establishes wrongdoing by any named person
or organization.

He backs this with two screenshots of search-engine AI answers. The first, in response to "with
explosives does radial expansion first then blunt impact," returns:

> Yes, that sequence is correct. An explosion starts with a rapid, supersonic radial expansion of a
> shock wave and high-pressure gases, which then strikes a target to deliver a heavy blunt impact

— broken into **Radial Expansion**, **Blunt Impact**, then **Heave and Drag**. The second, asked
the same question about a bullet, returns the opposite ordering:

> No, a bullet does not experience blunt impact after radial expansion; rather, the initial
> physical resistance (blunt impact/deceleration) happens at the exact same micro-instant contact
> and triggers the radial expansion (mushrooming) simultaneously.

That contrast is the whole of point 6: if radial motion is visible in the shirt **before** any
wound opens, an explosive ordering fits and a ballistic ordering does not.

## Why this thread matters to the section

The six points are not new individually — [video frame analysis](./mic-explosion-video-analysis),
the [Canon XA55 audio](./Canon_XA55_Audio) separation, and the
[chest-shot witnesses](./Chest_Shot_Witnesses) each carry one strand. What this thread attempts is
to bind them into a single ordering argument: heat first (the flinch), then radial expansion in
fabric, then the wound, then the necklace's final resting position. Under a rifle hypothesis those
events would have to occur in a different order, or not at all.

The questions raised against it are answered elsewhere on this site. The
[shaped-charge demo](./Demo_of_shape_charge) page takes the missing-smoke-signature and geometry
questions and sets out investigators' answers. The
[Mic vs Pendant](./Mic_vs_Pendant) page compares the lapel pack with the pendant — both
chest-worn devices, both inconsistent with the rifle narrative. And the single measurement that would settle
the compound — an [explosive residue](./explosive-residue) result from the shirt, the necklace, the
soil, or the [SUV](./suv-transporting-charlie) — has still not been publicly released, which is why
the [Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview) list that disclosure as a target.

## Caveats

* Every quotation on this page is the author's own wording, reproduced as posted, including
  spelling as written.
* The medical readings offered here — absent cavitation, decorticate posturing, the C2–C7 point —
  are the author's interpretation of publicly circulating footage and filings. He is not presented
  here as a medical examiner, and no autopsy analyst is cited as agreeing with him.
* The crowd-audio "counting" claim identifies no person and is not independently verified.
* The search-engine AI answers he cites are general physics summaries, not case-specific expert
  analysis, and are shown here only because they are part of what he posted.
