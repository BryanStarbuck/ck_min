# SUV Transporting Charlie — Candace Owens Photos

According to a post by **@iluminomoly** on June 26, 2026 — reposted by investigator **@alleytopfiles** — a new set of photographs reportedly released by Candace Owens shows the interior of the SUV that transported Charlie Kirk after he was shot on September 10, 2025 at Utah Valley University. The original poster captioned the images: *"New photos released by Candace Owens — Charlie Kirk's SUV."*

These photos are included here because independent analysts examining them have focused on small fragments and debris visible inside the vehicle, which they have tied to the broader [exploding-mic theory](/Mic/overview) — the claim that the device clipped to Kirk's shirt may have produced an outward, explosive-type wound rather than a single rifle entry. The fragments and material in the vehicle are presented as evidence to be examined; the interpretations below are reported claims, not established facts.

The "shattered tempered glass (like from a RODE microphone)" reading is a reported interpretation, not an authenticated forensic finding. Investigators who conclude it was the [exploding mic](/Mic/overview) argue that a clip-on RØDE Wireless transmitter that fragmented would scatter small glass and electronic fragments inside the cabin, whereas a single rifle round would not. No forensic identification of the fragments has been released, and TPUSA has not publicly addressed the photographs. The video is preserved here as primary-source material for examination.

## Why This Matters to the Mic Theory

Proponents of the exploding-mic theory argue that small fragments and debris inside the transport vehicle are more consistent with a device that fragmented or detonated near Kirk's chest than with a single high-velocity rifle round. They point in particular to image 2, where a small dark object near a pale fragment is described by some viewers as resembling a microphone windscreen or transmitter component.

These are reported interpretations from social-media analysts, not findings from any official forensic examination. The images have not been independently authenticated here, and no official examination of the debris has been released. The photos are preserved as primary-source material so that the evidence itself — rather than any single interpretation — can be reviewed.
