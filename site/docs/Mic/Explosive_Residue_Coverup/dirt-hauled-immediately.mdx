# 10 Inches of Dirt Hauled Away Immediately

:::caution Legal Disclaimer
Nothing here states as fact that any agency destroyed evidence or acted illegally. Biohazard cleanup and campus remediation are ordinary after a violent death. This page records **what citizen investigators and a contractor have said** about **when** soil left the tent courtyard and **why residue analysts care**. Unproven allegations are labeled as such.
:::

If a small charge detonated under the tent on September 10, 2025, **soil is one of the best residual surfaces left behind**. Military explosives such as **PETN** are repeatedly described in public commentary as **highly soil-absorbed** — low water solubility means wash-down does not remove them; you dig. That is why the claim that roughly **ten inches of dirt were stripped and hauled away under the tent before the landscaping crew even arrived** sits on this residue-cover-up list as its own item — separate from the later [cement / paver install](./dirt-replaced-with-cement).

## The two-crew timeline (as reported)

The public record, as assembled from contractor interviews and online amplification, describes **two phases**:

| Phase | Who (as described) | When (as reported) | What |
|-------|--------------------|--------------------|------|
| **1 — Excavation** | Unidentified "government" / state / grounds / biohazard team | **Before** Sunday Sept 14 — some posts say within ~a day of the shooting | Strip grass and soil (~6–10 in.), haul it off, power-wash |
| **2 — Hardscape** | Landscaping / paver crew (Dan Merrell / Hardscape Utah in the dominant account) | **Sunday Sept 14** into Monday | Install pavers on already-dug ground under a "by Monday" deadline |

The user's point is phase one: **it did not wait** for the ordinary 48-hour-plus landscaping schedule. The dirt was already gone when the paver contractor showed up. Full contractor detail lives on
10 in. of dirt removed (Cover-Up) and
Dirt Removed Before Pavers (Tent). This page keeps the **residue** frame.

## Dan Merrell's first-hand marker

Utah contractor **Dan Merrell** (Hardscape Utah), in a Jimmy Rex Show interview widely clipped from May 2026, is the main **named witness** to the excavated state:

> *"When I showed up I took pictures of what it looked like, they had taken out about ten [inches] of it. It didn't cross my mind."*

He describes himself as the **second team**. Per Candace Owens' retelling of the longer interview (clipped by [@ProjectConstitu](https://x.com/ProjectConstitu/status/2067372027623715212)):

> *"before he had even arrived on that Sunday … they had sent another government team to the soil."*

Merrell also relays that on-site coordinators said the job was **"above our pay grade"** and that **"the governor and the FBI said they want this done by Monday."** Neither the Utah Governor's office nor the FBI has, in materials reviewed here, publicly confirmed that quote. Merrell's role was paver install on **pre-prepared** ground — see [cement and pavers](./dirt-replaced-with-cement).

## Why residue theory fixates on the haul-away

Candace Owens and parallel commentary argue:

- PETN and similar compounds **cling to soil**.
- Low water solubility means **pressure-washing is not enough** — published chemistry notes used in her segment say you would need to **dig the soil out**.
- Therefore a **deep, early excavation** of the tent pad is exactly what you would do if you needed energetic residue gone before any independent sampler arrived.
- Bomb-sniffing dogs and later swabs of the courtyard surface become **moot** once the matrix is in a dump truck.

Amplifying posts include [@FurkanGozukara](https://x.com/FurkanGozukara/status/2067541128144970109) ("excavating 10 inches of soil… hide PETN"), [@todd20006](https://x.com/todd20006), and high-reach threads from [Alex Jones](https://x.com/RealAlexJones/status/2057879916025376990) framing the dig-and-repave as classic scene sanitization. A July 2026 post by [@lime_joe](https://x.com/lime_joe/status/2082512321461531044) claimed **Judicial Watch** obtained **UVU employee texts** and ~**64 pages** of cleanup records in which the **FBI demanded soil removal ~10 inches deep** — that document set has not been independently reproduced on this site; treat as an **open lead**.

## Depth figures: six vs ten inches

As explained on CoverUp — Dirt Removed, circulating numbers are not necessarily contradictory:

- **~4–6 inches** — biohazard layer of blood-soaked sod (ordinary remediation language).
- **~10 inches** — total depth Merrell photographed once **paver base over-excavation** is included.

Residue analysts care about **any** porous layer that left the site without a **logged sample**. Ordinary paving **does** over-excavate. The contested questions are **who ordered the first dig**, **where the soil went**, and **whether any bag was retained for lab work**.

## The routine-procedure reading, and investigators' answer

The reading officials could give, and how investigators answer it:

- **Biohazard protocol.** Blood-soaked grass is medical waste; universities and contractors remove it.
- **Scene already released.** If investigators released the courtyard after processing the roof, woods, and body-related evidence, UVU facilities can alter the ground for reopening and trauma mitigation.
- **Lead investigator gap.** David Hull's reported testimony that he **learned of paving from the news** is serious chain-of-command criticism.
- **Investigators' answer:** biohazard removal and scene release do not require skipping a residue test, and the soil under the tent was the surface most likely to hold residue from a detonation at the mic. No court has examined why that test was never made public. This page makes no finding of wrongdoing by UVU, its contractors or any agency.

## What has not been established

- That PETN or any explosive was in the soil.
- That the FBI ordered excavation **for the purpose of** destroying residue.
- The identity of the first crew, the landfill or incinerator destination, or a disposal manifest.
- Wrongdoing by Merrell, UVU grounds staff, or any named official.

## Open questions

1. Exact calendar date and hour of the **first** excavation (before Sunday Sept 14).
2. **Manifest / CID number** for hauled soil — was any retained as evidence?
3. Were **explosive-residue or metal-fragment** screens run on retained soil before disposal?
4. Do Judicial Watch / GRAMA packages confirm an FBI **depth** directive, or only biohazard cleanup language?
5. Disclosure path: Tyler Robinson defense discovery and [Fix laws](/Fix/overview).
