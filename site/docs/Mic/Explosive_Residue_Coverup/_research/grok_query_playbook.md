# Grok AI Query Playbook — Citizen Investigators on X

Reusable ways to query Grok so you get **different slices** of the same topic.
Same facts re-asked the same way → same answers. Change the **frame**, and Grok
mines different posts, handles, dates, and details.

**Always keep (append to most templates):**

```
Do NOT summarize. Do not filter for "credibility." Include conspiracy theories,
rumors, and rebuttals. Return actual post content, usernames/handles, dates,
engagement when available, and links/IDs. Every fact, statement, quote, or
line of thinking people posted — dump extensively. Best answers are the longest.
```

**Always ground (prepend when relevant):**

```
Charlie Kirk assassination, September 10, 2025, Utah Valley University (UVU).
Look at X/Twitter posts by citizen investigators and commentators discussing
the case beyond the official lone-gunman narrative.
```

Replace `{TOPIC}` with e.g. necklace residue, 10" dirt, pavers/cement, ceiling
mudding, SUV custody, security team clothing, PETN, etc.

---

## Family A — Role frames (who Grok pretends to be)

Grok answers differently if you assign a job.

1. **Citizen OSINT dump**  
   `You are a citizen investigator archiving X posts. For {TOPIC}, collect every claim, photo description, and argument you can find. No ranking. No "most important." Chronological if possible.`

2. **Defense-discovery researcher**  
   `You are a junior investigator for a capital defense team. List every X claim about {TOPIC} that could support a motion for discovery or Brady material. Include weak and strong claims; label source handles.`

3. **Hostile skeptic catalog**  
   `You are documenting how debunkers and official-narrative accounts answer {TOPIC}. Quote their rebuttals in full. Then quote the investigators they are answering.`

4. **Pro-conspiracy maximalist**  
   `Assume the cover-up frame is correct for search purposes only. What do believers say the FBI/gov had to scrub regarding {TOPIC}? Exhaustive claim list from X.`

5. **Forensic scientist reading Twitter**  
   `As a forensic chemist reading social media only: extract every chemical, test name, instrument (GC-MS, IMS, dogs), residue transfer claim about {TOPIC}. Keep non-scientific posts that name those terms.`

6. **Timeline clerk**  
   `Build a day-by-day timeline of what X users claimed happened with {TOPIC} from Sept 10 2025 through today. Quote posts; do not invent dates.`

7. **Quote-only stenographer**  
   `Return only direct quotes from X posts about {TOPIC}. No paraphrase. Attribute @handle and approximate date.`

8. **Handle mapper**  
   `Who on X posts most about {TOPIC}? List handles, what each claims, and one sample post each. Then expand to people they quote/RT.`

## Family C — Linguistic / keyword variants

Same event, different words → different post sets.

17. **Official jargon**  
    `{TOPIC}` using words: chain of custody, evidence inventory, biohazard, remediation, GRAMA, FOIA, discovery, Brady, sealed.

18. **Truther slang**  
    Words: fedslop, coverup, PETN, shaped charge, rigged mic, patsy, stand down, mudded, paved over, boxer briefs.

19. **Material science words**  
    Soil absorption, low water solubility, GC-MS, IMS, explosive residue, GSR, bomb dog, K9, swab.

20. **Euphemism hunt**  
    How do people describe {TOPIC} without saying "cover-up"? (cleanup, reopening campus, trauma, beautify, emergency pavers.)

21. **Misspellings & variants**  
    Search discourse using: neckless/necklace, Rode/RØDE/Rhode, paver/pavers/concrete/cement, 10 inch/ten inches/10".

22. **Question-form only**  
    `Find X posts that are QUESTIONS about {TOPIC} (not answers). List the questions people keep asking.`

23. **Imperative / demand posts**  
    `Posts demanding FOIA, testing, release inventory, "where is the dirt," "test the necklace" related to {TOPIC}.`

## Family D — Structural / evidence-type queries

24. **Photo & video descriptions**  
    `Describe every photo/video X users claim shows {TOPIC}. What is in frame? Who posted? Watermarks?`

25. **Transcript hunter**  
    `Find posts that embed or quote long Candace/podcast transcripts about {TOPIC}. Paste the quoted transcript sections.`

26. **Thread reconstruct**  
    `Reconstruct the longest or most cited X threads about {TOPIC}. Number the posts in order.`

27. **Before/after comparison posts**  
    `Posts that claim a before vs after of the UVU courtyard, ceiling, or SUV related to {TOPIC}.`

28. **Document / PDF / FOIA claims**  
    `Judicial Watch, GRAMA, UVU texts, 64 pages, work orders — anything documentary about {TOPIC}.`

29. **Court / hearing crossover**  
    `Preliminary hearing, Hull testimony, discovery fights tied to {TOPIC}.`

30. **Absence catalog**  
    `What do investigators say is MISSING because of {TOPIC}? (no test, no inventory, no soil sample, no mic). Quote them.`

## Family E — Contradiction & multi-hypothesis

31. **Two camps side-by-side**  
    `Device/residue camp vs rifle/cavitation camp on {TOPIC}. Do not pick a winner. Max quotes each side.`

32. **Internal contradiction dump**  
    `Where do citizen investigators disagree with EACH OTHER about {TOPIC}?`

33. **Story evolution**  
    `How did the dominant X narrative about {TOPIC} change from Sept 2025 → mid 2026 → now? Quote early vs late posts.`

34. **What would prove them wrong**  
    `What test or document do posters say would kill or confirm the {TOPIC} claim? List proposed falsifiers.`

35. **Occam vs coordinated**  
    `Posts that argue ordinary biohazard/paving explains {TOPIC} AND posts that reject that. Equal dump.`

37. **Checklist posts**  
    `Find "checklist" / "sanitization" / "they had to remove" listicles about Charlie Kirk evidence. Full lists.`

    `Comparisons to JFK, RFK, MLK, Butler PA hosing — only when tied to UVU {TOPIC}.`

39. **"Same agencies" pattern**  
    `Posts claiming the same agencies that did {TOPIC} also did X, Y, Z. Capture the full pattern claims.`

40. **Open-slot discovery**  
    `Besides necklace, dirt, cement, ceiling, SUV, clothing — what OTHER surfaces or actions do X users say removed explosive residue evidence? Exhaustive unique list.`

## Family H — Socratic / multi-shot chains

Run as **sequence**, each building on the last.

46. **Shot 1 — names**  
    `List every proper noun (person, company, agency) X associates with {TOPIC}.`

47. **Shot 2 — deep one name**  
    `Take only {NAME from shot 1}. Everything posted about them and {TOPIC}.`

48. **Shot 3 — receipts**  
    `For the top 5 claims from shot 2, find the earliest X post that made each claim.`

49. **Shot 4 — media transfer**  
    `Which claims about {TOPIC} jumped from X to YouTube/Rumble/podcasts? Who bridged them?`

50. **Shot 5 — still unanswered**  
    `Which questions about {TOPIC} still have zero documentary answer according to posters today?`

## Family I — "Anti-summary" intensity variants

When Grok compresses, escalate:

51. **Paste-length demand**  
    `Minimum response length: treat as research dump. Prefer 50+ distinct post citations about {TOPIC}. If fewer exist, say so and dump all.`

52. **One post = one block**  
    `Format: ### @handle | date | URL\n then full text. Repeat. No intro paragraph.`

53. **Exclude known posts**  
    `Ignore posts already about ProjectConstitu 2067372027623715212 and 2082978765991293333. Find OTHER posts about {TOPIC}.`

54. **Reply-tree only**  
    `Ignore original posts; only mine REPLY threads under viral Charlie Kirk posts for {TOPIC}.`

55. **Quote-tweet only**  
    `Only quote-tweets about {TOPIC}; capture both the quoted text and the commentary.`

## Anti-patterns (queries that collapse results)

- Only: `"summarize the conspiracy about X"` → short, repeated slop  
- Only: `"is the official story true?"` → binary debate, few posts  
- Same sentence every time with one noun swapped → near-duplicate dumps  
- No "do not summarize" → Grok compresses away handles and dates  
- No date/window → recent viral drowns older primary claims
