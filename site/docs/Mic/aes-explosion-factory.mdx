# AES — Accurate Energetic Systems Factory Explosion

**Accurate Energetic Systems (AES)** is a Tennessee-based manufacturer of military-grade explosive devices and energetic materials. On **October 10, 2025** — approximately one month after Charlie Kirk's assassination at Utah Valley University on September 10, 2025 — AES Building 602 was destroyed in a massive explosion involving approximately **24,000 pounds of explosive materials**.

Speaking as someone who says she has run DoD procurements, the narrator pulled every [Accurate Energetic Systems](/Mic/AES) contract from USASpending.gov. She found one award to the company's Tennessee address for "miniature XS demolition devices, anti-personnel": **$440,494**, signed **April 22, 2025**, with performance running **May to August 2025**. She says it stands out on four counts: it is the only AES award shorter than a year, it went through simplified acquisition, it had a single bidder, and it sits outside the indefinite-delivery vehicles that carry AES's other federal work. She describes the devices as made for special operations forces. The contract details are on [The DoD Contract](/Mic/DoD_Contract).

She then notes that the plant at that address [exploded](/Mic/aes-explosion-factory) about a month after the assassination, killing the workers inside. She says "correlation doesn't mean causality," and she asks others to help verify or correct her findings. The clip also names a serving Defense Department official. That is the narrator's own speculation, offered without evidence. This site makes no claim that he or any other named person knew of, or had any involvement in, Charlie Kirk's death.

## The $440,000 Payment and the Flight

Investigators and independent researchers tracking financial connections to the Kirk assassination point to a payment they treat as significant. The items below are their claims, not verified findings:

- **$440,000** reportedly reached AES, with a delivery date of **August 25, 2025** — two weeks before Kirk's death.
- On that same date, a rare private flight departed from **Heber City, Utah** (the closest regional airport to the Utah Valley University event site) to **Nashville, Tennessee** (the closest major airport to the AES facility in Tennessee).
- **No return flight** was logged for that trip.

The question raised by investigators: who was on that flight, and could it have been someone picking up a concealed explosive device — specifically, a shaped charge or EFP-style device small enough to be hidden inside a lavalier microphone transmitter?

The flight has its own dedicated page — see **[AES — The Heber City → Nashville Flight](./aes-heber-nashville-flight)** — where the courier question is laid out with full attribution. No named person is accused of being aboard it.

## The Blast — Casualties and Findings

The AES facility sits on a roughly 1,300-acre site in **McEwen, Tennessee** (near Bucksnort, Humphreys County). According to public reporting on the disaster, on the morning of **October 10, 2025 — at approximately 7:47–7:48 a.m. CDT** — multiple catastrophic explosions tore through **Building 602**:

- **16 workers inside were killed** and **7 others injured**.
- Workers were reportedly producing **cast boosters with solid PETN cores** using a **melt-pour process** involving **TNT, RDX, PETN, Composition B, Tritonal, and Pentolite**.
- Roughly **23,000–24,600 lbs** of energetic material is described as having detonated, registering as a **seismic event of about 1.6 magnitude**.
- The blast was **felt more than 20 miles away**, threw up a massive smoke plume, and leveled the building.
- **ATF, FBI, DHS, Tennessee authorities, and the U.S. Chemical Safety Board (CSB)** investigated. Later reporting describes **fines for safety indifference** (including no sprinklers in the building) and **lawsuits alleging reckless conditions**. Some accounts call it the **deadliest U.S. explosion of the 21st century**.

AES produces high explosives, demolition charges, shaped charges, and boosters for military/DoD, aerospace, and commercial customers — the same product category at the center of the [DoD contract](./DoD_Contract) that independent researchers have flagged.

## Building 602 and the Scale of the Explosion

AES Building 602, the structure destroyed on October 10, 2025, held approximately **24,000 lbs of explosives** at the time of the blast. The scale of the destruction raised questions among investigators:

- Was the explosion accidental, or was it a deliberate destruction of evidence?
- The explosion occurred 30 days after Kirk's assassination — within the window where physical evidence tying a device to AES could have been traced if the investigation moved quickly.
- The timing mirrors the pattern of other physical evidence in the case being destroyed or made inaccessible — including the paving over of the UVU scene within days of the assassination.
- As one widely shared post framed it: *"Everyone working directly with that material that day is gone."*

## PETN and the Hezbollah Pager Precedent

A recurring point in the online analysis is that Building 602 was reportedly handling **PETN** — pentaerythritol tetranitrate — the same explosive used in the **2024 Hezbollah pager operation**, in which small quantities of concealed PETN were remotely detonated inside everyday devices. Proponents of the [exploding-mic theory](./overview) argue this precedent shows a miniaturized, remotely triggered PETN charge concealed in ordinary equipment is technically feasible, and that AES — a PETN handler — had the exact expertise required. This is presented as an argued parallel, not proof of any link to the Kirk case.

## The Post-Blast Aircraft and FOIA Denials

Independent researchers claim that **low-flying government aircraft deviated from their route and passed directly over the blast site immediately after the explosion**, and that **FOIA requests for the relevant flight logs were denied**. Combined with the separately tracked N1098L low-and-slow pass over the facility (below), these overflight claims are cited by proponents as further reason to question the "industrial accident" framing. None of these observations has been independently confirmed here, and readers who can verify or correct the flight records are encouraged to do so.

## Connection to the Microphone Theory

The AES connection becomes significant in the context of the exploding microphone theory. According to that theory:

- A small shaped charge or EFP (explosively formed penetrator) device was hidden inside or attached to Kirk's **RØDE Wireless PRO lavalier microphone transmitter**, which was clipped to his shirt near his left chest.
- The device was detonated at close range, producing an outward blast consistent with what multiple camera angles captured: omnidirectional fabric expansion from the mic position, not the directional kinetic transfer that a high-velocity rifle round produces.
- The magnetic clasp of the mic transmitter reportedly became a projectile, visible in multiple camera angles being ejected through Kirk's neck area — creating the rectangular wound seen in post-event descriptions.

AES specializes precisely in the type of small, precision energetic devices that could be miniaturized to fit inside commercial audio equipment. The company's product line includes shaped charges and custom energetic configurations for military and government customers.

## The AV Staffer and the Mic Placement

**An AV staffer** reportedly mic'd up Charlie Kirk at the UVU event. Investigators have noted an anomaly: Kirk's lavalier microphone was placed **under his shirt**, with the transmitter on his left belt — despite the RØDE Wireless PRO having a furry windscreen designed for outdoor placement **on top of clothing**. Multiple other outdoor debates featuring Kirk and TPUSA show that the standard practice was to clip the mic to the outside of the shirt.

An under-shirt transmitter sits against the body, where a shaped-charge device would produce maximum effect. We assume that every TPUSA employee and every audio-visual contractor working at UVU that day had no knowledge of any explosive that may have been in the mic, even if they handled the mic. Nothing indicates anyone on the security or AV team knew of any device.

## The N1098L Flight and the Tennessee Corridor

Separately from the Heber City–Nashville flight on August 25, independent aviation analysts tracked flight **N1098L** — a surveillance-capable aircraft with a documented history of ISR (intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance) missions. That flight's altitude was reduced when passing over the AES factory/plant area in Tennessee, at reduced speed, in a pattern consistent with aerial observation of the facility. See the Planes section for the full N1098L analysis.

## What the AES Evidence Suggests

Investigators conclude the device used in Kirk's assassination appears to have come from the AES DoD contract for miniaturized anti-personnel charges ([details](./DoD_Contract)). The convergence behind that conclusion:

1. $440,000 payment to AES, delivered August 25, 2025
2. A private flight Heber City, UT → Nashville, TN on the same date, with no logged return
3. AES Building 602 destroyed by explosion on October 10, 2025 — 30 days after Kirk's death
4. AES's product line includes precision energetic devices small enough to be concealed in audio equipment
5. Military veterans who reviewed the footage say the wound and shirt-movement pattern is consistent with a small explosion, not a rifle round

The destruction of Building 602 effectively eliminated any physical trail linking AES to a specific device or order — which would be consistent with evidence destruction if such a link existed.
