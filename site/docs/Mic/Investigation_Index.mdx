# Mic Investigation Index

This is the working memo for the **exploding microphone** thread in the Charlie Kirk
assassination. Citizen investigators argue Charlie was killed by an **internal device
detonation** at the stage — not a distant .30-06 from Tyler Robinson's attributed rooftop
position. Nothing indicates anyone on the security or AV team knew of any device, and no
named AV technician, TPUSA staffer, or AES employee is accused here.

## Why this thread ranks high in the investigation

Four evidence layers stack:

1. **Video** — [Frame analysis](./mic-explosion-video-analysis): shirt moves before neck wound;
 magnetic clasp ejected; spherical not rifle-vector motion (Jon Bray).
2. **Audio** — [Canon XA55](./Canon_XA55_Audio): three separated booms — muzzle blast ~120 m
   **and** stage detonation ~46 m at the tent.
3. **Post-event physical** — [SUV photos](./suv-transporting-charlie): fragments, charred material,
   reported "shattered tempered glass" like a RØDE transmitter.
4. **Procurement timeline** — the [AES DoD contract](./DoD_Contract) for
   miniaturized anti-personnel charges; the [factory exploded](./aes-explosion-factory) a month after UVU.

[Military veterans](./rob-oneill-navy-seal-testimony) (Rob O'Neill, Gen. Blaine Holt) publicly
rejected the shirt movement as rifle-caused on NEWSMAX.
