Every version of the [exploding-mic theory](./overview) eventually has to answer a physical question:
if a device functioned on Charlie Kirk's body, where is the device? This page
covers a claim that at least part of the answer was already recovered from the wound itself — and
described in court in a single vague phrase.

## The claim, as posted

According to [@ProjectConstitu](https://x.com/ProjectConstitu/status/2091998823044546659) on X,
August 24, 2026:

> "**BOMBSHELL: The Fragment In Charlie's Neck Appears To Be Black Charred ABS Plastic, Not Lead**
>
> New analysis of the fragments recovered from Charlie Kirk's neck is raising serious questions. One
> of the pieces recovered inside the wound appears to be black ABS-style plastic, the same type of
> material used in the housing of the Rode wireless microphone transmitter that was on him that day.
>
> When you zoom in, the fragment shows clear bubbles. Lead from a bullet does not melt and resolidify
> with that kind of bubbled texture. Charred, melted plastic does. The theory being examined is that
> the wireless mic transmitter was rigged with a PETN shaped charge causing an explosion that sent
> plastic shrapnel upward into Charlie's neck.
>
> In the preliminary hearing, the ATF analyst acknowledged recovering a jacket fragment 'as well as
> some other fragments.' The phrase 'other fragments' was left vague. If those other pieces are
> plastic, the public has a right to know why that detail has not been clearly addressed.
>
> Charlie was wearing a wireless mic transmitter. The material matches. The physical characteristics
> of the fragment do not match typical lead. The official description stays carefully incomplete.
> That combination demands a full, transparent accounting of exactly what was pulled from the wound."

The post credits [@Its_KK_1](https://x.com/Its_KK_1) with finding the video. As of August 24, 2026 it
carried roughly 80,000 impressions, 2,773 likes and 837 reposts.

## The three separate claims inside this

The post reads as one assertion, but it is really three, and they stand or fall independently:

1. **A material claim.** The fragment is a polymer, not metal. The stated evidence is visual: it is
   black, and it carries a bubbled surface texture. The argument is that lead deforms and smears
   rather than foaming, whereas a thermoplastic that is briefly superheated and then quenched does
   bubble.
2. **A source claim.** If it is a polymer, it came from the housing of the
   [RØDE Wireless PRO](./rode-wireless-mic) transmitter clipped to him. The housings of that class of
   device are ABS-family plastic in black.
3. **A mechanism claim.** The transmitter contained a PETN shaped charge, and the
   detonation drove that housing upward as shrapnel — the [shaped-charge](./Demo_of_shape_charge)
   argument this whole section examines.

Claim 1 is the only one that is testable from the imagery alone, and it is the one the post actually
argues. Claims 2 and 3 are inference layered on top of it.

## Why "some other fragments" is the operative phrase

The evidentiary hook here is not the photograph — it is the wording of the testimony. On the public
record from the preliminary hearing, an ATF analyst described recovering **a jacket fragment "as well
as some other fragments."** A bullet jacket is the copper-alloy skin of a rifle round, and the
ATF's own comparison on that jacket fragment reportedly produced
**class characteristics only — consistent with numerous makes and models, no individual match** to
the recovered Mauser.

What the record does not say, at least not publicly and not in plain language, is **what the other
fragments were made of**. That silence is the whole of the argument on this page. A material
identification of the remaining fragments would answer it in a sentence. If any of them are polymer, it
confirms what investigators have concluded about the [cause of death](/Cause_of_Death/overview).

Related unanswered material questions sit on the Ballistics & Wound Analysis,
Shape Charge vs Bullet, and
Trial & Autopsy Report pages.

## What would settle it

None of this requires new evidence — only the release of results on evidence that, by the state's own
testimony, already exists in custody:

* **Material identification of every recovered fragment.** FTIR or Raman spectroscopy distinguishes
  ABS from lead, copper and bone in minutes and is routine at any federal laboratory.
* **An itemized inventory** of what was recovered from the wound, with the composition of each piece.
* **The photographs at full resolution**, rather than the low-resolution reproductions circulating
  publicly. Much of the argument here rests on a bubbled texture visible only when zoomed.
* **An [explosive residue](./explosive-residue) result** on the fragments and the surrounding tissue.
  A PETN hit would independently corroborate the mechanism claim; a clean result would undercut it.

## What is not established

* **No laboratory has publicly identified any recovered fragment as plastic.** The material claim on
  this page is a visual interpretation by a citizen analyst of published imagery, not a lab result.
* **Compression artifacts can imitate texture.** Bubbles apparent in a zoomed, re-encoded video frame
  are not the same evidence as bubbles observed under a microscope, and the post does not state the
  provenance or resolution of the source image.
* **"I had AI break all this down, and it confirmed everything" is not corroboration.** A language or
  vision model agreeing with the person prompting it establishes nothing about the physical object.
  This page reports the narrator's method as he stated it, and flags it.
* **The vague phrase is a question, not proof.** "Some other fragments" may be ordinary courtroom
  shorthand; only an itemized inventory of what was recovered will say what they are.
* **Nothing here establishes wrongdoing** by the ATF analyst who testified, by any employee of the
  ATF, the FBI, Utah law enforcement, the medical
  examiner's office, [RØDE](./rode-wireless-mic), [AES](./AES), or TPUSA, or by any
  named individual.
* **Tyler Robinson is charged, not convicted.** The government's narrative is
  a rifle gunshot wound; most citizen investigators have concluded it was an explosive at the [mic](/Mic/overview).
