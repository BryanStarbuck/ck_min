Date: 2026-07-29

## Established public record
- Late 2016: U.S. diplomats/intel personnel in Havana report sudden directional sound/pressure, headache, vertigo, tinnitus, cognitive issues.
- Later reports: China, Russia, Austria, Colombia, CONUS.
- 2020 National Academies: directed pulsed radio-frequency energy most plausible mechanism for distinctive subset of cases.
- 2023 IC assessment (7 agencies): “very unlikely” foreign adversary responsible; many cases attributed to pre-existing/environmental/conventional illness. Not unanimous; contested by victims and some members of Congress.
- HAVANA Act (2021): compensation for qualifying brain injuries — acknowledges injury without settling cause/attribution.
- Dual messaging: injuries real enough for statute; attribution denied at IC level.
