# Research: Brain-to-Brain Communications
Date: 2026-07-29

## Peer-reviewed / lab (established)
- Nicolelis Duke 2013: rat-to-rat BBI, ~1 bit lever choice via electrodes.
- Rao/Stocco UW 2013–14: human-to-human EEG → TMS finger twitch.
- BrainNet UW 2018: 3-person EEG/TMS phosphene, ~81% accuracy, ~1 bit.
- Limits: consent, co-located hardware, pre-agreed codes, low bandwidth, recipient knows stimulus.
