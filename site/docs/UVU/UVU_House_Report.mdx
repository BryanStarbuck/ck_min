# Critical Request: UVU House / Charlie Kirk Report

<img src="/img/uvu_house_report/uvu_house_report_animated.gif"
  style={{width:'80%', maxWidth:'100%', height:'auto', display:'block', margin:'0.5rem auto 1.5rem', borderRadius:'6px', boxShadow:'0 3px 12px rgba(0,0,0,.3)'}}
  alt="Animated slides: the UVU-owned house at 691 W 925 S, the vehicles parked in front on September 10, 2025, the rooftop man's exit path, and where the gun was found. HolonCitizen demands UVU release a UVU House / CK Report." />

**UVU's own After-Action Report left out the one place that could crack this case open.** Utah Valley University owns a house on the hill directly across Campus Drive from the Losee Center, at **691 W 925 S, Orem**. It sits above the construction site. The rooftop man's exit path runs right beside it. The rifle was found at the base of that same hill. Vehicles were parked in front of it on the day Charlie Kirk was killed.

UVU's 158-page report on September 10, 2025 **never mentions that house once.** Not in the text, not in a footnote, not on a map.

So [@HolonCitizen](https://x.com/HolonCitizen) is asking UVU for a second report: the **UVU House / Charlie Kirk Report**. It should answer who was in the house, whose vehicles those were, who asked UVU to use it, who at UVU said yes, and what they were doing there.

:::caution Questions, not accusations
UVU, the house's prior owners, and the Theobald family are **not accused of anything** on this page. The "staging house" and "command center" claims are **citizen-investigation allegations**, not established facts. Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**. Everything below is framed as **questions UVU can answer** and **records UVU holds**. That is exactly why UVU should publish them.
:::

## Why this house matters

* **The gun was found at the base of the hill** between the house and the road. Citizen posts place the rifle recovery in the wooded strip in front of this block (gun recovery site, where the gun was dropped). UVU's report only says "follow-up search activity nearby led to the recovery of a rifle" (PDF p. 50). It never says where.
* **Strange vehicles were parked there.** Aerial imagery of September 10, 2025 shows a white box truck with a rooftop air-conditioning unit, a white trailer, a flatbed crane truck, and a dumpster packed into the driveway. One person called them **"operator vehicles"**, the kind a SWAT or tactical team uses. Citizen investigators say a box truck and a delivery truck were placed to barricade the house so there was only one way in (the staging allegation).
* **The house is elevated with a clear view.** It sits on the hill above Campus Drive. Citizen investigators say it looks straight down on the Losee Center roof, where the rooftop man was, and over his exit path.
* **The rooftop man's exit path ran directly beside the house.** He jumped from the roof, crossed Campus Drive, went through the construction area, and went up the sidewalk next to the house.
* **Then the trail goes cold.** We are asking whether the rooftop man's escape path led **into the house**. That would explain why no doorbell camera farther down the block picked him up after the house. That is a question, not a finding, and the house's use records would settle it.

## What UVU's report says, and where it stops

We searched all 158 pages. We extracted the text layer of every page, then rendered and OCR'd all 68 pages that are images only (the timeline tables, charts, and appendices). We searched for house, home, residence, 925, 691, construction, across the street, box truck, delivery truck, vehicle, parked, off-campus, and neighbor.

| Topic | In the report? |
|---|---|
| The UVU-owned house at 691 W 925 S | **No. Zero mentions.** |
| Any UVU-owned residence or off-campus property | **No** |
| The vehicles parked at the house | **No** |
| The construction site below the house | **No** (the only "construction" is a state agency in a post-event table, PDF p. 111) |
| Who used the house, who asked, who approved | **No** |
| The comms mast at the construction site | **No** |
| The shooter heading north toward the house | **Yes, in one line, then silence** |

What the report **does** say points straight at the house:

1. **The shooter walked north across Campus Drive.** The timeline (PDF p. 21, printed p. 20): "Surveillance footage captured the assailant fleeing across the roof and jumping off." Then: "Surveillance footage captured the assailant walking northbound across Campus Drive."
2. **Police told the EOC the suspect went north.** PDF p. 23: Chief Long and Orem police "briefed the EOC Staff that the suspect had fled northbound and remained at large."
3. **UVU's own words tie the house to Campus Drive.** When UVU bought the house, its board paper said it has "excellent access to the University from Campus Drive." The report follows the shooter across that exact road, toward UVU's own house, and then stops.
4. **The report cares about vantage points everywhere else.** It praises the Fugal Building command post as "a strong vantage point" (PDF p. 87). It tells UVU to plan for "rooftops, windows, stairways, ladders, parking structures, tree lines, and other areas that overlook the" event (Appendix D.5, PDF p. 148). It never asks who was looking down from UVU's own house on the hill.

## Why leaving it out matters

The After-Action Report reads as a **report built to limit UVU's liability**, not one built to find who killed Charlie Kirk:

* It says its job is planning, staffing, command, alerts, and law. It says it does **not** examine who killed Charlie Kirk.
* Its headline conclusion (printed p. 10) is that no UVU employee acted "in bad faith or in willful and wanton disregard for the safety of others." That is the legal threshold that matters in a lawsuit. UVU paid for the review.
* It drew on 70+ interviews and "thousands of pages" of records, and did not write one sentence about a UVU-owned property that overlooks the shooter's roof and his escape route.

If UVU doesn't cover this house, the report is a cover-your-backside report. **Covering this house is probably the single most useful thing UVU can do to answer who was in the house.**

## UVU owns the house

* **Address:** 691 W 925 S, Orem, UT 84058. College Heights, Plat J, Lot 2. 1.239 acres, 7,486 sq ft, 6 bedrooms, built 1980. County serial 36:443:0006.
* **UVU bought it** by warranty deed entry 46392-2019, recorded May 24, 2019, for **$900,000**.
* **UVU's board minutes** (June 18, 2019) call it **"contiguous to campus"** with **"excellent access to the University from Campus Drive."** The stated use was "hosting events or housing campus visitors."
* Tax notices go to UVU at 800 W University Pkwy. **UVU still holds title.**
* It is about a quarter-mile north of campus, on high ground over UVU.

## The man in black at the construction site

The construction site at the base of the hill is where a worker met a man in black minutes after the shot. It aired on CNN's **Erin Burnett OutFront** (correspondent Ed Lavandera) on the evening of September 11, 2025, while federal marshals were still searching.

* **The witness on camera:** Dylan Hope, 26, an electrician working next to UVU (Dylan Hope). The site is 785 College Dr, Orem.
* **The actual eyewitness:** Hope's coworker, the **excavator operator**, who spoke face to face with the man. He has never been named or interviewed on camera.
* **The description, in Hope's words:** "Black sunglasses. He had a black COVID mask. He had a black trench coat, black cargo pants, and he was wearing a small backpack, and he also had long, greasy black hair."
* The man came up from the direction of the shooting, said "someone's been shot," said he was "just trying to get home safe," and asked to cut through the site. This was **before any sirens** and before the evacuation. "The guy was already trying to leave."
* About an hour later a sheriff's officer showed the crew a photo, and the operator said it matched. Hope later said that photo "did not seem to look like the one the F.B.I. released to the public."
* Tyler Robinson (charged, not convicted) has short hair and was photographed in a T-shirt and jeans. That doesn't fit "long, greasy black hair."

## The full list of records UVU should release

1. Who occupied or used 691 W 925 S on September 8, 9, 10, and 11, 2025.
2. The vehicles in front: make, plate, USDOT number, registered owner, and who drove them.
3. Who asked UVU for permission to use the house, in writing, and when.
4. Who inside UVU approved it (name, title, date), plus every email and every key or access log.
5. How long they were there: arrival and departure times.
6. Every person present, and the agency, company, or contractor each worked for.
7. Every piece of equipment on site, including any antenna or comms mast, and who owned it.
8. Why they were there, and who they reported to.
9. UVU's own camera footage covering the house, Campus Drive, and the construction site.
10. The construction contractor's equipment and access logs for that week.
