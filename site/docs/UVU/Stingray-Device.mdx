# Stingray Device at UVU (Claims)

Commentary alleges that **Turning Point USA** brought an **IMSI catcher** ("Stingray")
to UVU on September 10, 2025 — the same day Charlie Kirk was killed — as part of a broader
**mass surveillance** pattern at TPUSA tour stops. This page documents the **claim and its
limits**. We do **not** assert that TPUSA or any employee operated an illegal device or
knew of the killing in advance.

## The allegation

From CK_FILE / X research threads (including [@ProjectConstitu](https://x.com/ProjectConstitu/status/1977209967485546880) surveillance reporting):

- TPUSA allegedly deploys Stingray-class devices at **"Make Heaven Crowded"** tour locations.
- Poster claims **photo proof** of a device at **UVU on Sept 10**.
- Framed as supporting the theory that TPUSA functions as a **mass surveillance operation**.

This is a **mass-surveillance allegation**, not proof anyone on the TPUSA side knew of violence in advance or participated in the killing. We do **not** assert illegal operation of an IMSI catcher or foreknowledge by any living person.

## What a Stingray does (technical context)

**IMSI catchers** mimic cell towers, forcing nearby phones to connect. Agencies use them to:

- Collect **subscriber identifiers** (IMSI) in bulk
- Log **movement paths** during an event
- **Fingerprint devices** for later matching even after SIM swaps

If present at UVU, such a device could theoretically log phones at the rally — including
foreign-registered devices discussed on Israel/NSA cellphone pages. Presence would not, by itself, prove operational involvement in violence.

Gaps in the **08:29–11:50** window and low-resolution public releases are **chain-of-custody questions**, not proof of Stingray deployment. They matter because foreign-phone and IMSI theories are often stacked atop the same September 10 timeline.

## Ring and WYZE neighborhood cameras (morning chain)

The **morning Tyler Robinson timeline** on Ring and WYZE cameras (**8:07**, **8:23**, **8:29**, **9:57**, **11:44**, **11:49** per Tyler on Cameras) is a **parallel surveillance layer** to campus CCTV — citizen-owned devices vs institutional HD. Researchers compare:

- **Outfit change** narrative between morning street sightings and afternoon rooftop attire
- **Identification** supplied by captions after the fact (Tyler as Placeholder)

Neighborhood footage does **not** confirm or refute a Stingray; it shows how **multiple sensor classes** surrounded the event.

## N1098L HADES passes near campus (same day)

Open-source ADS-B places U.S. Army **HADES** ISR jet **N1098L** in **two very low passes** near UVU on September 10 — **~9:16 a.m.** and **~12:48 p.m.** (September 10 Timeline, Proof Intel — N1098L). Analysts **speculate** drone deploy/retrieve or ISR collection; mission logs are not public.

An aircraft pass is **not** proof it collected phone identifiers or knew of the shooting in advance. It is another **surveillance-class data point** on the same calendar day as the Stingray allegation.

## Foreign-Registered Phones Allegedly Pinged at UVU

The Stingray claim is cited alongside a separate surveillance allegation in the CK research file. According to posts attributed to **Tony Seruga**, raw GPS data allegedly showed **44 foreign-registered cellphones** in the immediate area of Charlie Kirk when he was shot — reportedly **16 Israeli, 13 Chinese, 12 Russian, and 3 Iranian**. A separate, earlier report cited in the file claims the **NSA detected 12 Israeli-registered cellphones** at the site, handed the data to federal law enforcement, and passed it to the White House.

According to the same posts (attributed to **Tony Seruga**, **Ian Carroll**, and related X threads), **16 Israeli-registered phones** were allegedly present at UVU at the moment of the shot, with **4** later traced to the **Islamabad Serena Hotel** during Iran peace talks — see Israel — Islamabad Serena Tracking, Israelis at UVU, and Proof Intel — Israeli Phones Bumblehive.

These are **unverified claims**; presence of a foreign-registered device near an event is **not** proof of operational involvement in violence or foreknowledge by any person or government.

## Ryne Simmons witness video — FBI delete request (reported)

Witness **Ryne Simmons** reportedly filmed the assassination in **4K / 60 fps**, sent footage to the FBI, and was **instructed to delete** his copy (he says he kept one). Compiled on [UVU Social Media Response](./UVU_Social_Media_Response) and [UVU Student Reactions](./UVU-Student-Reactions). This is an **unadjudicated allegation** about evidence handling — not proof of Stingray use or TPUSA involvement.

## Bumblehive / NSA Utah Data Center (Cited Context)

The research file ties the surveillance theory to the **NSA's Utah Data Center** in **Bluffdale, Utah**, nicknamed **"Bumblehive."** According to Tony Seruga's posts, "cross-pollination" of call, text, location, app, and Wi-Fi metadata allegedly lets the system re-identify even a **"burner" phone** to its real owner after roughly **30–60 days of use**. An IMSI catcher operating on-site would, in theory, feed the same identifier-collection pattern. A noted alternate explanation in the file: UVU hosts the region's largest **Center for National Security Studies** program, so foreign nationals may legitimately be present.

## Geofencing Pattern: Show Faith By Works

The research file cites **"Show Faith By Works"** — described in posts as a company allegedly hired to **geotrack** Charlie Kirk's fans after his memorial and churchgoers across America. According to those posts, **FARA filings** reportedly reveal **523 US churches geo-fenced** for targeted advertising aimed at worshippers' phones. This is presented as part of the broader alleged mass-surveillance pattern, not as proof of any role in the shooting.

## Carbyne — Israeli-linked 911 intel system in Utah County (Cited Context)

Separate from the on-site IMSI-catcher claim, a CK_FILE thread stacks the Stingray allegation beside **Carbyne**, described in posts as far more than a "911 app" — a full **emergency-call intelligence system** reportedly deployed quietly across **Orem / Provo / Salt Lake City by 2023**. Per the note, Carbyne's capabilities include **live GPS, metadata extraction, and real-time audio and video** routed to responders **without any app installed** on the caller's phone.

The same thread traces the company's lineage to Israel: a 2013 Israeli consultancy (**Hyperion / E.B. 2013 Ltd.**) listing former Israeli PM **Ehud Barak** as a director, a 2014–15 predecessor (**Reporty**) reportedly funded via **Epstein-linked investments**, a **2018 rebrand to Carbyne** with Barak removed from the website, and a reported **November 2025 move by Axon to acquire it for $625M**. Commentators liken its data reach to **Pegasus**-class collection.

These are **unverified OSINT associations**, presented as context for why researchers view Utah County as a dense surveillance environment on September 10 — **not** proof that Carbyne, Barak, or anyone connected to the platform played any role in the shooting or foreknew it. A commercial 911 upgrade is an ordinary municipal procurement; its Israeli corporate history is noted only because it recurs in the same threads as the foreign-phone and IMSI-catcher claims.
