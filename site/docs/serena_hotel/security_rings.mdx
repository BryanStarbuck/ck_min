# Rings of Security — From the City Edge to the Negotiating Room

<img src="/img/serena_hotel/serena_security_rings.svg"
  style={{float:'right', width:'48%', marginLeft:'1.5rem', marginBottom:'1rem'}}
  alt="Diagram of six concentric rings of security around the Islamabad Serena Hotel in April 2026: city edge, Red Zone perimeter, 3 km army road cordon, hotel gate, inside the building, and the U.S.-secured area." />

To reach the room where the U.S. and Iranian delegations met in April 2026, a person had to pass six layers of security. Each one was run by the state, and each one narrowed who could go further. The diagram at right shows the layers. It was drawn by this site from the news reports cited below and is not to scale.

<div style={{clear:'both'}} />

## Ring 1 — The city edge

* Pakistan declared a **two-day public holiday on April 9 and 10** in Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Schools and government offices closed ([Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2601969/foolproof-security-set-for-islamabad-talks)).
* **Several entry points into the capital were closed**, and hiking trails 2 to 5 and the Saidpur trail in the Margalla Hills, which overlook the government district, were sealed ([Dawn, April 9](https://www.dawn.com/news/1990053)).
* Reuters reported that shops and offices had been **shut for two days** before Vice President Vance arrived ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/pakistani-five-star-hotel-becomes-unlikely-site-us-iran-talks-2026-04-11/)). The Guardian described the army as deployed and the streets as "eerily empty" ([The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/apr/10/islamabad-pakistan-negotiations-iran-us)).
* More than **10,000** police and security personnel were deployed across the city under military supervision ([Dawn, April 10](https://www.dawn.com/news/1990496); [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c3dl0g4rgn5o)). See [Who Guarded It](./security_forces).

## Ring 2 — The Red Zone perimeter

* Dawn reported that the Red Zone would stay **completely sealed** while the delegates were in the city, and that the arrangements were tighter than those for the **2024 Shanghai Cooperation Organisation summit** ([Dawn, April 9](https://www.dawn.com/news/1990053)).
* **Shipping containers** blocked Red Zone roads, and **army helicopters** patrolled overhead ([Dawn photo gallery, April 9](https://www.dawn.com/news/1990218)).
* An Interior Ministry official told The Media Line that **security control of the Red Zone would be handed to the army**, with an army quick-reaction force on standby ([The Media Line via Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-892588)).
* The delegations were given **VVIP "Blue Book" protocol**, Pakistan's highest level of protection for visiting leaders ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1990218)).

What the Red Zone contains, and how entry works on ordinary days, is on [Islamabad's Red Zone](./red_zone).

## Ring 3 — The army-held road cordon

* The Guardian reported that the roads for about **3 km around the Serena were shut to cars and put under army control** ([The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/apr/10/islamabad-pakistan-negotiations-iran-us)).
* Reuters said "thousands of security personnel, including paramilitary and army troops," manned **checkpoints and blockades** ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/pakistani-five-star-hotel-becomes-unlikely-site-us-iran-talks-2026-04-11/)).
* Traffic was turned back at **Serena Chowk**, the intersection at the hotel's corner ([Dawn, April 9](https://www.dawn.com/news/1990053)). Buses of riot police were posted on the routes leading to the hotel ([Dawn, April 10](https://www.dawn.com/news/1990496)).
* Even accredited journalists were **held at checkpoints for about an hour** while convoys passed ([AFP via France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20260411-buffets-baristas-but-no-briefings-journalists-frozen-out-of-iran-talks)).

## Ring 4 — The hotel gate

* The hotel told its guests that the **Government of Pakistan had requisitioned the hotel** and that they had to check out by **5 PM on April 8** ([TASS](https://tass.com/society/2114127); [Sunday Guardian](https://sundayguardianlive.com/world/us-iran-peace-talks-in-islamabad-what-is-happening-at-serena-hotel-islamabad-guests-asked-to-leave-ahead-of-possible-us-iran-talks-182733/)). The requisition ran until Sunday evening ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1990053)).
* The Serena's own layout helps. A former Islamabad police chief told Reuters that its entry and exit points sit "at an adequate distance from the main compound," and that its security staff are mostly retired security officials. A former inspector general described "multi-layered, strict security checks" ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/pakistani-five-star-hotel-becomes-unlikely-site-us-iran-talks-2026-04-11/); [Arab News mirror](https://www.arabnews.pk/pakistan/pakistani-five-star-hotel-becomes-unlikely-site-for-us-iran-talks-2639558)).
* In the second lockdown, the Serena could be reached **only through its front entrance**, with strict limits on who and what came through ([Pakistan Observer](https://pakobserver.net/islamabad-road-closures-update-as-red-zone-sealed-ahead-of-anticipated-islamabad-talks-2-0/)).

<div style={{clear:'both'}} />

## Ring 5 — Inside the building

* Al Jazeera's reconstruction: guests were evacuated days earlier, **floors were secured and staff were vetted** ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/13/how-the-us-iran-talks-in-islamabad-unfolded)).
* The National reported that **nearly all staff were confined to certain floors or kept off the property**, that security services swept the building, and that a Pakistani official called it a **"diplomatic fortress"** ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/04/13/inside-us-iran-talks-venue-in-islamabad-where-remnants-of-tense-diplomacy-linger/)).
* Operational staff told Reuters the talks ran across **two separate wings and one common area**: one wing for each side and a shared area for meetings with the Pakistani mediators ([The New Arab, citing Reuters](https://www.newarab.com/news/us-iran-leave-door-open-dialogue-after-tense-islamabad-talks)).
* **Offices of the Foreign Ministry and the Army Chief** were set up beside the negotiating corridor ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/04/13/inside-us-iran-talks-venue-in-islamabad-where-remnants-of-tense-diplomacy-linger/)).

## Ring 6 — The U.S.-secured area

At the top of a staircase near the lobby was a **U.S.-secured area**. Vance's private office sat at the foot of those stairs and was open only to **senior U.S. officials and the Secret Service** ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/04/13/inside-us-iran-talks-venue-in-islamabad-where-remnants-of-tense-diplomacy-linger/)). A U.S. advance team of about **30 security staff** had arrived before the delegation ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1990069); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/9/us-iran-talks-in-pakistan-whos-attending-whats-on-the-agenda)).

## What the rings add up to

No source describes a way for an ordinary resident, tourist or reporter to reach the Serena's lobby between **April 8 and April 12**. Each layer was controlled by the Pakistani army, police or the U.S. Secret Service, and each one screened people before the next. *This conclusion is this site's reading of the reports above; no official has published the access lists.* Who made it through is set out on [Allowed In vs Kept Out](./allowed_vs_kept_out).
