# What X Posted

These are the posts that spread the Serena claim, quoted as they appeared. They are **claims and commentary**, not findings of this site. The analysis is on [The Phone Claim](./phones_uvu_to_serena) and [How Phones Are Tracked](./how_phones_tracked).

## The origin: Tony Seruga

**@TonySeruga, April 11, 2026** (with graphic; tens of thousands of likes, millions of views):

> "Raw GPS data shows that 44 foreign-registered cellphones were in the immediate area of Charlie Kirk on that sad day: 16 Israeli, 13 Chinese, 12 Russian, and 3 Iranian. Our system just alerted us that 4 of the 16 Israeli devices that were in Utah that day, have arrived in Islamabad last evening, at the Islamabad Serena Hotel."

**@TonySeruga, earlier (on UVU):**

> "There were dozens of IMSI-catchers … present during Charlie Kirk's assassination … These tools helped identify phone activity, including reports of 12–14 foreign-registered cellphones (all potentially Israeli) near the site. The National Security Agency (NSA) surveillance data identified 12 Israeli-registered cellphones … This information was shared with federal law enforcement and subsequently passed to the White House and differs greatly from raw, unfiltered GPS data … Disclaimer: Alternative explanations … includes the possibility that they belonged to foreign nationals studying at Utah Valley University's Center for National Security Studies … however, that doesn't explain the Chinese, Russian, and Iranian devices."

**@TonySeruga, April 12, 2026** reported the talks breaking down: "'No Deal': Vance Leaves Islamabad As Iran Talks Stall Over Nukes."

## The amplifier: Project Constitution

**@ProjectConstitu, April 11, 2026:**

> "PROOF: Israeli Cell Phones Linked to Charlie Kirk's Assassination Just Surfaced At The Islamabad Serena Hotel — Where Iran Peace Talks Are Happening Right Now … We have been tracking 16 Israeli registered cell phones that were pinged at UVU the moment the shots were fired. FOUR of those phones have just been traced to the Islamabad Serena Hotel … Source: Data obtained via independent SIGINT tracking. **(Image of tracked phones is a recreation and NOT real)** … From: @TonySeruga."

This site does not suggest that any delegate was involved ([Who Was There](./delegations)).

## The platform's own note

X's Grok summaries attached to these threads describe the phone claim as unverified SIGINT from Seruga, note that the images are labeled recreations, and say no official confirmation exists. This is the same status this section records. The section's job is to list the records that would change that status ([Records That Would Settle It](./records_to_settle)).
