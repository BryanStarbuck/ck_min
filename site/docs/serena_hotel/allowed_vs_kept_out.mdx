# Allowed In vs Kept Out

From **April 8 to April 12, 2026**, the **Islamabad Serena Hotel** was not a hotel in the normal sense. It was a government site run by the state for a single event. This page sorts everyone mentioned in the reporting into two groups: people who could get in, and people who were kept out.

Everything on this page happened inside the Red Zone, where even the banners for the talks hung on government buildings that the public could not approach that week.

<div style={{clear:'both'}} />

## Could get in

| Who | Basis for access | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Pakistani officials** running the talks: Foreign Ministry, Army Chief's office, the Prime Minister's team | Offices were set up inside the building beside the negotiating corridor | [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/04/13/inside-us-iran-talks-venue-in-islamabad-where-remnants-of-tense-diplomacy-linger/) |
| **Pakistani security forces**: army, Rangers, police, security services | Swept the building and held the grounds and roads | [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/04/13/inside-us-iran-talks-venue-in-islamabad-where-remnants-of-tense-diplomacy-linger/); [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/apr/10/islamabad-pakistan-negotiations-iran-us) |
| **The U.S. delegation**, led by Vice President JD Vance | Accredited, and slept at the Serena | [The National, April 26](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/04/26/pakistan-trying-to-broker-us-iran-deal-but-prospects-keep-slipping-away/) |
| **U.S. advance and Secret Service teams** | Arrived before the delegation; held the U.S.-secured area | [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1990069); [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/04/13/inside-us-iran-talks-venue-in-islamabad-where-remnants-of-tense-diplomacy-linger/) |
| **The Iranian delegation**, led by Speaker Ghalibaf | Accredited; lodged at the Marriott and driven to the Serena for sessions | [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/04/13/inside-us-iran-talks-venue-in-islamabad-where-remnants-of-tense-diplomacy-linger/) |
| **Vetted hotel staff** | Background-checked; most were confined to certain floors or kept off the property | [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/13/how-the-us-iran-talks-in-islamabad-unfolded); [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/04/13/inside-us-iran-talks-venue-in-islamabad-where-remnants-of-tense-diplomacy-linger/) |

Reuters put the two delegations together at **more than 150 people** ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/pakistani-five-star-hotel-becomes-unlikely-site-us-iran-talks-2026-04-11/)). Other summaries give larger figures of about 300 Americans and 70 Iranians. The names of the principals are on [Who Was There](./delegations).

## Kept out

|---|---|---|

## Why this matters

For the phone claim ([The Phone Claim](./phones_uvu_to_serena)), the second table matters as much as the first. A private citizen could not have stayed at the Serena, walked into the lobby, or driven to the gate in that window. *That is this site's inference from the reports above, not a published access list.* If four phones were in the building on the evening of April 10, the people carrying them were almost certainly in the first table, and the Pakistani state kept the records of who they were. No one on this page is accused of carrying any device.
