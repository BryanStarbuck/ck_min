# Why the Serena — A Hotel Built to Be Sealed

<img src="/img/serena_hotel/jinnah_convention_centre_aerial_2014.jpg"
  style={{float:'right', width:'48%', marginLeft:'1.5rem', marginBottom:'1rem'}}
  alt="Elevated view of the domed Jinnah Convention Centre in Islamabad's Red Zone, with the government district behind it, 2014." />

Pakistan had other five-star hotels available. It chose the **Islamabad Serena** because it can be closed off more completely than any of them. This page collects what security experts and officials said about that choice, and the history behind it.

*Photo: the Jinnah Convention Centre, which the Serena lists as the building opposite its entrance and which served as the 2026 media centre. Humza Ahmed, 9 September 2014, CC BY-SA 3.0 — [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jinnah_Convention_Centre,_Islamabad.jpg).*

## What the security experts told Reuters

Reuters asked former police chiefs why the talks were at the Serena ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/pakistani-five-star-hotel-becomes-unlikely-site-us-iran-talks-2026-04-11/); [Arab News mirror](https://www.arabnews.pk/pakistan/pakistani-five-star-hotel-becomes-unlikely-site-for-us-iran-talks-2639558)):

* **Distance from the gate.** Former Islamabad police chief **Tahir Alam Khan** said its entry and exit points are "at an adequate distance from the main compound." A vehicle stopped at the gate is far from the buildings.
* **Security staff from the security services.** The hotel's own security staff are mostly **retired security officials**, according to the same report.
* **Layered checks.** Former inspector general **Kaleem Imam** described "multi-layered, strict security checks" and close coordination with state agencies.
* **Close to the seat of government.** Khan also cited the hotel's "smooth access" to the Prime Minister's House and Parliament House ([Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/abnews/islamabads-serena-hotel-hosts-high-stakes-us-iran-talks-under-heavy-security)).
* **The officials' choice.** Pakistani security officials viewed the Serena as the most secure option, pointing to its fortified grounds and its long record of hosting high-profile international visitors ([Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/abnews/islamabads-serena-hotel-hosts-high-stakes-us-iran-talks-under-heavy-security)).
* **Room to spread out.** About **15 acres** and more than **400 rooms**, enough for both delegations, which Reuters put at over 150 people, to have separate wings.

## Where it sits

The Serena is on **Constitution Avenue** beside the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs**, opposite the **Jinnah Convention Centre**, inside the [Red Zone](./red_zone). Pakistan can seal that district on short notice, as it did for the [talks](./security_rings) and again for the [second lockdown](./second_lockdown). The hotel sits in its own grounds of about 5.5 hectares ([The Hotel Itself](./islamabad_serena_hotel)).

## It has done this before

* **SCO summit, October 2024.** Before the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation heads-of-government meeting in Islamabad, a superintendent of police from the Prime Minister's Office inspected security at the Serena and promised "robust security for the delegations" ([APP](https://www.app.com.pk/national/sp-inspects-security-measures-at-serena-hotel-for-sco-summit/)).
* **Tighter in 2026.** Dawn reported that the April 2026 arrangements were **more stringent than** those for the SCO summit ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1990053)).
* **Repeat bookings by the state.** The government requisitioned the hotel twice in two weeks in April 2026 ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1990053); [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/04/19/evacuations-start-at-marriott-serena-hotels-for-next-round-of-us-iran-talks/)).

## Why the chain is hardened

Serena hotels in the region have been targeted before:

* **Kabul Serena, January 14, 2008.** Attackers in police uniforms stormed the hotel and six people were killed ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Kabul_Serena_Hotel_attack)). The Kabul hotel was attacked again in 2014 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Kabul_Serena_Hotel_shooting)).
* **Quetta Serena, April 21, 2021.** A car bomb in the parking area killed five people. The Chinese ambassador was reported to be the likely target ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quetta_Serena_Hotel_bombing)).
* **Islamabad Marriott, September 2008.** The truck bombing of the Marriott happened a short distance away in the same city. Reuters noted the Serena was picked despite that history ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/pakistani-five-star-hotel-becomes-unlikely-site-us-iran-talks-2026-04-11/)). In 2026 the Iranian delegation stayed at the Marriott.

## What this means for the phone claim

The Serena was chosen because it keeps outsiders out and because the state controls who comes in. That is also why the [phone claim](./phones_uvu_to_serena) points to such a small group of people. *This link between the hotel's design and the claim is this site's reasoning, not something the sources above state.*
