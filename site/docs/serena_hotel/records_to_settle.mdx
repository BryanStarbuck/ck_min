# Records That Would Settle It

The Serena claim can be tested. Each half depends on records that exist somewhere. This page lists them, who holds them, and what each would show. A claim that points to specific, existing records is one that can be proven or disproven.

## Records for the UVU half

| Record | Held by | What it would show |
|---|---|---|
| Tower dumps for the UVU cell sites, Sep 10, 2025, 11 AM – 1 PM MDT | Carriers; FBI and Utah County via warrant | Every IMSI/IMEI on the local network, including roaming Israeli SIMs (MCC 425) |
| IMSI-catcher logs from the manhunt | FBI, Utah DPS, whoever deployed them | Devices captured around the scene |
| NSA / Utah Data Center collection on the event | NSA | The alleged 12-device count and how it was filtered |
| The White House briefing Owens describes | NSC / White House | Whether the count was reported upward, and when |
| NCTC foreign-nexus review notes | NCTC (Joe Kent) | Whether foreign devices were among the leads before the review was halted |

## Records for the Serena half

| Record | Held by | What it would show |
|---|---|---|
| Red Zone gate and hotel entry logs, Apr 8–12, 2026 | Pakistan Interior Ministry, Islamabad Police | Every person admitted to the Serena |
| Hotel staff and vendor access list | Serena management, Pakistani security | The vetted staff on site |
| Accreditation lists for advance teams and delegations | Pakistan MoFA; U.S. State Department and Secret Service; Iranian MFA | Who was cleared to enter, and when they arrived |
| Pakistani carrier roaming logs for the Red Zone | PTA and carriers; Pakistani intelligence | Whether any Israeli SIM registered at all |
| The Serena's own RF / IMSI-catcher sweep | Pakistani and U.S. security teams | Devices detected inside the cordon |

## Records for the link between them

| Record | Held by | What it would show |
|---|---|---|
| The device identifiers behind "4 of the 16" | Tony Seruga / his data vendor | Whether the same IDs appear in both places |
| The "system" that "alerted" on April 11 | Same | Whether it is commercial ad-location data, SIGINT, or something else ([How Phones Are Tracked](./how_phones_tracked)) |
| The geofence used for "the Serena" | Same | Whether the fix is inside the hotel or somewhere nearby in the Red Zone |
| Later sightings of the same IDs, e.g. Bürgenstock in June | Same | Whether the devices follow the diplomatic track |

## Questions for the source

The fastest progress would come from a published answer to five questions:

1. What kind of data is it: carrier, SIGINT, or commercial advertising IDs?
2. Is the "16" at UVU a raw count and the "12" a filtered NSA count, and is the "4" drawn from the 16?
3. What time zone is "last evening"? Was it the evening of **April 10 in Islamabad**, before the U.S. principals arrived?
4. How large is the Serena geofence, and does it include the Foreign Ministry or One Constitution Avenue next door?
5. Were the four devices seen anywhere else, such as Butler, the Marriott, or Bürgenstock?

## How the records could be forced out

U.S. records can be reached by FOIA, congressional request, or discovery in *State of Utah v. Robinson*. Pakistani records cannot be forced by any U.S. process. If the four phones were real, the Pakistani state is the party that knows whose they were. The proposed [Charlie Kirk disclosure laws](/Fix/overview) would compel the U.S.-held items above, including NSA collection and foreign-lead files.
