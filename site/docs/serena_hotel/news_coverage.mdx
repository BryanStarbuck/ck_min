## The lockdown, round one

* Dawn, April 9 — [Red Zone sealed as capital prepares for high-stakes Iran-US talks](https://www.dawn.com/news/1990053). The sealing plan, and the comparison with the 2024 SCO summit.
* Express Tribune, April 9 — [Foolproof security set for Islamabad talks](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2601969/foolproof-security-set-for-islamabad-talks). Public holidays and Interior Minister Naqvi's assurances.
* Dawn, April 9 — [Islamabad takes centre stage](https://www.dawn.com/news/1990069). The U.S. advance security team.
* The Media Line via Jerusalem Post, April 10 — [Pakistan heightens security in capital](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-892588). The army takes over the Red Zone; U.S. advance teams land.
* The Guardian, April 10 — [Islamabad prepares to host historic negotiations](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/apr/10/islamabad-pakistan-negotiations-iran-us). Guests evicted and a 3 km army-held cordon.
* BBC, April 10 — [The high-stakes diplomacy that led to Pakistan hosting the talks](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c3dl0g4rgn5o). Why Pakistan was host, and about 10,000 personnel deployed.
* Gulf News, April 10 — [Pakistan ramps up security ahead of US-Iran talks](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/pakistan-ramps-up-security-ahead-of-us-iran-talks-in-islamabad-1.500501499).
* TASS, April 8 — [the hotel's notice to guests](https://tass.com/society/2114127) that the government had requisitioned it.

## Inside the hotel

* Reuters, April 11 — [Pakistani five-star hotel becomes unlikely site for US-Iran talks](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/pakistani-five-star-hotel-becomes-unlikely-site-us-iran-talks-2026-04-11/). Why the Serena was chosen. See [Why the Serena](./why_serena_chosen).
* Al Jazeera, April 13 — [How the US-Iran talks in Islamabad unfolded](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/13/how-the-us-iran-talks-in-islamabad-unfolded). Floors secured, staff vetted, and the 21 hours of talks.
* The National, April 13 — [Inside the US-Iran talks venue](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/04/13/inside-us-iran-talks-venue-in-islamabad-where-remnants-of-tense-diplomacy-linger/). The "diplomatic fortress" and the room layout.
* Al Jazeera, April 9 — [Who's attending, what's on the agenda](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/9/us-iran-talks-in-pakistan-whos-attending-whats-on-the-agenda).
* Arabian Business, April 11 — [Islamabad's Serena Hotel hosts high-stakes talks under heavy security](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/abnews/islamabads-serena-hotel-hosts-high-stakes-us-iran-talks-under-heavy-security). Why officials judged it the most secure option.
* Gulf Times (Reuters), April 11 — [Five-star hotel becomes unlikely site for US-Iran talks](https://www.gulf-times.com/article/723775/international/five-star-hotel-becomes-unlikely-site-for-us-iran-talks). The full Reuters text on the hotel's design.
* Reuters via Jerusalem Post — [Iran negotiators got Pakistan escort home from peace talks](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-893369). About two dozen jets and airborne early warning.

* Xinhua, April 16 — [Security in Islamabad beefed up again](https://english.news.cn/20260416/64b81d232c7f4d6288e49d7d70fb6165/c.html). Forces on the Nur Khan–Serena road almost doubled.
* Al Arabiya (Reuters), April 19 — [Islamabad ramps up security near hotels](https://english.alarabiya.net/News/middle-east/2026/04/19/islamabad-ramps-up-security-near-hotels-as-preparations-appear-under-way-for-usiran-talks). Barbed wire and police near the Serena; guests told to leave.
* ProPakistani, April 19 — [Evacuations start at Marriott, Serena hotels](https://propakistani.pk/2026/04/19/evacuations-start-at-marriott-serena-hotels-for-next-round-of-us-iran-talks/).
* Pakistan Observer, April 19 — [Road closures as Red Zone sealed](https://pakobserver.net/islamabad-road-closures-update-as-red-zone-sealed-ahead-of-anticipated-islamabad-talks-2-0/). Margalla Gate only.
* Dawn, April 20 — [Red Zone sealed again](https://www.dawn.com/news/1993271). Snipers, rooftops and force numbers.
* Pakistan Today, April 21 — [Islamabad locked down, offices and schools move online](https://www.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/04/21/islamabad-locked-down-offices-schools-move-online-as-red-zone-sealed-ahead-of-us-iran-talks).
