# Who Was There — The Delegations

This page records who had official access to the **Islamabad Serena Hotel** during the **April 11–12, 2026** talks. It exists because the phone claim turns on who was inside a sealed building ([Who Could Get Inside](./hotel_lockdown_access)).

<div style={{border:'1px solid #d0d7de', borderLeft:'4px solid #1a73e8', padding:'0.75rem 1rem', margin:'1rem 0'}}>

**Read this first.** No one named on this page is alleged by this site, or by the sources it quotes, to have carried any of the phones described in [The Phone Claim](./phones_uvu_to_serena). Being inside the hotel is not evidence of anything. The list shows how small the access circle was.

</div>

## United States (about 300 people)

Official reporting put the U.S. team at about 300 members, including subject-matter experts, with more support staff in Washington ([Wikipedia: Islamabad Talks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad_Talks)). The U.S. delegation **stayed at the Serena** ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/04/26/pakistan-trying-to-broker-us-iran-deal-but-prospects-keep-slipping-away/)).

* Vice President **JD Vance**, head of delegation. He stopped briefly at the U.S. Embassy before arriving at the Serena ([Fox News live updates](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/trump-iran-war-strait-hormuz-pakistan-talks-israel-04-11-26)).
* **Steve Witkoff**, Special Envoy.
* **Jared Kushner**, presidential adviser without an official title.
* **Andrew Baker**, Deputy National Security Advisor and National Security Advisor to the Vice President.
* **Michael Vance**, Special Advisor to the Vice President for Asian Affairs.
* U.S. **Secret Service** and advance teams, who controlled the U.S.-secured area of the hotel ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/04/13/inside-us-iran-talks-venue-in-islamabad-where-remnants-of-tense-diplomacy-linger/)).

President Trump did not attend and took part by phone. Some summaries say Witkoff and Kushner stayed home. That describes the **late-April** second round, which Trump called off. For the April 11–12 round, Pakistan's Foreign Ministry and contemporary reporting place both men in Islamabad ([Pakistan MoFA](https://mofa.gov.pk/press-releases/arrival-of-the-us-delegation-for-islamabad-talks); [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1990743)).

## Iran (about 70 people)

The Iranian delegation arrived late on **Friday, April 10**. Its flight reportedly turned off its call sign over Pakistani airspace ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/13/how-the-us-iran-talks-in-islamabad-unfolded)). The delegation was lodged at the **Marriott** and driven to the Serena for sessions.

* **Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf**, Speaker of Parliament, head of delegation.
* **Abbas Araghchi**, Foreign Minister.
* **Reza Amiri Moghadam**, Ambassador to Pakistan.
* **Ali Akbar Ahmadian**, Supreme National Security Council; **Ali Bagheri Kani**, deputy to the SNSC.
* **Esmail Ahmadi Moghadam**, president of the National Defence University.
* **Abdolnaser Hemmati**, Central Bank Governor.
* Deputy foreign ministers, members of parliament, and senior IRGC representatives ([Wikipedia: Islamabad Talks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad_Talks)).

## Pakistan (host and mediator)

* Prime Minister **Shehbaz Sharif**.
* Field Marshal **Asim Munir**, Chief of Army Staff / Chief of Defence Forces. He is widely reported to have a direct line to President Trump, and his office inside the hotel sat beside the negotiating corridor.
* Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister **Ishaq Dar**.
* Interior Minister **Mohsin Naqvi**, whose ministry ran the security cordon.
* Pakistani police, Rangers, army and intelligence personnel, plus vetted hotel staff.

## Who was not there

**Israel had no delegation.** Pakistan does not recognize Israel or have diplomatic relations with it, and Pakistani passports are marked valid for "all countries of the world except Israel" ([Wikipedia: Pakistani passport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_passport)). That is why the claim stands out: there was no Israeli party at these talks to account for Israeli-registered phones. Accounts conflict over whether Vance spoke with Prime Minister Netanyahu by phone during or after the talks, and those accounts are unverified.

## Later rounds

The follow-up talks at **Bürgenstock**, Switzerland, from **June 21, 2026** again involved Vance, Witkoff and Kushner, Ghalibaf and Araghchi, Sharif and Munir, with Qatar's Prime Minister as co-mediator ([swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/foreign-affairs/the-iran-us-summit-at-b%C3%BCrgenstock-in-the-north-west-has-officially-begun/91624667)). No one has claimed that any UVU phone appeared there. See the [timeline](./talks_timeline).
