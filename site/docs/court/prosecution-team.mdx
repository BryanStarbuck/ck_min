# Prosecution Team — Utah County

The **Utah County Attorney's Office** prosecutes **State of Utah v. Tyler Robinson** as a **capital
aggravated-murder** case. All named prosecutors are **alive**; we document **public roles and court
findings**, not allege they knew of the assassination in advance or committed crimes.

The office's theory rests on the state's account that Charlie Kirk was killed by a single rifle shot — an account citizen investigators dispute at length in [Cause of Death](/Cause_of_Death/overview).

## Reported prosecution theory

Per charging narratives and court commentary, the state relies on a bundle of evidence:

* **Scene rifle** — Mauser-pattern firearm recovery and **spent casing** comparison (casing match
  reported separately from **inconclusive** bullet-jacket comparison).
* **DNA** — reported consistency with Robinson on trigger, casing, cartridges, towel, screwdriver.
* **Surveillance and witness statements** — Losee rooftop timeline in affidavit (UVU Losee).
* **Communications** — alleged **Discord messages** and letter ([discord evidence](./discord-evidence-court)).

The state is also reported to be moving the autopsy jacket fragment to **FBI Virtual Comparison
Microscopy** after ATF returned **inconclusive** ([ballistics](./ballistics-atf-cbla)).

## Discovery and scheduling fights

Commentary alleges prosecutors:

* **Object** to some defense discovery requests while pushing the **preliminary hearing** forward.
* Produce a very large discovery set (reported **~600,000 files**) under protective orders while
  contested originals remain disputed ([discovery page](./discovery-brady-disputes)).
* May **limit forensic extractions from electronics** offered at preliminary hearing per research notes.

Defense-aligned commentary names **FBI Director Kash Patel** in withholding disputes — that is
**commentary**, not a court finding against any prosecutor.

## June 2026 — Ballard contempt

Judge **[Tony Graf](/court/judge-tony-graf)** reportedly found **Christopher Ballard** in **civil contempt** for media
statements describing "ample evidence" of guilt despite inconclusive ballistics on the jacket
fragment — statements attributed to TMZ, Fox, and USA Today that violated pretrial-publicity
restrictions. Graf ordered **prosecutors pay defense fees** tied to the motion; **death penalty
exposure remained**. See Medical — Trial & Autopsy.

Ballard is **not accused of wrongdoing** on this site beyond the documented contempt finding.

## Public-facing prosecution moments (attributed)

- **David Sturgill** appears in July 2026 pool photos presenting surveillance and managing hearsay objections with the court.
- **Christopher Ballard** (Utah County prosecutor/spokesperson) drew **civil contempt** in June 2026 after commenting on overall case strength in the wake of **inconclusive ATF** publicity — a process fact widely discussed on X (@grok summaries, news clips).
- Prosecution theory in public: DNA on rifle/towel, confession-style statements to partner, surveillance path matching rooftop route, digital messages. Defense contests each pillar's reliability and completeness.
- Investigators should separate **charging theory** from **proven facts** and track whether raw lab/digital production keeps pace with press-friendly exhibits.
