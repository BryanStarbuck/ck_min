## What happened

Week-long **probable-cause** hearing opens. Appearances, camera/decorum protocol, Erika Kirk present. State opens with four planned law-enforcement witnesses.
None of the opening testimony addresses the citizen-investigation finding that Charlie Kirk was killed by an explosive device rather than the rifle round the State describes — see the [microphone analysis](/Mic/overview).

### Witnesses on the stand (Day 1)

* **Chris Bagley** (Spanish Fork PD; former UVU) — heard the shot ~**12:23 p.m.**; reached Losee Center roof ~**12:44 p.m.**; described disturbed gravel/"sniper pad," screwdriver, shoe print; empty pistol holster in grass; **body-worn camera** stopped recording (battery account). Cross on security posture and holster.
* **David Hull** (SBI / Utah DPS case agent) — begins authentication of UVU surveillance, eyewitness/Ring video, and medical-examiner materials; in-court identification of Robinson for prelim purposes. Testimony continues Day 2.

### Other people in the record

* Prosecution: Chad Grunander, Ryan McBride, David Sturgill, Lauren Hunt; Jeff Gray / Chris Ballard present.
* Defense: Kathryn Nester, Richard Novak, Michael Burt, Staci Visser.
* Case agents permitted to remain: Hull and Brian Davis.

## Key public takeaways (press / X)

* Bagley's **body-cam gap** and second **unidentified badge-carrier** dominate citizen-investigator clips.
* Graphic shooting video shown to parties; **not** fully published to public cameras.
* Full color-coded transcript: [Day 1](../Preliminary_Hearing/Day_1).
