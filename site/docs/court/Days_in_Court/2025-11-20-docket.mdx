## What happened

**11/20/2025** appears in research-file **docket notices** for State v. Robinson between the September first appearance and the **12/16/2025** [gag order](../gag-orders-sealing). Public coverage of this specific calendar day is thinner than the first appearance or the July 2026 prelim week; treat it as a **scheduling/filings** node unless full minute entries are released.

## Context in the larger calendar

* Sits after early discovery production begins and before the court's **own-motion gag** that later shaped what participants could say publicly.
