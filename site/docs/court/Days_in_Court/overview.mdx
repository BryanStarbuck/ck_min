# Days in Court

:::caution Legal Disclaimer
**Tyler Robinson is charged, not convicted.** Nothing on this page is a finding that any living person committed a crime, lied under oath, or acted improperly. Court testimony and press accounts are **reported** and may be incomplete. All persons named are **presumed innocent**.
:::



Every reported **court date** in **State of Utah v. Tyler Robinson** (Case No. **251403576**) before **[Judge Tony F. Graf Jr.](/court/judge-tony-graf)** at the **Fourth District Court in Provo**. Format for each entry: **date** · **category** · **what happened**. Names and topics hyperlink into investigation pages. Robinson is **charged, not convicted**.

Sources include public docket notes, press (CNN, AP, ABC, Deseret, KSL, Salt Lake Tribune), pool reporting, and this site's [preliminary-hearing transcripts](../Preliminary_Hearing/overview). Some pretrial dates are known from filings and secondary reporting rather than full public session transcripts.

Read the calendar alongside the physical evidence: the proceedings below all rest on the government's account of how Charlie Kirk was killed, which the [cause-of-death review](/Cause_of_Death/overview) shows is disputed by the majority of independent investigators.

## Full calendar (reported)

* **[09/16/2025](./2025-09-16-first-appearance)** — First appearance — Tyler Robinson appears by video; held without bail on aggravated-murder and related capital-exposure charges.
* **[09/23/2025](./2025-09-23-docket)** — Pretrial docket / status — Early post-charging docket activity under [Judge Graf](/court/judge-tony-graf) (research-file docket notice).
* **[11/20/2025](./2025-11-20-docket)** — Pretrial docket / status — Further pretrial scheduling and filings (research-file docket notice).
* **[12/16/2025](./2025-12-16-gag-order)** — Gag order day — Court issues a broad **own-motion gag order** limiting public commentary by participants ([Gag Orders & Sealing](../gag-orders-sealing)).
* **[03/10/2026](./2026-03-10-seal-motion)** — Sealing fight — Defense seal motion over the **inconclusive ATF** report; media coalition opposition ([Ballistics ATF](../ballistics-atf-cbla)).
* **[05/08/2026](./2026-05-08-camera-access)** — Camera / publicity motions — Defense efforts to restrict courtroom cameras; open-court posture of the case ([preliminary hearing motions](../preliminary-hearing-motions)).
* **[06/2026 — Contempt](./2026-06-ballard-contempt)** — Contempt ruling — Christopher Ballard held in **civil contempt** for pretrial publicity after commenting on case strength ([Gag Orders](../gag-orders-sealing)).
* **[07/06/2026](./2026-07-06-prelim-day-1)** — Preliminary hearing day 1 — Chris Bagley and David Hull open the State's case (rooftop, surveillance, autopsy).
* **[07/07/2026](./2026-07-07-prelim-day-2)** — Preliminary hearing day 2 — Hull surveillance cross; Jennifer Faumuina; FBI DNA analyst Amanda Bakker.
* **[07/08/2026](./2026-07-08-prelim-day-3)** — Preliminary hearing day 3 — Brian Davis on surrender/booking; fight over Lance Twiggs Rule 1102 video.
* **[07/09/2026](./2026-07-09-prelim-day-4)** — Preliminary hearing day 4 — Twiggs recorded interview published; texts/note; Faumuina rifle recovery; Samantha Karner ballistics.
* **[07/10/2026](./2026-07-10-prelim-day-5)** — Preliminary hearing day 5 — Caitlin Oliver ATF DNA; State rests; bind-over briefing set; oral argument **09/01/2026**.
* **[09/01/2026](./2026-09-01-bindover-argument)** — Bind-over oral argument — Scheduled closing arguments on whether probable cause binds the case over for trial.
