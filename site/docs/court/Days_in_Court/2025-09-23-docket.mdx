## What happened

Research-file and site case notes list **09/23/2025** among early **docket notices** after the **09/16** first appearance. Public secondary sources treat this window as ordinary **capital-case scheduling** — counsel, custody, and future hearing dates — rather than a full evidentiary day.

Exact minute-entry text should be confirmed against the official Utah courts docket when available. Until certified docket printouts are published end-to-end, this page records the date as a **reported case-activity day**.
