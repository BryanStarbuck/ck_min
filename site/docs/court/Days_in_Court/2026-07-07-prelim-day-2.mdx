## What happened

### David Hull (continued)

Walks the court through campus surveillance allegedly showing Robinson:

* ~**8:30 a.m.** car into parking garage; courtyard/TPUSA interaction; leave ~**9:25 a.m.**
* Return ~**10:30** hour; food; wooded area; backpack missing on return; leave before **11 a.m.**
* ~**11:53 a.m.** different clothes, limp; rooftop run/crawl prone ~**12:22 p.m.** near shot time; escape to woods with object.
* ~**12:30 a.m. 09/11** vehicle stop on campus road — plate tied to Robinson/mother.

Hull also addresses **crime-scene paving** (learned via news, per citizen clips of testimony), discarded leads, and an **unfired** round on another roof attributed to an officer's rifle.

### Jennifer Faumuina (SBI sergeant)

Search warrants; wooded-area **rifle in towel**; engraved rounds; Dremel and casings from residence; returns later in the week.

### Amanda Bakker (FBI DNA — **defense** witness; only FBI live stand)

Called **out of order** after Faumuina's limited direct so the **defense** could examine the FBI report author (schedule accommodation — McBride on the record). Hours on mixture DNA (towel, screwdriver), **"possible contributor"** language, degradation, and FBI policy against absolute-ID / infallibility claims. Michael Burt leads the technical cross. **No other FBI agent** took the live stand this week.
