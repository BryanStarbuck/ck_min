# Probable Cause Affidavit — Tyler Robinson

This is the Probable Cause Affidavit filed by law enforcement in connection with Tyler Robinson's
arrest. This is the police-side booking document that established the legal basis for Robinson's
detention.

The affidavit contains the official law enforcement account of the timeline of events, including
references to the "early morning hours of September 12, 2025." Investigators have noted that
this language refers to a distinct, later encounter by Utah County investigators — not the initial
Miranda encounter documented in Bates 003996-R2, which occurred at approximately 6:25 PM MDT
on September 11, 2025.

The affidavit is also the document in which the government first commits to its theory of how Charlie Kirk was killed on September 10, 2025 — a theory that the [independent cause-of-death analysis](/Cause_of_Death/overview) argues cannot survive contact with the physical evidence.

Tyler Robinson is charged, not convicted. All references to his alleged conduct are allegations
drawn from official court documents. Nothing in this document constitutes a finding of guilt.

## Key Points

* The affidavit is the law enforcement counterpart to the defense's Miranda timeline argument.
* Its reference to "September 12, 2025 early morning hours" describes Utah County investigators
  arriving after a multi-hour drive from Provo — not the initial Miranda reading at 6:25 PM.
* The document was filed as part of the formal arrest record and establishes the legal basis for
  Robinson's continued detention without bail.
* Readers should consult the original filing and defense responses for the full legal context.

The September 11 turn-in is no longer only a citizen reading of a leaked exhibit. Three records now
point at the same evening: the discovery index lists exhibit **Bates 3996-R1** as
**&quot;Video at WCSO on 9/11/2025&quot;**; page 70 of a defense filing quotes companion exhibit
**Bates 003996-R2** showing the Miranda warning at **06:25 PM** and the invocation of counsel at
**06:26 PM**; and in the clip here the state&apos;s own witness, given an explicit chance to move the
date to September 12, keeps it at September 11.

If Tyler Robinson was in custody and Mirandized at **6:25 PM**, he could not have posted the Discord
confession timestamped **7:57 PM** that same evening. That is the argument, and it is now
document-backed rather than inferred.

Two ordinary explanations stay open and are recorded in full on the linked page: the 6:25 PM
reading could be a misread frame or an internal video clock not synced to wall time, or someone
other than Robinson could have had the account &mdash; which would undo the confession as a
confession by a different route. Nothing here states that any witness or official said anything
untrue.
