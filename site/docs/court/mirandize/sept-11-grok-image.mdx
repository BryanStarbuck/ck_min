generated in the context of research into the Tyler Robinson case and the question of whether

> "Bates 003996-R2" is a Bates-stamped discovery exhibit (standard numbering used by the
> prosecution/Utah County Attorney's Office when producing large volumes of materials to the
> defense). It refers to a video recording (likely bodycam, stationhouse interview, or surveillance
> footage) of Tyler Robinson's encounter with law enforcement. The video shows him being read his
> Miranda rights at approximately **6:25 p.m. MDT on September 11, 2025** (with an internal video
> timestamp around 03:36:53 or similar, depending on the exact frame).
>
> The exhibit has been referenced in recent defense filings and analysis (particularly around
> late March 2026) to highlight alleged timeline discrepancies: Robinson was already in custody
> and Mirandized (and reportedly invoked his right to counsel) hours before the official
> "surrender" narrative and a Discord confession message attributed to him. Podcaster/investigator
> Baron Coleman has prominently discussed and quoted from it in his coverage.

## Analysis Notes

Key facts confirmed by the Grok analysis and OCR extraction:

* **Exhibit identity:** Bates 003996-R2 is a discovery exhibit produced by the Utah County
  Attorney's Office — a video recording of Robinson's law enforcement encounter.
* **Miranda time:** The video shows Robinson being read his rights at approximately
  **6:25 PM MDT on September 11, 2025**.
* **Internal timestamp:** The video's internal clock shows approximately 03:36:53, consistent
  with a UTC-offset from the actual wall-clock time.
* **Significance:** Robinson allegedly invoked his right to counsel immediately after the
  Miranda reading — and the Discord messages attributed to him appeared hours later, after
  this Miranda encounter.
* **Coverage:** Investigator Baron Coleman has publicly discussed and quoted from this exhibit
  in his reporting on the case.

The September 11 turn-in is no longer only a citizen reading of a leaked exhibit. Three records now
point at the same evening: the discovery index lists exhibit **Bates 3996-R1** as
**&quot;Video at WCSO on 9/11/2025&quot;**; page 70 of a defense filing quotes companion exhibit
**Bates 003996-R2** showing the Miranda warning at **06:25 PM** and the invocation of counsel at
**06:26 PM**; and in the clip here the state&apos;s own witness, given an explicit chance to move the
date to September 12, keeps it at September 11.

If Tyler Robinson was in custody and Mirandized at **6:25 PM**, he could not have posted the Discord
confession timestamped **7:57 PM** that same evening. That is the argument, and it is now
document-backed rather than inferred.

Two ordinary explanations stay open and are recorded in full on the linked page: the 6:25 PM
reading could be a misread frame or an internal video clock not synced to wall time, or someone
other than Robinson could have had the account &mdash; which would undo the confession as a
confession by a different route. Nothing here states that any witness or official said anything
untrue.
