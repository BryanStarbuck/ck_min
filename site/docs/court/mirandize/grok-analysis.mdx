# Grok AI Analysis: Mirandizing Date

This document is an AI analysis (Grok) of the Bates 003996-R2 exhibit and surrounding court
filings. It addresses the question of when Tyler Robinson was Mirandized — September 11 or
September 12, 2025 — based on publicly available court documents and reported discovery materials.

The analysis references the bodycam video showing Robinson's Miranda encounter, the defense
filing from March 30, 2026, and the Probable Cause Affidavit. Key findings include the 6:25 PM
timestamp on the Miranda reading and the subsequent invocation of counsel at 6:26 PM.

This document is provided as research reference material. All claims about Robinson, who is
charged but not convicted, are allegations. Use attribution language when citing this analysis.

The September 11 turn-in is no longer only a citizen reading of a leaked exhibit. Three records now
point at the same evening: the discovery index lists exhibit **Bates 3996-R1** as
**&quot;Video at WCSO on 9/11/2025&quot;**; page 70 of a defense filing quotes companion exhibit
**Bates 003996-R2** showing the Miranda warning at **06:25 PM** and the invocation of counsel at
**06:26 PM**; and in the clip here the state&apos;s own witness, given an explicit chance to move the
date to September 12, keeps it at September 11.

If Tyler Robinson was in custody and Mirandized at **6:25 PM**, he could not have posted the Discord
confession timestamped **7:57 PM** that same evening. That is the argument, and it is now
document-backed rather than inferred.

Two ordinary explanations stay open and are recorded in full on the linked page: the 6:25 PM
reading could be a misread frame or an internal video clock not synced to wall time, or someone
other than Robinson could have had the account &mdash; which would undo the confession as a
confession by a different route. Nothing here states that any witness or official said anything
untrue.
