XCHANGE (subscription, PAID):
  portal:  https://pubapps.utcourts.gov/XchangeWEB/XchangeWebServlet
           -> redirects 200 to https://xchange.utcourts.gov/XchangeWEB/XchangeWebServlet
  subscribe by emailing signed agreement to xchange@utcourts.gov, approval ~48 business hrs
  Monthly account: $25 setup + $40/mo, 500 searches free, $0.35/extra search, $1.00 PER DOCUMENT
  One-time/guest:  $10 + $0.35/search + $1.00/document
  Coverage: "All district court public documents available in electronic format and
  filed since July 1, 2010 are available on Xchange."  <-- this is the ONE route that
  reaches EVERY non-sealed filing in 251403576 including everything after Apr 2026.

REQUEST A COURT RECORD (clerk, paid, no subscription):
  Form: Request for Court Record — https://www.utcourts.gov/content/dam/media/docs/Request_for_Record.pdf
  Copies $0.25/sheet; electronic media $15/unit; audio/video $15 per half-day of testimony;
  staff time first 15 min free then $15-$100/hr; email delivery $5 for <=10 pages then $0.50/page.
  Response usually within 10 business days.

TRANSCRIPTS (separate system — this is the route to hearing transcripts):
  https://pubapps.utcourts.gov/TranscriptWEB/publicrequest.jsp    200 (24,379 b)
  Statutory rates, quoted verbatim from that page:
    $6.00/page regular (within 30 days)  ~ approx $250.00 per court hour
    $7.50/page 14 calendar days
    $9.00/page 7 calendar days
    $10.50/page 1-3 day
    $12.00/page 24 hour
  Page also offers "Request Audio Copy" and "Court Contact Information".

AUDIO/VIDEO RECORDINGS: request from the court where the hearing was held; fee per
Rule 4-202.08; $15 per half day of testimony; media = data CD (up to 27 hrs) or audio CD (80 min).
