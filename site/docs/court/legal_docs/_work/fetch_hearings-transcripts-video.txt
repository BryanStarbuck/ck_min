The intake said of the 6-page scanned attachment: "SCANNED IMAGE ONLY, CCITT
300 dpi, no text layer. pdftotext returns nothing. NEEDS OCR to identify with
certainty. Context strongly suggests it is Judge Graf's signed order releasing

It has now been OCR'd and the guess was right in substance. It is:

  RULING AND ORDER RE STIPULATED MOTION TO CLASSIFY TRANSCRIPT AND AUDIO AS
  PRIVATE — Fourth Judicial District Court, Utah County, Case No. 251403576,
  JUDGE TONY F. GRAF, JR. Dated 24 December 2025. Certificate of Notification
  signed by clerk Treena Hansen, 12/29/2025.

Docket references inside it: Dkt. 152 (the stipulated motion), Dkt. 172 (the
11 December 2025 closure hearing), Dkt. 209 (the joint status report identifying
~20 provisions for redaction).

WHAT IT ACTUALLY DECIDES — this is more useful than "the release order" implies.
It rules line by line on what stays hidden in the 24 Oct 2025 transcript:
  * GRANTS the security-based redactions (about half the requests), citing
    Utah R. Jud. Admin. 4-202(2)(B) and State v. Harding, 635 P.2d 33.
  * DENIES redaction at 35:16-17 and 80:22-25 — "Privacy interests do not
    outweigh the public interest in this case."
  * DENIES the four fair-trial redactions at 55:2-7, 60:24-25, 61:1-6 and
    61:13-14, and 64:11-17, holding the material is already in the charging
    instrument and the public arena and the issue is therefore moot
    (State v. Allgier, 2011 UT 47).
  * Orders the AUDIO unsealed only long enough for the clerk to timestamp the
    redacted passages, then RESEALED, with no copy released until that is done.

METHOD, AND ONE THING WORTH KNOWING: ocrmypdf REFUSED the file — "Input PDF has
a digital signature. OCR would alter the document, invalidating the signature."
So the PDF is digitally signed, and it was left byte-for-byte untouched (md5
1b24f84ad575d433dbd4aae2b3b835f7 re-verified after the rename). Pages were
rasterised to 300-dpi greyscale PNGs with pdftoppm and read with tesseract
OUTSIDE the repo. The OCR is written to a separate sidecar:
  2025-12-24_..._Graf.ocr.txt   13,988 B
It is machine OCR of a scan and contains recognition errors (the signature-line
date reads "DATED this 24 day of Yel, 2025" for "Dec."). Cite the PDF, never the
sidecar.
