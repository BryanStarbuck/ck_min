The court held an evidentiary hearing on February 3, 2026, during which there was a closed
portion of the proceeding to protect the identifying information of one of the witnesses who
testified. The closed portion lasted a matter of seconds.

When a hearing has closed portions, the practice of certified court reporters is to indicate
that the court has entered a closed proceeding in the public transcript. The court reporter files a
separate sealed transcript that contains the content of the sealed hearing.

Because the closed portion of the hearing was very short, the court orders that instead of
filing two separate transcripts (a sealed and public transcript), the court reporter may file a private
transcript that includes the entirety of the proceeding. The court reporter is ordered to prepare a
version of the transcript that redacts the closed portion and any other reference to the identifying
information of the protected witness. The redacted transcript may be filed as a public document.

Dated this 23rd day of February, 2026.
