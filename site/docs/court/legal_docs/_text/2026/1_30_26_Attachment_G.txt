A couple of comments:
    - As a general rule, I would deny all GRAMA requests regarding this case as a case still under investigation
       and pending prosecution. Further I would cite to rule 3.6 of the rules of professional responsibility in that
       any material our office releases has the potential to prejudice the potential jury pool.
    - On records that could go either way, please err on the side of denial.
    - I am especially concerned about the release of information from the jail phone calls. In my view, these
       statements are under investigation. Jail phone calls are often the source of confessions and the like. In my
       experience, as soon as the suspect learns his calls are being listened to (and especially public), he will
       stop talking on the phone about the case and we will be unable to gather valuable evidence. We ought to
       treat these as statements under investigation.
    - 911 calls, I understand referring these to other agencies, but I would add that we would deny them as
       potential evidence in the case which is pending.

I know others on the team also have input, so please hold off until you hear from the prosecutors.

Dear Records Custodian,


Pursuant to Utah Code § 63G-2-305(10), this letter serves as formal notice from our office that the records
described below are part of an open and active investigation being conducted by this office. As such, we
request these records be classified as protected and not subject to public disclosure at this time.


Records Subject to Restriction:


All printed and electronic correspondence, arrest records, witness statements, reports, notes,
recordings, internal communications, interviews, body camera footage, or other records related to
the shooting of Charlie Kirk on September 10, 2025.


Under Utah Code § 63G-2-305(10), records are protected if their release would reasonably be expected to
interfere with an investigation, disclose investigative techniques, or deprive a person of a right to a fair
proceeding. Based on this statutory authority, we request that you withhold the release of the above-
referenced records until further written notice from this office.


Please ensure that all staff responsible for handling GRAMA requests are informed of this restriction. If
you receive any requests for these records, please notify our office immediately before taking any action.


If you have any questions or require clarification, feel free to contact my office at 801-851-8001 or
UCAOrecords@utahcounty.gov.


Sincerely,
