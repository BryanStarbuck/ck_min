Rule 4202.02 of the Utah Rules of Judicial Administration provides that “[cJourt records
are public unless otherwise classified by this rule.” There is a presumptive right to access public
court records. State v. Aligier, 2011 UT 47, § 13. However, the public’s right to access “is not
absolute.” Jd. ¥ 14.

Rule 4-202.04 allows a party to file a motion to classify a record as non-public. When a
motion to classify a record has been filed, the party filing the motion must serve the motion on any

representative of the press who has requested notice in the case. UT R J ADMIN 4-202.04(4).

The court has received requests from The Salt Lake Tribune and its reporter Jessica Miller;
St. George News; The Associated Press; Fox13 News; Millard County Chronicle Progress; KOAL
(Castle Country Radio); KUER-FM; Lehi Free Press; The Park Record; CBS Broadcasting; The
New York Times; CNN; Fox News; and Law & Crime Network (collectively, “Media

Intervenors”) for notice of all motions to classify or close records in this case. (Dkt. 164).

The court has also received requests from Deseret News, Deseret Digital Media, Inc. d/b/a
KSL.com, KSL-TV, KSL NewsRadio, KUTV 2News, KSTU Fox13, the Utah News Dispatch,
and the Utah Headliners Chapter of the Society of Professional Journalists (collectively, News

Media) for notice of all motions to reclassify or close records in this case. (Dkt. 150).
The parties filed a joint motion to order a transcript of the October 24, 2025, hearing and
to classify both the transcript and audio as private. (Dkt. 154). This is a motion to classify and to

close a court record that is subject to the process outlined in Rule 4-202.04.

The parties are ORDERED to serve their motion to classify on the members of the press

who have requested notice. The parties must file proof of service with the court.

The court ORDERS that all future motions to classify a record in this case must be served

on representatives of the press who requested notice. The parties filing the motion is responsible

for service of the motion.

The court is required to hold a closure hearing in the following circumstances: “when a
motion or petition to close a record is contested, when the press has requested notice of closure
motions or petitions in a particular case, or when the court decides public interest in the record

warrants a hearing.” UT R J ADMIN 4-202.04(4).

Because the press has requested notice, the court will set a closure hearing at the earliest
date available for all parties, including representatives of the press, to be present. The press has a
right to be heard at the closure hearing. Representatives of the press may file their respective

positions in writing before the hearing, if desired.'

In deciding whether to classify a record, the court may consider “any relevant factor,

interest, or policy.” Utah R. Jud. Admin. 4-202.04(6).
