TYLER JAMES ROBINSON, JUDGE TONY F. GRAF, JR.

The State of Utah has charged Defendant Tyler James Robinson with aggravated murder
and notified the Court and Defendant that the State intends to seek the death penalty. (See State’s
Notice of Intent to Seek the Death Penalty at 1, Dkt. #10.) The matter, as a result, is a capital
felony under Utah Code section 76-5-202(3)(a). Consequently, pursuant to rule 4-201(1)(B) of
the Code of Judicial Administration, the Court hereby ORDERS that “a court reporter licensed
in Utah shall maintain a verbatim record in all court proceedings” in this matter.
