       Pursuant to Rule 14-806 of the Utah Code of Judicial Administration, Kathryn N.

Nester moves the Court for an order admitting Richard G. Novak pro hac vice to act as

co-counsel in the above-captioned matter.

       Mr. Novak, along with Ms. Nester and Mr. Michael Burt, are contracted with the

County of Utah to represent Mr. Robinson, having been charged by the State of Utah

with aggravated murder. This Court has already determined that Mr. Robinson is indigent

and qualifies for the appointment of qualified counsel.

       Mr. Novak is neither licensed to practice law in the State of Utah nor a resident of

Utah. Mr. Novak is admitted to practice in the State of California where he is an attorney

       Undersigned counsel hereby agrees to serve as designated local counsel for the

subject case. She agrees to comply with all applicable provisions of the Utah Rules of

Criminal Procedure and the Utah Code of Judicial Administration, and to readily

communicate with opposing counsel and the Court regarding the conduct of this case.

She also agrees to accept papers when served as well as recognizes the responsibility to

act for and on behalf of the clients in all case-related proceedings, including hearings,

pretrial conferences, and trials, should Mr. Novak fail to respond to any court order.
