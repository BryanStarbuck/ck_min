                 THIS MATTER IS BEFORE THE COURT on Defendant’s request for the appointment

          of counsel in his criminal proceeding pursuant to Rule 8 of the Utah Rules of Criminal Procedure

          and the Utah Indigent Defense Act. The Court hereby finds as follows:

                     1. Defendant has been charged with one count of Aggravated Murder, a first-degree
                     felony, in violation of section 76-5-202 of the Utah Code. The State has indicated its
                     intent on the record that it intends to seek the death penalty. Therefore, Defendant has
                     been charged with an offense for which the punishment may be death. Furthermore,
                     he has been charged with other felonies, and there is a substantial probability that the
                     penalty to be imposed may be confinement in jail or prison. The Court concludes
                     Defendant is entitled to two attorneys whose combined experience meets the
                     requirements of rule 8 of the Utah Rules of Criminal Procedure.
                     2. Kathryn N. Nester has been appointed by the Utah County Commission to
                     represent the Defendant in this matter. Ms. Nester has elected Mr. Richard G. Novak
                     and Mr. Michael N. Burt as co-counsel in this matter.
                     3. On September 16, 2025, the Court determined that Defendant was indigent for the
                     purpose of appointing counsel and orders the Rule 8 qualifications of counsel be filed
                     prior to the hearing on September 29, 2025.
                     4. On September 25, 2025, Ms. Nester filed declarations describing her
                     qualifications, and the qualifications of co-counsel, to appear as counsel and co-

                     counsel in this case.
                     5. Ms. Nester is an attorney duly licensed to practice law in the State of Utah and is
                     qualified under Rule 8 of the Utah Rules of Criminal Procedure to be appointed as
                     lead defense counsel for an indigent defendant charged with an offense for which the
                     punishment may be death.
                     6. The Court concludes that Ms. Nester is a suitable appointee to represent
                     Defendant as lead counsel in this case under Rule 8 of the Utah Rules of Criminal
                     Procedure.
                     7. Ms. Nester has requested Mr. Richard G. Novak and Mr. Michael N. Burt, serve
                     as co-counsel in this case. Both Mr. Novak and Mr. Burt are duly licensed to practice
                     law in the State of California. This Court has admitted Mr. Novak and Mr. Burt to
                     practice in the State of Utah pursuant to Ms. Nester’s duly filed Motion and Consent
                     for Pro Hac Vice Admission of Out of State Counsel and as such, Mr. Novak and Mr.
                     Burt are admitted to practice in Utah, in this case.
                     8. The Court finds Mr. Novak to be suitable and qualified to act as co-counsel in this
                     matter.
                     9. The Court finds Mr. Burt to be suitable and qualified to act as co-counsel in this
                     matter.

                 Based on the foregoing findings, and good cause appearing, the Court hereby orders as

          follows:

                                                       ORDER

                 IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that Kathryn N. Nester, Richard G. Novak, and Michael N.

          Burt are appointed to represent Defendant as counsel in this criminal case. This is the order of

          the Court for the matter addressed herein. No further order is necessary.
