        COMES NOW, Jeffrey A. Neiman, on behalf of Charles James Kirk, the deceased

victim in the above-named case, and files this request for the Court to designate a

representative to exercise the rights of the deceased victim under Utah Code Ann. 77-38-

9(2). That statute provides that “[i]n cases where the victim is deceased … upon request

from the victim’s [family,] the court shall designate a representative … of the victim to

exercise the rights of a victim under [the Rights of Crime Victims Act] on behalf of the

victim.” Id.

        It is requested that Erika Kirk, the widow of Charles James Kirk, be designated the

representative of the victim in this case to exercise all the rights afforded the victim pursuant

to Utah Code Ann. Chapters 77-37 and 77-38. Mrs. Kirk will be represented by attorney

Jeffrey A. Neiman, Esq., of Neiman Mays Floch & Almeida, PLLC, Miami, Florida.
