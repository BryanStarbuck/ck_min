# The Keyboard Note

> **The claim:** The "look under my keyboard" note wasn't a genuine confession — it was a setup, planted or engineered so that Robinson would take the fall and cover for someone else.

**Verdict: UNSUPPORTED** — the note is real and documented; the "it was a setup" reading has nothing behind it.

## The documented facts
Per the [[2025-09-17 - The Search Warrant Affidavit|search-warrant affidavit]], on **9/10/2025** Robinson sent Lance Twiggs a message: *"drop what you are doing, look under my keyboard."* Twiggs found — and photographed — a **handwritten letter**, addressed to *"Luna"* (Robinson's name for Twiggs):

> *"Luna, If you are reading this per my text, then I am so sorry. I left the house this morning on a mission, and set an auto text. I am likely dead, or facing a lengthy prison sentence. **I had the opportunity to take out Charlie Kirk, and I took it.** I don't know if I will/have succeeded… I wish we could have lived in a world where this did not feel necessary… I love you, always, -Tyler."*

See [[The Discord and Text Messages]] and [[Lance Twiggs]].

![[info-confession-note-crop.png|700]]
*From the [[2025-09-16 - The Information and Charges|Information]] (p. 7), under **"The Roommate"**: the "drop what you are doing, look under my keyboard" text and the note it led to — *"I had the opportunity to take out Charlie Kirk and I'm going to take it."* Full filing on its [[2025-09-16 - The Information and Charges|summary page]].*

> **Day 3 (July 8) — the note is now part of the State's motive package being admitted for probable cause.** The State offered the "look under my keyboard" note together with the [[The Discord and Text Messages|Discord/text thread (Exhibit 16)]] and **[[Lance Twiggs|Twiggs's]]** recorded Rule 1102 statement, through **[[Brian Davis|Agent Brian Davis]]**, for **motive** and probable cause. Notably, the defense (**Novak**) kept a **disciplined posture**: fighting the **public replay** of the note on **pretrial-publicity** grounds (*Estes v. Texas*, *Rideau v. Louisiana*, *Archuleta*) [02:50:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=10256s), [03:16:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=11774s) while **declining to concede** the writing is a confession — "I am **not** calling the statements of Mr. Robinson confession[s]" [02:51:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=10260s). That is a **procedural** objection to publicity and confrontation, not evidence that the note is a "setup." The court **overruled** Rule 403 for admissibility (magistrate, not jury) but pledged **redaction / non-publication to protect the jury pool** [02:44:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9886s)–[02:45:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9918s). The note's authenticity was **not** contested on Day 3 — the "setup" reading remains **unsupported**. See [[2026-07-08 - Preliminary Hearing Day 3|Day 3 Legal Review]] and [[The Confession Is Coerced or Fabricated]].

> **Day 4 (July 9) — the note photo was admitted (Exhibit 16.3) and Twiggs testified he read and photographed it.** The court's publication ruling broke Exhibit 16 into its parts, with **16.3 the photograph of the keyboard note**, published with targeted redactions [00:36:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=2192s). In his immunized, recorded statement played the same day, **[[Lance Twiggs]]** described finding the note per Robinson's text — he **read it and photographed it** himself [02:20:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8420s). That puts the note's discovery in the mouth of the person who found it (still un-cross-examined), and again nothing on Day 4 touched its **authenticity** — the "setup" reading stays **unsupported** on the current record. See [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4|Day 4 Legal Review]] and [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4 Transcript|Day 4 Transcript]].

## What fuels the "setup" reading
It is a short leap from the [[Robinson Is a Patsy|patsy]] theory: if you already believe Robinson was framed, a written confession looks like the frame's final piece — something he was steered into leaving to protect a "real" mastermind.

## What the evidence shows
- The note is **first-person and self-directed** — a private goodbye to a partner, tied to a pre-set "auto text," not a statement to police. That is the opposite of a coerced or performative confession.
- It is **corroborated** by the whole convergent record: [[The Rifle|the rifle]] with his DNA, [[The Casing Match and DNA|the casing match]], the [[The Surveillance and the Gait|gait surveillance]], and the other [[The Discord and Text Messages|messages]] (the rifle "drop point," changing clothes, "grandpa's rifle," the surrender note). A forger would have to fake all of that too — see [[A Hypothetical Cover-Up]].
- There is **no evidence** — no second author, no coercion, no inconsistency — offered for the "setup" reading. It is inference from a premise, not a finding.

## The criminal-liability angle
Legally, the note cuts hard **for** the State: *"I left the house this morning on a mission"* and *"I had the opportunity … and I took it"* are strong evidence of **premeditation and intent** — the mental state for aggravated murder. That matters for the defense in two ways covered in [[Defense Strategies and Degrees of Liability]]:
- It undercuts an **intent-negating** defense (extreme emotional distress / "I snapped"), because it reads as planned.
- If the defense ever argued the note was **coerced/planted**, the path is a **suppression / authenticity** fight, or a **duress** narrative — both of which the note's private, voluntary character and its corroboration make very difficult.

## Bottom line
The note is genuine and is among the most damaging pieces of evidence. "It was a setup to cover for someone" is an **unsupported** overlay on a documented confession.
