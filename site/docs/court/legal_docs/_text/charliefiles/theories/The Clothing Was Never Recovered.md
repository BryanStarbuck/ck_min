# The Clothing Was Never Recovered

> **The claim:** The State never recovered the clothing — or the supposed hat and glasses — the shooter wore, so it cannot actually show the person on that roof was Robinson.

**Verdict: the *fact* is TRUE** — on the current record nothing the shooter wore was recovered — **but as proof of innocence it is UNSUPPORTED.** The absence is a genuine identity gap *and* is equally consistent with the State's own theory that Robinson **disposed of** his clothing.

## What fuels it
The State's timeline has the shooter **change clothes** and **ditch a backpack**, and the Information then charges Robinson with **disposing of his clothing** — yet none of it was ever produced in court.

## What the evidence shows
- **The appearance markers.** On the two morning visits the figure wears a **maroon shirt**, shorts, and **Converse** (Day 2 [00:47:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=2868s)); on the **shooting visit** he is in **"different clothing"** with a **limp** (Day 2 [00:51:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=3085s)). That shooting-visit outfit is documented in the **FBI-released person-of-interest stills** (shown in the Day-2 video): a **dark cap, sunglasses, and a black American-flag-graphic long-sleeve shirt** over dark jeans — a **face-obscuring disguise**.
- **Never recovered:**
  - the **shooting-visit disguise** — the **cap, the sunglasses, and the flag shirt** (visible in the released images, but **none recovered**);
  - the **blue backpack** carried on the second visit, which vanishes after the wooded area (Day 2 [00:48:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=2917s)). *(A separate DNA-tested backpack turned out to be a **bystander's**, not the suspect's — Day 2 [07:27:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26860s).)*
- **What *was* recovered:** the towel-wrapped **rifle** (in the woods), the **screwdriver**, and DNA swabs — not the clothing, hat, glasses, or backpack.
- The State **charges Robinson with disposing of his clothing** (obstruction — see [[2025-09-16 - The Information and Charges|the Information]]). So on the State's *own* theory, the clothes were destroyed, which explains the absence. *(Police **did** search his residence — turning up a **matching engraved casing** and **targets with bullet holes** — but **not** the shooting-visit clothing, hat, or glasses, consistent with disposal. See [[The Engraved Casings]].)*

## The two readings
- **The State:** the disposal **is** the obstruction charge — consciousness of guilt — and the identification rests on the **DNA, the surveillance track, the confession, and the surrender**, not on producing the clothing. See [[The Established Record]].
- **The defense:** with no recovered clothing, hat, or glasses, there is **no appearance match** tying the roof figure to Robinson. Identity is built entirely by [[How Robinson Was Identified|inference]], and the missing items are a real [[Insufficiency of the Evidence|reasonable-doubt]] gap — the State cannot put the shooter's clothes on Robinson.

## Bottom line
It is **TRUE** that nothing the shooter wore was recovered — a genuine hole in an already inferential identity case. But that absence is **not proof of innocence**; it cuts as **reasonable doubt** and is consistent with the State's disposal theory. Whether it matters is a **trial** question, on the current record. See [[How Robinson Was Identified]] and [[Gaps in the Investigation]].
