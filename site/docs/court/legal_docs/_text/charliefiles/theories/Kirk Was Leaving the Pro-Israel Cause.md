# Kirk Was Leaving the Pro-Israel Cause

> **The claim:** Shortly before his death, Charlie Kirk wrote in a private group chat that he had "no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause"; Candace Owens revealed the messages and TPUSA confirmed they were authentic.

**Verdict: TRUE** that the message exists and TPUSA confirmed it — **UNSUPPORTED** as evidence of a motive for the assassination.

## What fuels it
In October 2025, **Candace Owens** released screenshots of a private group chat. Amplified across X and beyond, they were framed as showing Kirk "made powerful enemies in the pro-Israel sphere" shortly before he was killed.

## The messages, as reported
The messages come from a **private WhatsApp group** that included Jewish associates — among them *Newsweek*'s **Josh Hammer**, who confirmed he was on the thread. As quoted in the reporting, Kirk wrote:

> *"Just lost another huge Jewish donor. $2 million a year because we won't cancel Tucker."*
> *"I'm thinking of inviting Candace."*
> *"Jewish donors [are] playing into all the stereotypes."*
> *"I cannot and will not be bullied like this"* — and said the donors were *"leaving me no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause."*

Another member of the thread replied *"Ugghhh"*; someone later added *"Please don't invite Candace."* The unnamed **$2 million donor** was identified by the *New York Times* as tech figure **Robert Shillman**, who pulled the gift over **Tucker Carlson**'s participation in a TPUSA event. *(Quotes as reported by JTA, Oct 10, 2025; authenticity confirmed by TPUSA's [[Andrew Kolvet]].)*

## What the evidence shows
- **The messages are real, and TPUSA confirmed it.** Spokesman **[[Andrew Kolvet]]** confirmed their authenticity on Kirk's own show (early Oct 2025) — *"I actually am really excited that the truth is out there"* — the quoted text is above.
- **Context matters.** Per TPUSA, the dispute was with a specific donor (reported to be tech figure **Robert Shillman**) angry that TPUSA kept **Tucker Carlson** at its AmericaFest event. Kolvet and colleagues characterized Kirk's actual position as **"complicated and nuanced"** — he wanted the Gaza war to end but **remained broadly pro-Israel** (see [[Netanyahu Read Kirks Letter]], where his own letter affirms deep support). The chat reportedly included commentator **Josh Hammer**.
- **The inference is where it breaks.** The "48 hours before" timing is **Owens's characterization**, not independently confirmed, and — crucially — **no evidence connects this private donor dispute to the shooting.** Tyler Robinson is charged as the sole shooter; no law-enforcement body has alleged a pro-Israel or foreign motive.

## Bottom line
The text is real and was confirmed — that part is **true**, and worth documenting honestly. But a venting message about a donor fight is **not evidence of who killed him**. The kernel is true; the murder-motive inference is unsupported. See [[The Security Detail Was Israeli]] and [[The Investigation Was Limited]] for the adjacent claims.
