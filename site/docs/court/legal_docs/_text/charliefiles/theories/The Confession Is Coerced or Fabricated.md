# The Confession Is Coerced or Fabricated

> **The claim:** The confession was coerced or fabricated.

**Verdict: UNSUPPORTED.**

> **See also [[Confessions and Admissions]]** — the full catalog of Robinson's **five** separate admissions and how each is treated at trial: the party-opponent rule (a defendant's own words are not hearsay), authentication and chain of custody, the **burned-note** best-evidence problem, the *Mauchley* trustworthiness standard, why voluntariness/*Miranda* is a non-issue (all to private parties), and the mechanics of **recanting** a confession in Utah.

## What fuels it
General distrust of confessions — often justified in cases built *only* on a confession.

## What the evidence shows
This case is **not** built only on a confession:
- It surfaced through a **family friend who contacted the sheriff**, and Robinson **surrendered** — this was **not a custodial interrogation**. See [[The Surrender]].
- It is **corroborated** by the recovered [[The Rifle|rifle]], the [[The Casing Match and DNA|casing match and DNA]], the **shoe impression**, and his **own messages**. See [[The Discord and Text Messages]].

> **Reasonable-doubt read (for the defense):** any confession is fair to scrutinize — the messages were released as **excerpts with ellipses**, no full thread is public, and a written note proves **intent, not the trigger-pull**. "Unsupported" means nothing yet shows fabrication — not that the confession is beyond challenge. See [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]].

> **Day 3 (July 8) surfaced a *publicity* dimension — distinct from coercion.** When the State moved the [[The Discord and Text Messages|Discord thread (Exhibit 16)]] and the [[The Keyboard Note|keyboard note]] in through **[[Brian Davis|Agent Davis]]**, the defense's (**Novak**) sharpest argument was **not** that the statements were coerced or fabricated — it was that **publicly replaying "what the State will call a confession"** before a jury is seated is the **due-process harm** condemned in *Estes v. Texas* and *Rideau v. Louisiana* (reachable under *Archuleta*) [02:50:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=10256s), [03:16:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=11774s). Novak was **careful not to concede** the writings are confessions at all — "I am **not** calling the statements of Mr. Robinson confession[s]" [02:51:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=10260s). The court **admitted** the material for probable cause (magistrate, not jury) but pledged **redaction / non-publication to protect the jury pool** [02:44:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9886s)–[02:45:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9918s), reserving the fuller confrontation/publicity question for a defense brief. This is a **fair-trial** objection about *how the confession is aired*, not a showing that it was **coerced or fabricated** — the verdict here stays **UNSUPPORTED**. See [[2026-07-08 - Preliminary Hearing Day 3|Day 3 Legal Review]].

> **Day 4 (July 9) added a second, *in-person verbal* confession — with real weight for the defense to test.** [[Lance Twiggs]]'s immunized, recorded statement (played Day 4) describes an **in-person exchange on Sept. 11**: Twiggs asked Robinson whether what he'd said was true; "**he said it was**" and "**wishes he hadn't done it**" [02:26:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8778s). That is no longer only a written note or a text thread — it is a person recounting Robinson confessing to his face. **But the defense's scrutiny channels here are exactly the ones this page is about:** the statement is **immunized** (federal + state) [02:12:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=7934s), **un-cross-examined**, and the prosecutor conceded the recording has "**large gaps of … black empty silent space**" [02:10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=7810s) — an interested, immunized witness whose account can't be probed live and is partly inaudible. So Day 4 makes the confession **broader**, not **coerced or fabricated**: nothing shows fabrication, and the verdict stays **UNSUPPORTED** — while the reasonable-doubt read above gains a concrete new target. See [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4|Day 4 Legal Review]] and [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4 Transcript|Day 4 Transcript]].

> **A same-case example — and why it cuts both ways.** [[George Zinn]] **falsely confessed to this very shooting** within minutes (*"I shot him, now shoot me"*) — hard proof that people **do** claim crimes they did not commit, even here, and a reason never to take a bare *"I did it"* at face value. But Zinn's confession **collapsed on contact**: he was **unarmed**, knew **no details**, and **recanted within hours** — the opposite of Robinson's **corroborated** statements (rifle, DNA, the engraved bullets his messages predicted). Zinn is both the reason to **test** a confession and the illustration of the **test** that a real one survives. See [[George Zinn]] and [[Robinson Is a Patsy]].

## Bottom line
A statement that emerges from a voluntary surrender — not an interrogation room — and that lines up with the physical and digital evidence is the opposite of the classic coerced confession. To call it fabricated, you'd have to explain away the rifle, the DNA, and the messages too.
