# They Have the Wrong Person

> **The claim:** They grabbed the wrong guy / Robinson was framed.

**Verdict: UNSUPPORTED.**

## What fuels it
A genuine Day-1 moment: on cross-examination, **one officer couldn't identify the roof figure's face** from a single camera angle ([hearing 04:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15180s)), and had described the object in its hand as "a long black object," not a gun, in his original report.

> 🎥 **Forbes Breaking News:** the defense grills the State's case agent over the moment of the killing and the rooftop identification.

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EY5Ws89jaa0)

## What the evidence shows
That is **one witness on one camera** — not the case. The full record is **convergent**:
- **Gait-tracked surveillance** across multiple cameras. See [[The Surveillance and the Gait]].
- The **recovered rifle** with **Robinson's DNA** on trigger, casing, and towel. See [[The Rifle]] and [[The Casing Match and DNA]].
- A **shoe-impression match**.
- The **Discord/text messages** describing the plan. See [[The Discord and Text Messages]].
- The **voluntary surrender**. See [[The Surrender]].

**On the early detentions (documented):** officials churned at first — the FBI announced a subject "in custody" on Sept. 10 and then "released after an interrogation," and Utah DPS named two men briefly detained and released, **George Zinn** (charged with obstruction) and **Zachariah Qureshi**, with *"no current ties to the shooting."* That the process detained and released people early is real — and is exactly why the case against Robinson rests on the **later, convergent** evidence, not those initial stops. See [[Official Statements and the FBI Case File]].

At a **preliminary hearing** the State shows the *start* of its case; the forensic matches (DNA, footwear, casing) come in as the hearing proceeds — so a single officer's inability to face-ID a figure from one angle doesn't mean "wrong person."

## Appearance: the "he's too tall" and "maroon-shirt decoy" arguments
A **live line of attack** (pushed hard on X after Day 2) is that the person in the released footage looks **too tall** to be Robinson, and that the grainy figure is just a generic **brown-haired man in a maroon shirt** — one of many at the event.

- **On height, neither the shooter nor Robinson has an official measurement in the record.** A **measuring tape** appears in the perch crime-scene photos (Day 1 [02:27:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=8871s)), but it was **never quantified into a height** — the officer wasn't present for any measurement and could only say the figure **"wasn't heavyset"** (Day 1 [04:12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15146s)). And there is **no official height for Robinson** either: the charging documents list none; the only "~6 feet" figure is a reporter's **on-air eyeball guess** from the profile mugshot; and the **"5′5″"** number in circulation belongs to an **early, abandoned person-of-interest description — not Robinson.** So "he's much taller than Robinson" compares two numbers that were **never established.**
- **The "mugshot on a height chart" image is not the real booking photo.** Utah released Robinson's booking photos (Gov. Cox's office, Sept 12, 2025) as a **plain-backdrop** front-and-profile set — **not** a photo against a ruled height scale. Any image showing him against height gradations does **not** match the official release and should be treated as **edited or mis-sourced** — the more so because this case already has a **documented AI-manipulation pattern** (fact-checkers traced the "face doesn't match" hoax to an **AI-warped** version of the FBI surveillance still). See [[AI and Deepfake Evidence]].
- **On the "maroon-shirt decoy" framing,** that many people wore maroon and khaki at a Turning Point event is unremarkable and **does not defeat the identification**, because the ID does not rest on shirt color. It rests on a **multi-factor track** — the recurring [[The Surveillance and the Gait|gait across separate cameras]], a **shoe-impression match**, the **silver Challenger** on campus ([[Robinson on Campus]]), the **DNA**, and the **surrender**. Take away the clothing and the track still runs.
- **The Ring-camera "bald driver + three others" detail is on the record — and unexplained.** The doorbell clip itself was testified to as a vehicle **"consistent with"** the silver Challenger, the figure never facially identified (Day 1). On **Day 2**, the defense drew out that the Ring witnesses (the Nobles) described the car as driven by a **bald man with three other people in it** — a detail Agent Hull had **not** surfaced on direct (Day 2 [02:06:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7560s)). A bald driver and three passengers sit **unexplained against the single-occupant, lone-Robinson theory** — a genuine [[Insufficiency of the Evidence|reasonable-doubt]] thread on the current record, though the owners' full underlying statement is still discovery-only. See [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].

> **Reasonable-doubt read (for the defense):** the fair version is that the State **never pinned the shooter's height or face** — identity is built by inference, not by a clean physical match — which is legitimate [[Insufficiency of the Evidence|reasonable-doubt]] material. With **no verified height for the shooter or Robinson**, the comparison is unanchored. An eyeballed "he looks taller" in a blurry frame is **not affirmative proof of innocence.**

## Bottom line
One weak identification does not outweigh a convergent forensic, digital, and surveillance record all pointing at the same person. See [[Robinson Is a Patsy]] for the "didn't act alone" variant.
