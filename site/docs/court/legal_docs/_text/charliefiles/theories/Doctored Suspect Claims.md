# Doctored Suspect Claims

> **The claim:** Various "gotchas" about the suspect — a photoshopped shirt, false party-registration or donation records, and the like.

**Verdict: FALSE.**

## What fuels it
The rush to slot the shooter into a political narrative — which happened in **both** directions.

## What the evidence shows
These claims **circulated in both political directions** and were **manufactured or misattributed**:
- a **photoshopped shirt** image;
- **false party-registration** claims;
- **false donation** claims.

The reliable picture of the suspect comes from the **court record** — the [[2025-09-12 - Affidavit of Probable Cause|Affidavit of Probable Cause]], the [[2025-09-16 - The Information and Charges|Information]], and the [[2025-09-17 - The Search Warrant Affidavit|search-warrant affidavit]] — not from viral screenshots.

## If the defense raised it
> To the extent any doctored image were used *against* Robinson, exposing it would go to innocence — but the charging record does not rest on viral images. See [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]].

## Bottom line
Doctored "suspect facts" tell you about the partisan tug-of-war over the story, not about Robinson. See [[AI and Deepfake Evidence]].
