# The Shot Came from the Courtyard

> **The claim:** There was a second shooter, and the shot actually came from the courtyard — not the roof.

**Verdict: UNSUPPORTED.**

## What fuels it
An **unattributed pistol holster** on the grass in the courtyard, **armed responders**, and a person **briefly detained in the courtyard** in the first chaotic seconds. See [[The Unattributed Holster]].

## What the evidence shows
- **The State's Day-2 track places the shot on the roof, not the courtyard.** Case agent Hull testified the figure was prone at the SW corner of the Losee roof and "the reported shot was fired at **12:23 and twenty-eight**," after which the individual stood and moved north across the rooftop [00:55:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=3327s). See [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]] and [[The Surveillance and the Gait]].
- **The banner rebuts the courtyard sightline, but cuts both ways.** The pop-up-tent banner behind Kirk is **opaque — "white… you cannot see through it"** [02:18:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=8306s); you cannot see Kirk from the tunnel/breezeway side either [02:19:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=8346s). So the breezeway camera behind the banner offers **no line of sight in either direction** — it neither shows a courtyard shooter nor rules the courtyard in.
- The **murder weapon was recovered along the rooftop-to-north escape route**, not the courtyard. See [[The Rifle]].
- It was an **open-carry event** with police and private security. An abandoned **holster** and armed responders are **unremarkable** at a chaotic mass-casualty scene — they don't imply a second gunman.
- The people briefly **detained and released** in the chaos — **[[George Zinn]]** (who falsely confessed as a diversion; later charged with obstruction) and **Zachariah Qureshi** — had *"no current ties to the shooting,"* per Utah DPS; the FBI likewise announced a subject "in custody" on Sept. 10, then "released." Robinson **fled and surrendered the next day**. See [[Official Statements and the FBI Case File]], [[The Surrender]], and [[They Have the Wrong Person]].
- The geometry supports a single rooftop round. See [[Line of Sight and Shot Geometry]].

> **Reasonable-doubt read (for the defense):** the **holster** thread got sharper on Day 2. Off-duty trooper **Gonzales's report (via Davis, Exhibit Hull-13)** states there was **a gun found in the holster** in the amphitheater [01:48:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6538s) — a documented second firearm in the courtyard area, whose owner, collection, and testing are unresolved. That, with the person **detained in the courtyard** and the [[A Bullet on the Other Roof|bullet the defense says was found on another roof]], is a genuine **second-armed-person** thread — reasonable doubt about *who and how many*, even if it doesn't prove a courtyard *shooter*. See [[The Unattributed Holster]] and [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]].

## Bottom line
The courtyard details are real but prosaic. They point to a crowded, armed, panicked scene — not to a second firing position. The physical trail runs from the roof to the north, where the rifle was found.
