# A Staffer Pulled the SD Cards

> **The claim:** A Kirk staffer pulled the SD/memory cards from the event cameras right after the shooting.

**Verdict: PARTLY TRUE** — the act is real; nothing in the record shows it was done to hide anything; the chain of custody is a fair open question.

## What fuels it
**Aftermath video** shows a man using Kirk's chair to reach a stage-side camera and **remove a memory card**. It went viral (Candace Owens: *"Who Moved The Camera Right Above Charlie's Head?"*). Unlike most claims here, **the underlying act genuinely happened.**

## What the evidence shows
- It was **Kirk's own TPUSA A/V team**, not police or an outsider. The multi-camera rig was run by contractor **Terryl Farnsworth / Visual Impulse**, whose sworn **Voluntary Statement is Plaintiff's Exhibit 8.1**. See [[The SD Cards]].
- TPUSA producer **Andrew Kolvet** and COO **Tyler Boyer** said their **head of tech** grabbed the cards so the footage "wouldn't be stolen," and that **all footage was turned over to the FBI** (who now hold the cards).

So: the **act happened**; it was **Kirk's own media team**; the footage **went to the FBI**. Nothing in the record shows the cards were pulled to hide anything.

## Bottom line
This is the honest one to sit with. Because the explanation comes from **Kirk-affiliated spokesmen** — not an independent investigator, and not yet sworn testimony on the *card handling* — the **chain of custody is a legitimate thing to test on cross-examination.** That's on the [[What Is Genuinely Open|open list]]. What it is **not** is control of the UVU surveillance — see [[Kirk Team Seized the Security Footage|UVU footage claim check]].
