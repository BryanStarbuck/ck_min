# The Defense Is Throwing the Case

> **The claim:** Robinson's own attorneys are throwing the case.

**Verdict: FALSE.**

## What fuels it
The idea that a public / taxpayer-funded defense must be going through the motions.

## What the evidence shows
The team is **elite capital-defense counsel**, and its filings are **aggressive**:
- **Kathryn Nester**; **Michael Burt** (editor of the *California Death Penalty Defense Manual*); **Richard Novak** (25+ death-eligible cases). See [[The Defense Team]].
- Motions to **disqualify the prosecutor's office**, to **hold prosecutors in contempt** (partly successful — see [[Judge Tony Graf]]), to **exclude cameras**, and the **ballistics motion** that publicized the inconclusive-bullet result. See [[The Inconclusive Bullet]].

**Day 2 showed the same aggression in the hearing itself.** Nester's cross of the State's case agent surfaced the **gun-in-the-holster** contradiction [01:48:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6538s), the **Ring-camera "bald driver + three others"** detail [02:06:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7560s), and the "**Overwatch**" spectator reading of the rooftop figure [01:55:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6919s). More strikingly, the defense **called the FBI's own DNA examiner out of order, by stipulation, during the State's case-in-chief** and ran an expert-level Rule 702-style cross — extracting that STR measures **length, not sequence** [05:37:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=20268s), that the **Twiggs likelihood ratio was 1 ("uninformative")** yet he was still reported as a contributor [07:20:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26404s), and that samples were **consumed**, leaving retesting doubtful [07:32:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=27127s). That is the opposite of a defense going through the motions. See [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]] and [[The Casing Match and DNA]].

## Bottom line
This is one of the most contested defenses imaginable — pressing disqualification, contempt, camera exclusion, and the ballistics fight. "Throwing the case" is the opposite of what the docket shows.
