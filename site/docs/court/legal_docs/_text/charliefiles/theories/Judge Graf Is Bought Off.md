# Judge Graf's Rulings

> **The claim:** Judge Tony Graf is biased toward a conviction.

**Verdict: FALSE.**

## What fuels it
Distrust of the process generally, and the assumption that any adverse ruling proves the fix is in.

## What the evidence shows
Graf was appointed to the 4th District by **Gov. Spencer Cox in 2025**. His rulings **cut both ways** — the opposite of what a bought judge produces:
- He **excluded** a State-annotated compilation video (the defense's biggest Day-1 win). See [[The Surveillance Video Was Doctored]].
- He **held prosecutor Christopher Ballard in civil contempt** for a gag-order/publicity violation (June 26, 2026) — while, the same day, **keeping the death penalty in place** over the defense's objection. See [[Prosecution Problems]].
- He has **also ruled against the defense** — admitting reliable hearsay at the prelim, and denying live testimony from Lance Twiggs.
- **Day 2 shows the same both-ways pattern on a single exhibit.** On the State's motive exhibit (5.1, the TPUSA "Orthodox Christian views" statement), Graf **provisionally admitted** it for the political-expression enhancement — a partial win for the State — while **expressly reserving** whether it connects to Robinson's state of mind and **declining to publish** it to the courtroom or media, and he **sustained** the objection to the prosecutor reading the unadmitted exhibit aloud — partial wins for the defense [03:42:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=13354s). He likewise kept the **DNA-reliability fight to the probable-cause standard**, treating the length-not-sequence and PCAST attacks as a **future Rule 702 trial issue**, not a prelim ruling [07:15:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26145s) — a call that neither side got wholesale. See [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].

There is **no evidence of bias or corruption**. See [[Judge Tony Graf]].

## Bottom line
A judge who excludes the prosecution's exhibit and holds a prosecutor in contempt, while also ruling against the defense, is not biased toward a conviction. The record shows an ordinary judge making calls both ways.
