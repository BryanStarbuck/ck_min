# Others Had Foreknowledge

> **The claim:** Other people knew the attack was coming — there were posts on X/social media predicting it, even "warning" Kirk about UVU — which points to organized foreknowledge rather than a lone actor.

**Verdict: PARTLY TRUE** (real pre-shooting posts exist and were investigated) — **UNSUPPORTED** (that they prove coordinated foreknowledge). The specific "warned him not to come to UVU" post is **not documented**.

## What fuels it
A Sept. 15, 2025 *Washington Free Beacon* report that the **FBI was examining posts from "at least seven" accounts that appeared to indicate foreknowledge**, plus statements by then-NCTC director [[Joe Kent]] that "there were people who were posting about Charlie Kirk being killed before Charlie Kirk was killed."

## What the evidence shows
**The kernel is real.** Authenticated, archived pre-shooting posts do exist (screenshots given to the FBI):
- **Sept. 3, 2025 (X):** *"itd be funny if someone like charlie kirk got shot on september 10th LMAO."*
- **Aug. 6, 2025:** *"september 10th will be a very interesting day"* — followed, after the shooting, by *"I plead the fifth."*
- An account that **appeared to follow [[Lance Twiggs|Robinson's roommate]]'s TikTok** posted *"WE FUCKING DID IT"* minutes after Kirk was pronounced dead.
- A TikTok the night before: *"…does not know what's coming tomorrow… This isn't a threat. It's a promise."*

And the **investigation is real**: the FBI reviewed those accounts, FBI Director Kash Patel said the Bureau was investigating the "possibility of accomplices," and investigators separately probed whether activist groups had advance knowledge.

**But the inference is not established.**
- **No source links any poster to Robinson.** The Free Beacon itself says the posts "do not establish that any of the individuals knew or conspired with Robinson." **Sept. 10 was the publicly announced date of Kirk's UVU event**, which explains date-specific "jokes" without operational foreknowledge. Most read as pre-existing hostility plus grim coincidence or opportunistic reaction.
- **The "warned Kirk not to go to UVU" post is a mutation.** No authenticated, discrete post *warning Kirk to avoid UVU* surfaced; that framing appears to be a paraphrase of the general "something is coming" posts.
- **[[The Investigation Was Limited|Joe Kent's "prior knowledge / foreign nexus"]]** claims are, by his own framing, **leads not run down** — and are contested by his peers; officials (Gov. Cox included) maintain **Robinson acted alone**.
- **The "media knew in advance" spin is FALSE:** the viral "NYT published 15 hours early" claim used a Google-cache timestamp (the post ran at 3:02 p.m., after the shooting), and a rabbi's "September 2" condolence letter was a typo for September 12.

## Bottom line
Real, ugly posts and a real FBI review — **not** proof of a coordinated plot. It stays **open** in the narrow sense that the accomplices question was investigated and the full results aren't all public; but nothing on the record connects those posters to the shooter. This is the factual heart of the "[[Robinson Is a Patsy|not alone]]" defense theme — see [[Defense Strategies and Degrees of Liability]].
