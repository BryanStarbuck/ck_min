# Netanyahu Read Kirk's Letter

> **The claim:** Shortly after Kirk's death, Benjamin Netanyahu read/quoted portions of a letter from Kirk and publicly characterized him as very pro-Israel — even as Kirk had privately told his own team he had to leave the pro-Israel cause.

**Verdict: TRUE** — Netanyahu did quote the letter and call Kirk pro-Israel; the "selective quoting" critique is **PARTLY TRUE**, but the letter genuinely contains both affection and criticism.

## What fuels it
Netanyahu himself. Set against [[Kirk Was Leaving the Pro-Israel Cause|Kirk's private "leaving the pro-Israel cause" text]], his public tribute struck critics as an attempt to claim Kirk as an uncomplicated ally.

## What the evidence shows
- **Sept 11, 2025, on Fox News' *The Faulkner Focus*:** Netanyahu called Kirk "a once-in-a-generation figure" and quoted his **May 2, 2025 letter**: *"One of my greatest joys as a Christian is advocating for Israel..."* He said he'd recently phoned Kirk and invited him to Israel — a visit that never happened.
- **Sept 18, 2025, in a video on X:** Netanyahu **explicitly denied Israel had any role** in the killing (invoking the "big lie"), and again quoted the letter.
- **The nuance:** Owens argued he quoted **selectively** — omitting Kirk's private warnings that Israel was **"losing support even in conservative circles"** ("This should be a 5 alarm fire") and needed a "communications intervention." When the **New York Post published the full 7-page letter** (~Sept 30), it turned out to contain **both** heavy pro-Israel affirmation **and** that criticism. So Kirk was genuinely pro-Israel *and* privately critical of Israel's PR strategy.

## Bottom line
The core claim is **true**: Netanyahu quoted Kirk's letter and praised his pro-Israel support, and separately denied Israeli involvement. The "he cherry-picked" charge is **partly true** — he quoted the affectionate lines, not the critical ones — but the letter really does contain both, so it doesn't show what the plot-narrative wants it to. This is context about Kirk's views, **not evidence about who killed him**.
