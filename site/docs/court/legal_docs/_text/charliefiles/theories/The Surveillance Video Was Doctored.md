# The Surveillance Video Was Doctored

> **The claim:** The prosecution doctored/manipulated the video evidence — and it was so tainted the judge threw it out.

**Verdict: FALSE** — the "doctoring" was **annotation** on a compilation exhibit; the judge excluded it on **authentication** grounds, and expressly said he did **not** mean "manipulate" in a nefarious sense. The raw footage stands.

## What Exhibit 12.1 actually was
As the State built its identity case — the [[Robinson on Campus|four campus visits]] tracked on UVU surveillance — it offered **States Exhibit 12.1**, a **compilation video** assembled by the **Utah County Attorney's Office**. The agent testified that *"Ms. Dufler is the one that produced the video"* — **Kimberly Dufler**, a County Attorney's Office staffer ([06:42:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=24156s)).

**What it showed:** the UVU surveillance footage tracing the suspect's movements — with **zooming and circling added** to direct the eye to the figure. Two witnesses who had seen the raw footage said the compilation *"accurately depicts the video that was captured by UVU surveillance,"* and the State said the additions *"were only added to make it easier to view"* ([06:51:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=24705s)).

## Why the judge rejected it
The problem was **not** content-faking — it was **who altered it and whether they could vouch for it**. Under Utah's preliminary-hearing hearsay rule (1102), the person who added the circles/zoom (Dufler) wasn't there to authenticate them:

> **THE COURT** ([06:50:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=24625s)): *"Do you have a version of this video that does not have alterations such as zooming or circling… I don't know… how much was done to **manipulate the video. I say manipulate to not saying it in a nefarious way**, but I don't know what the scope of that is…"*
> **THE COURT** ([06:53:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=24799s)): *"I do not have the person who made the alteration… I'm missing a link, and it may be minor, but in this type of case I want to be thorough… I'm going to sustain the objection."*

The State **couldn't produce an unaltered version "not right now, not today"** ([06:51:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=24705s)), and offered to call Dufler as a witness to cure it. **12.1 was excluded.**

## Day 2: the exclusion was cured with the clean version
On **Day 2**, the State did exactly what the Day-1 ruling invited — it produced the unaltered footage — and the "doctoring" claim ran out of room. See [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].
- **The clean compilation (Exhibit 12.4) was admitted and published.** The court admitted **12.4** — the same surveillance track **without** the zooming or circling — and it was **played in the courtroom and broadcast to the media** [00:40:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=2407s). That is the version the public and the fact-finder actually saw.
- **The enhanced version (12.1) was also admitted — but not re-played to the room.** After the State detailed exactly what the enhancements were — **red circles, blurred faces, and a rooftop zoom**, with the agent testifying each **"does not change the accuracy"** of the footage [01:00:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=3640s)–[01:03:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=3798s) — the court **received 12.1 into evidence** but, finding it *"the exact same video"* apart from the annotations, declined to broadcast it, choosing to **review it himself on the monitor** rather than publish it again [01:08:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=4089s)–[01:11:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=4262s).

So the Day-1 authentication gap was **procedural, and it was fixed**: the raw footage is in, published, and unchallenged as to accuracy; the annotated cut is in for the judge's review. Nothing in the Day-2 record suggests the underlying video was faked — the opposite: both parties treated the surveillance itself as authentic.

## Bottom line
This is the **system working**, not tampering succeeding: an over-annotated exhibit was caught by the defense and flagged by the court on Day 1, then **cured on Day 2** when the State admitted and published the **clean, unaltered compilation (12.4)** [00:40:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=2407s). The **underlying UVU surveillance is authentic** and was shown raw. It is often cited *as if* it proved fabrication; it proves the opposite. Note the irony: the Day-1 exclusion was the **defense's biggest Day-1 win**, yet the annotations turned out to be red circles, face blurs, and a rooftop zoom "with no change to accuracy" — and it cuts against the broader "[[Kirk Team Seized the Security Footage|footage was hidden/faked]]" narrative.
