# Robinson Is a Patsy

> **The claim:** Robinson is a patsy — he didn't act alone, or he was "handled."

**Verdict: UNSUPPORTED.**

## What fuels it
The instinct that a killing this consequential *must* involve more than one person.

## What the evidence shows
- **Nothing in the record shows a co-shooter.** See [[The Shot Came from the Courtyard]] and [[A Second Shooter from a Lower Angle]].
- The **digital messages** are between Robinson and an associate (roommate **Lance Twiggs**) who **cooperated with police**, and they describe **Robinson** carrying out the plan — not a handler directing him. See [[The Discord and Text Messages]].
- The physical evidence (rifle, DNA, casing match, shoe impression) points to **one actor**. See [[The Established Record]]. **Day 2 leaned the same way:** the DNA examiner placed Robinson as the **major contributor** (95%/89%) on the tested items [08:05:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=29158s), and the [[The Rifle|rifle]] was recovered in the wooded area Robinson is believed to have entered [02:20:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=8441s). See [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].
- **His own residence tied him to the ammunition:** a **shell casing with matching engravings** and **several targets with bullet holes** were found in his home (per [[2025-09-16 - The Information and Charges|the Information]]) — his own physical evidence, not a handler's plant. See [[The Engraved Casings]].

> **Reasonable-doubt read (for the defense):** "patsy" overstates it, but the softer version — that the case is **built backward from the surrender** and rests on *consistent-with* evidence — is a real reasonable-doubt argument. **Day 2 gave that softer version one new thread:** the Ring-camera **Nobles described a *bald* driver and *three other people* in the car** [02:06:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7560s)–[02:07:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7627s) — unexplained against the single-occupant theory, and a fair "not alone" point ([[Defense Strategies and Degrees of Liability|Layer 1]]), though it speaks to *company*, not a handler directing a patsy. See [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]] and [[The Gun Was His But Planted]]. **Day 3 (July 8) added a second thread — but a legal-posture one, not a factual one:** the roommate [[Lance Twiggs]]'s account now formally enters as an **immunized, recorded, un-cross-examined** hearsay statement under **Rule 1102** (case agent [[Brian Davis]] laid the foundation; the defense preserved a **Confrontation-Clause** objection) [01:05:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3909s). That the defense can't test the key roommate live is fair reasonable-doubt material — but it still **cuts against** "patsy": nothing shows a handler, and Twiggs **cooperated**. See [[2026-07-08 - Preliminary Hearing Day 3|Day 3 Legal Review]]. **Day 4 (July 9) pushed the same way on the merits:** Twiggs's immunized recorded statement, now **played**, describes Robinson **confessing in person** — asked if it was true, "he said it was" and "wishes he hadn't done it" [02:26:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8778s) — with **no handler and no co-planner** anywhere in his account. That directly undercuts the patsy reading against the current record; the fair reasonable-doubt counter stays that the account is immunized and un-cross-examined. See [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4|Day 4 Legal Review]] and [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4 Transcript|Day 4 Transcript]].

## Bottom line
The current evidence points to a **lone actor**. If later proceedings reveal more, that would warrant revisiting — an honest resource says that plainly — but "patsy" has nothing in the present record behind it.
