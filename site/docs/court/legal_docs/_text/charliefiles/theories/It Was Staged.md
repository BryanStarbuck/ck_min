# It Was Staged

> **The claim:** It was staged — crisis actors, a "blood bag," the whole thing faked.

**Verdict: FALSE.**

## What fuels it
The same low-quality video that feeds [[The Microphone Exploded|the exploding-mic]] reading — plus the visible shirt "bulge."

## What the evidence shows (the primary record)
- The **medical examiner certified a gunshot wound to the neck; manner, homicide** ([[2025-09-17 - The Search Warrant Affidavit|The Search Warrant Affidavit]]; [[The Medical Examiner and the Wound]]) — a real death, officially certified.
- The staging theory has to explain away a recovered **[[The Rifle|rifle]]**, **[[The Engraved Casings|engraved casings]]**, a **bullet fragment recovered at autopsy**, **[[The Casing Match and DNA|DNA]]**, **[[The Surveillance and the Gait|surveillance]]**, a **charged defendant**, and a **[[The Surrender|surrender]]**. See [[The Established Record]].
- The shirt "bulge" is Kirk's microphone rig.

## If the defense raised it
> As an alternative theory it would go **directly to innocence** (no real shooting → no shooter to convict), but **on the current record** it runs into the autopsy finding and the physical evidence, and the defense has **not, so far**, gone near it. That could change — the hearing is ongoing — but nothing in the record to date supports "crisis actors." See [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]] and [[A Hypothetical Cover-Up]].

## Bottom line
"Crisis actor / blood bag" claims run against an officially certified homicide with a recovered weapon, forensics, and a defendant in custody. **On the current record** there is nothing to support them; contrary evidence at trial would have to be weighed, but none has been offered.
