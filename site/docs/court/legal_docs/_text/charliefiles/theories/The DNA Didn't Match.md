# The DNA Didn't Match

> **The claim:** The State's DNA doesn't actually match Robinson — the "match" is manufactured, unreliable, or "didn't match."

**Verdict: MISLEADING** — the results are **not** a clean single-source match, and the mixture interpretation is genuinely contestable; but the reported statistics are astronomically large and align the **major** contributor with Robinson, so "the DNA didn't match" overstates it. The real point is **reliability and conditioning**, not "no match."

## The actual results (State's Exhibit 31)
On Day 2, Sgt. Faumuina read in — and FBI examiner **Amanda Bakker** was examined on — **State's Exhibit 31**, the FBI Laboratory DNA report. Two evidence items carried interpretable DNA, each a **two-person mixture**:

| Item | Source | Reported likelihood ratio | Mixture split (Twiggs / Robinson) | Profile |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **7.1** | swab of the **towel** the rifle was wrapped in | **1.7 octillion** ([05:11:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18662s)) | ~**5%** Twiggs (minor) · ~**95%** Robinson (major) ([08:05:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=29158s)) | full |
| **8.1** | swab of the **screwdriver** handle (the perch) | **30 quintillion** ([05:11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18701s)) | ~**11%** Twiggs (minor) · ~**89%** Robinson (major) ([08:06:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=29184s)) | partial (degraded) |
| **Twiggs** (as tested) | his own profile, run against the mixtures | **1 — "uninformative"** ([07:21:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26471s)) | *included by the examiner's visual judgment, not the statistic* | — |

- **What the likelihood ratio means.** Each figure is *how many times more likely the results are if the contributors are **Twiggs + Robinson** than if they are **Twiggs + an unknown, unrelated person**.* The only descriptor the DOJ's uniform-language rules allow for numbers this size is **"very strong support for inclusion"** ([07:59:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=28747s)). (For scale: 1.7 octillion ≈ 1.7 × 10²⁷; 30 quintillion ≈ 3 × 10¹⁹.)
- **Method.** STR typing (the FBI's **GlobalFiler** kit, 21 loci + 3 sex markers), interpreted with **STRmix** probabilistic-genotyping software; reference DNA taken by buccal swab from Robinson and [[Lance Twiggs|Twiggs]]. The statistic is **conditioned on Twiggs** as an assumed "elimination" contributor — for item 7, Bakker first estimated **three** contributors, then revised to **two** after adding Twiggs ([06:33:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=23632s)).
- **Provenance of the Twiggs reference sample (Day 3).** Day 3 (July 8) established where that Twiggs "elimination" profile came from: case agent [[Brian Davis|Agent Davis]] testified that Twiggs gave a **buccal DNA swab** [01:02:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3767s) (see also [00:46:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=2788s)) under a grant of **use immunity** [01:05:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3909s). That swab is the **reference sample** behind the Day-2 "uninformative" (likelihood ratio 1) Twiggs result — so the provenance is now on the record, though it does not change the reliability picture below. See [[2026-07-08 - Preliminary Hearing Day 3|Day 3 Legal Review]].
- **Not part of Exhibit 31.** The **trigger** and **casing** DNA attributed to Robinson come from the earlier probable-cause affidavit — Day 2's report covers only the **towel and the screwdriver**. See [[The Casing Match and DNA]].

**On the raw results:** Robinson aligns with the **major** contributor on both items, at astronomical likelihood ratios — so on its face this is the *opposite* of "no match." The real contest is over **how** those numbers were produced.

> **In plain terms:**
> - **STRmix** is the **software** the FBI lab uses to make sense of a DNA **mixture** (DNA from more than one person on the same item). Rather than a person eyeballing whose DNA is whose, the program models the mixture mathematically and produces the likelihood-ratio statistic. Because it's a probabilistic model, it **doesn't give the exact same number twice** (it can vary within roughly a factor of 10).
> - **PCAST** is the **President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology** — a White House science panel whose **2016 report** questioned whether some forensic techniques, including **complex, low-level DNA mixtures** like these, had been scientifically validated well enough to be relied on in court.

## What genuinely fuels the doubt (the cross)
The defense (Bird/Burt) drew out real, preserved reliability problems — this is a **mixture** case, not single-source:
- **Both items are two-person mixtures**, and the **minor contributor is below 20%** (5% and 11%) — exactly the category the **PCAST report** flagged as needing more validation for probabilistic-genotyping software ([05:52:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=21150s), [05:53:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=21189s)). The software used was **STRmix** ([05:52:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=21144s)).
- **Robinson's inclusion is *conditioned on assuming Twiggs* is the other contributor** — and when Bakker ran **Twiggs's own** profile through STRmix, the result was **uninformative, a likelihood ratio of 1**; she kept Twiggs in **by visual judgment**, not the statistics ([07:21:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26471s), [07:22:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26538s)).
- At several loci the **evidence sample lacks Twiggs's alleles** — no **9** at D16, no **16** at D19, no **7** at another ([07:23:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26635s)–[07:27:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26822s)). Under the **single-source rule** in Butler's standard textbook, one non-matching locus = an **exclusion** — but Bakker testified that rule doesn't apply to mixtures with possible **dropout** ([07:25:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26748s)). Whether that reasoning holds is a classic **trial reliability (Rule 702 / *Daubert*)** fight.

## The Other Side
The likelihood ratios are enormous; Bakker testified that the **degradation didn't prevent her conclusions**, that STRmix/probabilistic genotyping is **validated** and used nationwide, that her lab is **accredited and ground-truth-tested**, and that the field has advanced since PCAST (2016) ([08:03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=28990s), [08:04:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=29071s)). "Very strong support for inclusion" of Robinson as the major contributor is the honest read of the report — it is **not** a non-match.

## Day 5: a second DNA witness — *presence, not action*
On **Day 5 (July 10)** the defense called a *second* DNA witness — **[[Caitlin Oliver]]**, the **ATF DNA Section Chief** who performed the DNA work **for the State** — to press the reliability point once more, over the State's objection that it was cumulative. Examined by **Burt**, she drew the line the whole defense case turns on: DNA proves **presence, not action**.
- **No source, no activity, no timing.** "**None of the testing … could speak to activity or what activity led to the deposition of the DNA**" [02:03:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=7382s); you "**can't assign a source to a DNA sample**" [02:11:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=7907s); and "**a person's DNA may be present when they were not involved in the crime**" [02:12:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=7972s). Calling it "Mr. Robinson's DNA on this item" is a **source attribution** that "would not be scientifically" proper [02:09:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=7799s).
- **No certainty; likelihood ratios only.** "**Impossible to state with 100% certainty anything**" [01:56:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=6983s); no "reasonable degree of scientific certainty" in her reports [01:56:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=7000s); the "**true number of contributors … can never be known**" [02:07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=7620s).
- **But the cross gave the State its number.** Like Karner, Oliver conceded the inculpatory core on **McBride's cross**: the profiles are "**at least 1,000,000,000,000 times more likely … [if they] originated from Tyler Robinson as the major contributor**" [02:46:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=9988s)–[02:46:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=9992s) — the **trillion being her lab's reporting cap** [02:44:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=9877s).

So the honest read is unchanged from Day 2: **Robinson aligns with the major contributor at astronomical strength; the real contest is reliability and *what the DNA cannot say* (source, activity, timing) — not "no match."** See [[2026-07-10 - Preliminary Hearing Day 5|Day 5 Legal Review]], [[Caitlin Oliver]], [[The Casing Match and DNA]].

## Bottom line
"The DNA didn't match" is **MISLEADING**: the reported result is strong support for including Robinson as the major contributor. The honest, powerful version of the defense point is narrower and real — the result is a **low-template, minor-contributor mixture**, interpreted by **STRmix while conditioning on a co-contributor (Twiggs) whom the software itself couldn't support**, in precisely the regime **PCAST** flagged. That's a serious **reliability** attack preserved for trial — not proof the DNA "didn't match."
