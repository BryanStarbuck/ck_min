# The Plane Theories

> **The claim:** An aircraft was involved — a foreign (Egyptian) plane, a jet that "turned off its transponder" and fled, or a military "spy plane" loitering over the scene — pointing to a foreign or state-backed operation.

**Verdict: FALSE / UNSUPPORTED.** Three real aircraft anchor these theories; **every "connection to the shooting" inference is debunked**, and the **FBI and the FAA affirmatively ruled out** the central one.

## What fuels it
One structural fact dressed up as evidence: UVU sits **~5 miles from Provo's general-aviation airport**, so routine private, government, and military traffic overhead is constant — and **ADS-B gaps over mountainous terrain are normal**. Screenshots of flight-tracking data get retrofitted into a plot.

## The N888KG "transponder / fled by plane" jet — **FALSE**
- **Kernel (real):** **N888KG** (a Bombardier Challenger 300) genuinely flew **Provo → Page, AZ → Provo** on Sept 10, and ADS-B shows a signal gap ~30 min in.
- **Debunked:** the gap is an innocuous **reception dead zone** (few ground receivers + terrain near Page); the transponder was **never off**. The owner stated the outbound leg carried **two pilots and no passengers** and called any connection "inaccurate, false and without any credible basis." **FBI Director Patel** (above) and **DOT Secretary Sean Duffy / the FAA** confirmed the FAA's radar tracked it and the flight matched its plan.

## The "Egyptian plane" (SU-BTT / SU-BND) — **FALSE**
Pushed almost entirely by **Candace Owens** ("an Egyptian Air Force plane flew out of Provo… tail SU-BTT"), later expanded to two planes "shadowing" Erika Kirk "73 times."
- **Kernel:** those Egyptian tail numbers exist in public flight data. That's the only verifiable piece.
- **Debunked, fast and across the spectrum:** the Egyptian plane departed Provo **~7 a.m. — hours *before* the 12:23 p.m. shooting**; Owens **misread UTC as "Utah time."** The "73 overlaps" surveillance claim was dismantled (an analysis found ~66% of the plane-location claims wrong, many placing the aircraft **on a different continent**) — even **Alex Jones / Infowars** published a debunk. **Erika Kirk publicly told Owens to stop.** See [[Erika Kirk and the Security Team]] and [[Kirk Was Leaving the Pro-Israel Cause]].

## The "HADES spy plane" N1098L — **MISLEADING / UNSUPPORTED**
The claim: an Army ISR jet (**N1098L**, callsign "AXLE10") flew "204 feet over the murder scene, twice."
- **Kernel:** N1098L is a real **Leidos-operated Bombardier Global 6500** — a **near-daily training jet** for the Army's HADES program. Its presence over the interior West is routine.
- **Debunked:** flight data has it on a **routine El Paso → Kalispell, MT** training run that morning (arriving ~10:38 a.m. MDT — not over Utah at 12:23). Aviation-OSINT analysts found **"no credible evidence"** and called it an "information attack"; its own promoters contradict each other on who even owns the plane.

## The "open airspace / no TFR" angle — kernel **PARTLY TRUE**, inference **UNSUPPORTED**
It's true **no temporary flight restriction was placed over Provo** and the airport stayed open — but TFRs are **not automatic** for a ground shooting, and there is **no evidence anyone escaped by air** (Robinson fled on the ground and [[The Surrender|surrendered]] ~33 hours later).

## Bottom line
Real aircraft, ubiquitous innocuous aviation phenomena, **zero** verified link to the shooting — and the flagship version was **ruled out by the FBI and the FAA by name**. This is the cleanest example on the site of normal background noise (a nearby GA airport, reception dead zones, daily military ISR) being reshaped into a conspiracy.
