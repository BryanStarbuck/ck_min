# The Scene Was Paved Over

> **The claim:** UVU or police paved over / resurfaced the shooting site to destroy or hide evidence — some versions add that the paving was done by a company owned by Tyler Robinson's father.

**Verdict: MISLEADING** — the site really was resurfaced within days, but not where the evidence was, and not to destroy it.

## What fuels it
Two true facts collided within a week. UVU crews visibly went into the Sorensen Center courtyard around **Sept 15, 2025**, removed the grass where Kirk's tent had stood, and covered the area with pavers/concrete (footage circulated widely). Separately, online sleuths noted that Robinson's father, **Matt Robinson**, owns a stone/countertop company. Posts fused these into a "crime scene destroyed" narrative.

## What the evidence shows
- **Factual kernel (true):** UVU closed campus Sept 11–14 and reopened Sept 15; the grass under the tent was replaced with pavers, though "the overall design of the courtyard remains the same," and a large flag was hung above the spot.
- **Where the evidence actually was:** This was a **rooftop** shot. Per the FBI, trace evidence — **shoe impressions, a forearm imprint, and a palm print** — was collected from the **rooftop**, and [[The Rifle|the rifle]] and ammunition were recovered in a **wooded area**. The forensically load-bearing scenes were the roof and the woods, **processed in the days right after Sept 10** — not the courtyard lawn that was later paved. See [[The Surveillance and the Gait]] and [[Line of Sight and Shot Geometry]].
- **Day 2 confirmed the paving — and put the lead investigator outside it.** On cross, the State's case agent [[David Hull|Agent Hull]] acknowledged the **dirt under the tent was paved over**, but said he learned of it **"only because it was in the news,"** and did not know **when it happened or who ordered it** [02:08:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7704s). See [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]]. Read fairly for the defense, that the head of the investigation was **not consulted** on altering a scene he'd worked is a legitimate [[Gaps in the Investigation|process]] point. But it also **cuts against a destroy-evidence plot**: this was a UVU facilities decision the investigators weren't steering, not a coordinated cover-up — and it happened where the case's physical evidence **wasn't**.

> 🎥 **Forbes Breaking News — the courtroom exchange at the heart of this theory:** Robinson's lawyer asks the lead investigator to explain why the UVU site was paved over days after the killing.

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IWuYlLsH-_4)

> **Key exchange** (defense counsel Nester ↔ State case agent [[David Hull|Hull]]): **Nester —** *"…were you involved in the decision to pave over the dirt that was under the tent, at UVU?"* **Hull —** *"No."* **Nester —** *"Did you know that happened?"* **Hull —** *"Only because it was in the news."* **Nester —** *"But you don't know when, or who ordered that?"* **Hull —** *"Nope."*
- **The "father's company paved it" element:** **FALSE.** No source links Matt Robinson or his company to any UVU work; the contractor was not publicly named, and Matt Robinson had in fact **cooperated with investigators** in identifying his son.

## The UVU records (2026): what the primary documents show
Public records released via Utah's open-records law (**GRAMA**, obtained by **Judicial Watch**) now let us **date this precisely** and separate fact from inference. Sworn declarations and the contractor text thread establish: the site was **under law-enforcement control for evidence collection until "on or about Friday, September 12, 2025," when the scene was released back to UVU**; **paver installation "was completed" by Tuesday, September 16**; **UVU Police "did not have control of the crime scene" and were not involved**; and **UVU coordinated the work through Facilities VP [[The Site Cleanup and Paving Records|Frank Young]]**. The records **sharpen** the timeline (paved Sep 13–16, not "around Sep 15") and **confirm** the kernel — a fast, emergency cleanup — while **still placing the paving after the FBI released the scene** and away from the roof/woods where the evidence was.

But they also add **two genuinely open threads** the theory can fairly point to: (1) UVU paid for the **cleanup** on an **emergency, no-bid** contract (**$6,090.52**, ordered **9/11**) but is **withholding the vendor's name and the payment check** (safety exemption); and (2) UVU swears it **"did not pay for or install the pavers"** — so an **unnamed non-UVU party paved over the spot** where Kirk was shot. **No document names the contractor.** Full ingest, timeline, and the verbatim texts: **[[The Site Cleanup and Paving Records]]**.

## Bottom line
A reopened campus getting its lawn resurfaced is ordinary; it is not where the case's physical evidence lived. The kernel (the site was changed — fast, on an emergency contract, by an **undisclosed** paver) is real and now **documented**; the inference (to destroy evidence) is still **unsupported** — the FBI collected evidence and released the scene first, and the forensics were on the roof and in the woods; and no document names the vendor, which remains redacted. See [[The Site Cleanup and Paving Records]].
