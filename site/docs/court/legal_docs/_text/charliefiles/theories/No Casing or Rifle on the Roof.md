# No Casing or Rifle on the Roof

> **The claim:** No spent casing and no rifle were found on the roof — so the whole rooftop-shooter story is fake.

**Verdict: FALSE.**

## What fuels it
Day-1 cross-examination. Officer [[Chris Bagley|Bagley]] testified he searched the rooftop for a spent casing and *"couldn't find a shell casing at that moment"* ([04:11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15085s)) — no casing, and no rifle, on the roof itself. (On the same cross, the defense asked whether he was aware of *"a bullet found on another roof"*; he was not — see [[A Bullet on the Other Roof]].)

## What the evidence shows
The premise misunderstands the weapon. [[The Rifle]] is a **Mauser 98 bolt-action, retrofitted to a .30-06 barrel** ([Day 2] [05:03:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18214s)) — and a **bolt-action rifle is cycled by hand**, so the fired casing **stays with the shooter** rather than ejecting onto the ground.

**Day 2 confirmed both halves of the premise on the State's own case agent.** On cross, [[David Hull|Agent Hull]] agreed investigators **never found any bullets or casings on the Losee roof, or immediately around the Losee building** [01:49:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6572s)–[01:49:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6597s) — so "nothing on the roof" is now **undisputed**. And on redirect, Hull confirmed the **rifle was recovered from the wooded area Robinson is believed to have entered, on the 10th** [02:20:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=8441s)–[02:21:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=8484s). See [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].

The items were in fact recovered — **together, along the escape route**, not on the roof:
- The **rifle** (Mauser 98), wrapped in a **towel**, in the wooded area north of the shooting position.
- The **casings** (one fired, three unfired), **engraved**. See [[The Engraved Casings]].
- The fired casing was **positively matched** to the rifle, with **Robinson's DNA** on trigger, casing, and towel. See [[The Casing Match and DNA]].

> **Reasonable-doubt read (for the defense):** the fair version isn't "the story is fake" — it's that the **shot-origin proof is soft**: nothing on the roof, and a [[The Inconclusive Bullet|fatal fragment that doesn't match]]. That is a legitimate reasonable-doubt point. See [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]].

## Bottom line
"Nothing on the roof" is exactly what a bolt-action shooter who carried his weapon off would leave. The rifle and casings weren't missing — they were found where he fled, and they tie back to Robinson.
