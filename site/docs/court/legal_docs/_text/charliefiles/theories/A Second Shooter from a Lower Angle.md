# A Second Shooter from a Lower Angle

> **The claim:** The wound trajectory doesn't match the roof — a second shooter fired from a lower angle.

**Verdict: UNSUPPORTED** (formally **OPEN** pending the full autopsy trajectory).

## What fuels it
No detailed **wound-trajectory** analysis has been publicly released, which leaves room to argue the angle is "wrong." Some "wrong building" videos also circulated.

## What the evidence shows
- The State's charging [[2025-09_charging_information-charges.pdf|Information]] describes the **bullet's trajectory passing close to bystanders in front of Kirk** — consistent with a **single line of fire**.
- A **~430 ft rooftop shot is only a shallow ~6° downward angle**, so a **near-horizontal neck wound is not inconsistent** with the rooftop. See [[Line of Sight and Shot Geometry]].
- There is **no affirmative evidence** of a second shooter. The "wrong building" videos were **debunked**, and the murder weapon was recovered along the **rooftop-to-north escape route**, not a second position. See [[The Rifle]].

## What Day 2 moved — in both directions
Day 2 ([[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]]) touched the second-shooter question on three fronts, and on balance it **narrows** the theory rather than feeding it:
- **The "bullet on another roof" got an innocent explanation.** What could have looked like a second firing position was, per [[David Hull|Agent Hull]], **an ejected round from an officer who cycled his rifle** — its location now unknown [01:43:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6203s)–[01:44:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6265s). That deflates a key feeder. See [[A Bullet on the Other Roof]].
- **No casings anywhere near the Losee building or the courtyard** [01:49:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6572s)–[01:49:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6597s) — no ballistic sign of a second firing point.
- **Two genuinely open threads remain, though neither shows a second *shooter*.** A **gun was reportedly found in the amphitheater holster** (Gonzales via Davis, Exhibit Hull-13) [01:48:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6538s) — a second *weapon*, unattributed, but a **sidearm at an open-carry event**, not evidence of a second gunman (see [[The Unattributed Holster]]); and the Ring-camera **Nobles reported a *bald* driver and *three other people* in the car** [02:06:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7560s)–[02:07:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7627s), which the State hadn't surfaced — a fair "not alone" point for [[Defense Strategies and Degrees of Liability|Layer 1]], but about *company*, not a second shot.

## Bottom line
The honest status is **UNSUPPORTED, but formally open**: there's no evidence for a second **shooter**, and the geometry fits a single rooftop round — and Day 2, if anything, **removed** the strongest feeder (the "other roof" bullet) while leaving no casings at a second position. Because the full wound-trajectory report isn't public, this stays on the [[What Is Genuinely Open|open list]] rather than being called flatly false. Compare the courtyard version: [[The Shot Came from the Courtyard]].
