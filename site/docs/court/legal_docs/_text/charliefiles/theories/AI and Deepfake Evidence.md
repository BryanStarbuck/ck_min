# AI and Deepfake Evidence

> **The claim:** "Evidence" proves the plot — a pre-recorded death message, a fake photo of Kirk's widow, and similar clips.

**Verdict: FABRICATED / FALSE** — the "evidence" is the fake.

## What fuels it
A flood of AI-generated and doctored media that spread faster than it could be checked.

## What the evidence shows
Multiple viral images and clips were **fabricated** — several identifiable as fakes on their face:
- a **distorted TPUSA logo**;
- a **misspelled fake news chyron**;
- **AI-generated accounts** and images (including a purported photo of his widow and a "pre-recorded death message").

These are **manufactured artifacts, not evidence** about the shooting.

## If the defense raised it
> If the defense argued the *evidence against Robinson* is AI/deepfaked, that would go **directly to innocence.** But the State's case rests on **physical evidence and sworn testimony** — the [[The Rifle|rifle]], [[The Casing Match and DNA|DNA and casing match]], [[The Discord and Text Messages|his own messages]], and live witnesses — **not** on viral images. Discrediting a fake image doesn't touch the physical record. See [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]].

## Bottom line
When the "proof" is itself an AI fabrication, it tells you about the information war around the case — not about what happened at UVU. See [[Doctored Suspect Claims]] and [[The Surveillance Video Was Doctored]].
