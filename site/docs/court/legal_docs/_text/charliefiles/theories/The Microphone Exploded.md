# The Microphone Exploded

> **The claim:** It wasn't a gunshot at all — Kirk's microphone (or a device on him) exploded.

**Verdict: FALSE.**

## What fuels it
Low-quality video of a sudden, violent moment, plus a visible "bulge" on Kirk's shirt read as a hidden device.

## What the evidence shows (the primary record)
- The **medical examiner certified the cause of death as a gunshot wound to the neck** — the official finding, quoted in the [[2025-09-17 - The Search Warrant Affidavit|search-warrant affidavit]] (Dr. Guajardo). A device is nowhere in it. See [[The Medical Examiner and the Wound]].
- A **Mauser .30-06 rifle** and **engraved casings** were recovered, and a **bullet-jacket fragment was recovered at Kirk's autopsy** — see the [[2025-09-16 - The Information and Charges|The Information and Charges]], the [[2025-09-12 - Affidavit of Probable Cause|Affidavit of Probable Cause]], [[The Rifle]], and [[The Engraved Casings]].
- The responding officer testified he **recognized gunfire** and found the rooftop prone "sniper" position ([[2026-07-06 - Preliminary Hearing Day 1 Transcript|Day 1 Transcript]]).
- There is **no device, no residue, and no blast finding** anywhere in the record. The shirt "bulge" is the **microphone rig** Kirk wore.

## If the defense raised it
> As an alternative theory, "it wasn't a gunshot" would go **directly to innocence** — no shooting means no shooter to convict. To run it, the defense would have to discredit the **ME's gunshot finding, the recovered rifle and casings, and the autopsy fragment** — which is why the defense has **not, so far, gone near it**. That could change — the hearing is ongoing and the defense may yet call its own experts — but **nothing in the record to date** supports it. See [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]].

## Bottom line
A gunshot to the neck is documented by the autopsy, the recovered weapon, and the scene. **On the current record**, the exploding-mic reading is **contradicted**, not merely unsupported — and no contrary expert evidence has been offered. If the defense later put on an expert who could impeach the autopsy, the rifle, and the fragment, that would have to be weighed; **as the record stands**, it fails.
