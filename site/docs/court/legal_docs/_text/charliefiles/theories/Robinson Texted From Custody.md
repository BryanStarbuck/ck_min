# Robinson Texted From Custody

> **The claim:** Robinson sent text messages **after he was already in custody** — proof the messages were planted or the timeline is fake.

**Verdict: UNSUPPORTED.**

## What fuels it
Court filings released the incriminating messages **without precise timestamps**. That gap lets people assume some were sent post-arrest.

## What the evidence shows
- **No sourced evidence** supports any message timestamped **after** his ~9 p.m. surrender.
- The incriminating messages — including the Discord note *"im surrendering through a sheriff friend in a few moments"* — are all **pre-surrender**. See [[The Discord and Text Messages]] and [[The Surrender]].
- A **separate, real** story is being conflated with this one: the sheriff's office had **no surveillance footage** of the surrender (deleted after a 30-day retention window). That is about **missing footage**, not messaging from custody. It's on the honest [[What Is Genuinely Open|open list]].

> **Reasonable-doubt read (for the defense):** the fair kernel is that the **missing timestamps** are a real gap the defense is entitled to probe on cross — even though nothing shows a message actually sent from custody. See [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]].

## Bottom line
The "texted from custody" claim rests entirely on the **absence of published timestamps**, not on any message actually shown to post-date the surrender. Missing timestamps ≠ messages from a cell.
