# Who Held the UVU Security Footage

> **The claim:** Kirk's team took control of the **UVU security** footage.

**Verdict: FALSE.**

## What fuels it
Confusion between two **different** bodies of video — and the (real) [[A Staffer Pulled the SD Cards|SD-card removal]] from Kirk's *own event cameras*, mistaken for control of the *campus surveillance*.

## What the evidence shows
- The **campus surveillance** at the core of the State's case is held by **law enforcement / the Utah County Attorney's Office** — hundreds of hours, reviewed and **played in open court** ([[2026-07-06 - Preliminary Hearing Day 1 Transcript|Day 1 Transcript]]). Kirk's team never controlled it. See [[The Surveillance and the Gait]].
- **Day 2 made the source explicit.** The State's case agent, ex-SBI investigator **David Hull**, testified the compilation is "footage that was obtained from **Utah Valley University** as part of the investigation" [00:23:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=1418s) — **footage the university provided to the FBI** (including the parking-structure cameras) and that Hull personally reviewed against the raw feed [00:43:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=2635s). That is a **university-owned, law-enforcement-reviewed** chain, not a TPUSA one. See [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].
- What a TPUSA staffer handled were the **event A/V cards** — a separate rig, later turned over to the **FBI**. See [[The SD Cards]].

## If the defense raised it
> A "the footage was controlled or hidden" argument would feed **reasonable doubt** if it were true — and the [[A Staffer Pulled the SD Cards|SD-card chain of custody]] is a fair thing to test on cross. But the **UVU surveillance that matters was law-enforcement-held and shown in court.** See [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]].

## Bottom line
Two separate camera systems get merged in this claim. The one that matters — UVU's surveillance — was in law-enforcement hands throughout and played in open court.
