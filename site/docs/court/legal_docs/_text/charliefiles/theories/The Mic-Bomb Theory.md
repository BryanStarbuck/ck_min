# The Mic-Bomb Theory

> **The claim:** Charlie Kirk was killed by an explosive charge in or at the wireless microphone on his collar, not by a .30-06 round fired from a distant rooftop.

**Where it stands:** Citizen investigators who have worked the evidence seriously have converged on this. By a large majority on X they conclude that it appears to be the truth, and it is supported in several independent ways. The government's narrative remains a single .30-06 round from a Mauser 98.

## What supports it
Each line below stands on its own. What makes the theory persuasive is that they agree with each other.
- **The wound and the energy.** There is no blood spray and no exit wound, which does not fit a .30-06 at that range. The front neck wound fits a point-blank source at the collar.
- **The shirt moves before the blood.** In slow-motion video the shirt and necklace push outward before any blood appears, which is how a blast drives fabric.
- **The audio.** The Canon XA55 recording puts a loud event at the tent, separate from any distant rifle report, and the acoustics point south rather than to the Losee rooftop.
- **The microphone's placement.** The transmitter sat under the shirt against the chest and neck, not clipped over it.
- **The debris.** A burnt object recovered from the SUV is consistent with a destroyed RODE transmitter.
- **The ballistics gap.** The ATF could tie the recovered fragment only to class characteristics. It could not match it to the Mauser.
- **The residue.** The necklace and the residue evidence were removed rather than tested.

## What remains open
- Who built and placed the device, and how it was set off, has **not been established**. None of the lines above points to any identified person, and this file names no one.
- The official case file still rests on the medical examiner's gunshot-wound finding, the recovered **[[The Rifle|rifle]]** and **[[The Engraved Casings|casings]]**, and the bullet fragment recovered at autopsy. See [[The Casing Match and DNA]]. The citizen analysis above is the case against that account.

## If the defense raised it
> This goes **to innocence**. If a jury accepted that Charlie Kirk died from a charge at the microphone, a rifle on a distant roof could not be the cause of death. See [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]].

## Bottom line
The mic-bomb theory is the explanation citizen investigators have converged on, and it fits more of the physical record than the rooftop-rifle account does.
