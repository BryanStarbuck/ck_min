# Erika Kirk and the Security Team

> **The claim:** Erika Kirk promoted, hired, or was tied to the security team/company — often folded into a broader claim that she has Israeli-intelligence connections.

**Verdict: UNSUPPORTED** — no evidence links her to the UVU security firm; the broader "Israeli-intelligence" claims are **FALSE / debunked**.

## What fuels it
The conspiracy ecosystem — including **Candace Owens**, who has cast suspicion on Erika Kirk and, per CNN (Dec 16, 2025), declined to retract her Israel-related claims even after meeting with her.

## What the evidence shows
- **No documented evidence** that Erika Kirk hired, owns, promoted, or selected **Rockhouse / Integrity Security Solutions** (see [[Kirks Security Detail]]). Reporting ties that firm to **Charlie Kirk / TPUSA operations** and to **Brian Harpole** — not to Erika.
- **The mundane kernel:** like any high-profile widow, she **has a personal protective detail whose recommendations she follows** — e.g., canceling an April 2026 University of Georgia appearance over "credible threats" (the Secret Service reportedly found no evidence of danger). That's unremarkable, and doesn't tie her to the UVU firm.
- **The broader intelligence-tie claims** about her rest on **no records, filings, or documents** linking her to Israeli entities — none have surfaced. One real but minor episode: the Israeli Government Press Office announced she would accept a posthumous award for Charlie, and **TPUSA said she would not** — an ordinary diplomatic mix-up, not a covert tie. Erika Kirk has also publicly condemned right-wing antisemitism.

## Bottom line
There's a real detail here — she has security and defers to it — inflated into a false claim that she is tied to the UVU firm or to Israeli intelligence. No evidence supports either; the ethnic-intelligence version is a debunked, and often antisemitic, narrative. See [[The Security Detail Was Israeli]] and [[AI and Deepfake Evidence]].
