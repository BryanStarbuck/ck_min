# The Site Cleanup and Paving Records

In mid-2026, **Utah Valley University released public records** — obtained under Utah's open-records law (**GRAMA**) by **Judicial Watch** — documenting the **cleanup and paving of the courtyard where Charlie Kirk was shot**. They are **primary documents** (sworn declarations, an emergency-payment form, and the contractor↔UVU text thread), and they let us **date the cleanup precisely** and separate what the record actually shows from the "**crime scene was paved over to hide evidence**" narrative. This page ingests them and corroborates the timeline; the *claim* itself is rated on [[The Scene Was Paved Over]].

> **What these are (and their limits).** These are **UVU records** released by **Judicial Watch**, a conservative open-records group whose framing ("clean-up of the assassination site") is a *characterization*. The **underlying documents are primary and reliable**; where Judicial Watch fills in a **redacted or cut-off** line (e.g., a text it reads as "keeping quiet"), this page flags it as *their* reading, not verified text. Redactions are UVU's, under cited GRAMA exemptions. Commentary on the public record — **not** proof of a plot.

## The timeline these records establish
The sworn **Declaration of Frank Young** (UVU Associate VP of Facilities Planning, the official who coordinated the work) and the **contractor text thread** fix the dates:

| Date (2025) | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Sep 10, ~12:23 p.m.** | Kirk is shot | — |
| **Sep 10–12** | The site is **under the control of external law-enforcement agencies for collection of evidence** | Young Decl. ¶2 |
| **Sep 11** | UVU places an **emergency order with a vendor** — product description **"Clean up for shooting incident"** | Emergency Payment Form (Prod 2, p.49) |
| **Fri, Sep 12** | **"The scene was released back to UVU"**; crews begin (initially just a wash-down — *"we're not gonna need to cut out any sod… we can just wash it all down"*); *"The FBI is still there. I guess."* | Young Decl. ¶3; Prod 1 texts |
| **Sat, Sep 13** | Cleanup continues (*"any gloves?"*); *"Pavers or concrete for the courtyard?"* — *"they want to do something tomorrow. Contractors are cleaning their tools."* | Prod 1 texts |
| **Sun, Sep 14, 7:46 p.m.** | *"Our guys busted their [butts] today. **The first pavers are being laid down now.**"* — **gray pavers** chosen (*"Red is too much"*); *"the media was bothering your team"* | Prod 1 texts |
| **Mon, Sep 15** | Campus reopens (established previously) | — |
| **Tue, Sep 16** | **Paver installation "was completed"** | Young Decl. ¶5 |
| **Sep 19** | Cleanup **invoice** dated — **$6,090.52** | Emergency Payment Form |

So the courtyard where the tent stood went from **shooting to fully paved in six days** (Sep 10 → Sep 16), with the **crime scene released by law enforcement on Sep 12** and the **pavers laid Sep 14–16**. This **sharpens** the earlier "around Sept 15" estimate on [[The Scene Was Paved Over]] to the **sworn/documented dates**.

## Who controlled and coordinated it
- **Law enforcement first, then UVU.** Per Young, the scene was **under external law-enforcement control for evidence collection** until it was **released back to UVU "on or about Friday, September 12"** (Young Decl. ¶¶2–3). The texts corroborate the FBI still finishing on Sep 12 (*"The FBI is still there"*).
- **UVU Police were *not* involved.** Both declarations state **UVUPD "did not have control of the crime scene" and were "not involved in the removal of the evidence or material"** (Fowler Decl. ¶4; Young Decl.). UVU used that to justify **not searching UVUPD emails/texts** at all — itself a point the requester contested.
- **Facilities ran it.** **Frank Young** (AVP Facilities Planning since 2014) "helped coordinate the **cleaning and paving** of the site after it was released back to UVU control" (Young Decl. ¶4).

## The money — and the piece that doesn't add up cleanly
This is the sharpest thing in the records: **UVU paid for the *cleanup* but says it did *not* pay for — or install — the *pavers*.**
- **The cleanup: UVU paid, vendor withheld.** An **"Emergency Payment Request Form"** (Prod 2, p.49) shows an order placed **9/11/2025**, invoice **$6,090.52** (dated 9/19), description **"Clean up for shooting incident."** UVU **bypassed its own procurement rules** (no purchase order, no competitive bid) because it was **"an extreme emergency."** The **vendor's name is redacted**, and UVU is **withholding in full "a check relating to payment to the vendor that cleaned the site… which includes banking information and the name of the vendor"** — under GRAMA's business-confidentiality and safety exemptions (§ 63G-2-305(11)–(12)), citing "**concerns of threats to their life and safety should their identity become known.**"
- **The pavers: UVU says *not us*.** Young swore that **"UVU does not have estimates, invoices, or purchase orders for the paver installation project because the work was not paid for or performed by UVU"** (Young Decl. ¶6); Fowler swore Young told her **"UVU did not pay for or install the pavers at the site"** (Fowler Decl. ¶5). So **a non-UVU party paid for and installed pavers over the spot where Kirk was shot — and the records do not name who.**
- **A wrinkle in the "no records" story.** When first asked, UVU/Procurement said there were **no invoice records** for the cleanup; UVU later **"since received… the purchase and invoice records related to the site cleanup"** (Fowler Decl. ¶¶7–8) — i.e., the $6,090.52 form. The "no responsive records / didn't pay" framing was true only of the **paving**, not the **cleanup**.

- **Sun, Sep 14:** *"We heard the media was bothering your team. Shall we send a uniform over?"* — *"Maybe that would be good. ## The records fight (GRAMA)
Judicial Watch investigator **Bill Marshall** filed the request **Nov 20, 2025**. UVU (via the **Utah AG's office**, AAG **Nicole Ferguson**) **denied it Dec 15** as "not reasonably specific," was **partly reversed on internal appeal** by CAO **Val Peterson** (Jan 8, 2026), and defended its search before the state **Government Records Office** (March 2026). UVU's positions: for **"destruction of evidence," "there are no responsive records"**; any **evidence-*removal*** police record is **withheld** under the law-enforcement exemption (§ 63G-2-305(10)) because **"there is an ongoing criminal investigation and prosecution"** and release "could… deprive a person of a right to a fair trial." Notably, the **certificate of service lists both [[The Defense Team|Richard Novak]] (Robinson's defense) and [[The Prosecution|Jeffrey Gray]] (Utah County Attorney)** as served parties — the records touch the criminal case, so both sides were noticed.

## What this actually substantiates
Read honestly against the "**crime scene was paved over to destroy evidence**" claim (rated **MISLEADING** on [[The Scene Was Paved Over]]):
- **Confirmed and sharpened:** the courtyard **was** cleaned and paved **within days** (Sep 12 release → Sep 16 paved), on an **emergency, no-bid** cleanup contract, with the **vendor's identity withheld**, and the **paving paid for/performed by an unnamed non-UVU party.** The **speed, the redactions, and the "who paid for the pavers?" gap** are real and are what the theory legitimately points to.
- **Still cuts against a "destroy evidence" plot:** the **FBI/law-enforcement collected evidence first** and only **released the scene on Sep 12**; **UVU Police were not involved**; the load-bearing **forensic evidence was on the roof and in the woods** (shoe/forearm/palm prints, [[The Rifle|the rifle]], casings), **not the courtyard lawn** that was paved (see [[The Surveillance and the Gait]], [[Line of Sight and Shot Geometry]]); and the paving was a **UVU Facilities decision the investigators weren't steering** — Day-2 testimony had the lead case agent [[David Hull|Hull]] learning of it **"only because it was in the news"** ([[The Scene Was Paved Over]]).
- **The genuinely open pieces** (now on [[What Is Genuinely Open]]): **who paid for and installed the pavers** (UVU disclaims it), and **the withheld vendor identity** — the documents do not name the vendor. See [[The Scene Was Paved Over]].
