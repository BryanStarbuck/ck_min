# The Engraved Casings

Found with [[The Rifle]] — wrapped in the same dark towel, in **trees along the suspect's filmed escape route** north of the shooting position — were **four engraved cartridges: one fired and three unfired.** The plan to **engrave bullets** appears in the [[The Discord and Text Messages|Discord/text messages]] *before* the shooting, tying the physical objects to the digital record.

## Where they were found
**Not on the roof** — with the rifle, along the escape route. The single **fired** casing was **positively matched** to the rifle; **Robinson's DNA** was recovered on the **fired casing and two of the three unfired rounds** (plus the trigger and other rifle parts) — see [[The Casing Match and DNA]]. Why nothing was on the roof: a **bolt-action** rifle does not auto-eject a casing — see [[No Casing or Rifle on the Roof]].

> **Day 2 (light) confirms the "nothing on the roof" premise.** On cross, Agent Hull agreed there were **no casings found on or around the Losee** rooftop [01:49:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6572s)–[01:49:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6597s) — consistent with a bolt-action and with the engraved cartridges being recovered **with the rifle** in the wooded area, not at the firing position. Day 2 did not re-cover the inscriptions themselves. See [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].

- **Unfired**: *"hey fascist! CATCH!"* (with arrow symbols) · *"O Bella ciao, Bella ciao, Bella ciao, Ciao, ciao!"* · *"If you read This, you are GAY Lmao."*

## In his home: a matching casing and targets
The link is not only at the rifle. Per **the Information**, when police searched **Robinson's residence** they found:
- **a shell casing with etchings *like* those on the rifle's rounds** — i.e. **matching engraved ammunition** in his home; and
- **several targets with bullet holes** — evidence of shooting/practice.

![[info-home-search-crop.png|700]]
*From the [[2025-09-16 - The Information and Charges|Information]] (p. 9): under **"The Search of Robinson's Residence,"** police found **a shell casing with etchings like** those on the rifle's rounds and **several targets with bullet holes** — the home corroboration of the hand-engraved ammunition.*

With the messages that describe **engraving bullets** *before* the shooting (*"remember how I was engraving bullets?"*) and the **DNA** on the fired casing and two unfired rounds, this is a strong tie between Robinson and this specific, hand-engraved ammunition. See [[2025-09-16 - The Information and Charges|the Information]].

## Day 4 (July 9) — the engraving tool is identified, and matched to the murder rounds
Day 4 tied the hand-engraved ammunition to a specific tool recovered from Robinson's home:
- **Twiggs's recorded statement:** Robinson borrowed a **Dremel** "to create messages on bullets," about a month before, framed as going "hunting with his family" [02:18:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8315s). See [[Lance Twiggs]].
- **The rifle rounds:** the towel-wrapped rifle held **one fired .30-06 cartridge case + three unfired cartridges, all engraved** [04:52:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=17524s) (headstamp "Remington .30-06 Springfield").
- **The home search:** Sgt. [[Jennifer Faumuina]] recovered a **Dremel tool and a bits container** plus two Remington **.30-06** boxes in the townhome [05:01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18067s); on top of the **safe** in the spare bedroom next to Robinson's were **five .30-06 casings — one engraved "test shot"** (Exhibit 23) [05:04:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18264s), the **same caliber** as the round in the rifle [05:04:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18284s).
- **The ATF tool-mark match (via Faumuina reading the reports):** the marks on the rifle's case/cartridges are "consistent with … a Dremel tool" [05:29:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=19794s), and the **home engraving cutter (Exhibit 15A) was identified as having made the tool marks** on the rifle's cartridge case, cartridges 3–5, and the safe casing (18A) [05:40:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=20401s).

So the engraving is no longer inferred only from the messages and a "like" casing — the physical chain now runs **Robinson's Dremel bit → the murder rifle's engraved rounds → a "test shot" casing on his safe.** See [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4|Day 4 Legal Review]] and [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4 Transcript|Day 4 Transcript]].

## What the Dremel's *DNA* actually showed (Day 5) — a mixed, defense-usable picture
It's worth being precise here, because the Dremel's DNA is often described as "Robinson's and Twiggs's" — the record is more nuanced, and the **defense used it, not the State.** On Day 5, ATF DNA Section Chief [[Caitlin Oliver]] testified to her Dremel report (report "Oliver-157"). She had taken **elimination samples** from the three people **associated with the apartment** — Robinson's **mother (Amber)**, **father (Matthew)**, and **[[Lance Twiggs]]** [02:17:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=8224s). The results:
- **The Dremel itself → Twiggs.** The swabs of the **Dremel's buttons and other areas** returned **support for inclusion of Lance Twiggs** [02:19:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=8382s)–[02:20:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=8432s).
- **The murder-rifle *bolt* swabs → the *father*.** Items **1.4 and 1.6** (swabs of the rifle's bolt) returned **support for inclusion of Matthew Robinson — Tyler's father** [02:18:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=8314s), [02:19:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=8358s).
- **The defense's "household DNA" point.** [[The Defense Team|Burt]] used these hits to argue the DNA is **presence-in-the-house, not use-in-the-crime**: a household member's DNA on a shared tool shows up "**not because they're a suspect, but because they're associated with the premises**," and Oliver agreed it's "**reasonable to assume anyone living in the household could have contributed to DNA on an object found in that household.**"

**So the Dremel's weight is the *tool-mark match* + *Twiggs's account*, not a clean "Tyler's DNA on the Dremel."** The Dremel-body DNA the record actually shows is **Twiggs's**; the rifle-bolt DNA on those particular swabs is the **father's**. Robinson's *own* DNA anchors the items that matter most — the **fired casing (Ex. 2)**, the **trigger**, the **towel**, and the engraved **"test shot" casing (18A)** at ≥1 trillion — **not the Dremel body**. See [[The Casing Match and DNA]], [[The DNA Didn't Match]].

> **Note on the engraving tool.** As of the *charging record and Day 1–2 testimony*, the carving/engraving tool itself had **not** been identified — the engraving was established only through the **messages** and the **matching casing**. **This changed on Day 4** (see above): a **Dremel** engraving cutter recovered from Robinson's home (Exhibit 15A) was **matched by the ATF** to the tool marks on the murder rifle's rounds, and Twiggs said Robinson borrowed a Dremel "to create messages on bullets." *(The screwdriver found at the perch is a separate item, not tied to the engraving.)*

## How the Dremel was "matched" to the etchings — and how thin the showing was
This is the strongest single **physical** link in the case — and, as presented, one of the **least explained**. The **ATF tool-mark report (Exhibit 34)** made the claim in **two tiers**:

- **Class level (a Dremel-*type* tool).** The etchings show "numerous sets of [tool marks] … **consistent with being produced by engravings and rotary cutting tips commonly found installed in handheld rotary tools such as a dremel tool**," with "**marks of value for comparison if a suspect tool is located**" [05:30:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=19808s)–[05:30:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=19823s), [05:37:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=20275s). That alone says only *a* rotary tool, not *his*.
- **Individual level (this specific bit).** The report then goes further: "**the exhibit 15A engraving cutter was identified as having made** tool marks located on the **exhibit two cartridge case** [the fired case from the rifle], **exhibits three through five** [the three unfired engraved rounds from the rifle], and the **exhibit 18A cartridge case** [the "test shot" casing from his home safe]" [05:40:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=20401s). Exhibit 15A was **"the longest middle bit"** of the Dremel's bit set [05:39:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=20371s). *That* is the load-bearing claim: **one particular bit from his home made the messages on the murder rounds.**

**How thin the courtroom showing was.** You're right that the prosecutor essentially **marked the report and moved on.** The whole tool-mark identification came in as a **conduit read-in** — Sgt. [[Jennifer Faumuina]], *not* a tool-mark examiner, **read the ATF report's conclusions paragraph by paragraph**; **no live examiner explained *how* the identification was made**, and it was **never cross-examined** (the Confrontation objection is preserved). The one live firearm/tool-mark examiner, [[Samantha Karner]], was the **defense's** witness and was examined about the **bullet and casing** (firearm) comparisons — she did **not** walk the court through the engraving-cutter match.

**How a rotary-engraving-tool identification *is* actually done** (from Karner's general tool-mark testimony + the AFTE framework): a tool transfers **class characteristics** (the shape/size of the cutting tip) and **individual characteristics** — microscopic imperfections from **manufacturing (grinding)** plus "**wear, use, and abuse of a tool**" that are "**distinct to that tool**" [06:42:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=24154s)–[06:42:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=24173s). To identify, the examiner makes **test etchings with the seized bit** on a like brass surface, then compares those test marks to the questioned etchings under a **comparison microscope**, looking for **reproducible "sufficient agreement"** of the striations. Two honest caveats the defense's own expert supplied: "sufficient agreement" is **an examiner's opinion** — "**the subjective part**" [07:05:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=25537s) — and the marks are **never reproduced identically** ("almost like a stamp … the marks aren't going to be left … the same 100% of the time") [06:55:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=24926s). The **NAS/PCAST foundational-validity** critiques Karner conceded for firearm/tool-mark ID (see [[The Inconclusive Bullet]]) apply here too — arguably **more** so, since **rotary-engraving-tool marks are far less studied** than the fired-bullet/casing comparisons those validation studies examined.

**Bottom line on the match.** For **probable cause** it is strong and unrebutted — the same bit that etched the murder rounds etched a casing on his safe. For **trial** it is (1) currently **unconfronted hearsay**, and (2) a **subjective tool-mark opinion** of exactly the kind the defense is positioned to attack under Rule 702 — and it was seated with almost no explanation.

## What was in the home — engraved *and* plain rounds
Answering the natural question directly: the home held **both** kinds of ammunition —
- **An engraved, test-fired casing.** The **"test shot"** .30-06 casing on top of the safe (**Exhibit 18A**) was **hand-etched "test shot,"** was **fired in the murder rifle** (with 18B–18E) [05:39:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=20348s), was **etched by the same 15A bit** [05:40:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=20401s), and carried **Robinson's DNA** — "at least 1,000,000,000,000 times more likely if it originated from Tyler Robinson" [05:35:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=20138s). That one object ties the **rifle**, the **bit**, and **his DNA** together — the home counterpart of the murder rounds.
- **Plain, same-type rounds.** Also in the townhome: **two Remington .30-06 ammunition boxes** and loose **.30-06 cartridge cases** — the **same brand/caliber, unengraved** — plus the other safe casings (18B–18E) and **shooting targets with bullet holes** (practice) [05:07:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18472s).

So it is neither "just same-brand rounds" nor "only engraved rounds" — it is **both**, and the **engraved "test shot" casing is the load-bearing item**: the one that ties his home, his DNA, the murder rifle, and the engraving bit into a single object.
