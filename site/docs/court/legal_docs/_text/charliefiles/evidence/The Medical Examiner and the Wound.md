# The Medical Examiner and the Wound

This is where it is most important to separate the **official finding** from the **dramatic details** — because the two get merged online, and only one is confirmed.

## What is official
The **only official finding** is a **gunshot wound to the neck; manner of death, homicide.** Per the search-warrant affidavit, **Dr. Guajardo** of the Utah Office of the Medical Examiner (news coverage spells the name "Guajardo") certified the cause of death, with Sgt. Barrett of the State Bureau of Investigation attending the autopsy.

At the Day-1 hearing, the ME **did not testify** — investigator **Sgt. David Hull** summarized the report as reliable hearsay (permissible at a preliminary hearing, but, as the defense noted, it "could never come in at trial" that way). The **full autopsy report has not been publicly released** under Utah law.

> **Day 2 (light) kept the wound at arm's length from the record.** On cross, Hull confirmed he was **not present at the autopsy** and knows it only "from my meeting with the medical examiner and his report" [02:03:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7385s)–[02:03:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7407s). The autopsy produced **fingerprints and autopsy photos on a CD** and, per Hull, **"some fragments of a bullet casing"** transferred through a chain of custody [02:04:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7456s)–[02:04:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7488s) — he did not know how many fragments. The **autopsy report itself was not published** at the hearing (media counsel noted the press had **not objected** to withholding it) [00:29:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=1760s). So the wound specifics stayed exactly where this note places them: **documented in an unreleased report, summarized through an investigator**, not proven in open court. Day 2 did **not** cover the neck wound's trajectory, any exit wound, or the spine detail. See [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].

## What is only staff-sourced (plausible, not confirmed)
The widely-repeated specifics — **no exit wound**, the round **fragmenting on the cervical spine**, recovered **under the skin** — come from **Kirk-staff and surgeon accounts**, not a released ME statement:
- Producer **Andrew Kolvet** (the "absolute miracle" surgeon account).
- Security chief **Brian Harpole** (*Salt Lake Tribune*, Nov. 19, 2025; and, in far more detail, the [[2025-11-17 - Brian Harpole on the Shawn Ryan Show|Shawn Ryan Show]]) — who had his hand in the wound and says that, per the operating-room doctor, the round **struck the spine around C6, "crushed" the vertebra, and fragmented** ([02:35:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S0fmq1zffGw&t=9355s)), with **no exit** ([02:11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S0fmq1zffGw&t=7871s)). His is a **participant/hearsay account**, not the ME's finding.

Forensic commentators call these details **medically plausible**; they are **not officially confirmed**. This is why the *"the wound is too clean / too contained for a rifle"* claim (a.k.a. "it would have blown his head off") is rated **MISLEADING** — it argues from an assumed external wound and ignores that a round which **stops in the neck** dumps its energy *internally*, not into a dramatic exit. See [[The Missing Exit Wound]].

## Why it stays on the open list
Because the trajectory and wound specifics are not officially released, the **full autopsy and wound trajectory** remain a legitimate open question — see [[What Is Genuinely Open]]. It does not follow that the neck wound is inconsistent with the rooftop: a shallow ~6° downward shot from 143 yards produces a near-horizontal neck wound. See [[Line of Sight and Shot Geometry]] and [[A Second Shooter from a Lower Angle]].
