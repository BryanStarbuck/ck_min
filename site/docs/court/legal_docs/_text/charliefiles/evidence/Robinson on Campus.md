# Robinson on Campus (the four visits & the Challenger)

A pillar of the State's identity case: UVU surveillance placing **Robinson's vehicle on campus about four times on September 10**, built out from a DMV workup after he surfaced.

## What the agents testified
- **The vehicle chain:** after Robinson was identified (via the surrender tip), an SBI agent pulled his **driver's-license record** — photo, date of birth, address — and a **DMV record for an associated vehicle**: a **silver Dodge Challenger** ([06:32:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=23573s)). It had **two registered owners — Robinson and his mother** ([06:33:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=23609s)).
- **The four visits:** investigators testified Robinson was on campus *"approximately four times throughout the day — twice before the shooting, the time of the shooting, and then after the shooting later in the evening into the early hours of the eleventh"* — *"all based on UVU surveillance video"* ([06:35:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=23700s)).
- **The doorbell clip:** a **Ring doorbell** video showed *"a vehicle consistent with the one we had identified as belonging to Mr. Robinson"* ([06:57:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=25052s)).
- **The wheels:** the agent said the Challenger has *"very distinct wheels — I won't say that they're wholly unique,"* but they *"stand out on the video footage"* ([07:00:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=25236s)).

This is part of what the compilation ([[The Surveillance Video Was Doctored|Exhibits 12.1 / 12.4]]) was built to illustrate, and it pairs with the [[The Surveillance and the Gait|gait tracking]] of the person.

## What Day 2 added to the track
On **Day-2** direct, the State's case agent [[David Hull|Agent Hull]] walked the same campus track frame-by-frame, with timecodes ([[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]]):
- **Arrival ~08:30.** The vehicle "believed to belong to Mr. Robinson" arrives in the parking garage at **approximately 08:30 on 09/10/2025**; Hull ties it to Robinson by DMV records and by the car's **"very distinctive" wheels** [00:44:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=2695s).
- **Amphitheater / TPUSA contact.** On foot to the quad, the figure **makes contact with representatives from TPUSA** [00:46:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=2763s).
- **Chick-fil-A, then the woods.** He buys food at the **Chick-fil-A in the Sorensen Center**, and later enters the **wooded area** — where, by a later visit, the **backpack is gone** [00:48:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=2889s).
- **An independent plate flag predating the surrender.** A Spanish Fork officer, **Goforth**, on security detail, stopped/contacted the vehicle and — on "cop intuition" — noted its plate at **~00:30 on 09/11**. Running a **partial plate** later returned **two registered owners, Tyler Robinson and Amber Robinson**, and Goforth believed the person he'd contacted was Tyler Robinson [00:57:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=3469s)–[00:59:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=3581s). This is a thread the State says **predates** the surrender tip — a partial answer to the "circular starting point" objection below, though it still runs through the co-registered car, not a face.

## On parking (a common question)
The record shows only that his vehicle **entered and parked in the UVU parking structure/garage.** Hull described the driver exiting the vehicle and heading *"out of the parking garage on foot into the campus"* [00:46:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=2763s), and later *"coming up the stairs of the parking structure"* across the visits [00:52:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=3120s). **No testimony in the current record addresses whether a student parking pass or permit was required, whether Robinson — who was *not* a UVU student — had one, or whether visitor/event parking was open that day.** So *how* he got into the structure (a permit, open event parking, or an ungated deck) is simply **not established on the record**; it is a fair, minor open question that nothing in the preliminary hearing turns on.

> 🎥 **Forbes Breaking News:** officials confirm Robinson was **not a UVU student** — the backdrop to why his car was on a campus he had no class reason to be on.

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NVjewRbuyd4)

> **From the clip** (Gov. Spencer Cox, Sept. 11 press conference), asked whether Robinson was a student: *"He was not a student here at UVU. He was living, and had lived for a long time, with his family in Washington County."*

## From the defense's perspective
The vehicle chain is real, but it is **interpretive, not forensic** — and the defense has clear angles:
- **A co-owned car.** The Challenger is **registered to Robinson *and his mother*** — so "his car on campus" does not, by itself, prove *he* was driving it on any given trip.
- **Identified by vehicle, not face.** The match is by **wheels** that are *"not wholly unique"* and by *"consistent with"* language — hedged, not definitive; the **doorbell figure was never facially identified**, and there was no plate confirmation on that clip.
- **A largely circular starting point.** The four-visits workup began **from the surrender tip / DMV record**, not from independent forensic identification — investigators knew to look for *his* car because he had already surfaced. The **Goforth plate flag** (~00:30 on 9/11) is the State's answer that *one* thread ran independently — but it still identifies a **co-registered car and a believed-to-be driver**, not a confirmed face, and it too fed *back into* the same DMV record. See [[They Have the Wrong Person]].
- It feeds the [[The Gun Was His But Planted|"his car/gun, but he wasn't the shooter"]] reasonable-doubt construction and **Layer 1** of [[Defense Strategies and Degrees of Liability]].

The State's answer is **convergence**: the vehicle sits alongside the gait surveillance, the [[The Casing Match and DNA|DNA]], the [[The Discord and Text Messages|messages]], the [[The Keyboard Note|confession]], and the [[The Surrender|surrender]] — no single thread carrying the case alone.
