# The Surveillance and the Gait

The State's identity case does not rest on one camera. **Continuous campus surveillance** — hundreds of hours reviewed by investigators — tracks a suspect distinguished by a **stiff-right-leg gait** from arrival, to a rooftop **prone position** overlooking the tent, through the shot, and off the roof in flight.

> 🎥 **Forbes Breaking News:** the surveillance footage of the suspect, played in the courtroom during the hearing.

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bHTOyOYvovE)

## The gait as an identifier
The suspect's **distinctive walk** appears on approach across multiple cameras. It is **absent during the rooftop run** — consistent with adrenaline — which some have tried to turn into a contradiction (see [[The Limp Proves an Impossible Timeline]]). In fact the gait *aids* identification precisely because it is distinctive and recurs across separate camera angles.

## What Day 1 showed — and didn't
On Day-1 cross-examination, one officer could **not** identify the rooftop figure's face, clothing, or height from a **single** camera angle, and had called the object in its hand "a long black object," not a gun, in his original report. That is a real cross-examination point — but it is **one witness on one camera**, not the surveillance case, which is built from continuous multi-camera tracking plus the [[The Surrender|surrender]], the [[The Rifle|recovered rifle]], the [[The Casing Match and DNA|DNA]], and a shoe-impression match. See [[They Have the Wrong Person]] and [[2026-07-06 - Preliminary Hearing Day 1|Day 1 Legal Review]].

## What Day 2 made explicit — the ID is built by inference
On **Day 2**, the State's case agent [[David Hull|Agent Hull]] walked the surveillance himself and laid the method bare — the identity travels by **gait, "same shoes," and clothing**, not by a face ([[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]]):
- **The markers are interpretive.** On the on-foot return, Hull ties the figure to Robinson because *"the shoes are the same"* and *"he also is walking with a gait or a limp"* — while conceding the person is *"noticeably wearing different clothing"* [00:51:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=3085s).
- **The rooftop figure is not facially identifiable.** Hull agreed that the released stills were the **parking-garage/stairwell** images, and that the **video of the person on the roof, as a standalone, "was not [distinguishable]"** [01:53:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6830s). The identification is carried *to* the roof by the continuity track, not read *off* the roof footage.
- **The "gun" is still not a visible gun.** The object crossing Campus Drive is *"concealed or in a bag… long,"* and — on training and experience — *"could be a firearm,"* but Hull confirmed you **do not see an actual gun** in the video, and no passerby reported a man with a rifle standing by their car [01:56:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6966s).

Read fairly for the defense, this is the identity case's soft seam: it rests on a **distinctive walk plus footwear**, not a face or a visible weapon. The State's answer is the same as ever — **convergence** — the gait/shoes track corroborated by the [[The Surrender|surrender]], the [[The Rifle|recovered rifle]], the [[The Casing Match and DNA|DNA]], and the vehicle in [[Robinson on Campus]], no single thread carrying it alone.

## The prone "sniper pad"
Investigators documented a **prone impression** (elbows, knees, feet) at the rooftop edge, photographed with a measuring tape, plus a matching **escape route**: gravel, northeast scuff marks, and a **deep shoe imprint** at the drop-off. See [[Line of Sight and Shot Geometry]].

## Day 5: the enhanced "sniper's-perch" video (Exhibit 12.1)
On the final day (**July 10**), at the **Kirk family's request**, the court allowed the State to publish **Exhibit 12.1** — an **enhanced/zoomed** version of the previously-shown rooftop surveillance, with **added markings** highlighting the figure prosecutors identify as Robinson **crawling to the perch**. This is the same enhanced exhibit that had been **excluded on Day 1** for authentication; on Day 5 it was shown **in the courtroom only**, with the court switching the **livestream to audio-only** and barring any video or still capture of the monitors while it played [02:55:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=10500s)–[02:56:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=10587s). Per courtroom reporting, **Erika Kirk** and Kirk's mother became emotional; **Robinson's monitor was turned off** and he did not watch. Because it was **not broadcast**, the enhanced footage itself is not in the public record — only the fact of its in-court publication. The defense's authentication and [[The Surveillance Video Was Doctored|"doctored video"]] objections remain **preserved**. See [[2026-07-10 - Preliminary Hearing Day 5|Day 5 Legal Review]].

**What a reporter in the courtroom described.** Since the footage was never broadcast, the closest public account of its content comes from credentialed press who watched it in the room. **KUTV anchor/reporter [Heidi Hatch](https://x.com/tvheidihatch/status/2075645376208773609)** wrote from court (July 10) that "**even zoomed in the quality of the video [was] surprisingly high**," that "**you could see the man on the roof — reportedly Tyler Robinson — getting down into that … prone position before the shot was fired,**" and that the video "**showing Robinson allegedly returning to campus with jeans on shows that very distinct limp with the rifle likely in the leg of his pants.**" That last point — the **limp explained by a rifle concealed in his pant leg** — is a **reporter's interpretation of a non-public exhibit**, not sworn testimony, so treat it as a contemporaneous press account (the defense's authentication objection is still preserved). It does, however, line up with the **gait/limp frames above** and the towel-wrapped rifle later stashed off-campus. See [[The Limp Proves an Impossible Timeline]].
