# The Discord and Text Messages

Alongside the physical evidence sits a **digital record**: text and Discord messages between Robinson and his roommate, **Lance Twiggs**. They discuss, in Robinson's own words, **retrieving and hiding a rifle**, **engraving bullets**, **changing clothes**, and — after the shooting — surrendering.

![[info-text-messages-exchange.png|620]]
*The Robinson ↔ roommate ("Luna") exchange as reproduced in the [[2025-09-16 - The Information and Charges|Information]] (p. 8): the confession (*"I am, I'm sorry"*), the rifle **"drop point,"** **"a bit over a week"** of planning, and the engraved **"notices bulge uwu"** bullets. Note that Robinson describes police having **"grabbed some crazy old dude"** ([[George Zinn]], the false confessor) **"then interrogated someone in similar clothing"** (Zachariah Qureshi).*

The defense (**Novak**) fought admission and especially **publication** on **Confrontation Clause** and **pretrial-publicity** grounds (*Archuleta*, *Estes v. Texas*, *Rideau v. Louisiana*), arguing that replaying "what the State will call a confession" before a jury is seated taints the pool [02:50:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=10256s), [03:16:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=11774s) — the fuller confrontation/publicity question **reserved** pending a defense brief. So the thread is **in** for the limited probable-cause purpose, with relevance and publication of portions still open. See [[2026-07-08 - Preliminary Hearing Day 3|Day 3 Legal Review]] and [[The Confession Is Coerced or Fabricated]].

## What they establish
- They **corroborate the physical evidence** before the fact: the plan to engrave bullets matches [[The Engraved Casings|the engraved cartridges]] recovered with [[The Rifle|the rifle]]; hiding the rifle matches where it was found on the escape route.
- They describe **Robinson** carrying out the plan — the associate (Twiggs) **cooperated with police**. Nothing in them describes a co-shooter or a handler. See [[Robinson Is a Patsy]].
- One Discord note — *"im surrendering through a sheriff friend in a few moments"* — is **pre-surrender**, and is the likely source of the "he texted from custody" confusion, because filings released the messages **without precise timestamps**. See [[Robinson Texted From Custody]] and [[The Surrender]].

## The keyboard-note letter
Robinson's 9/10 message *"drop what you are doing, look under my keyboard"* led Twiggs to a **handwritten confession letter** addressed to *"Luna"* — *"I left the house this morning on a mission … I had the opportunity to take out Charlie Kirk, and I took it."* It is among the most damaging items in the case, and the target of a "[[The Keyboard Note|it was a setup]]" theory. Full text and analysis: [[The Keyboard Note]].

## On authenticity
Because the messages are corroborated by the rifle, the casing match, the DNA, the shoe impression, and the surrender, the claim that the [[The Confession Is Coerced or Fabricated|confession was coerced or fabricated]] has to explain away a convergent record, not a single statement.
