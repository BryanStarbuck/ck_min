# The Phone and Location Data

Once the "least-detailed thread in the record," the **phone evidence became central on Days 3–4** of the preliminary hearing: the State's confession case rests on **text and Discord messages recovered from [[Lance Twiggs]]'s phone**, laid in through case agent **[[Brian Davis|Agent Brian Davis]]**. This page covers **how those messages were captured and authenticated**, **when and by whom the phone was photographed**, **the dates/times of the messages and where Robinson was**, and the defense thesis that **Twiggs could have authored the messages himself** — what would prove or disprove it, and what the record shows now. The separate question of the phone's own **geolocation / map history** remains pursued but undeveloped (bottom of page).

## Three independent ways the messages were captured
The same conversation reached the court through **three channels**, which matters for authentication (next section):

1. **Photographs of Twiggs's phone screen.** During Twiggs's **first FBI interview**, he showed his phone and **Agent Larson photographed the messages**; the images were then **shown back to Twiggs in a second interview, where he confirmed** they were the same messages (Day 4 [02:51:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10275s)). These photographs are **Exhibit 16.1** — "**18 pages of photographs of text messages**" (Day 4 [01:23:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=5026s)) — and **Exhibit 16.2**, the Discord thread; the **same agent (Larson) took both**, and Davis spoke with him to confirm (Day 4 [02:51:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10300s)).
2. **A Cellebrite forensic extraction of Twiggs's phone** (see explainer below), which Davis **personally reviewed** and which supplied the **timestamps** (Day 4 [02:39:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=9580s)).
3. **A subpoena to Discord.** Law enforcement **sent a subpoena to Discord**, and the Discord messages were **recovered via that subpoena** — i.e., from **Discord's own servers**, independent of Twiggs's phone — which Davis also reviewed (Day 4 [02:52:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10357s)). Robinson's Discord username on the thread was **"tylerikum"** (Day 4 [02:52:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10344s)).

### When, and by whom, was the phone photographed?
- **Who:** **Agent Larson** (FBI), personally, during a recorded interview of Twiggs.
- **When:** at Twiggs's **first interview** (the Sept. 12, 2025 FBI interview — see [[2026-07-08 - Preliminary Hearing Day 3|Day 3]]), with **confirmation at his second, counseled interview**. Twiggs also **photographed the [[The Keyboard Note|keyboard note]] himself** and "put it back down on the desk after I took a picture of it" (Day 4 [02:20:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8450s)) — a separate image from the phone-screen photos.
- **What the photos show:** the iMessage thread with the **"Tyler" contact** at the top, and — because they are literal photos of the phone — the **native iPhone rendering** that becomes the backbone of authentication.

## What "Cellebrite extraction" and the data-imaging chain actually mean
A **Cellebrite extraction** is a **forensic download (imaging) of a phone** using specialized hardware/software that copies the device's data — messages, timestamps, contacts, and metadata — into a reviewable file, ideally preserving the underlying **metadata** a plain screenshot cannot (sender identifiers, exact send/receive times, thread structure). Davis described the chain in this case (Day 4 [02:58:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10697s)–[03:00:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10821s)):

- The FBI **transported Twiggs's phone to the RCFL — the Regional Computer Forensic Laboratory — in Salt Lake City**, where it was **"downloaded"** (extracted).
- The download was placed on a **flash drive** and passed to a **SIAC analyst** — the **Statewide Information Analysis Center** — who located the relevant images.
- Davis **spoke personally** with both the **RCFL** examiners who performed the download and the **SIAC analyst** who found the material (Day 4 [03:00:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10802s)).

**Relative to the subpoenas:** the extraction and photographs come **from the phone**; the **Discord subpoena** comes **from Discord's servers**. When the two match, each **validates the other** — the phone-side capture is confirmed by a third party's independent records, and vice versa. That cross-validation is the heart of why the "Twiggs faked it" theory is hard to sustain (below).

**Where Robinson was, per the messages themselves.** The **first text came at 11 PM on Sept 10 — about 11 hours after the shooting** — while Robinson was **still at large**, and the content places him **near the rifle "drop point"** (the wooded area near campus where the towel-wrapped rifle was later recovered — see [[The Rifle]], [[The Engraved Casings]]):
- *"got to grab my rifle still"* [t=9766](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=9766s); *"I had planned to grab my rifle from the drop point shortly after but most of that side of town got locked down… there's one vehicle lingering"* [t=9794](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=9794s); *"there is a squad car parked right by it"* [t=9848](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=9848s).
- By the **final messages (Sept 11 evening)** he had moved **home**: *"I'm going to turn myself in willingly… one of my neighbors here is a deputy for the sheriff"* [t=10166](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10166s) — consistent with the surrender being brokered through the family's retired-deputy neighbor [[Mike Mitchell]] and his ~9 PM arrival at the Washington County Sheriff's Office.

So the **texts trace his movement**: still lingering near the Orem/UVU **drop point the night of Sept 10**, unable to retrieve the rifle because of the police lockdown, then **home in Washington County by the Sept 11 surrender**.

> **Independent location data is thinner.** The only *non-message* location testimony is vague: cell-phone data "**indicated Mr. Robinson was in the area**" of the family home when his vehicle appeared on a **Ring doorbell** camera (Day 1 [07:00:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=25201s)), and a UVU officer's **partial license plate**, noted the morning of **09/11/2025**, was traced to Robinson (Day 2 [00:58:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=3517s)). The **detailed cell-site / GPS / map-history results** — the data that would independently place *his device* at the drop point during the 11 PM texting window — have **not been presented** (see the Apple-warrant section below). The location narrative during texting therefore rests on the **message content**, corroborated by the rifle's recovery where the texts said it was.

## Who authored the messages?
Because the texts were recovered **from Twiggs's phone** and Twiggs is an **immunized, unconfronted witness** ([[Lance Twiggs Was More Involved|Twiggs claim check]], [[The Confession Is Coerced or Fabricated]]), a fair defense question is how the authorship of each side of the thread was verified. Here is how authorship would be **proven or disproven**, and where the evidence stands.

**How it was authenticated (what cuts against self-authorship):**
- **The iPhone itself distinguishes the speakers.** Davis: *"any of the messages in gray would be from Tyler and messages in blue would be from Lance… this is where the iPhone distinguishes the messages"* [t=9707](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=9707s). On Twiggs's phone, the **gray (received) bubbles come from the "Tyler" contact's number**, the blue are Twiggs's own — a two-device conversation, not one person typing both sides.
- **Twiggs confirmed attribution** — "Lance in his interview also acknowledges who was saying what" [t=9720](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=9720s).
- **The Discord subpoena is the strongest check.** The Discord thread was **independently produced by Discord** in response to a subpoena, tied to Robinson's account **"tylerikum"** [t=10344](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10344s). **Twiggs cannot fabricate Discord's server-side records** — so a matching Discord thread from Discord's own systems is powerful corroboration that the account holder (not Twiggs) authored those messages.
- **The content is corroborated by physical evidence.** The messages describe the **rifle wrapped and stashed at a drop point**, a **note under the keyboard**, and an **intent to surrender** — and the **rifle was found in a towel in the bushes**, the **note was recovered** ([[The Keyboard Note]]), and **Robinson did surrender** on the timeline the messages predicted. A fabricator would have had to know all of that in advance.

**What would show the messages were not authentic (what a defense would need):**
- **Cellebrite metadata** showing the "Tyler" messages were **not received from Robinson's number/device** — e.g., locally created/injected rather than delivered — or timestamps that don't line up.
- **The absence of a matching thread on Robinson's own device or Discord account.** Notably, the record here relies on **Twiggs's phone and Discord's servers**; a **Cellebrite extraction of Robinson's own phone** confirming the *outgoing* side has **not been presented** in the hearing. That is the cleanest confirmation the State has not yet put on the record (and a fair [[Gaps in the Investigation|gap]] to note).
- **Impeachment of Twiggs** on the stand — but the defense **never cross-examined him** (his immunized statement came in recorded), so the confrontation test is **deferred to trial**.

**What the evidence shows now.** On the current record the self-authorship thesis is **weak**: the messages are a **two-sided iMessage thread** on Twiggs's phone, **independently mirrored by Discord's subpoenaed records** under Robinson's username, and **corroborated by the physical evidence** the messages describe. The honest **defense-side residue** is narrower — the texts are proven up **through Twiggs (immunized, unconfronted)** and Discord's servers, **not yet through Robinson's own device**, and no independent geolocation ties *his phone* to the drop point during the texting. So the fight is about **confrontation and completeness at trial**, not a viable "Twiggs typed it all himself" story. See [[The Discord and Text Messages]], [[Confessions and Admissions]].

## Still pending: the phone's own geolocation and map history
Separate from the messages, investigators obtained a **search warrant for Apple** covering two accounts — **`memesinsteadofdreams@gmail.com`** (Robinson) and **`lancetwiggs@icloud.com`** — seeking **location information, IP/login records, FaceTime and call history, messages, and Safari *search* and *map* navigation history** and "any other information associated with location" ([[2025-09-17 - The Search Warrant Affidavit|search-warrant affidavit]]). That is a direct pursuit of whether the device's **geolocation and map searches** place him at UVU and show **planning** (e.g., navigation to campus). Surveillance also caught him **pulling a phone from his pocket** on campus ([[2025-09-12 - Affidavit of Probable Cause|PC affidavit]]) — so he **had and used a phone there**. But those **location/map results have not been laid out in the hearing**; that thread remains **pursued, not yet detailed**.

## Bottom line
The phone evidence is no longer "undeveloped." The **message record is strong and multiply-sourced** — photographed from Twiggs's phone by Agent Larson, forensically **extracted via Cellebrite** through the RCFL/SIAC chain, and **independently confirmed by a Discord subpoena** — with **content corroborated by the recovered rifle, note, and surrender**, and **timestamps** anchoring Robinson near the **drop point the night of Sept 10** and **home by the Sept 11 surrender**. The remaining **open** pieces are the defense's real ones: the texts are proven **through an immunized, unconfronted Twiggs and Discord's servers rather than Robinson's own device**, and the **independent geolocation/map data is still not in evidence**.
