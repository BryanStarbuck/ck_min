# The Rifle

The murder weapon was recovered. It is a **Mauser Model 98, .30-06 bolt-action rifle with a scope**, serial number **8863**, found wrapped in a **towel** in trees along the suspect's filmed **escape route** north of the shooting position — not in the courtyard, and not left on the roof.

> **Day 2 confirmed the weapon's identity and chain of custody.** The State's evidence-response-team lead, Sgt. Jennifer Faumuina, testified that the long gun recovered from the **wooded area northeast of campus** was a **Mauser 98 bolt-action**, wrapped in a dark towel [05:03:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18194s)–[05:03:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18221s); the towel bore serial number **800863** (item 7 on the FBI report) [05:08:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18518s). Agent Hull placed the recovery in the **same wooded area he watched Robinson enter**, on the 10th [02:21:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=8473s)–[02:21:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=8484s). The gun was taken into **FBI custody** the evening of the 10th and then routed to the **ATF lab** [05:04:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18268s). See [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].

## Why the details matter
- **Bolt-action** is the key mechanical fact. A bolt-action rifle is cycled **by hand**, so a fired casing does **not** eject and fly free the way it does from a semi-automatic. That single fact answers a whole theory — see [[No Casing or Rifle on the Roof]].
- **.30-06** is a common, powerful hunting cartridge. Whether such a round produces a dramatic exit wound depends on bullet construction and whether it strikes bone — it does **not** automatically "blow a head off." See [[The Missing Exit Wound]].
- **Scoped, ~143 yards, ~6° down** is an ordinary shot for this weapon. See [[Line of Sight and Shot Geometry]].

## How it ties to Robinson
- The **fired casing** found with the rifle was **positively matched** to it by firearms examiners.
- **Robinson's DNA** was recovered on the **trigger**, the **casing**, and the **towel**. On Day 2 the DNA the State actually put in was from the **towel wrapped around this rifle** (item 7) and the rooftop **screwdriver** (item 8): item 7 read **1.7 octillion** times more likely if Twiggs + T. Robinson than an unknown [05:11:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18662s), with Robinson the **major (95%)** contributor [08:06:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=29166s). See [[The Casing Match and DNA]].
- Two **unfired** cartridges and one fired cartridge — all **engraved** — were with it.
- **Robinson's own text names the towel — a detail only the person who hid the rifle could know.** In the Sept-10/11 thread, he wrote: *"only thing I left was the **rifle wrapped in a towel** — I'll bet that canine sniffed it out"* (Day 4 [02:47:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10044s)). The rifle was in fact recovered **wrapped in a dark towel** in the bushes — a wrapping Faumuina described *unwrapping* to reveal the gun ([04:47:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=17231s)). When he sent that message the recovery was **not yet public**, so the text is **perpetrator's-knowledge corroboration** of both the confession and this weapon — and a hard fact for any "the messages were fabricated / [[The Phone and Location Data|Twiggs typed them himself]]" theory to explain. See [[Confessions and Admissions]].

See [[The Casing Match and DNA]] and [[The Engraved Casings]].

> **Still to come on the rifle itself.** The Mauser went to the **ATF lab** [05:04:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18268s); its **tool-mark/ballistics comparison** to the recovered round, and any prints, were **not yet in the Day-2 record**. ## Day 4 (July 9) — the ATF ballistics comparison lands; no other .30-06 in the home
The tool-mark/ballistics comparison flagged as "still to come" above is **now in the record.** On Day 4, Sgt. [[Jennifer Faumuina]] (recalled) and the ATF reports established:
- **The casing-to-rifle match:** the **Exhibit 2 cartridge case was "identified as having been fired in the Exhibit 1 rifle"** [05:28:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=19737s) — the ATF confirmation of the positive casing match this page relies on. (The ATF examiner [[Samantha Karner]] later confirmed the same on the stand — see [[The Casing Match and DNA]].)
- **What was inside the towel:** one **fired .30-06 cartridge case + three unfired cartridges, all engraved** [04:52:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=17524s), headstamp "Remington .30-06 Springfield."
- **No other .30-06 firearm in the house.** Asked directly, "Did you find any .30-06 weapons in that house? No." [05:05:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18302s) — so the **only** .30-06 weapon tied to the case is this recovered Mauser, while the home held .30-06 *ammunition* (including five casings on the safe, one engraved "test shot," the **same caliber** as the round in the rifle [05:04:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18284s)). See [[The Engraved Casings]].

See [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4|Day 4 Legal Review]] and [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4 Transcript|Day 4 Transcript]].

## The one genuine caveat
A separate **bullet-jacket fragment** recovered from the scene was tested by the ATF and came back **inconclusive** — it could not be identified *or excluded* as fired from this rifle. That is about a fragment, not the casing, and it is the most-misused fact in the case. See [[The Inconclusive Bullet]].
