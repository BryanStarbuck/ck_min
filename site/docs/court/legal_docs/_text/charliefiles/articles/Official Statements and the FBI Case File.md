# Official Statements and the FBI Case File

Primary, on-the-record sources from the agencies that investigated and charged the case — the **FBI**, **Utah DPS / State Bureau of Investigation**, **Governor Cox**, and the **Utah County Attorney**. Quoted verbatim and linked to the official page or the agency's verified account wherever possible.

> **On embedded posts:** Obsidian Publish is a static site and cannot render live X/Twitter embeds, so official posts are shown as **quoted text, styled cards, and — for the FBI's released images — the actual attached media**, each linked to the verified account (data retrieved via the X API). Key official **pages** are also captured as **screenshots** below. The live links remain authoritative.

## The FBI's assembled case page
The FBI compiled its investigation on one page — **[Utah Valley Shooting Updates](https://www.fbi.gov/news/press-releases/utah-valley-shooting-updates)** (fbi.gov, Sept. 11–14, 2025):
> "Around 12 p.m. Mountain Time on September 10, 2025, the subject climbed up to a rooftop; after he shot and killed Charlie Kirk, he jumped off and ran away. **He left a gun and ammunition in a wooded area near the university.** Trace evidence collected from **the rooftop scene** included shoe impressions, a forearm imprint, and a palm print."

The page hosts the released **shooter video** and **six person-of-interest photos**, a **$100,000 reward**, and the tip line (1-800-CALL-FBI · fbi.gov/utahvalleyshooting). It is the primary basis for [[Line of Sight and Shot Geometry]], [[The Rifle]], and [[The Scene Was Paved Over]] — the forensic scenes were the **roof and the woods**, not the courtyard lawn.

## FBI — official remarks & posts
- **SAC Robert Bohls**, Sept. 11 press conference ([fbi.gov](https://www.fbi.gov/contact-us/field-offices/saltlakecity/news/remarks-delivered-by-special-agent-in-charge-robert-bohls-at-the-september-11-2025-press-conference-regarding-the-shooting-at-utah-valley-university)): *"we have recovered what we believe is the weapon used… a **high-powered bolt action rifle**… recovered in a wooded area where the shooter had fled. Investigators have also collected **footwear impression, a palm print, and forearm imprints**."*

- **Patel on X** — [@FBIDirectorKash, Sept. 10](https://x.com/FBIDirectorKash/status/1965903392934633587): *"The subject for the horrific shooting today… is now in custody…"* — then ~90 minutes later, [Sept. 10](https://x.com/FBIDirectorKash/status/1965928054712316363): *"The subject in custody has been **released** after an interrogation by law enforcement. Our investigation continues…"* (the initial detain-and-release — see [[They Have the Wrong Person]]).

## Utah DPS / State Bureau of Investigation
**DPS media advisory** ([dps.utah.gov](https://dps.utah.gov/press-releases/updates-on-charlie-kirk-shooting-at-uvu/)):
> "The shooting is believed to be a **targeted attack**. The shooter is believed to have **fired from the roof of a building** down to the location of the public event in the student courtyard."

The advisory also documents that **two men were initially detained and released** — **[[George Zinn]]** (released; charged by UVU police with obstruction after a false confession) and **Zachariah Qureshi** (released after interrogation) — with *"no current ties to the shooting with either."* And that **UVU PD had six officers** for ~3,000 attendees. See [[They Have the Wrong Person]] and [[The Shot Came from the Courtyard]].
