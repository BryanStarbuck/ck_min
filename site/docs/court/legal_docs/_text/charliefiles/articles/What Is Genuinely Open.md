# What Is Genuinely Open

Not everything is resolved. Keeping these honest — separating a real open question from a debunked claim — is what makes the rest of this site credible. These are **legitimate open questions**, not theories:

1. **The full autopsy and wound trajectory** have not been publicly released, and the medical examiner did not testify (investigator Sgt. David Hull summarized the report). The **only official finding is "gunshot wound to the neck," homicide.** The dramatic specifics — no exit wound, the round fragmenting on the cervical spine, recovered under the skin — come from **Kirk-staff and surgeon accounts** (producer Andrew Kolvet; the security chief in the *Salt Lake Tribune*), not from a released ME statement. Forensic commentators call it medically plausible; it is not officially confirmed. **Confirmed still open after the full five-day hearing:** the medical examiner **never testified on any of the five days** and the **autopsy report was never published** — so the **internal injury detail and the bullet's trajectory inside the body remain officially unconfirmed**, reaching the record only secondhand (Hull) and, on Day 4, via Faumuina/Karner reading the firearms report. Day 4 also surfaced a **bullet-fragment-count discrepancy** (the ME's photo shows up to seven fragments; the ATF received five) — see item **21**. → [[The Medical Examiner and the Wound]], [[The Missing Exit Wound]], [[The Inconclusive Bullet]].

2. **The bullet-fragment ballistics are inconclusive** — the jacket fragment could not be matched *or excluded* to the rifle. (The casing match and DNA remain.) → [[The Inconclusive Bullet]], [[The Casing Match and DNA]].

3. **The SD-card chain of custody.** Kirk's own TPUSA team removing and holding the event-camera cards before handing them to the FBI is **confirmed** — but the handling has not yet been examined under oath. A legitimate question for the hearing. → [[The SD Cards]], [[A Staffer Pulled the SD Cards]].

4. **Missing surrender footage.** The sheriff's office retained no video of Robinson's surrender after a 30-day retention window elapsed. → [[The Surrender]].

5. **The unattributed pistol holster** in the courtyard was noted in Day-1 testimony (an "empty concealed holster" the officer saw at ground level) but not collected, printed, or identified — and on **Day 2** the account shifted: trooper Gonzales's report (via Davis, Exhibit Hull-13) states there was a **gun found in the holster** ([Day 2, 01:48:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6538s)), contradicting the "empty" framing. Whose gun, whether ever collected or printed, is unresolved — and it was **not revisited on Days 3, 4, or 5** (zero mentions), so it stands **unresolved at the close of evidence.** → [[The Unattributed Holster]].

6. **A bullet "found on another roof" — now largely explained (Day 3).** On **Day 2**, Hull recast it as an **officer's ejected cartridge** and said **nobody knew where it was** ([Day 2, 01:43:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6203s)). **Day 3 gave the specifics:** [[Brian Davis|Agent Davis]] testified it was a **.223-caliber *live* (unfired) round** found on the **Computer Science building roof** — a *different* roof from the shooter's **Losee** roof, on its "far east side" — with **no line of sight to Mr. Kirk's tent**, documented and **collected by SBI Agent Ben Schneider** ([Day 3, 00:59:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3549s)–[01:00:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3622s)). **Wrong caliber (.223 vs. the .30-06 murder round), wrong roof, no sightline** — consistent with an officer's round cycled from a rifle, not a second-shooter position. The one loose thread: Hull said its whereabouts were unknown (Day 2) while Davis said Schneider **collected** it (Day 3). → [[A Bullet on the Other Roof]], [[No Casing or Rifle on the Roof]].

## Never presented across all five days — gaps in the closed record
The evidentiary record is now **closed**, which reframes these from "not yet reached" to **"never put in":**

19. **The cell-phone geolocation and map history — never produced, for either phone.** Investigators obtained an **Apple search warrant** reaching **location information, and Safari *search* and *map*-navigation history**, for **both** `memesinsteadofdreams@gmail.com` (Robinson) and `lancetwiggs@icloud.com` ([[Lance Twiggs]]) — a direct pursuit of whether the devices' **geolocation places Robinson at UVU and shows route/planning searches**. But across all five days the only location testimony was **vague** ("cell phone data… indicated Mr. Robinson was in the area," Day 1 [07:00:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=25201s)) plus a **partial license plate** (Day 2). The **actual geolocation/map results were never presented**, and — notably — **Robinson's own phone was never forensically extracted on the record** (the confession texts came from **Twiggs's** phone and **Discord's servers**, not Robinson's device). → [[The Phone and Location Data]], [[Gaps in the Investigation]].

20. **The parents' own accounts are not in the record.** Robinson's **mother and father were each interviewed** by the FBI/SBI (Day 3 [00:37:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=2275s)–[00:39:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=2344s)) and he **surrendered "with his parents"** (Day 2 [02:10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7845s)), but those interviews were **never played or detailed**, the **parents never testified**, and the **only in-record version of the family confession is Agent Hull's secondhand, hedged** "*He had informed, **I believe**, his immediate family that he had been involved… and that he wanted to turn himself in*" (Day 2 [02:23:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=8593s)). Unlike [[Lance Twiggs|Twiggs]] (recorded Rule 1102 statement) and family friend [[Mike Mitchell]] (**written** 1102 statement), the parents' accounts sit **entirely outside the evidentiary record** — untested and unconfronted. → [[Confessions and Admissions]], [[The Surrender]].

21. **The "seven fragments" are unexplained.** The medical examiner's photo documented **up to seven bullet fragments** recovered from Kirk at autopsy, but the **ATF received only five** (jacket **6A** + four lead **6B–6E**) — leaving **roughly two unaccounted for** in the autopsy-to-lab handoff. Examiner [[Samantha Karner]] confirmed the discrepancy, said she was **not** asked "to see if maybe the **ATF had lost** certain portions of the evidence," and learned of the seven only "**a few months ago, from a photo**" (Day 4 [06:37:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=23832s)–[06:37:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=23860s)). A **mechanism is now documented** — a Dec. 22, 2025 State testing-notice records that the **fragile jacket fragment sheds pieces during handling** (a "small portion… detached" between ATF and FBI; more "may inadvertently become detached") — which makes any fixed count unreliable, but that notice **isn't on the public docket** and the **7↔5 count was never reconciled under oath.** → [[The Inconclusive Bullet]], [[Chain of Custody Problems]].

22. **The enhanced "sniper's-perch" video (Exhibit 12.1) is not in the public record — and its authentication is preserved for trial.** The **zoomed, red-marked** version of the rooftop surveillance was **excluded on Day 1 for authentication**, then shown **in the courtroom only** — at the Kirk family's request — on the final day, with the court **cutting the livestream to audio-only** and barring any video or still capture of the monitors while it played (Day 5 [02:55:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=10500s)–[02:56:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=10587s)). So the **enhanced footage the magistrate saw is not publicly viewable**, and the defense's **authentication / "doctored video"** objection to the *altered, marked* version remains **preserved** for trial — what it actually shows, and whether the enhancement is reliable, stays open. The closest public account is from press in the room: KUTV's **[Heidi Hatch](https://x.com/tvheidihatch/status/2075645376208773609)** reported the zoomed video showed "the man on the roof … getting down into that … prone position before the shot" and Robinson "returning to campus with jeans on … that very distinct limp with the rifle likely in the leg of his pants" — a reporter's description, not the exhibit itself. → [[The Surveillance and the Gait]], [[The Surveillance Video Was Doctored]].

23. **Who paved the crime scene — and who paid.** Newly released **UVU public records (GRAMA)** confirm the courtyard where Kirk was shot was **cleaned and paved within six days** — the scene was **released back to UVU on ~Sep 12, 2025** and paver installation **"was completed by Tuesday, September 16"** — on an **emergency, no-bid** cleanup contract (**$6,090.52**, ordered **Sep 11**). But UVU is **withholding the cleanup vendor's name and the payment check** (business/safety exemptions) and swears it **"did not pay for or install the pavers"** — meaning an **unnamed non-UVU party paved over the site.** Who paid for and installed the pavers, and the redacted vendor's identity, are **unresolved on the public record**. → [[The Site Cleanup and Paving Records]], [[The Scene Was Paved Over]].

---

None of these, individually or together, unravels [[The Established Record]] — but each is a fair thing to press on cross-examination, and an honest resource says so plainly. The difference between the questions here and the claims elsewhere on this site is simple: these are **gaps in the public record**; those are **contradicted by it**.
