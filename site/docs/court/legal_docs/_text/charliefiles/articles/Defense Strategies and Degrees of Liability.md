# Defense Strategies and Degrees of Liability

> **⚠️ What this is.** A map of the defense theories that *could* be raised, arranged from **full innocence** down through **reduced liability**. This is legal analysis of the public record — **not legal advice, not a prediction, and not a claim about what happened.** Robinson's [[The Defense Team|defense team]] has **not announced a theory**, and he is **presumed innocent.** In a capital case there are really two contests: **guilt**, and — only if guilt is found — whether the **death penalty** applies. Several strategies below aim only at the second.

> **How to read it.** These tiers are **not mutually exclusive** — defenses are pleaded in the alternative. Some are **complete** defenses (acquittal), some are **partial** (reduce the offense), and some are **penalty-phase** (concede guilt, fight death). Utah statutes are cited as general framing; treat them as signposts, not a brief.

The charge is **aggravated murder** (Utah Code § 76-5-202), a capital offense. Everything below is measured against [[The Established Record|the convergent evidence]] — the rifle and DNA, the casing match, the gait surveillance, the surrender, and the [[The Keyboard Note|keyboard-note confession]] — which is what any of these theories has to overcome.

> **Companion page:** the pure **reasonable-doubt** posture — attacking the *sufficiency* of each piece of evidence, in the light most favorable to the defense, **without** claiming innocence — has its own detailed teardown: **[[Insufficiency of the Evidence]]**. It is the engine behind Layer 1 and behind defeating the death-penalty aggravators.

## Layer 1 — Factually innocent ("not him")
*Not the shooter · not there · evidence planted · a patsy.* A **complete** defense: acquittal.

- **The argument:** misidentification plus fabrication — the wrong man, or a framed one, and reasonable doubt throughout. Draws on [[They Have the Wrong Person]], [[Robinson Is a Patsy]], [[The Shot Came from the Courtyard]], [[A Second Shooter from a Lower Angle]], [[The Surveillance Video Was Doctored]], [[The Scene Was Paved Over]], and the scope problem laid out in [[A Hypothetical Cover-Up]].
- **The sharpest version — "his gun, but planted":** concede the rifle is Robinson's family gun (so his DNA on it is *innocent* — you'd expect it there), but argue he wasn't the shooter and that the weapon and [[The Engraved Casings|casings]] were planted along the escape route. Its spine is the forensic gaps: **no casing on the roof** ([[No Casing or Rifle on the Roof]]), the [[The Inconclusive Bullet|inconclusive fragment]], and [[A Bullet on the Other Roof|a bullet the defense suggested was found on another roof]]. Full note: [[The Gun Was His But Planted]].
- **What supports it in the record:** the identification softness across both hearing days — an officer who couldn't face-ID the roof figure, and, on **Day 2**, the State's own case agent conceding the rooftop figure is **not facially distinguishable** and that the tie runs on **gait, "same shoes," and clothing** ([Day 2, 01:53:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6830s)) — the [[The Inconclusive Bullet|inconclusive bullet]], chain-of-custody gaps, the [[The Unattributed Holster|unattributed holster]] (now reportedly holding a **gun**, per Day 2), and the [[The Body Camera Conveniently Failed|body-cam gap]]. See [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].
- **What cuts against it:** the convergent record — DNA on the trigger, casing, and towel ([[The Casing Match and DNA]]); the [[The Surveillance and the Gait|multi-camera gait tracking]]; the [[The Surrender|voluntary surrender]]; and above all the [[The Keyboard Note|handwritten confession]] and the [[The Discord and Text Messages|contemporaneous messages]].
- **Candid assessment:** the steepest climb. Pure factual innocence has to explain away the confession *and* the DNA *and* the messages at once. More realistically, this layer is mined for **reasonable doubt on specific links** rather than a full alternate-perpetrator case.

## Layer 2 — Shooter under duress ("he was made to")
*Did the act, but a third party coerced him.* Draws on [[Robinson Is a Patsy]], [[Others Had Foreknowledge]], and the "not alone" theories.

- **Utah law:** duress (§ 76-2-302) requires a threat of imminent serious harm — and, critically, Utah makes duress **unavailable as a defense to murder.** So as a **complete** defense to aggravated murder it is essentially off the table.
- **Where it can still matter:** as **penalty-phase mitigation** (he acted under pressure or domination by another), or to support a narrative that someone else **directed** the crime — which bears on the aggravators, not on guilt.
- **Record:** nothing in the messages shows a coercer, and the [[The Keyboard Note|note]] frames the act as his own choice (*"I had the opportunity … and I took it"*). That reads against duress.
- **Assessment:** weak as a guilt defense on this record; more a **mitigation theme** than an acquittal path.

## Layer 3 — Shooter lacking the required intent ("not aggravated murder")
*Did the act, but without the mental state charged.* A **partial** defense — reduces the offense.

- **Utah law:** **"special mitigation"** (§ 76-5-205.5) can reduce what would be aggravated murder or murder to **manslaughter** where the actor caused the death **under the influence of extreme emotional distress for which there is a reasonable explanation**, or under a **reasonable (but mistaken) belief** in circumstances that would justify the act. Separately, a **mental-illness** defense in Utah (§ 76-2-305) is narrow — it negates only the required *mens rea* — and **competency to stand trial** (Title 77, Ch. 15) is a different question entirely.
- **Record:** the **planning** evidence is the obstacle — the [[The Engraved Casings|engraved cartridges]], the pre-positioned rifle and "drop point," changing clothes, and *"I left the house this morning on a mission"* in [[The Keyboard Note|the note]] all point to **premeditation**, the enemy of a heat-of-passion / EED theory.
- **Assessment:** hard as a guilt-phase intent theory given the planning; but a **mental-health mitigation** case is standard in capital defense and is far more plausible at the **penalty phase**.

## Layer 4 — Shooter but not alone (accomplices / facilitators)
*Others helped, facilitated, or directed — "antifa," a foreign nexus, foreknowledge.* Draws on [[A Second Shooter from a Lower Angle]], [[Others Had Foreknowledge]], [[The Investigation Was Limited]], [[The Security Detail Was Israeli]].

- **The counterintuitive legal point:** under Utah party liability (§ 76-2-202), **helping others generally makes it worse, not better** for the defendant. "He didn't act alone" is rarely a **defense** to *his* guilt.
- **Where it matters:** (a) **reasonable doubt** about the lone-actor/who-fired theory; (b) support for a **duress/patsy** narrative (he was directed by others); (c) it is the factual heart of several theories on this site.
- **Record:** [[Lance Twiggs]] **cooperated** and described **Robinson** carrying out the plan; there is **no co-shooter evidence**, and the note is first-person and solo. See [[Robinson Is a Patsy]].
- **Assessment:** limited as a liability-reducer; usable as a **reasonable-doubt** or **penalty** theme.

## Layer 5 — Other paths (procedure, aggravators, mitigation)
Not "innocence," but real routes to a **better outcome**:

- **Suppression / procedural attacks** — challenge the searches (e.g., the [[2025-09-17 - The Search Warrant Affidavit|iCloud warrant]] on Twiggs's accounts), the admissibility of statements, and the process. Live fights already in the file: the [[2025-12-10 - Motion to Disqualify the Utah County Attorneys Office|disqualification motion]] and the discovery/ballistics [[2026-03-27 - Motion to Vacate or Continue the Preliminary Hearing|motion to vacate or continue]]. See the [[Court Documents Index]], and the detailed [[Chain of Custody Problems|chain-of-custody]] and [[Gaps in the Investigation|investigative-gap]] pages.
- **A Rule 702 DNA-reliability challenge** — foreshadowed on **Day 2**, where the defense drew out that STR testing measures allele **length, not sequence**, that both mixtures had **minor contributors below 20%**, that STRmix is **non-reproducible** run-to-run, and that the roommate's likelihood ratio came back **"uninformative"** yet he was still reported as a contributor ([Day 2, 07:20:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26404s)). The court and both sides treated this as a **future admissibility hearing**, not a prelim issue — a live trial-stage fight to keep or limit the DNA. See [[The Casing Match and DNA]] and [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].
- **Defeat the death-penalty aggravators** — even conceding a killing, contest the aggravators (e.g., the *"grave risk to others"* trajectory allegation — see [[Line of Sight and Shot Geometry]] and [[2025-09-16 - The Information and Charges|The Information and Charges]]) to **take death off the table.** This is much of what an [[The Defense Team|elite capital team]] is for. See [[Beating Some Charges Losing Others]] for the count-by-count breakdown.
- **Penalty-phase mitigation** — youth (Robinson was 22), no prior record (per the [[2025-09-12 - Public Safety Assessment|Public Safety Assessment]]), mental health, and background: the case to **spare his life** if he is convicted.
- **Insanity / competency** — narrow in Utah, but always evaluated in a capital case.

## The realistic posture
Given *this* record, the likeliest defense center of gravity is **not** "he didn't do it" but a combination of: **holding the State to its burden and attacking each evidentiary link**, **contesting the death-penalty aggravators**, and **building penalty-phase mitigation** — with the pretrial fights (discovery, disqualification, suppression) shaping the ground. But again: the defense has announced none of this. This page maps what the **record makes available**, not what the defense will do.

For the honest catalogue of what remains genuinely unresolved, see [[What Is Genuinely Open]]; for the weight of a full-fabrication theory, see [[A Hypothetical Cover-Up]].
