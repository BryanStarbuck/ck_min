# Chain of Custody Problems

> **What this is.** The defense's **chain-of-custody and authentication** challenges — how the State's evidence was collected, handled, altered, and vouched for — read **in the light most favorable to the defense.** Robinson is **presumed innocent**; the **State carries the burden.**
>
> **Caveat both ways:** in Utah, **reliable hearsay is admissible at a preliminary hearing** (Rule 1102), so most of these are **trial-admissibility** arguments, not reasons a bind-over fails — and the judge did enforce the rules where it mattered (below). Still, the custody record has real holes.

## Broken at the first link
- The **[[The Unattributed Holster|pistol holster]] was never collected, tagged, or fingerprinted** — "Did you ever take custody of it? Nope… fingerprinted? I have no idea" [02:53:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=10393s).
- The foundational **police report was a multi-agency compilation** the first officer didn't author [02:49:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=10162s) — no single custodian.
- The **crime-scene tape was thrown up to the officer** by someone below [04:10:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15058s); the roof and drop-off scenes were **handed to responders who arrived after the shooting** [02:47:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=10053s); and there was an **unaccounted-for window** on the roof before it was secured [04:10:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15005s).
- The first officer's body-worn camera is the **only body-cam footage of the initial response**, and it **runs just 27 min 35 sec — starting at the Hall of Flags and cutting out while he is still on the roof, before the crime tape went up.** He never restarted it — *"that twenty-seven minute and thirty-five second body cam that… ends while you're still on the roof but haven't put the crime tape up yet — that's the only body cam you have that day? **Yes**"* [04:12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15146s). So there is **no continuous recording** of the perch from the moment the screwdriver was found through the scene being secured and tagged.

## The exhibits show a staged scene, not the scene as found
Evidence **markers and tags were added later by techs**, so the photos don't depict what the officer saw — "there's an evidence tag… was that present when you found the screwdriver? No" [02:14:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=8042s). The defense's objection: the photo "is **not a fair and accurate representation** of how he saw it… we don't have any indication about when this picture was taken or by whom" [02:15:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=8152s).

## Authenticated by people who never testified (Rule 1102)
Nearly all the video and photo evidence came in through **absent declarants**:
- The night "sniper-perch" photos were taken by forensic examiner **Melissa Richards**, who did not testify; the officer vouched for them from a phone call weeks earlier [02:20:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=8410s).
- The civilian videos (Amber (Amanda) Wright, the Phillips minor, **Farnsworth / Visual Impulse**, **Olson / UVU surveillance**) came in via **1102 statements**, over the defense's standing confrontation objection [04:42:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=16939s).
- The video **clips were edited out of larger originals by unknown hands** — "someone made clips, and we don't know who… what they did" [05:28:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=19733s); the UVU custodian "**verified**" the surveillance but "**doesn't say how**" [05:44:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=20695s).
- The **medical-examiner report** was introduced through a **non-expert case agent** who wasn't at the autopsy — "an expert report without the expert" [06:05:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=21935s) — and the conduit even **misstated the manner of death as "murder,"** corrected to "homicide" [06:10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=22234s).
- **Day 2 — the DNA came in the same way.** The State's custody witness, Sgt. **Jennifer Faumuina** (Utah DPS), testified that the towel-wrapped rifle and the screwdriver were routed to the **FBI lab** (Day 2 [05:04:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18281s)) and presented the result as *"an FBI report that was provided to me by the FBI"* ([05:05:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18352s)) — a report she neither authored nor tested. The FBI examiner who actually ran the analysis, **Amanda Bakker**, was called by the **defense**, not the State (see [[The Casing Match and DNA]]).

## The marquee exhibit was excluded on custody grounds
The State's compilation video (**Exhibit 12.1**) was assembled by a County Attorney staffer (**Kimberly Dufler**) — not a testifying witness — with zooms, blurs, and circles, and the State **could not produce an unaltered version** "not right now, not today" [06:51:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=24705s). Judge Graf **sustained** the objection: "I'm missing a link… I don't have the person who made the alteration" [06:53:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=24799s). See [[The Surveillance Video Was Doctored]] — the biggest defense win of Day 1.

## The confession comes in without confrontation
At the **June 22, 2026** hearing, the court let the State prove probable cause through **reliable hearsay** — [[Lance Twiggs]]'s **recorded interview** plus the [[The Discord and Text Messages|Discord/texts]], the [[The Keyboard Note|handwritten note]], and **hearsay ATF/forensic lab reports (Exhibits 30–34)** — **without calling Twiggs live** ([June 22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MThH8bJzgn4&t=103s)). Twiggs holds **state *and* federal immunity**; the court acknowledged immunity as a "source of bias" but **deferred confrontation to trial**. The defense lined up **seven live forensic examiners** to contest the State's hearsay reports ([June 22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MThH8bJzgn4&t=2160s)), and noted the ATF lab reports were produced only "late last week." Separately, the [[The SD Cards|event-camera SD cards]] passed through **Kirk's own TPUSA team before reaching the FBI**.

> **Day 4 (July 9) — the custody witness finally faces cross, and the digital chain is laid out.** On recall, Sgt. [[Jennifer Faumuina]] took the cross that Day 2 had deferred, and it exposed collection gaps. The **towel and the rifle were grabbed together** by FBI ERT lead **Michelle Kamak** — *not bagged separately* — with Faumuina only **observing**, not collecting [06:05:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=21916s); two items whose surfaces could cross-contaminate were handled as one. She **did not personally observe** the rooftop **screwdriver** seizure [06:08:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=22103s) — the perch item passed through hands she could not vouch for. And the **Fulton Library backpack, jacket, and gloves** were *"deemed unrelated"* and their testing *"pulled"* [06:02:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=21774s) — potential evidence set aside without analysis. On the **digital** side, [[Brian Davis|Agent Davis]] laid the **Cellebrite** phone-extraction and **Discord**-subpoena foundation — Robinson's Discord username *"tylerikum,"* Twiggs's phone routed to the **RCFL Salt Lake City** and then a **SIAC** analyst [02:52:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10336s)–[02:58:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10703s) — a documented chain, though the [[The Discord and Text Messages|Discord/text]] content still reached the court as **hearsay** through Davis (excused with **no cross** [03:19:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=11992s)) and the [[Lance Twiggs]] **recorded statement was played without live confrontation**. The **State then rested** [06:14:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=22472s). A more probed custody record than Day 2 left it — but the two central handling gaps (co-collection, un-observed seizure) and the un-confronted digital hearsay survive for trial. See [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4|Day 4 Legal Review]] and [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4 Transcript|Day 4 Transcript]].

> **Day 3 (July 8) — the roommate/digital evidence enters through a conduit, and Twiggs stays off the stand.** The State put its roommate and digital pillar in through a **single live foundation witness**, SBI case agent [[Brian Davis|Agent Brian Davis]] (the affidavit affiant), on direct by Lauren Hunt — so much of what he conveyed about [[Lance Twiggs]] and the messages is **conduit/foundational hearsay**, permissible at a prelim under Rule 1102 but never subject to live cross. Davis laid the foundation for Twiggs's **buccal swab** [01:02:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3767s) and his grant of **use immunity** [01:05:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3909s), then the State moved in Twiggs's **recorded Rule 1102 statement** (Twiggs not live — his compel-subpoena was **quashed June 22**) plus the **Discord/text thread (Exhibit 16)** [01:13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=4396s) and the note. The defense **preserved a running Confrontation-Clause objection**: a central State source it cannot cross-examine. The court admitted the material for the **limited probable-cause purpose** — **overruling** Rule 403 because a magistrate is not a jury [02:44:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9886s) — while **reserving** the relevance of some Exhibit 16 portions [02:44:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9867s) and pledging **redaction / non-publication** to protect the jury pool [02:45:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9908s). Fine for probable cause now; a **preserved confrontation problem** for trial. See [[2026-07-08 - Preliminary Hearing Day 3|Day 3 Legal Review]] and [[2026-07-08 - Preliminary Hearing Day 3 Transcript|Day 3 Transcript]].

## The Other Side
**Reliable hearsay is admissible at a Utah prelim**, so most of this doesn't stop a bind-over — and the judge **did** exclude the annotated video and correct the "murder/homicide" error, which is the **system working**, not failing. These are largely **trial-admissibility and confrontation** fights, preserved for later. But they are real: the custody record is stitched, annotated, and largely un-confronted — and the defense has **seven forensic witnesses** ready to test it. See [[Gaps in the Investigation]] and [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]].
