# Insufficiency of the Evidence

> **What this is.** The **reasonable-doubt** case, read deliberately **in the light most favorable to the defense** — as the presumption of innocence requires. It does *not* assert Robinson is innocent (that's [[The Gun Was His But Planted|a different posture]]); it asks the narrower, more powerful question: **taking the State's own evidence, does it prove guilt beyond a reasonable doubt?** The defendant is **presumed innocent**, the **State carries the entire burden**, and each item below is examined for what it *fails* to establish.
>
> **A caveat both ways:** a **preliminary hearing** tests only *probable cause* — a low bar — so many of these are **trial** arguments, not reasons a bind-over fails. And the State's answer is **convergence** ([[The Established Record]]). Here, though, the doubt is given its full due.

## The method
Attack each pillar on its own terms — its weakest link, an innocent alternative explanation, and what is missing — then ask whether what remains is *proof*, or only *consistent with*.

## 1. The identification — built backward, never a face
- **No physical-feature ID.** The first officer on the roof could not identify the figure's **face, clothing, height, shoes, or hat** — only "not heavyset" ([04:13:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15180s)). See [[They Have the Wrong Person]].
- **Day 2 confirmed the ID is built by inference, not a face.** The State's case agent, Hull, traced "the individual believed to be Mr. Robinson" across campus by **gait/limp, the "same shoes," and clothing** ([Day 2, 00:51:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=3085s)), while conceding the **rooftop figure itself is not facially distinguishable** ([Day 2, 01:53:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6830s)) — interpretive markers, not identity. See [[The Surveillance and the Gait]] and [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].
- **A gun that wasn't a gun.** In the Day-1 officer's report it was **"a long black object,"** not a firearm ([04:14:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15263s)); it became "a rifle" only *"knowing what you know about the case today"* ([04:16:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15369s)) — **hindsight-contaminated**. On Day 2, Hull described the object crossing Campus Drive the same way — concealed or bagged, "**could be a firearm**," never a visible gun ([Day 2, 01:56:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6966s)).
- **Nobody identified the shooter for two days** ([04:17:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15449s)); the ID came from a **surrender tip and then a DMV workup**, not independent forensic matching ([06:31:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=23519s)). The case ran **backward from Robinson**, not forward to him.
- **The vehicle is hedged:** wheels *"not wholly unique"* ([07:00:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=25236s)), a car **co-owned with his mother** ([06:33:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=23609s)), a doorbell figure **never facially identified**. See [[Robinson on Campus]].
- **Doubt:** an identity assembled from a tip and "consistent-with" video, with **no witness who can say *that is the man on the roof.***

## 2. The DNA — his own family's rifle, and a reliability fight
- The strongest forensic tie is **Robinson's DNA on the trigger, casing, and towel** ([[The Casing Match and DNA]]). But the messages call it **"grandpa's rifle"** — a **family gun**, so **his DNA on it is exactly what you'd expect**, whether or not he fired it on 9/10. See [[The Gun Was His But Planted]].
- Touch/transfer DNA shows **contact, not the trigger-pull**; the FBI examiner (Bakker) gave **no activity-level opinion** — she cannot say *when* or *how* the DNA was deposited ([Day 2, 05:31:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=19864s)).
- **Day 2 opened a real reliability attack** on the FBI report (Exhibit 31), all of it trial-stage Rule 702 material: STR testing measures allele **length, not sequence** ([Day 2, 05:37:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=20268s)); both samples had **minor contributors below 20%** that PCAST flags ([Day 2, 05:58:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=21534s)); **STRmix is non-reproducible** run-to-run (within a factor of ~10) ([Day 2, 06:10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=22236s)); and the STRmix run for roommate Twiggs returned a **likelihood ratio of 1 — "uninformative"** — yet he was still reported as a contributor on the analyst's visual judgment ([Day 2, 07:20:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26404s)). The **samples were consumed**, leaving ~3µL she expected to be insufficient for independent retesting ([Day 2, 07:32:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=27127s)). See [[The Casing Match and DNA]] and [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].
- **The other side, honestly:** on cross Bakker held that her lab is **ANAB-accredited, validated, and ground-truth-tested**, and reported **very strong support for inclusion** — with Robinson as the **major** contributor (~95%/89%) ([Day 2, 08:05:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=29158s)). For probable cause, the octillion/quintillion numbers on Robinson likely suffice; the doubts are **trial doubts** for a Rule 702 hearing.
- **Doubt:** the DNA proves the gun is *his* — which no one disputes — not that *he* was on that roof, and the reliability of the mixture math is a live fight.

## 3. The ballistics — the bullet doesn't match
- The **bullet-jacket fragment (Exhibit 6A) is inconclusive** — *"could not be identified or excluded"* as fired from the rifle ([[The Inconclusive Bullet]]). The one thing that would tie the **death round** to *this* rifle **did not come back a match**.
- **No spent casing on the roof** — the officer searched and found none ([04:11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15085s)), and on Day 2 Hull confirmed **no casings** on or around the Losee ([Day 2, 01:49:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6572s)). The fired cartridge was recovered with the rifle in the woods, not at the perch. See [[No Casing or Rifle on the Roof]].
- **Day 4 (July 9) confirmed the mismatch from the State's own examiner — and opened a foundational-validity attack.** ATF firearm/tool-mark examiner [[Samantha Karner]] — who performed the exams *"at the request of the state of Utah"* ([Day 4, 06:24:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=23099s)) but was called by the **defense** — testified the murder bullet-jacket fragment (Item 6A) is **inconclusive**, *"could not be identified or excluded"* ([Day 4, 07:11:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=25864s)) and *"may or may not have originated from the same source"* ([Day 4, 07:14:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=26042s)). She conceded firearm-ID cannot claim **uniqueness, individualization, infallibility, a zero error rate,** or a *"reasonable degree of scientific certainty"* ([Day 4, 06:30:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=23441s)); that AFTE identification *"is subjective in nature"* ([Day 4, 07:04:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=25469s)); that **NAS (2009)** found a *"lack of a precisely defined process"* ([Day 4, 07:25:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=26736s)) and **PCAST (2016)** found firearms analysis *"falls short … of foundational validity"* ([Day 4, 07:57:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=28643s)); and that her own **2023 bullet proficiency test carried a 20.4% error rate** ([Day 4, 08:03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=28990s)). See [[The Inconclusive Bullet]] and [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4|Day 4 Legal Review]].
- **No .30-06 weapon in the home.** Sgt. [[Jennifer Faumuina]] confirmed *"Did you find any .30-06 weapons in that house? No"* ([Day 4, 05:05:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18302s)) — the townhome held .30-06 ammo, boxes, and casings, but the **weapon that fired the death round** was the wooded rifle alone, tied to no one at the residence by possession.
- **For probable cause, the casing still carries.** Karner's **cartridge-case** conclusion is a **positive ID** — it *"identifies the cartridge case as being fired in the firearm"* ([Day 4, 06:31:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=23487s)); that positive casing match (with the engraving/tool-mark chain) does the probable-cause work, so the reliability limits above are **trial-stage Rule 702** doubts, not a reason a bind-over fails.
- **Doubt:** the fatal bullet is **not matched** to Robinson's rifle — a mismatch now confirmed by the State's own examiner — and the method behind the match that *did* come back has **no measured foundational validity**.

## 4. The confession — a private note, not a trigger-pull
- The [[The Keyboard Note|keyboard-note letter]] and [[The Discord and Text Messages|messages]] are powerful — but the defense can press that they were **released as excerpts with ellipses** (no full thread), advance a **false-confession / coerced / [[Defense Strategies and Degrees of Liability|duress]]** theory, and note a **written statement is not physical proof he fired the shot**. See [[The Confession Is Coerced or Fabricated]].
- **Day 3 (July 8) — the roommate account and the digital "confession" reached the court as hearsay the defense cannot cross-examine live.** The State put Twiggs's account and the Discord/note in through the **recorded Rule 1102 statement** of an **immunized** roommate (Twiggs not live; his compel-subpoena was quashed June 22) and through case-agent [[Brian Davis|Agent Davis]] as a conduit — over a **preserved Confrontation-Clause objection**. Defense counsel was careful to **contest that the material is a "confession" at all** — "I am **not** calling the statements of Mr. Robinson confession[s]" [02:51:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=10260s). The court admitted it for the **limited probable-cause purpose** only. See [[2026-07-08 - Preliminary Hearing Day 3|Day 3 Legal Review]] and [[Chain of Custody Problems]].
- **Day 4 (July 9) — the immunized statement was *played*, still untested by cross.** Twiggs's **sworn, recorded** statement — given under **federal *and* state immunity** ([Day 4, 02:12:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=7934s)), *"in lieu of your sworn testimony at a preliminary examination"* ([Day 4, 02:11:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=7882s)), and which the prosecutor warned had *"large gaps of … black empty silent space"* ([Day 4, 02:10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=7810s)) — was played to the court; [[Lance Twiggs]] never took the stand and was **never cross-examined**. It cut both ways: he described a Sept. 11 **in-person admission** ([Day 4, 02:26:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8778s)) but also said he had *"never heard him talk about Charlie Kirk before specifically"* and recalled no LGBTQ/gender-identity discussions ([Day 4, 02:30:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=9052s)), and **hedged** his FBI-photo ID — *"I wouldn't say with 100% certainty"* ([Day 4, 02:28:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8904s)). And [[Brian Davis|Agent Davis]], who read the texts into the record, was **excused with no cross at all** ([Day 4, 03:19:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=11992s)). See [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4|Day 4 Legal Review]] and [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4 Transcript|Day 4 Transcript]].
- **Doubt:** words describe **intent**, not the act's execution — a note is not a bullet — and here they arrive as **immunized, recorded hearsay** untested by cross. This is **reasonable-doubt / trial** material, not a reason a bind-over fails.

## 5. The unresolved holster — a second armed person, walked past
- On Day 1, an **empty pistol holster** lay in the courtyard; the officer **never took custody of it, never printed it, left it on the grass** ([01:40:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=6056s), [02:53:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=10393s), [04:07:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=14867s)). A **second weapon's** holster, unowned and uncollected. See [[The Unattributed Holster]].
- **Day 2 made it worse, not better.** Trooper Gonzales's account (via Davis's report, Exhibit Hull-13) states there **was a gun found in the holster** in the amphitheater ([Day 2, 01:48:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6538s)) — contradicting the Day-1 "empty" framing and leaving a **second firearm** unowned, uncollected, and unexplained.
- **Doubt:** the scene held **another armed person's gear** — now reportedly a gun, not just a holster — that the State never explained.

## 6. The bullet on the other roof — now a lost officer cartridge
- On Day-1 cross, the defense asked whether a **bullet was found on *another* roof** ([04:11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15113s)); the officer didn't know. See [[A Bullet on the Other Roof]].
- **Day 2 partly answered — and raised a new problem.** Hull recast the "unfired bullet" as an "**ejected cartridge from an officer who cycled his rifle**," and testified **nobody knows where it is now** ([Day 2, 01:43:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6203s)). That deflates the "second shooter" reading of this item, but it is now a piece of scene evidence that was **reclassified and lost** — a chain-of-custody gap in its own right.
- Paired with the holster-with-a-gun and the persons **detained in the courtyard**, the broader **alternative hypothesis** — that the single-rooftop-shooter theory doesn't foreclose another armed person — survives, even if this particular thread does not. See [[The Shot Came from the Courtyard]] and [[A Second Shooter from a Lower Angle]].

## 7. The scene — softer than it looks
- A **~21-minute window** where the roof was unsecured ([01:37:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=5827s) → [01:43:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=6185s)); **markers added later** by techs ([02:14:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=8042s)); the **body-cam died** before the crime tape went up ([04:12:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15141s)); **no medical examiner testified** ([06:05:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=21935s)); and **nearly everything authenticated by absent declarants** via Rule 1102. See [[2026-07-06 - Preliminary Hearing Day 1|Day 1 Legal Review]].
- **Doubt:** the objective record has **gaps**, and the people who made the key exhibits weren't in the room.

## The alternative hypothesis
Read charitably to the defense, the pieces assemble not into "Robinson is innocent" but into **doubt with a shape**: an identity built **backward** from a surrender, **DNA that only proves the gun is his**, a **fatal bullet that doesn't match**, a **"confession" that is a private note**, and a scene with a **second weapon's holster** and a **possible bullet on another roof** — i.e., room for **another armed person or a second position** the State never ran down. That is not proof of innocence. It is the **reasonable doubt** the presumption of innocence entitles him to.

## The Other Side
The State's reply is that **no single item carries the case** — weapon, DNA, gait surveillance, messages, confession, and surrender **converge**, and coincidence would have to explain all of them at once ([[The Established Record]]; [[A Hypothetical Cover-Up]]). And a prelim needs only probable cause. A jury will weigh convergence against doubt; **this page is the doubt, given its full and fair due.** For the same evidence stated at its **strongest for the State** — the mirror of this page — see **[[Defense Problems]]**.
