# Beating Some Charges, Losing Others

> **What this is.** Analysis of a real capital-defense posture: in a **multi-count** case, "winning" rarely means acquittal on everything — it means a **better verdict**. A jury can convict on some counts and acquit on others; each count has its **own elements** to attack; and the single biggest prize is **taking the death penalty off the table.** Not legal advice, not a prediction. Robinson is **presumed innocent.**

## "Ten charges"… or seven?
The **Information charges seven counts** (plus a sentencing enhancement and an aggravating factor). *A July 7, 2026 report described "**10 charges**" while listing these same seven — likely counting the enhancements as separate; it should be checked against any amended Information.* Per the primary document:

| # | Charge | Degree | What the State must prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Aggravated Murder** (§ 76-5-202) | **Capital felony** | Intentional/knowing killing **+ an aggravating circumstance** — here, that he "**knowingly created a great risk of death to another**" (the bystanders). |
| 2 | Felony Discharge Causing Serious Bodily Injury (§ 76-11-210) | 1st-degree felony | Fired in Kirk's direction, endangering him, causing serious injury. |
| 3 | Obstruction of Justice (§ 76-8-306) | 2nd-degree felony | **Hid the rifle** (Sept 10). |
| 4 | Obstruction of Justice (§ 76-8-306) | 2nd-degree felony | **Discarded the clothing** he wore (~Sept 11). |

| 6 | Tampering with a Witness (§ 76-8-508) | 3rd-degree felony | Told the roommate **not to talk to police** (~Sept 11). |

## The two highest-value targets (beat these even if the murder stands)
- **The death-penalty aggravator — Count 1's "great risk to others."** This is the whole ballgame. Even conceding a homicide, if the defense **defeats the aggravating circumstance**, it is **no longer a capital case** — a murder conviction *without* the aggravator is not death-eligible. That aggravator rests on the **bullet's trajectory passing near bystanders**; contesting the geometry and trajectory is the fight. See [[Line of Sight and Shot Geometry]] and [[A Bullet on the Other Roof]].
- **The victim-targeting enhancement.** A sentencing enhancer tied to *motive* (political selection). Defeating it reduces exposure and denies the State its "political assassination" framing. On **Day 2** the State's proof for it — **Exhibit 5.1**, a self-authenticating Rule 1102 statement about TPUSA's mission and "Orthodox Christian views" — was only **provisionally admitted and not published**, the court **reserving** whether it even bears on Robinson's state of mind (Day 2 [02:52:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=10370s); court's handling [03:22:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=12132s)).

## The add-on counts — each stands or falls on its own evidence
- **Obstruction ×2 (Counts 3–4)** turn on proving **concealment** — hiding the rifle, discarding clothes. They ride on the same physical and message evidence as the murder; hard to beat if the killing is proven, but each is separately elementable.
- **Witness tampering ×2 (Counts 5–6)** turn on proving Robinson **directed [[Lance Twiggs|Twiggs]] to delete texts and stay silent** — which lives entirely in the [[The Discord and Text Messages|messages]]. If the attribution or the "instruction" reading is contested, these are the **softest felonies** to pick off.
- **Child-presence (Count 7)** — a **misdemeanor** — requires a **child under 14 knowingly present**. Narrow and low-stakes; a plausible off-ramp for a jury.

## The strategic logic
- **Concede-to-win.** A defense that **concedes the hardest-to-defend counts** can spend its credibility fighting the **capital charge and the aggravator** — the only counts that carry death. A jury that sees a defense "admit the obvious" sometimes gives it more weight where it does fight.
- **Every count beaten cuts exposure.** Even if the murder sticks, acquittals on the felonies and the enhancement reduce the sentence and the parole calculus.
- **The realistic center of gravity** (per [[Defense Strategies and Degrees of Liability]]) is **death-penalty avoidance + [[Insufficiency of the Evidence|reasonable doubt]] + mitigation** — not a clean sweep. "Beat some, lose others" is how that is actually executed.

## Day 4 (July 9) — the enhancement's motive proof weakened; the casing ID held
Day 4 moved both high-value targets — in opposite directions — and set the **bind-over** schedule. See [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4|Day 4 Legal Review]] and [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4 Transcript|Day 4 Transcript]].

- **Victim-targeting enhancement — proof got weaker.** The State's own immunized witness, [[Lance Twiggs]], said he **"never heard [Robinson] talk about Charlie Kirk before specifically"** and recalled **no gender-identity discussions** [02:30:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=9052s) — undercutting the "selected because of political expression" motive the enhancement needs. See [[Prosecution Problems]].
- **The murder bullet stays inconclusive — but the casing ID holds.** The defense's own ATF expert, [[Samantha Karner]], found the fatal **bullet-jacket fragment (Item 6A) inconclusive** — "could not be identified or excluded" [07:11:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=25864s) — yet confirmed the **cartridge case is a positive identification** to the rifle [06:31:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=23487s). The weapon-to-defendant link survives; the **fatal-round-to-weapon** link does not — useful for contesting whether the recovered rifle fired the killing shot. See [[The Inconclusive Bullet]] and [[The Casing Match and DNA]].
- **Firearm-ID reliability is contestable.** Karner conceded she cannot claim uniqueness, individualization, or a "reasonable degree of scientific certainty" [06:30:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=23441s)–[06:33:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=23612s); AFTE identification "is subjective in nature" [07:04:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=25469s); **PCAST (2016)** found firearms analysis "falls short … of foundational validity" [07:57:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=28643s); and her own **2023 bullet proficiency test had a 20.4% error rate** [08:03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=28990s). On State cross the method was rehabbed as accredited, verified, and "routinely admitted" [07:51:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=28313s), [08:04:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=29088s) — a Rule 702 fight for trial, not the prelim.
- **No bind-over yet — it is now briefed.** The court took **no probable-cause ruling**; the State's bind-over memo is due **July 28**, the defense response **Aug 11**, the State reply **Aug 18**, with **oral argument Sept 1, 2026** [08:18:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=29884s). Robinson **waived speedy-trial** [08:16:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=29761s). The "beat some, lose others" contest now plays out in that briefing.

> **The Other Side.** At a **preliminary hearing** the bar is only **probable cause**, so all seven counts are likely to bind over regardless — this is a **trial / penalty-phase** strategy, not a prelim one. And the core homicide evidence is strong ([[The Established Record]]). The leverage here is real but bounded.
