# The Established Record

Before weighing any single theory, it helps to see what investigators have actually presented — in charging documents and in open court. This is the **convergent** body of evidence that every claim on this site has to be measured against: not one thread, but weapon, forensics, surveillance, digital record, and surrender all pointing the same way.

## What the record shows
- **A single gunshot to the neck** killed Kirk; manner of death: **homicide**. Per the search-warrant affidavit, **Dr. Guajardo** of the Utah Office of the Medical Examiner certified the cause of death (Sgt. Barrett of the State Bureau of Investigation attended the autopsy). See [[The Medical Examiner and the Wound]].
- **The bullet's trajectory** "passed closely to several other individuals," including the questioner standing directly in front of Kirk, with children nearby — the State's basis for the "grave risk to others" aggravator supporting the death-penalty request. *(From the charging [[2025-09_charging_information-charges.pdf|Information]].)*
- **The weapon was recovered:** a **Mauser Model 98, .30-06 bolt-action rifle with a scope** (serial #8863), found wrapped in a towel in trees along the filmed escape route. See [[The Rifle]].
- **Engraved cartridges** were found *with the rifle, along the escape route* — **one fired, three unfired** — bearing internet-meme and mixed political phrases. See [[The Engraved Casings]].
- **The fired casing was positively matched** to that rifle by firearms examiners. See [[The Casing Match and DNA]].
- **Continuous campus surveillance** tracked a suspect — distinguished by a **stiff-right-leg gait** — from arrival, to a rooftop prone position overlooking the tent, through the shot, and off the roof in flight. See [[The Surveillance and the Gait]].
- **Digital and physical evidence** tie Robinson to the plan: text/Discord messages (with roommate Lance Twiggs) about retrieving and hiding a rifle, engraving bullets, and changing clothes; **his DNA on the trigger, casing, and towel**; a **shoe-impression match**; and his vehicle on campus that morning. See [[The Discord and Text Messages]].
- **In his home:** a search of Robinson's residence found **a shell casing with etchings *matching* the rifle's engraved rounds** and **several targets with bullet holes** (per [[2025-09-16 - The Information and Charges|the Information]]) — corroborating the hand-engraved ammunition and shooting practice. See [[The Engraved Casings]].
- **He surrendered** ~33 hours later in a family-brokered voluntary surrender. See [[The Surrender]].

## What Day 2 added
The July 7 hearing **strengthened the convergence** — while, honestly, leaving parts of it contested (see [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]]):
- **A real FBI-lab chain of custody now exists.** The rooftop screwdriver and the towel-wrapped rifle were collected by the state crime lab, **handed to the FBI** that evening, and routed to the FBI/ATF labs [05:02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18145s) — answering the Day-1 "no chain of custody" complaint. *(Contested: the custody witness, Faumuina, read the FBI's numbers as a **conduit** and her **cross is still reserved**.)*
- **The DNA report (Exhibit 31) put Robinson forward as the *major* contributor** — ~95%/89% of the mixtures on the towel and screwdriver, "very strong support for inclusion," with likelihood ratios of **1.7 octillion** and **30 quintillion** [05:11:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18662s), [08:05:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=29158s). *(Contested: a **Rule 702 reliability fight** is coming — STR length-not-sequence, sub-20% mixtures, non-reproducible STRmix, an "uninformative" likelihood ratio for the roommate; the examiner's lab is **accredited and validated**.)* See [[The Casing Match and DNA]].
- **The surveillance track and the rifle in the woods held up.** Hull tied the campus video into one continuous narrative — arrival, wooded area, onto the Losee roof, the shot at 12:23:28, and flight [00:44:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=2695s) — and the **Mauser 98** was recovered in the wooded area the suspect entered [02:20:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=8441s). *(Contested: the rooftop figure is **not facially identifiable**; the tie runs on **gait, "same shoes," and clothing**.)* See [[The Surveillance and the Gait]].

## What Day 3 added
Day 3 (July 8) moved the **roommate + digital pillar** into the record: through case agent [[Brian Davis|Agent Davis]] (direct by Hunt), the State laid the foundation for **[[Lance Twiggs]]'s** account — **two recorded interviews** (Sept. 12, 2025 and April 20, 2026, with counsel), a **buccal DNA swab**, and **use immunity** — and moved the **Discord/text thread (Exhibit 16)** and the **[[The Keyboard Note|keyboard note]]** for **motive** and probable cause, with Twiggs's statement entering as a **recorded Rule 1102 statement** rather than live testimony. The court admitted the digital material for the **limited probable-cause purpose** (Rule 403 overruled — a magistrate, not a jury), while **reserving** the relevance of some portions and pledging **redaction / non-publication** to protect the jury pool. *(Contested: the defense preserved a **Confrontation-Clause** objection — Twiggs cannot be cross-examined live — and a **pretrial-publicity** objection to airing "what the State will call a confession.")* See [[2026-07-08 - Preliminary Hearing Day 3|Day 3 Legal Review]].

Day 4 (July 9) **completed the State's case — it rested** [06:14:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=22472s) — and tightened the physical chain (see [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4|Day 4 Legal Review]] and [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4 Transcript|Day 4 Transcript]]):

[[Lance Twiggs]] said Robinson had borrowed a **Dremel "to create messages on bullets"** about a month earlier [02:18:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8315s), and a **"test shot"** engraved .30-06 casing sat on the safe in the room next to his [05:04:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18264s). See [[The Engraved Casings]] and [[The Casing Match and DNA]].
- **Twiggs's account entered as a recorded, immunized statement** — offered "in lieu of … sworn testimony," with state and federal immunity [02:11:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=7882s), [02:12:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=7934s) — describing the keyboard note he **photographed** [02:20:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8420s) and an **in-person confession on Sept 11** ("he said it was"; "wishes he hadn't done it") [02:26:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8778s). *(Contested: not live, not cross-examined — Confrontation preserved — and Twiggs "never heard [Robinson] talk about Charlie Kirk … specifically" [02:30:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=9052s).)* See [[The Keyboard Note]] and [[The Discord and Text Messages]].
- **The casing ID held; the fatal bullet did not.** Called by the defense, ATF examiner [[Samantha Karner]] confirmed the **cartridge case is a positive identification** to the rifle [06:31:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=23487s), while the **murder bullet-jacket fragment (Item 6A) was inconclusive** — "could not be identified or excluded" [07:11:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=25864s). *(Contested: firearm-tool-mark ID is a subjective method facing a Rule 702 challenge.)* See [[The Casing Match and DNA]] and [[The Inconclusive Bullet]].

## Why this matters for the theories
Almost every theory on this site isolates **one** soft spot — an inconclusive bullet fragment, an officer who couldn't ID a face from one camera, a body-cam that died — and treats it as if it were the whole case. The honest reading runs the other way: any single item can be questioned, but they **converge**. That is the frame for reading each claim.

It is also why the distinction between a **preliminary hearing** and a **trial** matters. The prelim only tests *probable cause*, and in Utah reliable hearsay is admissible there. Many real weaknesses in the Day-1 and Day-2 testimony — the identity-by-inference gap, the DNA-reliability questions — are **trial** problems, not signs the official story is fake. See [[2026-07-06 - Preliminary Hearing Day 1|Day 1 Legal Review]], [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]], and [[What Is Genuinely Open]].

> **For balance.** This page **neutrally states** the State's convergent case. Robinson is **presumed innocent** and the State bears the burden — so for that **same evidence read in the light most favorable to the defense**, attacked piece by piece, see **[[Insufficiency of the Evidence]]** and **[[Defense Strategies and Degrees of Liability]]**. For it argued at its **strongest for the State** (the defense's problems), see **[[Defense Problems]]**.

## What the State still has to present (the trial preview)
The five-day hearing was built to clear **probable cause**, not to try the case — so the State deliberately held back, and used shortcuts a trial won't allow. Expect the prosecution's trial case to add:

- **The parents' live testimony.** Robinson's mother and father were **interviewed** but never testified; the family-confession reached the prelim only through Agent Hull's secondhand *"I believe he informed his immediate family."* At trial the State would **call the parents** to testify directly to what Robinson told them (a party admission, no hearsay once they're on the stand). → [[Confessions and Admissions]], [[What Is Genuinely Open]] #20.
- **Cell-phone location + map history.** The Apple warrant reached **geolocation and Safari map/search history** for both Robinson's and [[Lance Twiggs|Twiggs]]'s accounts, and **Robinson's own phone was never forensically extracted on the record** — none of it was presented. Location data placing his device on the route (or map searches showing planning) is the kind of independent digital anchor a trial jury would expect. → [[The Phone and Location Data]], [[What Is Genuinely Open]] #19.
- **Live forensic examiners, subject to cross.** At the prelim, the reports came in largely as **conduit read-ins** ([[Jennifer Faumuina]] reading the FBI/ATF reports) and a **recorded** Twiggs statement — both permissible only because reliable hearsay is admissible at a preliminary hearing. At **trial** the **Confrontation Clause** forces the State to call the **actual examiners live**: the FBI DNA analyst ([[Amanda Bakker]]), the ATF firearms/tool-mark examiner ([[Samantha Karner]]), the ATF DNA chief ([[Caitlin Oliver]]), the tool-mark examiner behind the Dremel match — and **[[Lance Twiggs]] himself** (the recorded 1102 statement will not substitute).
- **The medical examiner + the full autopsy.** The **ME never testified** and the **autopsy report was never published**; the wound/cause-of-death came in only secondhand. At trial the State would put the ME on the stand and enter the autopsy. → [[The Medical Examiner and the Wound]], [[The Inconclusive Bullet]].
- **The enhanced / higher-quality video.** The zoomed, marked **"sniper's-perch" video (Ex. 12.1)** was shown **in-courtroom only** (livestream cut to audio); it — and any further-enhanced imagery — would be presented and authenticated at trial. → [[The Surveillance and the Gait]], [[What Is Genuinely Open]] #22.

None of these gaps means the case is thin — they reflect a **prelim tuned to a low bar**. But they are the concrete things to watch for as the case moves toward trial. See [[What Is Genuinely Open]].
