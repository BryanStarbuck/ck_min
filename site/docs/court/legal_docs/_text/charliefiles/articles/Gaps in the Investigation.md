# Gaps in the Investigation

> **What this is.** The defense's "**absence of evidence**" argument — sometimes called the "shoddy investigation" theme — read **in the light most favorable to the defense**, as the presumption of innocence requires. The point is not that an alternative suspect is *proven*; it is that investigators **left gaps** — people not identified, items not collected, the scene not secured, alternatives not run down — and the State **cannot exclude possibilities it never investigated.** Robinson is **presumed innocent**; the **State carries the burden.**
>
> **Caveat both ways:** a **preliminary hearing** tests only *probable cause*, so most of this is a **trial** argument; and the State's answer is that identity rests on a **convergent** record ([[The Established Record]]). Here the doubt gets its full due.

## The scene was never set up to catch anyone
On Day-1 cross, the first officer on scene ([[Chris Bagley]]) confirmed a near-total absence of security infrastructure:
- **No operational or tactical plan, and no briefing** for the event [04:05:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=14700s).
- **Six officers** for a crowd of thousands [02:44:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=9844s); **no magnetometers or screening**, and **no drones** until Provo PD brought one *after* the shooting [02:45:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=9900s).
- **The Losee rooftop — the shooter's perch — was never assigned or covered** before the shot [02:45:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=9936s).

## What the investigation didn't identify or collect
- The **rooftop figure could not be identified by any feature** — no face, clothing, height, shoes, or hat [04:12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15146s).
- The officer's **report never called the object a gun** — "just an object… a long object" [04:15:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15334s); "rifle" is a later interpretation.
- **No spent casing and no bullet on the roof** [04:11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15113s) — see [[No Casing or Rifle on the Roof]].
- The **[[The Unattributed Holster|pistol holster]] was left on the grass, never collected or printed**; the **screwdriver** at the perch was left untouched [02:43:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=9815s).
- An **unidentified armed civilian** went up to the roof — "I don't know who he was… what agency" [04:08:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=14890s); the officer didn't know whether **other guns were confiscated** [04:05:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=14727s).
- The person **seized within ~30 seconds was next to Kirk, not where the shot came from** — and it "didn't occur to" the officer that they might have the wrong person [02:55:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=10511s). See [[They Have the Wrong Person]].

## The identification came from a surrender, not detective work
After all of Day 1, no one on scene had identified the shooter [04:17:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15427s); the case agent testified the break came when **"an individual… reached out… wanting to turn themselves in"** [06:31:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=23519s) — i.e., **hundreds of hours of video produced no independent identification**; the ID came from a **walk-in surrender**. The campus-movement case is **single-source** UVU surveillance [04:35:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=16541s). See [[Robinson on Campus]] and [[The Gun Was His But Planted]].

## What Day 2 added to the gap ledger
Day 2 of the preliminary hearing (July 7) deepened several of these gaps and opened new ones — see [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]]:
- **The holster now reportedly held a gun.** Off-duty trooper Gonzales's account, relayed through Davis's report (Exhibit Hull-13), states there **was a gun found in the holster** in the amphitheater [01:48:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6538s) — contradicting the Day-1 "empty holster" framing. Whose gun, whether it was ever collected or printed, remains unanswered. See [[The Unattributed Holster]].
- **No casings on the Losee.** Hull confirmed **no casings** were found on or around the Losee roof, the shooter's perch [01:49:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6572s) — the fired cartridge instead turned up with the rifle in the woods. See [[No Casing or Rifle on the Roof]].
- **The "other-roof bullet" is an officer's lost cartridge.** The "unfired bullet" on another building shifted, mid-answer, to an "**ejected cartridge from an officer who cycled his rifle**" — and **nobody knows where it is now** [01:43:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6203s). A piece of scene evidence, reclassified and then lost. See [[A Bullet on the Other Roof]].
- **The tent dirt was paved over.** Hull learned only through news reporting that the ground beneath the event tent — where any missed round or fragment might have been recovered — was **paved over** [02:08:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7704s). See [[The Scene Was Paved Over]].
- **Misidentification ran in every direction.** A "**huge variety**" of public tips came in, including **multiple tips naming other possible suspects** who "recognized" the released photos [02:09:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7770s); the first person detained was wrongly assumed to be the shooter [01:45:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6321s). See [[They Have the Wrong Person]].
- **The DNA samples were consumed.** The FBI examiner (Bakker) testified that many swabs were **fully consumed** in testing, leaving ~3µL she expected to be **insufficient for independent retesting** [07:32:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=27127s)–[07:34:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=27265s) — against the norm that a wrongly accused person's best insurance is the chance to repeat the test. See [[The Casing Match and DNA]].
- **Faumuina's cross is still outstanding.** The chain-of-custody witness (a conduit for the FBI's DNA report) was **paused before cross** [05:13:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18801s); the handoff to the FBI and the ATF rifle testing have not yet been probed. See [[Chain of Custody Problems]].

## What Day 4 added — the firearm science has no measured foundation
Day 4 (July 9) put the reliability of the firearm-identification method itself into the gap ledger — through the **State's own** ATF firearm/tool-mark examiner, [[Samantha Karner]], who did the exams *"at the request of the state of Utah"* ([06:24:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=23099s)) but was called by the **defense** (see [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4|Day 4 Legal Review]] and [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4 Transcript|Day 4 Transcript]]):
- **The murder bullet was never matched.** The bullet-jacket fragment (Item 6A) is **inconclusive** — *"could not be identified or excluded"* [07:11:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=25864s). The one item that would tie the **death round** to the rifle returned nothing. → *Fill:* nothing can retroactively supply a match the physical evidence didn't yield. See [[The Inconclusive Bullet]].
- **No measured error floor, no defined process.** The method cannot claim **uniqueness, individualization, infallibility, or a zero error rate** [06:30:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=23441s); AFTE identification *"is subjective in nature"* [07:04:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=25469s); **NAS (2009)** found a *"lack of a precisely defined process"* [07:25:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=26736s); **PCAST (2016)** found firearms analysis *"falls short … of foundational validity"* [07:57:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=28643s). → *Fill:* a validated, error-rate-measured method — which, per PCAST/NAS, the field does not yet have.
- **A real, measured error rate exists — and it isn't small.** Karner's own **2023 bullet proficiency test carried a 20.4% error rate** [08:03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=28990s) — an absence the State cannot wish away.
- *The other side:* the **cartridge-case** conclusion is still a **positive ID** [06:31:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=23487s), verified by a second analyst, from an ANAB-accredited lab whose method is *"routinely admitted"* [08:04:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=29088s) — enough for **probable cause**; these are foundational-validity limits for a **trial** Rule 702 fight, not a reason a bind-over fails.

## The identity holes — and what would fill each
The identification is entirely **inferential** (no eyewitness saw the shooter's face — see [[How Robinson Was Identified]]). The specific holes as the record now stands, and what would close each:
- **No face, and no recovered disguise.** There is no face on the roof video, and the **hat, glasses, and the shooting-visit clothing** were (per the State's own obstruction theory) discarded and are **not shown recovered** ([Day 2 01:53:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6793s); [Day 1 04:14:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15250s)). → *Fill:* the recovered clothing/hat/glasses themselves, or the search-and-disposal evidence showing where they went.
- **The vehicle ID is interpretive.** Wheels *"not wholly unique,"* the car only *"consistent with,"* and no readable plate on the doorbell clip — which the Nobles described as a **bald driver and three others** ([Day 1 07:00:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=25236s); [Day 2 02:06:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7560s)). → *Fill:* the Nobles' full statement, the cell-site records, and any plate-readable frame.
- **No independent ID until the surrender.** "Person of interest" for two days; the name came from the walk-in, not the scene ([Day 2 01:51:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6719s)). → *Fill:* nothing can retroactively supply an at-scene identification — a permanent reasonable-doubt feature of the case.
- **Why was he even there?** The record does not establish that Robinson was a **UVU student** or otherwise explain his presence beyond attending the event. → *Fill:* enrollment records / the reason for his presence.
- **Twiggs's account is untested.** [[Lance Twiggs]]'s interview came in **without live confrontation**. → *Fill:* the full Twiggs interview and the message timeline (see [[Chain of Custody Problems]]).

## Discovery gaps compound it
At the **Dec. 11, 2025** hearing the defense pressed that it **still lacked thousands of discovery files** — "we still don't have all the discovery… significant discovery materials that have not been received" ([Dec 11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuIwe9z61xc&t=16559s)) — and that the State announced the **death penalty at arrest but had "not provided" the aggravation basis** ([Dec 11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuIwe9z61xc&t=16747s)). The State conceded discovery "is going to be ongoing until… trial."

## The absence-of-evidence logic
The defense doesn't have to prove a second shooter or any alternative. It points to **what the investigation never did** — never identified the holster or the armed civilian, never secured the roof, never found a casing there, never independently identified the shooter — and argues the State **cannot rule out alternatives it never investigated.** That is reasonable doubt built from **negative space**. See [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]], [[A Bullet on the Other Roof]], and [[Chain of Custody Problems]].

## The Other Side
"Absence of evidence is not evidence of innocence," and a prelim needs only **probable cause**. Under-resourced event security is ordinary negligence, not proof of a frame; and the ID, though surrender-triggered, is backed by the [[The Casing Match and DNA|DNA]], the [[The Discord and Text Messages|messages]], and the [[The Keyboard Note|confession]] ([[The Established Record]]). The gaps are real **reasonable-doubt** and **penalty-phase** material — not, by themselves, an acquittal.
