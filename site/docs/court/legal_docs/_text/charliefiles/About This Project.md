# About This Project

This site documents the **claims and theories** that spread after the assassination of Charlie Kirk, and weighs each one against the **public record** of *State of Utah v. Tyler James Robinson* — the charging documents, the full court file, open-court testimony, official statements, and mainstream reporting.

The goal is neither to fuel theories nor to wave them away, but to do the thing that is actually useful: **take each claim seriously enough to check it**, show the evidence, and mark honestly where the record runs out.

## The verdict key
Every claim gets a plain verdict:

- **FALSE** — contradicted by the evidence.
- **MISLEADING** — a real fact stretched into a false conclusion.
- **UNSUPPORTED** — no evidence for it (which is different from disproven).
- **PARTLY TRUE** — a real core wrapped in an unproven claim.
- **FABRICATED** — the "evidence" itself is fake (a doctored image, an AI clip).
- **OPEN** — not yet answerable from the public record.

## How to read the site
- Start at [[Home]] or [[The Established Record]].
- Each **theory note** states the claim, what fuels it, what the evidence shows, a verdict, and sources — and links to the **evidence notes** and **court documents** it relies on.
- **Timecodes** like `[04:11:25]` in the hearing material are **clickable deep-links** into the Court TV hearing video — they jump to that exact second.
- [[What Is Genuinely Open]] collects the questions that are *not* resolved. Keeping those honest is what makes the rest credible.

## Sourcing
Primary sources are preferred throughout: the [[Court Documents Index|court filings]], the sworn [[2026-07-06 - Preliminary Hearing Day 1 Transcript|hearing testimony]], the **FBI's case page**, and statements from **Utah authorities** — over pundits and secondhand accounts. This resource deliberately **leans on primary and official sources and avoids resting conclusions on advocacy groups or dedicated fact-check outlets**, which carry their own slant. Where a widely-repeated detail comes only from staff or media accounts rather than an official finding (for example, the [[The Medical Examiner and the Wound|wound specifics]]), the note says so. Full citations live on each note and are collected in [[Sources]].

## Cautions & disclaimers
- **Not legal advice.** This is commentary and analysis of a public proceeding.
- **The case is ongoing.** Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, and is presumed innocent. A preliminary hearing tests only *probable cause* — a low bar — not guilt beyond a reasonable doubt.
- **Images — fair-use commentary.** Third-party images (news photos, broadcast screenshots) appear here for **commentary, criticism, and news reporting** — in low resolution, **credited to their source**, and always alongside substantive analysis. Where a clean rights-free image isn't available, the site sometimes uses an **original reconstruction** of the underlying facts instead (e.g., the [[Kirk Was Leaving the Pro-Israel Cause|reconstructed message thread]]). Freely-licensed images (Wikimedia Commons) and government/court materials (FBI-released images, court exhibits, the Court TV hearing video) are used under their own terms. Court filings and the hearing video are public record.
- Speaker labels in the raw transcript come from automated diarization and should be **verified against the video** before quoting a specific person by name.

*Corrections and better sources are welcome — the atomic, note-per-claim structure exists precisely so the record can be tightened over time.*
