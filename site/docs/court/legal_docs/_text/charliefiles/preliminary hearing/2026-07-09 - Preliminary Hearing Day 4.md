## Summary
Day 4 was the **longest and most consequential session yet** (~8.5 hours) and again **ended mid-hearing**, recessed to **Day 5 (July 10, 9:00 a.m.)**. Four things happened, in order:

1. **The Day-3 publication fight was resolved — against the defense.** After overnight briefing (including a motion by Erica Kirk's counsel), the court adopted a **three-tier** approach — (1) admit for probable cause, (2) display to the gallery, (3) publish to the public through the camera — and, exhibit by exhibit, **overruled the defense's objection** to publishing the digital record [01:28:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=5336s). It **distinguished *Rideau*** (no "inherent or presumed prejudice" here) [01:27:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=5263s) and let the State publish **Exhibit 16.1 (texts), 16.2 (Discord), 16.3 (the note photo)** and **16.4 (FBI images)** — with targeted redactions to protect the jury pool.
2. **[[Lance Twiggs]]'s immunized, sworn recorded statement finally came in** — the roommate's own account, under a **federal and state immunity agreement** with a false-statement admonition, offered "**in lieu of your sworn testimony at a preliminary examination**" [02:12:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=7934s) — played during case agent **[[Brian Davis|Agent Davis]]**'s testimony, who read the **[[The Discord and Text Messages|texts]]** into the record and was then **excused with no cross** [03:19:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=11992s).
3. **Sgt. [[Jennifer Faumuina]] was recalled** and walked the **physical evidence**: the towel-wrapped rifle in the woods (a fired .30-06 case + three engraved unfired cartridges inside), and the **searches of the townhome and the parents' home** — a **Dremel tool + bit set**, two Remington **.30-06** boxes, **.30-06** cartridge cases, **five .30-06 casings on the safe (one engraved "test shot")**, long guns, a revolver, burned paper, and clothing/shoes — with **ATF tool-mark reports** tying the **home Dremel bit to the murder-rifle casings**. At **~06:14 the State RESTED its case-in-chief** [06:14:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=22472s).
4. **The defense opened its case** by calling the **State's own ATF firearms/tool-mark examiner, Samantha Karner**, and using her to establish the **limits of firearm-and-tool-mark identification** — the murder **bullet fragment is "inconclusive,"** the discipline is **subjective**, and it cannot claim uniqueness, a zero error rate, or "reasonable scientific certainty."

Crucially, **no probable-cause ruling was made.** The court set a **briefing schedule** — the State's bind-over memorandum by **July 28**, defense response **Aug. 11**, State reply **Aug. 18**, and **oral argument Sept. 1, 2026** [08:18:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=29884s) — and Robinson **waived his speedy-trial rights** [08:16:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=29761s). One short defense witness remains for **Day 5**.

> **What a preliminary hearing is.** Not a trial. The burden is only **probable cause**, and in Utah **reliable hearsay is admissible** (Utah Const. Art. I §12; Utah R. Evid. **1102**) — which is why Twiggs's *recorded* immunized statement could substitute for live, cross-examined testimony. A magistrate does **not** weigh competing inferences or apply a jury's Rule 403 filter. Robinson is **presumed innocent**; the entire burden is the State's. See [[Charges and Elements]] for the standard.

## The State's Day-4 case (closing the physical + digital record)
Days 1–3 built the shot, the rooftop track, the recovered rifle, the DNA, and the roommate foundation. Day 4 was built to **seat the digital confession and the physical corroboration and then rest**: (a) publish and admit the **texts, Discord, and the note** through Davis; (b) present **Twiggs's immunized statement** in his own voice; and (c) close the loop between the **home** (the Dremel engraving tool, boxes of .30-06, the "test shot" casings) and the **murder rifle** via **ATF tool-mark identifications**. See [[The Established Record]], [[The Rifle]], [[The Engraved Casings]].

> **How to read this.** (1) Preliminary hearing — burden = probable cause, hearsay admissible (Rule 1102). (2) Day 4 **ended mid-hearing** — no probable-cause ruling; that is now **briefed and argued Sept. 1**. (3) Speaker names are our best reading of the audio, cleaned and re-attributed by role; every quote links to the exact second. (4) Commentary on the public record, **not legal advice**; the defendant is **presumed innocent**.

## 1. Prosecution's strongest points
- **The confession is now in Robinson's own words, three ways — and public.** The **texts** (Exhibit 16.1, read by Davis) include *"I had enough of his hatred, some hate can't be negotiated out"* [02:43:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=9819s), the rifle *"wrapped in a towel"* in a bush where he *"changed outfits"* [02:46:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10009s), *"delete this exchange"* [02:48:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10131s), and *"I'm going to turn myself in willingly"* [02:49:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10166s). Twiggs's statement adds a **verbal, in-person confession**: he asked Robinson if what he said "was true," and Robinson "said it was" and "wishes he hadn't done it" [02:26:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8778s). See [[The Discord and Text Messages]], [[The Keyboard Note]].
- **The Dremel ties the home to the murder rifle.** Faumuina recovered a **Dremel tool + bits** from the townhome [05:01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18067s); the **ATF report (Exhibit 34)** identified the **home engraving cutter (Exhibit 15A) as having made the tool marks** on the wooded-rifle cartridge case and cartridges *and* on a safe casing [05:40:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=20401s) — and the **Exhibit 2 cartridge case was identified as fired in the Exhibit 1 rifle** [05:28:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=19737s). Twiggs independently said Robinson borrowed a **Dremel to "create messages on bullets"** [02:18:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8315s). See [[The Engraved Casings]].
- **The ammunition and the "test shot."** Two Remington **.30-06** boxes and loose **.30-06** cases at the home [05:01:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18116s), plus **five .30-06 casings on the safe next to Robinson's room — one engraved "test shot"** [05:04:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18264s) — all the **same caliber as the round in the rifle** [05:04:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18284s).
- **The casing match survived the defense's own expert.** Even the reliability witness the *defense* called conceded her one **positive** conclusion: *"the cartridge case … being fired in the firearm"* [06:31:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=23487s). For probable cause, the casing-to-rifle identification stands.
- **The digital foundation is documented.** Davis laid the **Cellebrite** phone-extraction chain (Twiggs's phone → RCFL Salt Lake City → SIAC analyst) and the **Discord subpoena** for username "tylerikum" [02:52:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10336s)–[02:58:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10703s).

## 2. Defense's strongest points
- **The murder *bullet* is inconclusive — from the State's own ATF examiner.** [[Samantha Karner]] testified the bullet-jacket fragment (Item 6A) *"could not be identified or excluded … the result of the comparison was inconclusive"* [07:11:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=25864s) and "may or may not have originated from the same … source" [07:14:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=26042s). See [[The Inconclusive Bullet]].
- **The firearm-ID discipline is subjective and error-prone.** Karner conceded she cannot claim **uniqueness**, **individualization**, **infallibility**, a **zero error rate**, or **"reasonable degree of scientific certainty"** [06:30:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=23441s)–[06:33:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=23612s); that AFTE "individualization … is **subjective in nature**" [07:04:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=25469s); that **NAS (2009)** and **PCAST (2016)** found the field "**falls short … of foundational validity**" [07:57:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=28643s); and that **her own 2023 bullet proficiency test carried a 20.4% error rate** [08:03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=28990s). See [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]], [[Gaps in the Investigation]].
- **Twiggs's account undercuts the political-targeting enhancement.** Twiggs said he **"never heard [Robinson] talk about Charlie Kirk before specifically"** and recalled **no LGBTQ / gender-identity discussions** [02:30:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=9052s), and that the engraving was framed as **hunting** and the messages "**mostly a big meme**" [02:18:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8315s), [02:47:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10069s) — cutting against the [[Charges and Elements|victim-targeting enhancement]]'s "selected because of political expression." See [[Prosecution Problems]].
- **Twiggs is an immunized, unconfronted witness.** His account arrives as a **recorded statement in lieu of live testimony**, given under **federal + state immunity** [02:12:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=7934s) — the defense **never cross-examined him**, and the prosecutor warned the recording has "**large gaps of … black empty silent space**" [02:10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=7810s). Twiggs also hedged his own FBI-photo ID ("**I wouldn't say with 100% certainty**") [02:28:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8904s). See [[Chain of Custody Problems]], [[Lance Twiggs Was More Involved|Twiggs claim check]].
- **No .30-06 weapon at the home, and hands-off collection.** Faumuina agreed **no .30-06 firearm was found in the townhome** [05:05:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18302s); on cross she conceded the **towel-and-rifle were grabbed together** by FBI ERT lead **Michelle Kamak** (not bagged separately), that she didn't personally seize the rooftop screwdriver, and that the **Fulton Library backpack/jacket/gloves** were "**deemed unrelated**" and testing "**pulled**" [06:02:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=21774s)–[06:08:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=22103s). See [[Chain of Custody Problems]], [[The Unattributed Holster]].

## 3. The marquee events
### (a) Twiggs's immunized statement — the "video testimony," finally on the record
This is the sequel to Day 3's fight. Rather than live cross-examined testimony, the State played **Twiggs's recorded, sworn, immunized statement** — the Rule 1102 substitute the June 22 ruling had blessed. Its substance is **corroborative and damning** (the note "under his keyboard" that he photographed [02:20:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8420s), the Dremel, the in-person confession), **but** it is **unconfronted**, **immunized**, partly **inaudible**, and — on motive — **helps the defense** (no prior Charlie Kirk talk, no gender-identity discussions). The Confrontation objection is preserved for trial; for probable cause, it comes in. See [[Lance Twiggs]], [[The Confession Is Coerced or Fabricated]].

### (b) The defense turns the State's ATF examiner into its own witness
The sharpest tactical move of the hearing: after the State rested, the defense called **[[Samantha Karner]]** — the **ATF examiner who did the firearm/tool-mark work *for the State*** — and walked her through the **DOJ uniform-language policy**, **AFTE subjectivity**, the **NAS/PCAST** critiques, her **20.4% proficiency error**, and, above all, the **inconclusive** bullet comparison. On cross, **McBride rehabilitated** her (accredited lab, second-analyst verification, a method "**accepted by courts across the country**" [08:04:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=29088s)). The **net**: the **casing→rifle** identification holds for probable cause, but the **bullet→rifle** link is openly **inconclusive**, and the defense has built a clean **Rule 702 / reasonable-doubt** record for trial. See [[The Inconclusive Bullet]], [[The Casing Match and DNA]], [[Beating Some Charges Losing Others]].
