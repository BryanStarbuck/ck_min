# Preliminary Hearing, Day 5 — Summary & Legal Review
*State of Utah v. Tyler James Robinson* · Case No. 251403576 · Utah 4th District · **July 10, 2026** · before **[[Judge Tony Graf]]**

## Summary
Day 5 was the **short final session** (~3 hours) that **closed the five-day preliminary hearing**. Three things happened, in order:

1. **The camera sanction.** The day opened on the fallout from Day 4: a **gallery-only** side-by-side image of the [[The Discord and Text Messages|Robinson–Twiggs communication]] (Exhibit 16) had been **briefly caught by the media camera** and pushed to the livestream. Defense counsel **Richard Novak** asked the court to **bar the news-media camera** for the rest of the hearing and for the Sept. 1 argument [01:19:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=4787s), calling it "the appropriate sanction for what happened yesterday" [01:21:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=4906s). Media counsel (**Michael Judd**) and prosecutor **Ryan McBride** agreed the image should not have aired but stressed that the *text* of the messages was already public in law-enforcement filings. The court found its no-broadcast order as to Exhibit 16 had been **violated** [01:33:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=5629s), **denied** the motion to exclude cameras (defense motion 305) and the alternative to prohibit all cameras and electronic equipment [01:34:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=5650s)–[01:34:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=5671s), and imposed a **narrower sanction**: "**members of the media shall no longer capture or broadcast any exhibits displayed by the parties during today's proceedings**" [01:34:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=5691s) — "**proportional and appropriate**" [01:34:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=5698s).
2. **The defense's last witness — ATF DNA Section Chief [[Caitlin Oliver]].** Over the State's objection that her testimony was **cumulative**, the defense called the ATF's **DNA** examiner — the forensic-DNA counterpart to Day 4's firearms witness [[Samantha Karner]]. On direct (**Michael Burt**) she established the **limits** of DNA science; on **McBride's cross**, like Karner, she **confirmed the inculpatory core** — Robinson as the **major contributor** at the lab's maximum stated strength. The defense then **rested** — "**we have no further witness**" [02:52:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=10336s).
3. **The enhanced video, and adjournment.** With **both sides done**, the State published **Exhibit 12.1** — the **zoomed, marked "sniper's-perch" surveillance video**, shown in the courtroom at the Kirk family's request — and the court switched the **livestream to audio only**, barring any video or still capture of the monitors while it played [02:55:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=10500s). The court then **adjourned the hearing to September 1, 2026 at 10:00 a.m.** [03:07:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=11271s). **No probable-cause ruling was made** — the bind-over question goes to the Day-4 **briefing schedule** and **oral argument on Sept. 1**.

> **What a preliminary hearing is.** Not a trial. The burden is only **probable cause**, and in Utah **reliable hearsay is admissible** (Utah Const. Art. I §12; Utah R. Evid. **1102**). The magistrate is a **gatekeeper** who does *not* weigh competing inferences or resolve the reliability of admitted evidence — which is exactly the point the State pressed to limit the defense's reliability witnesses. Robinson is **presumed innocent**; the entire burden is the State's. See [[Charges and Elements]].

## How Day 5 fit the whole hearing
Days 1–4 built the shot, the rooftop track, the recovered rifle, the DNA, the roommate/confession record, and the physical evidence from the homes — and the **State rested** on Day 4. Day 5 was the **defense's closing move and the housekeeping to end the hearing**: a second reliability expert (DNA, after Day 4's firearms expert), then the enhanced-video publication the family had requested, then adjournment to briefing. The defense's two-witness case (Karner + Oliver) was never about *winning* the probable-cause ruling — the bar is too low — but about building a **clean Rule 702 / reasonable-doubt record** on the two forensic pillars (firearms and DNA) for trial. See [[The Casing Match and DNA]], [[The DNA Didn't Match]], [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]].

> **How to read this.** (1) Preliminary hearing — burden = probable cause, hearsay admissible (Rule 1102). (2) The hearing **ended with no bind-over ruling**; that is now **briefed and argued Sept. 1**. (3) Speaker names are our best reading of the audio, cleaned and re-attributed by role; every quote links to the exact second. (4) Commentary on the public record, **not legal advice**; the defendant is **presumed innocent**.

## 1. Prosecution's strongest points
- **The defense's own DNA expert confirmed Robinson as the major contributor — at the lab's ceiling.** On cross, Oliver testified the DNA profiles are "**at least 1,000,000,000,000 [one trillion] times more likely … if they originated from Tyler Robinson as the major contributor**" [02:46:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=9988s)–[02:46:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=9992s) — and that a **trillion is the maximum figure her lab reports** ("we cap … at a trillion") [02:44:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=9877s), i.e. the real strength is even higher. Two defense reliability witnesses in a row (Karner, then Oliver) each **conceded the core inculpatory conclusion** on cross. See [[The Casing Match and DNA]].
- **Reliability is a trial question, not a probable-cause one — and the court agrees.** The State (Grunander) argued that once the magistrate admits evidence as a gatekeeper, "**any questions of additional reliability are really questions of credibility of the witness and weighing of the evidence**" that the court "**does not engage in at the probable cause standard**" [02:33:05-ish]([01:37:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=5827s)–[01:37:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=5834s)). Under *State v. Clark* / Rule 7B, the evidence is read in the **light most favorable to the prosecution**. See [[Charges and Elements]].
- **The record now closes with the confessions, the casing match, and the DNA all in.** Nothing on Day 5 disturbed the Day-4 record: the immunized [[Lance Twiggs]] statement, the [[Confessions and Admissions|five confessions]], the [[The Engraved Casings|casing→rifle identification]], and the [[The Casing Match and DNA|DNA]] all remain in evidence for the bind-over. The State signaled it needs no rebuttal.
- **The enhanced "sniper's-perch" video is now before the court.** Exhibit 12.1 — the zoomed, marked version of the rooftop-crawl surveillance — was published in the courtroom [02:55:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=10500s), putting the clearest visual of the alleged shooter's movement in front of the magistrate for the bind-over. See [[The Surveillance and the Gait]], [[Line of Sight and Shot Geometry]].

## 2. Defense's strongest points
- **DNA shows presence, not action — and not a "source."** Oliver conceded that "**none of the testing … could speak to activity or what activity led to the deposition of the DNA**" [02:03:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=7382s), that you "**can't assign a source to a DNA sample**" [02:11:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=7907s), and that "**a person's DNA may be present when they were not involved in the crime**" [02:12:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=7972s). The DNA proves Robinson **touched things**, not that he **fired the rifle** or **when** his DNA got there. See [[The DNA Didn't Match]], [[Chain of Custody Problems]].
- **No forensic certainty — by the State's own examiner.** "With forensic evidence, it's **impossible to state with 100% certainty anything**" [01:56:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=6983s); under DOJ uniform-language rules she does not use "reasonable degree of scientific certainty" and did not in her reports [01:56:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=7000s); the "**true number of contributors … can never be known**" [02:07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=7620s). Paired with Karner's **20.4% bullet-proficiency error** and the **inconclusive bullet** from Day 4, the defense has a full **Rule 702** record across *both* forensic disciplines. See [[The Inconclusive Bullet]], [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]].
- **The camera breach underscores the fair-trial / publicity problem.** Novak converted the inadvertent broadcast into a preserved **fair-trial-rights** record — the barred image "**has been published and is even able to be purchased as photographic prints**" — and won a sanction (no video capture of exhibits) even though the court kept the camera. It feeds the running [[Prosecution Problems|publicity]] and [[Chain of Custody Problems|handling]] narrative. See [[Prosecution Problems]].
- **The State fought to keep the DNA-limits testimony out — then it came in anyway.** The prosecution's move to **preemptively strike** Oliver as cumulative ([01:36:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=5803s) ff.) is itself a tell: the defense's reliability record is the part of this hearing built for the *next* stage, and the court let it be made.

## 3. The marquee events
### (a) The camera sanction — cameras stay, but no more exhibit video today
This is the sequel to Day 4's publication fight. After a **gallery-only** side-by-side of the Robinson–Twiggs messages hit the livestream, the court reconstructed the sequence on the record — Exhibit 24 shown only on the media-visible monitor, **Exhibit 16 briefly on all monitors and the livestream**, the **camera operator redirecting before any party flagged it** [01:17:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=4664s)–[01:18:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=4690s) (the operator caught it seconds before the court did). Balancing the **constitutional fair-trial rights of both Robinson and Erika Kirk** against **public transparency**, Graf **denied** the camera ban but ordered that the media "**shall no longer capture or broadcast any exhibits displayed … during today's proceedings**" [01:34:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=5691s) — later executed by switching the **livestream to audio-only** for Exhibit 12.1 [02:55:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=10500s). A **proportionality**, not a shutdown. See [[Prosecution Problems]], [[Judge Tony Graf]].

### (b) The defense turns the State's DNA examiner — then loses the point on cross
The mirror image of Day 4. The defense called **[[Caitlin Oliver]]**, the **ATF DNA Section Chief who did the DNA work for the State**, to walk the **limits** of DNA: likelihood ratios rather than "matches," no source attribution, no activity/timing, DNA present without involvement. Then **McBride's cross** did to Oliver what it did to Karner — extracted the **inculpatory headline** the defense had carefully avoided: the tested items are "**at least a trillion times more likely**" to carry Robinson's DNA **as the major contributor** [02:46:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=9988s). **Net:** the DNA→Robinson linkage holds for probable cause; the defense keeps a **presence-not-action / Rule 702** argument for trial. See [[The DNA Didn't Match]], [[The Casing Match and DNA]], [[Beating Some Charges Losing Others]].

### (c) The enhanced "sniper's-perch" video, shown in the room only
At the **Kirk family's request**, the court allowed the State to publish **Exhibit 12.1** — an **altered/zoomed** version of the previously-shown campus surveillance, with **added markings** highlighting the figure prosecutors say is Robinson crawling to the rooftop perch. Consistent with the sanction, the court took the **livestream to audio only** and barred any video/still capture of the monitors during publication [02:55:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=10500s)–[02:56:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=10587s). Per courtroom reporting, **Erika Kirk** and Kirk's mother became emotional during the footage, and **Robinson's monitor was turned off** — he did not watch. See [[The Surveillance and the Gait]].

## 4. The witnesses
- **[[Caitlin Oliver]]** (ATF DNA Section Chief) — the **defense's final witness**; DNA-reliability on direct (Burt), the major-contributor concession on cross (McBride). The only witness of the day.
- No other witnesses. The **State rested on Day 4** and offered **no rebuttal**; the defense **rested** after Oliver.

## 5. What Day 5 left open / what happens next
- **The bind-over is a briefing fight, decided later.** No probable-cause ruling was made. On the Day-4 schedule: **State's memorandum July 28**, **defense response Aug. 11**, **State reply Aug. 18**, and **oral argument Sept. 1, 2026 at 10:00 a.m.** [03:07:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=11271s). Robinson **waived speedy trial** (Day 4). Graf "tends not to make immediate rulings" — expect the bind-over decision after Sept. 1. See [[What Is Genuinely Open]].
- **The death penalty still rides on the bind-over.** Prosecutors are seeking it; the capital aggravator and the [[Charges and Elements|victim-targeting enhancement]] are argued Sept. 1 along with probable cause. See [[Beating Some Charges Losing Others]].
- **The reliability records built on Days 4–5 are for trial, not this ruling.** Firearms (Karner) and DNA (Oliver) subjectivity/limits don't defeat probable cause but frame the **Rule 702 / reasonable-doubt** fight to come. See [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]].
- **Reaction.** The Kirk family said in a statement that "**the world has witnessed the overwhelming evidence**" and prayed the process stays "**fair, transparent, and grounded in the facts**"; Sen. **Mike Lee**, who attended Day 4, called the evidence "**damning**." Robinson's presumption of innocence remains intact until a factfinder says otherwise.
