## Summary
Day 3 ran ~3.5 hours and again **ended mid-hearing**, recessed to **Day 4 (July 9, 9:00 a.m.)**. Unlike Days 1–2 (surveillance, chain of custody, DNA), Day 3 was **one live witness plus a long evidentiary argument** about the **roommate evidence** and the **digital record**. The State called **[[Brian Davis|Agent Brian Davis]]** — the SBI case agent and **affiant on the probable-cause affidavit** — examined on direct by prosecutor **Lauren Hunt**. Davis laid the foundation for **[[Lance Twiggs]]** (Robinson's roommate): **two recorded interviews** (the morning of **Sept. 12, 2025**, and a second on **April 20, 2026** with Twiggs's attorney present), a **buccal DNA swab**, and a grant of **use immunity** [01:01:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3703s)–[01:05:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3909s). The State then moved to put in **Twiggs's recorded statement** (as reliable hearsay under **Rule 1102** — an issue [[2026-06-22 - Twiggs Subpoena Hearing Transcript|already briefed and decided June 22]] when the court quashed the subpoena to compel Twiggs live), the **Discord / text-message thread (Exhibit 16)**, and the **[[The Keyboard Note|keyboard-note]]** — offered for **motive** and probable cause.

The defense (**Novak**, with **Burt**) fought admission and — especially — **publication** on two tracks: **Confrontation Clause** (Twiggs is not live and cannot be cross-examined) and **pretrial publicity / fair trial** (*Archuleta*, *Estes v. Texas*, *Rideau v. Louisiana*) — arguing that **televising "what the State will call a confession" before trial taints the jury pool** [02:50:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=10256s)–[02:51:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=10271s). **Media counsel** pressed the opposite way (public access to admitted exhibits). The court split the difference: it **reserved** whether some **Exhibit 16** portions are relevant ("may contain irrelevant information… reserve… until I've heard the rest of the State's evidence") [02:44:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9867s)–[02:44:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9880s), **overruled** the Rule 403 objection for admissibility because "as a **magistrate** I do not have the same [prejudice] concerns [as] a jury" [02:44:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9886s)–[02:45:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9905s), but pledged — "in the abundance of caution and **to protect the jury pool**" — to **redact / not publicize** portions [02:45:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9908s), **reserved** the fuller Confrontation/publicity question, asked the **defense to submit a brief**, and **recessed** [03:29:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=12544s)–[03:30:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=12653s). **No bind-over.**

> **What a preliminary hearing is.** Not a trial. The burden is only **probable cause**, and in Utah **reliable hearsay is admissible** (Utah Const. Art. I §12; Utah R. Evid. **1102**). A magistrate does **not weigh** competing inferences or apply the same Rule 403 prejudice filter a jury trial would — which is exactly why the court could admit the digital exhibits for this limited purpose while still protecting the jury pool from **publicity**. Robinson is **presumed innocent**; the entire burden is the State's.

## The State's Day-3 case (what the day was meant to add)
Days 1–2 built the shot, the rooftop track, the recovered rifle, and the DNA. Day 3 was built to seat the **roommate + digital** pillar: (a) **Twiggs's recorded interviews** as reliable hearsay (Twiggs having been immunized and his live testimony foreclosed by the June 22 subpoena ruling); (b) the **Discord/text thread (Exhibit 16)** — the messages in which, per the [[The Discord and Text Messages|charging record]], Robinson describes retrieving/hiding the rifle, engraving bullets, changing clothes, and surrendering; and (c) the **[[The Keyboard Note|"look under my keyboard" note]]** — to establish **motive** and Robinson's **state of mind**, and to corroborate the physical case. See [[The Established Record]].

> **How to read this.** (1) This is a **preliminary hearing**, burden = probable cause, hearsay admissible (Rule 1102). (2) Day 3 **ended mid-hearing** — no probable-cause ruling. (3) Speaker names are our best reading of the audio, cleaned and re-attributed by role; every quote links to the exact second. (4) Commentary on the public record, **not legal advice**; the defendant is **presumed innocent**.

## 1. Prosecution's strongest points
- **The roommate evidence is coming in — and it is corroborative, immunized, and twice-recorded.** Twiggs gave **two recorded interviews** and a **buccal swab**, under a grant of **use immunity**, with counsel present the second time [01:03:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3802s)–[01:05:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3909s). The **Rule 1102** admissibility of his recorded statement was **already litigated and decided** on [[2026-06-22 - Twiggs Subpoena Hearing Transcript|June 22]], so the State enters Day 3 with that door open.
- **The magistrate standard neutralizes the Rule 403 objection.** The court **overruled** the prejudice objection to Exhibit 16 precisely because a magistrate weighing probable cause is not a jury [02:44:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9886s)–[02:45:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9905s). For getting the digital record **in** at this stage, that is the whole ballgame.
- **Motive + corroboration in Robinson's own words.** The Discord/text thread and the note supply the **state-of-mind** the [[Prosecution Problems|Exhibit 5.1 fight on Day 2]] could not — this time from **Robinson himself**, not a third party's statement about Kirk's views. See [[The Keyboard Note]] and [[Defense Problems]].

## 2. Defense's strongest points
- **Confrontation.** Twiggs's account reaches the court as a **recorded statement, not live testimony subject to cross** — the defense's subpoena to compel him was **quashed** on June 22, and the defense preserved a running **Confrontation-Clause** objection here. A key State witness the defense **cannot question** is a real trial problem, even if Rule 1102 permits the hearsay now. See [[Chain of Custody Problems]] and [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]].
- **Pretrial publicity / a fair trial.** Novak's strongest, most disciplined argument was **not** that the note/Discord are inadmissible for probable cause — it was that **publicly replaying "what the State will call a confession"** before a jury is seated is the **due-process harm** condemned in *Estes* and *Rideau* (and reachable under *Archuleta*) [02:50:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=10256s)–[02:51:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=10314s), [03:16:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=11774s). He was **careful not to concede** the statements are confessions — "I am **not** calling the statements of Mr. Robinson confession[s]" [02:51:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=10260s) — preserving the point. See [[Prosecution Problems]] and [[The Confession Is Coerced or Fabricated]].
- **The relevance reservation is a partial win.** The court agreed some **Exhibit 16 portions "may contain irrelevant information"** and "may not be admissible at trial," **reserving** relevance until it hears the rest of the State's case [02:44:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9867s)–[02:44:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9880s) — and committed to **redaction / non-publication** to protect the jury pool [02:45:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=9908s). A clean, appellate-friendly containment even as the material comes in for probable cause.

## 3. The marquee fight — the Twiggs recorded statement (Rule 1102) and the "confession" publicity
This is the "**Lance Twiggs video testimony**" the coverage seized on — but it is his **recorded interview offered as reliable hearsay**, *not* live testimony. Two things converge:
- **Confrontation vs. Rule 1102.** Utah permits **reliable hearsay** at a prelim (Rule 1102), and the June 22 ruling already admitted Twiggs's recorded interview and quashed the compel-him subpoena. So at *this* stage the statement is **usable**; the confrontation objection is preserved for **trial**, where the Sixth Amendment (and *Crawford*) bites differently. See [[2026-06-22 - Twiggs Subpoena Hearing Transcript]] and [[Lance Twiggs]].
- **The immunity/interview posture cuts both ways.** Twiggs testified under **use immunity** and with **counsel** — the State says that makes the account reliable and non-self-interested-as-to-charges; the defense says an **immunized roommate** with his own exposure has every incentive to shade toward the State, and they **can't test it live**. See [[Lance Twiggs Was More Involved|Twiggs claim check]] and [[The DNA Didn't Match]] (Twiggs's own DNA on the towel was the Day-2 "uninformative" thread).

The **publicity** overlay is what made Day 3 unusual: the fight was less about **whether** the confession/Discord come in for probable cause (they largely did) than about **whether the public sees them now**. The court's answer — **admit for probable cause, protect the jury pool from publication** — is the magistrate's classic split.
