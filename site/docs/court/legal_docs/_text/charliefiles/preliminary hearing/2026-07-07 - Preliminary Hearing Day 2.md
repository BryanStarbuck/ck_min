# Preliminary Hearing, Day 2 — Summary & Legal Review
*State of Utah v. Tyler James Robinson* · Case No. 251403576 · Utah 4th District · **July 7, 2026** · before **[[Judge Tony Graf]]**
Full record: 🎙️ **[[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2 Transcript|Day 2 Transcript]]** (~8h, video id `y6ofpz6ReuE`; timecodes below are clickable to the second in the hearing video). Continues **[[2026-07-06 - Preliminary Hearing Day 1|Day 1]]**.

## Summary
Day 2 ran about eight hours and again ended **mid-hearing**, adjourned to **Day 3 (July 8)**. It finished the State's case agent, opened the chain-of-custody and forensic phase, and produced the first sustained fight over the **DNA**. Witnesses, in order: **[[David Hull|Agent David Hull]]** (State case agent — direct tracking Robinson through UVU surveillance by gait/limp/"same shoes," then cross by Nester); **Sgt. Jennifer Faumuina** (Utah DPS — chain of custody for the rooftop **screwdriver** and the towel-wrapped **rifle**, both routed to the FBI/ATF labs; she presents the **FBI's DNA report, Exhibit 31**, as a conduit, then is paused before cross); and **Amanda Bakker** (FBI DNA examiner, called **out of order by the defense — by stipulation, during the State's case-in-chief** — to challenge DNA reliability). **The State has not rested**, and the defense case has not begun; Bakker was a scheduling accommodation (an FBI examiner in from Quantico), examined by the defense on direct, the State on cross, the defense on redirect. No bind-over was argued or ruled.

> **What a preliminary hearing is.** It is **not a trial.** The State's burden is only **probable cause** — a low bar — and in Utah, **reliable hearsay is admissible** at a prelim (Utah Const. Art. I §12; Utah R. Evid. 1102). So many real weaknesses in the testimony are **trial** problems, not signs the case is failing. The defendant is **presumed innocent**, and the entire burden is the State's.

**What Day 2 did and didn't settle:**
- **Hull's ID is built by inference, not by a face.** He traces "the individual believed to be Mr. Robinson" across campus by **gait/limp, the "same shoes," and clothing** [00:51:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=3085s); the **rooftop figure itself is not facially distinguishable** [01:53:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6830s), and the object in hand is "**could be a firearm**," never a visible gun [01:56:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6966s). See [[The Surveillance and the Gait]].
- **A real FBI-lab chain of custody was laid — with Faumuina as a conduit.** The screwdriver and rifle/towel were collected by the state lab, **handed to the FBI** that evening, and tested at the FBI/ATF labs [05:02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18145s); Faumuina then reads the **FBI's** DNA numbers off a report she did not author [05:11:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18662s). See [[Chain of Custody Problems]].
- **The DNA is a genuinely two-way record.** Bakker gave the defense real limits — STR measures **length, not sequence** [05:37:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=20268s), the PCAST concerns, samples consumed, a Twiggs likelihood ratio of **1 ("uninformative")** [07:21:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26463s) — but on cross affirmed her lab is **accredited, validated, ground-truth-tested** [07:51:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=28299s). ## The State's Day-2 case (what the day was meant to add)
Day 1 established the shot, the rooftop position, and a surrender-tip identity chain. Day 2 was built to **close the forensic gap** the defense flagged on Day 1: it (a) walked the surveillance video frame-by-frame to put "the individual believed to be Mr. Robinson" on campus four times and onto the Losee roof at 12:23 [00:53:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=3202s)–[00:55:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=3327s); (b) laid a **chain of custody** for the two DNA-bearing items — the rooftop screwdriver and the towel-wrapped **Mauser 98** rifle found in the wooded area — and routed them to the FBI/ATF labs [05:02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18145s)–[05:04:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18281s); (c) put the **FBI DNA report (Exhibit 31)** in, with likelihood ratios of **1.7 octillion** (towel) and **30 quintillion** (screwdriver) favoring Robinson-plus-Twiggs [05:11:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18662s)–[05:11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18701s); and (d) tried to seat a **motive** theory via TPUSA Exhibit 5.1.

> **How to read this.**
> 1. **This is a PRELIMINARY HEARING, not a trial.** The burden is **probable cause**, and **reliable hearsay is admissible** (Art. I §12; Rule 1102). The judge repeatedly held the line to that standard, citing *State v. Ramirez*, *State v. Drosbeck*, *State v. Merrill* — the magistrate does **not weigh** the evidence or pick between competing inferences; that is for a jury [06:03:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=21839s), [07:13:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=25986s). Most of the "holes" below are therefore **trial** issues, not signs the case is failing.
> 2. **Day 2 ended mid-hearing**, adjourned to Day 3 — **no probable-cause ruling has been made yet.**
> 3. **A note on transparency.** Speaker names are our best reading of the hearing audio; every quotation links to the exact moment in the video so you can check it yourself.
> 4. This is **commentary on the public hearing record, not legal advice.** The case is ongoing, the defendant is **presumed innocent**, and evidence introduced later could change the picture.

- **A real chain of custody now exists.** Faumuina, the ERT team lead, testified the screwdriver and rifle/towel were collected by the state crime lab, transferred to the **FBI** by agreement that evening, and sent for testing [05:02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18145s)–[05:04:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18281s). - **DNA numbers that are, on their face, overwhelming.** Very strong support for inclusion: **1.7 octillion** (towel) and **30 quintillion** (screwdriver) favoring Twiggs + T. ## 4. Chain of custody & the DNA (Faumuina as conduit; Bakker's limits vs. the accredited-lab rebuttal)
This is the heart of Day 2, and it is **genuinely two-sided**. See [[The Casing Match and DNA]] and [[Chain of Custody Problems]].

**The State's build.** The screwdriver ("from perch") and the towel around the **Mauser 98** rifle were collected by the state crime lab, **transferred to the FBI** that evening by agreement, then the gun to the **ATF lab** and the towel/screwdriver to the **FBI lab** [05:02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18145s)–[05:04:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18281s). Faumuina then read the numbers off **Exhibit 31** — an FBI report **signed by Amanda Bakker, which Faumuina did not write** [05:07:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18430s). She is, in other words, a **conduit** for the FBI's forensic conclusion; the defense reserved cross until the end of her testimony [05:13:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18801s), and that cross **has not happened** — a live thread for Day 3.

**The defense's DNA attack (through the FBI's own examiner, called out of order).** Bakker gave the defense real, on-the-record limits:
- **STR measures length, not sequence.** She counts how many times a segment repeats ("boxcars"), not the letters inside them [05:36:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=20182s)–[05:37:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=20268s); at ~5–6 loci there are **iso-alleles** — same length, different sequence — she **cannot distinguish**, because the FBI does not sequence nuclear DNA and she is "not qualified in it" [07:47:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=28076s)–[07:48:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=28111s).
- **She can't say *when* or *how* the DNA was deposited** — no activity-level opinion; DNA on an item doesn't mean the person touched it [05:31:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=19864s)–[05:32:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=19937s).
- **Both samples were degraded and consumed.** Item 8 (screwdriver) was a **partial profile** [05:40:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=20456s); many swabs were **fully consumed** [07:32:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=27127s), leaving ~3µL she expected to be **insufficient for independent retesting** [07:34:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=27265s) — against the NRC/lab norm that a "wrongfully accused person's best insurance" is the chance to **repeat the test** [07:30:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=27014s).
- **PCAST.** She agreed PCAST flagged low-level/complex mixtures, that both her samples had **minor contributors below 20%** [05:58:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=21534s), and that STRmix is **non-reproducible** run-to-run (within a factor of ~10) [06:10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=22236s).
- **The number-of-contributors judgment moved.** She first called Item 7 **three** contributors, then, after a **Twiggs elimination sample**, revised to **two** [06:33:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=23632s) — a subjective, analyst-driven input that changes the math.
- **The Twiggs result is the sharpest point.** The STRmix run for Twiggs returned a **likelihood ratio of 1 — "uninformative"** — yet she reported him as a **contributor**, on visual judgment plus his submission as an "elimination" [07:20:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26404s)–[07:22:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26538s). She also knew the **towel was found in a bush at the university, not in Robinson's home**, undercutting the premise for treating Twiggs's DNA as "expected" [07:38:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=23906s)–[07:39:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=23944s).

**The accredited-lab rebuttal (why this is not a defense rout).** On cross, Bakker gave the State a strong record too: the lab is **ANAB-accredited**, externally audited every four years, and she is proficiency-tested [07:51:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=28299s)–[07:53:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=28433s); STRmix is validated **developmentally (peer-reviewed) and internally** and used in ~120 labs [07:55:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=28512s)–[07:56:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=28592s); **degradation is normal** and didn't impair her conclusions [08:02:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=28972s); and validation **is** a ground-truth test where the true answer is known [08:14:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=29642s). She rejected the PCAST "experience can't establish validity" passages on the ground that the science itself has been "heavily validated" [08:09:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=29362s)–[08:10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=29409s). **The honest read:** the defense scored real limits — length-not-sequence, an uninformative Twiggs LR, consumed samples, a moving contributor count — but did **not** show the result "wholly lacking," which is the only thing that matters at this stage. For probable cause on a light-most-favorable-to-the-State standard, the octillion/quintillion numbers on Robinson likely suffice; the doubts are **trial doubts** for a Rule 702 fight and a jury. See [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]].

## 5. The victim-targeting exhibit (5.1) — provisionally admitted, not published
The State moved Exhibit **5.1**, an 1102 statement by TPUSA's **David Engelhardt** describing the group's mission and "Orthodox Christian views on sexuality, marriage, gender," offered for **motive** and the **victim-targeting enhancement** on counts 1, 2, and 7 [03:06:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=11160s), [03:27:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=12443s). Novak's objection was disciplined and is worth preserving:
- **Relevance/state of mind.** The enhancement turns on **Robinson's** perception of **Kirk's *political* expression** (subsection k) — a statute that keeps **political expression** and **religion** (subsection m) separate. The exhibit is about **Kirk's and Engelhardt's** religious views, and says **nothing about Robinson's state of mind** [02:52:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=10370s), [03:31:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=12665s).
- **Opinion + 403.** It is untested lay/expert opinion about Matthew 19 and "Orthodox Christian values," carrying a real risk of **telling the jury pool "this is a case about religion"** — which the State does not even allege [03:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=11160s), [03:40:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=13224s).

The court **provisionally admitted** it as relevant to the political-expression enhancement, **expressly reserved** whether it connects to Robinson's state of mind, and **declined to publish** it to the courtroom or media until that later determination [03:42:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=13354s). It also **sustained** the objection to McBride *reading the unadmitted exhibit aloud* [03:10:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=11419s). For the defense this is a clean, appellate-friendly win on containment even though the exhibit is "in" for now. Note for balance: **McBride telegraphed the State's motive theory** — that Robinson's "gender persuasion" put him on the other side of Kirk's views, to be proven through "other witnesses" and Robinson's own statements [03:27:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=12443s) — so the enhancement fight is deferred, not defused.
