# Preliminary Hearing, Day 1 — Summary & Legal Review
*State of Utah v. Tyler James Robinson* · Case No. 251403576 · Utah 4th District · **July 6, 2026**
Full record: 🎙️ **[[2026-07-06 - Preliminary Hearing Day 1 Transcript|Day 1 Transcript]]** (Court TV, ~7h47m; timecodes below are clickable to the second in the hearing video).

## Summary
The five-day preliminary hearing opened **July 6, 2026**. Day 1 ran about **7 hours 47 minutes** and ended **mid-testimony**, with the case agent still on the stand. **No bind-over ruling** has been made.

> **What a preliminary hearing is.** It is **not a trial.** The State's burden is only **probable cause** — a low bar — and in Utah, **reliable hearsay is admissible** at a prelim (Utah Const. Art. I §12; Utah R. Evid. 1102). So many real weaknesses in the testimony are **trial** problems, not signs the case is failing. The defendant is **presumed innocent**.

**What Day 1 did and didn't settle:**
- **Defense's biggest win:** the judge **excluded** the State's annotated compilation video (12.1). See [[The Surveillance Video Was Doctored]].
- **Live soft spots:** an officer couldn't face-ID the rooftop figure from one camera; the [[The Unattributed Holster|unattributed holster]]; a [[The Body Camera Conveniently Failed|dead body-cam]]; and — because Day 1 predated the forensic testimony — no DNA/ballistics were presented yet.
- **Still open:** the [[What Is Genuinely Open|genuinely unresolved questions]].

The detailed, defense-side walk-through follows.

## The State's narrative (what the evidence, taken together, is meant to prove)
On **9/10/2025 at ~12:23 p.m.**, Charlie Kirk was shot in the neck under a white
tent at a Turning Point event at **Utah Valley University (UVU)**, thousands
present. The State's theory: a shooter fired one rifle round from the **Losee
Building rooftop** (clear line of sight), left behind a prone "sniper pad"
impression and a screwdriver, fled northeast off the roof, and was later
identified as **Tyler Robinson** after Robinson reached out to turn himself in
the next night. Cause of death per the ME: **gunshot wound of the neck; manner:
homicide.** Identity is built from a surrender tip → DMV workup → a **silver
Dodge Challenger** (co-registered to Robinson and his mother) → ~16 hours of UVU
surveillance → a Ring/Nest doorbell clip.

> **How to read this.**
> 1. **This is a PRELIMINARY HEARING, not a trial.** The State's burden is only
>    **probable cause** — a low bar — and in Utah, **reliable hearsay is
>    admissible at a prelim** (Utah Const. Art. I §12; Utah R. Evid. 1102). So
>    most of the "holes" below would NOT stop a bind-over; they are **trial**
>    issues. The State probably clears the prelim bar; the real doubts are about
>    a conviction beyond a reasonable doubt later.
> 2. **Day 1 ended mid-testimony.** The hearing recessed to resume the next
>    morning with the case agent still on the stand — **no probable-cause ruling
>    has been made yet.** Everything here is the State's case *in progress*.
> 3. **A note on transparency.** Speaker names are our best reading of the
>    hearing audio; every quotation links to the exact moment in the video so you
>    can check it yourself.
> 4. This is **commentary on the public hearing record, not legal advice.** The
>    case is ongoing, the defendant is **presumed innocent**, and evidence
>    introduced later could change the picture.

## 1. Prosecution's strongest points
- **Death + manner are locked in.** ME report admitted as reliable hearsay:
  gunshot wound of the neck, homicide [06:11:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=22311s). For a prelim, that element is done.
- **Physical shooting position is coherent and corroborated.** The Losee rooftop
  line of sight to the tent [01:21:12, 01:41:58]; a prone "sniper pad" impression
  (elbows/knees/feet) documented across four photos + a measuring tape
  [02:26:31, 02:29:00]; a matching escape route (gravel → NE scuff marks → deep
  shoe imprint at the drop-off) [02:37:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=9473s)–[02:39:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=9589s).
- **A credible, experienced investigative chain.** Lead SBI agent, ~40 homicides,
  multi-agency response (FBI/DEA/ATF/Marshals/state/county) [04:31–04:33].
- **A multi-source identity chain**, not a single thread: surrender tip → SIAC
  DMV workup (photo, DOB, address) → the co-registered Challenger → ~16 hrs of
  UVU video placing Robinson/vehicle on campus ~4 times that day → doorbell
  corroboration at 00:38 on 9/11 [06:31:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=23519s)–[07:00:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=25231s).
- **Disciplined foundation lawyering.** The State cleared every hearsay/foundation
  objection but one, citing the right authorities (*State v. Purcell*, *State v.
  Griffin*, Rule 1102(b), Art. I §12).
- **Strategic poise on its one loss.** When the compilation video (12.1) was
  excluded, the State chose to rebuild a clean unaltered version overnight rather
  than fight [07:41:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=27712s).

## 2. Defense's strongest points
- **The identification of the rooftop figure collapsed on cross.** The officer
  could **not** identify the figure's face, clothing, height, weight, shoes, or
  hat — *"only 'not heavyset'"* [04:13–04:14]. The "gun" was **not called a gun
  in his report** — *"a long black object in the left hand"* [04:14:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15263s); *"do you
  mention anywhere in this report that you saw a gun? I did not"* [04:15:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15334s); it
  became "a rifle" only *"knowing what you know about the case today"* [04:16:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15369s)
  — **hindsight-contaminated ID.**
- **The geographic contradiction.** The physical evidence points to a **rooftop**
  shooter; the person actually seized in ~30 seconds was **in the courtyard by
  the tent** — and the officer admitted he simply *assumed* that was the shooter:
  *"I figured those guys saw something I didn't see… I thought that was him"*
  [02:55:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=10548s).
- **Excluded the manipulated compilation (12.1).** Defense showed it was made by
  a third party (County Attorney staffer Dufler) with zooms/circles/blurs that no
  one who made them could authenticate; objection **sustained** [06:46:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=24403s)–[06:53:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=24799s)
  — the judge: *"I'm missing a link… in this type of case I want to be thorough"*
  [06:53:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=24799s). **The defense's biggest evidentiary win of the day.**
- **A clean, preserved record** of foundation/authentication objections to *every*
  photo and video exhibit — strong for trial and appeal.
- **No one identified the shooter for two days** [04:17:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15449s) — identification was
  driven by a **surrender tip, not independent forensic matching** [06:31:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=23519s).

## 3. Testimony that creates the most doubt about the government's story
Ranked by how much reasonable doubt it seeds (all trial-stage, not prelim-fatal):
1. **The rooftop figure was never identified by any physical characteristic**
   [04:13–04:14] — the ID rests on inference + the later surrender.
2. **The rooftop-shooter theory vs. the courtyard arrest** [02:53–02:55] — a
   fundamental "did they even grab the right person / was there more than one
   armed person?" tension the State has not reconciled on this record.
3. **The empty pistol holster** in the courtyard — a *second firearm's* holster,
   never collected, never printed [01:40:56, 02:53:13, 04:07:47]. It fits neither
   the rifle-from-the-roof theory nor any identified person.
4. **The body-cam battery died mid-roof** [04:12:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15141s), and footage ends *before*
   the tape went up — a gap in the one objective record of the scene.
5. **No spent casing and no rifle on the roof** [04:11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15085s), plus a **possible
   bullet found elsewhere** [04:12:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15123s) — the physical shot-origin proof is softer
   than the narrative.
6. **The manipulated video** the court itself worried about — *"how much was done
   to manipulate the video"* [06:50:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=24625s); an unaltered version couldn't be produced
   *"not right now, not today"* [06:51:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=24705s).

## 4. Evidentiary holes that emerged
- **No medical examiner testified** — cause/manner rests on hearsay + a non-expert
  agent's summary; Dr. Guajardo never appears [06:05:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=21935s) (fine at prelim, *"could
  never come in at trial"* per the defense).
- **No chain of custody** established for the screwdriver, holster, gravel
  impression, or shoe print; markers/tags were all added later by techs [02:14:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=8042s).
- **No forensics tying Robinson to the scene** on this record — no fingerprints,
  no DNA, no footwear comparison, no ballistics match presented in Day 1. *(The
  DNA came in on Day 2 — see [[The Casing Match and DNA]].)*
- **Absent declarants** authenticate nearly everything: forensic examiner Melissa
  Richards (night photos), Amber (Amanda) Wright, the Phillips minor, Terrell Farnsworth,
  Curtis Olson, the Nobles — all via 1102 statements, none in court.
- **No spent shell casing / no recovered rifle** on the roof [04:11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15085s)–[04:11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15113s).
- **Identity via video is interpretive**, not forensic — the vehicle wheels are
  *"not wholly unique"* [07:00:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=25236s), the Challenger is **co-owned with his mother**
  [06:33:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=23609s), and the doorbell figure is **never facially identified** [07:14].

## 8. What Day 2 took up (the tells that resolve the doubts)
- Any **forensic tie** to Robinson: prints on the screwdriver/holster/casing, DNA,
  footwear/tool-mark, or **ballistics** matching a recovered rifle.
- The **recovered rifle** and where — does it close the "no weapon on the roof" gap?
- Whether the **unaltered video** + raw UVU source footage come in, and whether the
  surveillance actually **tracks the figure continuously** to Robinson (vs. gaps).
- Who owned the **holster**, and whether any **second weapon** was recovered.
- Whether the **ME** or a live forensic witness appears (trial-admissibility).
