# The Kirk Case, Documented

**A sourced, cross-referenced review of the claims circulating about the assassination of Charlie Kirk — and what the evidence actually shows.**

[*State of Utah v. Tyler James Robinson](https://xchange.utcourts.gov/XchangeWEB/XchangeWebServlet) · Case No. 251403576 · Utah 4th District Court (Provo). Built from the charging documents, the full court file, and the preliminary-hearing testimony.*

---

Charlie Kirk was fatally shot in the neck on **September 10, 2025**, while speaking under a canopy at Utah Valley University. **Tyler James Robinson** is charged with aggravated murder. In the months since, dozens of claims and theories have spread online — some plainly false, a few pointing at genuinely unresolved questions.

This site takes each major claim one at a time, lays out **what fuels it**, **what the available evidence and reporting show**, and a **plain verdict** — with sources. Where a question is genuinely unresolved, it says so rather than guessing. Everything links back to [[The Established Record]] and, where possible, to the [[Court Documents Index|primary court documents]] themselves.

> **This is journalism and commentary on the *public* record — not legal advice, and the case is ongoing.** Read every verdict here as **provisional**, for three reasons:
>
> - **Asymmetry of information.** This site is built only from what is *public* — filings, open-court testimony, official statements, reporting. The **parties hold vastly more**: the full discovery file, sealed exhibits, witness statements, and forensic work that never reaches a public docket. Next to what the prosecution and defense actually have, we are reading a **sliver**.
> - **The fog of litigation.** Both sides deliberately **withhold** their strongest material for tactical reasons; evidence and strategy surface — or don't — on their own timetable. A gap here may simply be something **not yet disclosed**, and silence in the record is not the same as absence.
> - **Dynamic evidence.** Cases are not static. A pillar that looks decisive can be **shattered** by one new fact, a suppression ruling, or a spontaneously made statement — and a thin thread **strengthened** the same way. Facts emerge, and are made, *during* the process.
>
> So treat each verdict as a **snapshot against the current record**, not a final word — theories, substantiated or not, will **shift, be invoked, or be abandoned** as the parties maneuver and the record grows. See [[What Is Genuinely Open]] and [[About This Project]] for method, the verdict key, and disclaimers.
