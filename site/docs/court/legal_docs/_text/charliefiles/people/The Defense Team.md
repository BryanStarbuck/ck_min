# The Defense Team

Robinson's taxpayer-funded defense is an **elite capital-defense team** — a strong signal that the State is seeking **death**:

- **Kathryn Nester** (Nester Lewis PLLC, Salt Lake City).
- **Michael N. Burt** (pro hac vice, San Francisco) — editor of the *California Death Penalty Defense Manual*.
- **Richard G. Novak** (pro hac vice, Pasadena) — 25+ death-eligible cases.

## Their filings are aggressive
Far from throwing the case, the team has litigated hard:
- A **motion to disqualify** the Utah County Attorney's Office. → [[defense_motion-disqualify-county-attorney.pdf|the motion]]
- A push to **hold prosecutors in contempt** (partly successful — see [[Judge Tony Graf]]).
- A motion to **exclude cameras / still photographers** from the courtroom.
- The **ballistics/discovery motion** that publicized the "inconclusive bullet" result. → [[defense_motion-to-vacate-or-continue-prelim.pdf|Motion to Vacate/Continue]], [[The Inconclusive Bullet]]

That track record is why [[The Defense Is Throwing the Case]] is rated **FALSE**.

## A note on the ballistics motion
The defense motion characterized the ATF result as being "**unable to identify** the bullet." The State's opposition showed the full phrase was "unable to identify **or exclude**" — i.e., **inconclusive**, not exculpatory. Zealous advocacy, later contextualized by the State's filing. See [[The Inconclusive Bullet]].
