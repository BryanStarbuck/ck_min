# Hunter Kozak

**Hunter Kozak** was the **last person to ask Charlie Kirk a question** — he was standing at the audience microphone, roughly **5–10 feet from Kirk**, when the fatal shot was fired at about **12:23 p.m.** on September 10, 2025. He is a **civilian eyewitness**, not a participant, and nothing in the record ties him to the shooting. See [[How Robinson Was Identified]] and [[Roles and Responsibilities at the Scene]].

## Who he is
- **29 years old**; a **mathematics and philosophy student at UVU** — so, unlike [[Tyler Robinson|the accused]], he **was** an enrolled Utah Valley University student.
- **Married, and a father of two**; his wife was with him at the event.
- A **TikTok content creator** (handle **@staxioms**), described in reporting as **politically progressive/liberal** — he came to challenge Kirk, not to support him.
- *Contested detail:* some accounts (Wikipedia, citing reporting) describe him as connected to the **"Unfuck America Tour,"** a group of left-of-center influencers that followed Kirk's tour; **ABC News, The Hill,** and other outlets describe him simply as an independent progressive attendee and **do not** attach him to that group. Treat the tour affiliation as **unconfirmed**.

## The exchange
By his account he arrived about 30 minutes early with several prepared topics, told a staffer he disagreed with Kirk, and was directed to the question line; staff "liked his question." The exchange, as reconstructed from CNN/AP/KRON and other coverage, was about **transgender people and mass shootings** — the "[[The Confession Is Coerced or Fabricated|prove me wrong]]"-style debate format Kirk was known for:
- **Kozak:** *"Do you know how many transgender Americans have been mass shooters over the last 10 years?"*
- **Kirk:** *"Too many."*
- **Kozak:** *"It's five. Now, five is a lot, right? … Do you know how many mass shooters there have been in America over the last 10 years?"*
- **Kirk:** *"Counting or not counting gang violence?"* — **the shot rang out at this moment.**

He was at the mic and **saw the shooting**, but no report has him seeing the gunman, who fired from the **[[The Rifle|Losee Center rooftop]]** roughly 140+ yards away. See [[Line of Sight and Shot Geometry]].

## What he has said since
- In an Instagram video about a day later he condemned the killing: *"…you sick psychos that think this is the answer, it's not."*
- To ABC News: *"I was not worried for my life. It was just so visceral what I saw in front of me."*
- To the *Salt Lake Tribune* / *The Hill* he said he **wished he had asked a different question — "100 percent"** — and that he had been *"flooded with angry messages and conspiracy accusations."* He also stressed that he and Kirk shared a belief in **free speech.**

## On the theories about him
Because he asked the final question, Kozak became a target of online speculation — that he was a "plant," a "distraction," or somehow coordinated with the shooter. **Nothing in the public record supports any of that.** He was a **known, named, local student** who signed up in the ordinary way, asked a policy question in Kirk's own open-debate format, and remained at the scene; the shot came from a **rooftop far behind and above him**, from a suspect who reached the roof and fled long before reaching the mic. Reading his ordinary presence as sinister is the same **hindsight-pattern** error catalogued in [[It Was Staged]]. He is best understood as a **bystander who happened to be closest to the victim** — and who has since been harassed for it.

> **Note on naming.** He is a **private individual** who was publicly identified in mainstream reporting and chose to speak on the record. This page states only what he himself and major outlets have said, and exists partly to **correct** the unfounded "he was involved" framing rather than amplify it.
