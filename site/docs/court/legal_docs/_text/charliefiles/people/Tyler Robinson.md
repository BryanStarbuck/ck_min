# Tyler Robinson

![[tyler-robinson.png|260]]

**Tyler James Robinson**, DOB **04/16/2003**, of Washington, Utah — the defendant in *State of Utah v. Tyler James Robinson*, Case No. 251403576.

- **Charged** with **seven counts**: **aggravated murder** (a **capital offense**), felony discharge of a firearm, two counts of obstruction of justice, two counts of witness tampering, and a violent offense in the presence of a child — plus a **victim-targeting enhancement**. Held **without bail**. *(The Sept. 12 booking was on three initial charges; the Sept. 16 [[2025-09-16 - The Information and Charges|Information]] filed the seven.)* See [[Beating Some Charges Losing Others]].
- **Presumed innocent.** He is charged, not convicted; the July 2026 preliminary hearing tests only whether there is **probable cause** to stand trial.
- **Surrendered** ~33 hours after the shooting in a family-brokered voluntary surrender. See [[The Surrender]].

## The case against him (per the record)
A convergent set of evidence: [[The Rifle]] recovered on the escape route with his **DNA** on the trigger, casing, and towel ([[The Casing Match and DNA]]); [[The Engraved Casings]]; [[The Surveillance and the Gait|gait-tracked surveillance]]; a shoe-impression match; his vehicle on campus; and [[The Discord and Text Messages]] describing the plan. See [[The Established Record]].

(3) **Surrender:** confirmed **voluntary and pre-identification** — the State had only a "person of interest," not a name, when he walked in [01:51:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6719s).
