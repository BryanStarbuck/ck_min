# A Hypothetical Cover-Up

> **⚠️ Read this first — what this page is and is not.** This is a **thought experiment**, not an accusation. Several theories on this site imagine that the official account is false — that the evidence was fabricated, hidden, or corrupted. A useful way to weigh any such theory is to ask: *for this to be true, exactly who would have to be lying, and would they all have to be coordinating?* This page lists those roles. **No person named here is accused of any wrongdoing;** each is presumed to have done their job honestly, and Tyler Robinson is **presumed innocent**. The point is to make the **scope** of a cover-up explicit, so readers can judge its plausibility for themselves.

## The premise
Take the **maximal** version of the theories — that Robinson is a [[Robinson Is a Patsy|patsy]] or the [[They Have the Wrong Person|wrong man]], that the physical evidence was manufactured, the wound account falsified, the footage tampered, and the process rigged. For that to hold, **every one of the following independent actors and institutions would have to be either complicit or deceived** — at the same time, without any one of them breaking.

## Who the maximal theory requires

**The physical-evidence chain**
- The **Utah SBI** agents who recovered [[The Rifle|the rifle]], casings, and towel on the escape route ([[David Hull]], [[Chris Bagley]], Brian Davis).
- The **firearms examiners** who positively matched the fired casing to the rifle.
- The **DNA analysts** whose work put Robinson's DNA on the trigger, casing, and towel — plus the defense's *own* retained expert (**Dan Krane**), who is scrutinizing that same evidence. See [[The Casing Match and DNA]].
- The **ATF**, whose bullet-fragment result came back *inconclusive* — i.e., it did **not** obligingly "match." A fabricating conspiracy that could plant a casing match presumably would not leave the bullet inconclusive. See [[The Inconclusive Bullet]].

**The medical evidence**
- The **Office of the Medical Examiner** (Dr. Guajardo/Guajardo) and **Sgt. Barrett**, who attended the autopsy — the "who pulled the bullet" role imagined in some theories. See [[The Medical Examiner and the Wound]].

**The video record**
- **UVU** and **law enforcement**, who held the campus surveillance all along (not Kirk's team — see [[Kirk Team Seized the Security Footage|UVU footage claim check]]).
- Kirk's own A/V team — [[Terryl Farnsworth]] (Exhibit 8.1), the "head of tech," and [[Andrew Kolvet]] — over the [[A Staffer Pulled the SD Cards|SD cards]], now with the FBI.
- Note the theory has to explain why the State's **annotated** video was **caught and excluded** by the judge, not slipped through. See [[The Surveillance Video Was Doctored]].

**The security & scene**
- [[Brian Harpole]] and the [[Kirks Security Detail|security detail]] — the subject of online claims ([[The Security Detail Was Israeli]]) that they publicly deny; Harpole is **suing** over one such claim.
- Whoever "[[The Scene Was Paved Over|paved over]]" the courtyard — though the forensic scenes were the roof and the woods.

**The digital & surrender record**
- [[Lance Twiggs]], who **cooperated** and whose messages describe Robinson acting — plus **Apple/Discord** records behind the search warrant.
- The surrender chain: [[Mike Mitchell]] and **two county sheriffs**. See [[The Surrender]].

**The process**
- [[Judge Tony Graf]] — who has ruled **against the State** (excluding its video, holding a prosecutor in contempt), the opposite of a rigged bench. See [[Judge Graf Is Bought Off|Judge Graf claim check]].
- Robinson's **elite, aggressive [[The Defense Team|defense team]]** — whose entire job and demonstrated conduct is to attack this evidence. A framed client's own lawyers finding nothing would itself require their complicity. See [[The Defense Is Throwing the Case]].

**The federal layer** *(the one two-edged piece)*
- The **FBI** and **DOJ**. Here the honest picture cuts **both ways**: [[Joe Kent]] (NCTC) *tried to widen* the investigation — so a *unified* cover-up doesn't fit the man who was pushing to open it up, and he'd have been positioned to expose one. **But the FBI/DOJ then blocked him.** Actively shutting down a *widening* — rather than letting a foreign-nexus lead be checked and cleared — is the **one thread that points *toward* a narrowed investigation**, not away from it, and it is the **strongest genuinely-open piece in this entire exercise.** See [[The Investigation Was Limited]].

## Why the exercise matters
Count them: state investigators, independent firearms and DNA examiners, the ATF, the medical examiner, the university, the FBI, a cooperating witness, two sheriffs, an aggressive defense team, and a judge who rules against the prosecution — many with **adverse interests** and no reason to coordinate. A cover-up requires **all** of them to align and **none** to defect.

That is the honest weight of the thing. Individual [[What Is Genuinely Open|open questions]] remain fair to test — the SD-card chain of custody, the unreleased full autopsy, the inconclusive fragment, and — the sharpest of them — **why the FBI blocked [[The Investigation Was Limited|Kent's foreign-nexus review]]** instead of letting it clear. But the leap from "a few loose ends" (even a troubling one) to "*all* of these people are in on it" is what the maximal theory actually asks you to believe.

Each is a fair cross-examination point. **But Day 2 also enlarged the roster a maximal cover-up would need:** the FBI examiner **Amanda Bakker** (in from Quantico), the ATF and FBI labs, Sgt. **Faumuina**'s evidence-response team — and note the DNA that the defense's *own* expert is scrutinizing put **Robinson himself** as the **major (95%/89%) contributor** [08:06:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=29166s), while the examiner **volunteered the limits** rather than overclaim. Loose ends that stay loose are the honest read; a coordinated fabrication that also chose to seat an examiner who hands the defense its best material is not.
