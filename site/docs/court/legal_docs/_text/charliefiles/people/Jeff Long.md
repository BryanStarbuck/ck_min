# Jeff Long

**Jeffrey "Jeff" Long** is the **Chief of the Utah Valley University Police Department** — the command authority for law enforcement on the UVU campus, and therefore a central figure in the dispute over **who was responsible for the rooftops** on September 10, 2025. See [[Roles and Responsibilities at the Scene]] and [[Kirks Security Detail]].

*(His exact tenure/start date is not established in the public reporting — flagged as unconfirmed. One search summary rendered the name "Chief Jeff Shaw"; every primary source — AP, the Salt Lake Tribune, the FBI's Sept. 11 press-conference roster — confirms **Jeffrey/Jeff Long**.)*

## Why he matters
The campus was **UVU PD's jurisdiction**, so overwatch, drones, and the rooftops sit with his department in the responsibility map. Two things put him at the center:

### His day-of admission
At the September 11 press conference era, Long spoke candidly the day of the shooting:
> *"This is a police chief's nightmare… You try to get your bases covered, and, unfortunately, today, we didn't."*
He said **six officers** were on-site (some plainclothes in the crowd), with a UVU officer watching the crowd **from an elevated position.** An **AP investigation** later found UVU PD deployed only **6 of its 23 officers**, **flew no drone** (clear weather; drones available for ~$2,000), ran **no bag checks or metal detectors**, and **did not coordinate** with local agencies that had drones. The shot came from the **[[The Rifle|Losee Center]]** rooftop — accessible, connected to an adjacent building by a railed walkway.

### The "I got you covered" text
Kirk's private security lead, **[[Brian Harpole]]**, says that the Monday before the event he **messaged Chief Long** flagging a **student roof-access point** near the site and asking either that campus police secure the roofs or that he be allowed to post one of his own men there — and that Long's last reply was **"I got you covered."** No officer was posted on that roof. The *Salt Lake Tribune* reported the account under the headline that the **"UVU police chief promised to secure"** the roof.

## Weighing it (balance note)
This is **Harpole's side of a blame dispute**, and it should be read as such:
- Harpole is an **interested source** — a private contractor defending his own team's conduct and a [[Brian Harpole|defamation plaintiff]]. He cites texts, but **Long and UVU have not publicly confirmed or rebutted** the specific wording.
- Critics note the messages, as described, **didn't specifically name the Losee roof**, and that a diligent close-protection detail should have **verified** rooftop coverage itself rather than relying on a text.
- Since his day-of remarks, **Long and UVU's spokesperson have declined** to answer detailed security questions, citing UVU's pending **third-party review** (announced Sept. 22, 2025; no public findings yet). Both sides effectively invite a **records/FOIA** review of the chief's communications to settle it.

So Long is neither exonerated nor indicted on the current record: he made an early, honest admission that "we didn't" get the bases covered, and he is the **other half** of an unresolved *who-was-responsible-for-the-roof* dispute with the private detail. That is a **coordination-and-competence** question — see [[Gaps in the Investigation]] — not, by itself, evidence of anything more.
