# The People of the Case

Spellings marked *(sp?)* are uncertain in the source (OCR/audio) and should be verified before quoting.

## Prosecution witnesses (have testified)
Live testimony at the preliminary hearing, grouped in `people/prosecution witnesses/`. See [[2026-07-06 - Preliminary Hearing Day 1]], [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2]], [[2026-07-08 - Preliminary Hearing Day 3]], [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4]], and [[2026-07-10 - Preliminary Hearing Day 5]].
- **[[Chris Bagley]]** — UVU Police officer (now Spanish Fork PD); **first Day-1 witness**; first on scene, the rooftop screwdriver and "sniper" perch, the courtyard holster.
- **[[David Hull]]** — Utah SBI; **lead case agent**; testified Day 1 into Day 2 — the surrender tip, DMV workup, silver Dodge Challenger, ~16 hours of surveillance, the gait/limp, and the ME summary.
- **[[Jennifer Faumuina]]** — Utah DPS/SBI evidence-response team lead; **Day-2 chain-of-custody witness** for the screwdriver and towel-wrapped rifle; read in the FBI DNA report (Exhibit 31). **Recalled Day 4** for the physical evidence and the townhome/parents' searches — the **Dremel** engraving tool, boxes of **.30-06**, five "safe" casings (one engraved "test shot"), and the ATF tool-mark links — crossed by **Burt**.
- **[[Amanda Bakker]]** — FBI DNA examiner (Quantico); **author of the State's DNA report (Exhibit 31)**. A **government forensic witness**, but — unusually — the **defense called her out of order** (by stipulation, during the State's case) to attack the DNA's reliability, since the State proved the DNA through Faumuina as a conduit rather than calling Bakker live.
- **[[Brian Davis]]** — Utah SBI case agent; **affiant on the probable-cause affidavit**; the **Day-3 witness** (direct by **Lauren Hunt**). Laid the foundation for the **roommate evidence** — [[Lance Twiggs]]'s two recorded interviews (Sept 12, 2025 + April 20, 2026, with counsel), a **buccal DNA swab**, and **use immunity** — and moved the **Discord/text thread (Exhibit 16)** and the digital exhibits toward admission under Rule 1102. **Completed his direct on Day 4** (read the texts into the record, laid the Cellebrite/Discord foundation, identified Robinson) and was **excused with no cross**.

## Defense witnesses
The **defense case-in-chief opened on [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4|Day 4]]** after the State rested, and **rested on [[2026-07-10 - Preliminary Hearing Day 5|Day 5]]** — two forensic-reliability experts, both of whom had done the work *for the State*.
- **[[Samantha Karner]]** — **ATF firearm/tool-mark examiner** who performed the firearm examinations *for the State*; the **defense's first witness** (Day 4), called to establish the **limits** of firearm/tool-mark identification — the murder **bullet is "inconclusive,"** the discipline is **subjective** (AFTE), and its foundational validity is contested (PCAST/NAS; a 20.4% bullet proficiency error). On cross the State rehabilitated her (accredited lab, second-analyst verification, method "accepted by courts across the country").
- **[[Caitlin Oliver]]** — **ATF DNA Section Chief** who performed the DNA examinations *for the State*; the **defense's final witness** (Day 5), the DNA counterpart to Karner. On direct (**Burt**) she established DNA's **limits** — likelihood ratios not source attribution, no "activity" or timing, "a person's DNA may be present when they were not involved in the crime." On **McBride's cross** she confirmed Robinson as the **major contributor** ("at least a trillion times more likely" — the lab's reporting cap). Called over the State's cumulative-evidence objection.
- (The FBI examiner **[[Amanda Bakker]]**, whom the defense called *out of order* during the State's case, is listed with the government witnesses above — she is the government's analyst and Exhibit 31 is the State's evidence.)
