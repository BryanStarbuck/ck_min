# Chris Bagley

![[chris-bagley.png|260]]

**Officer Chris Bagley** — a UVU Police officer at the time of the shooting (now with Spanish Fork PD) — was on duty on September 10, 2025, and was the **first witness** at the Day-1 preliminary hearing.

## Why he matters
- He responded to the scene and went to the **Losee Building rooftop**, where he documented the **prone "sniper pad" impression** and found a **screwdriver** at the shooting position. See [[The Surveillance and the Gait]] and [[Line of Sight and Shot Geometry]].
- His **cross-examination** is the source of several of the defense's Day-1 points: he could not identify the rooftop figure by any physical feature, had described a "long black object" rather than a gun in his report, and his **body-cam battery died** after he reached the roof. See [[They Have the Wrong Person]], [[The Body Camera Conveniently Failed]], and the itemized list in [[2026-07-06 - Preliminary Hearing Day 1|Day 1 Legal Review]].
- He also described the unattributed [[The Unattributed Holster|pistol holster]] in the courtyard.

## What he testified to (for the State)
On direct (Chad Grunander), Bagley laid the on-scene foundation for the State's theory of the shot and the [[The Rifle|rooftop]] as the firing position.
- **First on scene / the shot.** He signed up for an overtime shift for the event and was posted on the south end of the Hall of Flags overlooking Kirk's tent. At about **12:23**, watching Kirk answer a question, he heard a single shot and saw Kirk "lean to the left." He recognized it as a **rifle** shot — "a longer violent bang or crackle" — and pinpointed it coming from **the east** ([01:36:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=5802s), [01:41:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=6071s), [01:41:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=6093s)).
- **The line of sight.** After an officer radioed a suspect in custody, he began preserving the courtyard, saw an empty pistol holster on the grass, and — realizing it was a rifle shot — looked up and saw the **Losee Building had a direct line of sight** to Kirk's tent ([01:41:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=6118s)). See [[The Surveillance and the Gait]].
- **The rooftop and the screwdriver.** He ran up four flights and reached the Losee roof about **12:44**, hopped the guardrail, and found a **red-and-black screwdriver** about 10–15 feet in that "looked out of place" (State's Exhibit 3.1). He did not touch it ([01:43:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=6185s), [01:43:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=6215s)). See [[The Rifle]].
- **The perch.** He described a **prone "sniper" position** in the disturbed gravel and, from a review of campus video at the department, an individual he saw jump off the roof carrying "a long black object" in the left hand ([04:11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15085s)).

## What the defense drew out on cross
Kathy Nester used Bagley to seed several reasonable-doubt threads — and much of what he "recognized" was refined only after the fact.
- **No physical identification.** Reviewing the roof video, Bagley agreed he **could not identify the figure's face, clothing markings, height, shoes, or headwear** — only that the person was "not heavyset" ([04:12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15146s)). See [[They Have the Wrong Person]].
- **"Long black object," not a gun.** He conceded his **report did not describe a gun** — only "an object" — and that he could not actually see a firearm, just a long, covered shape he now believes was the towel-wrapped rifle ([04:14:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15263s)). See [[The Rifle]].
- **The body camera cut out.** His body-cam footage — the only footage he had that day — **ended while he was still on the roof**, before he taped off the scene, because "the battery went dead"; he never restarted it ([04:12:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15141s)).
- **The unattributed holster.** He left the empty pistol holster on the grass, never took custody of it, never had it fingerprinted, and did not know what became of it ([02:53:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=10393s)). See [[The Unattributed Holster]].
- **A gap on the roof.** He admitted he **never reviewed video to confirm no one else was on the roof** between the figure jumping and his own arrival, and found **no spent casing** there ([04:10:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15005s), [04:11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15085s)). See [[Gaps in the Investigation]] and [[Chain of Custody Problems]].
- **No one had the shooter that day.** When he left campus around 9 p.m. and returned the next day, "not that I know of" did anyone yet know who the shooter was ([04:17:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=15445s)).

On the current record this is a preliminary hearing on probable cause; the State's foundation held and the exhibits were admitted, but the defense's cross points remain live for trial.

## Day 2: not recalled — but his screwdriver carried the day's DNA
Bagley was a **Day-1** witness and was **not recalled on Day 2**. His Day-1 find nonetheless anchored the Day-2 forensic case: the **red-and-black screwdriver** he located on the Losee roof is **"item 8, screwdriver from perch"** in the FBI DNA report (Exhibit 31), which read **30 quintillion** times more likely if Twiggs + T. Robinson than an unknown, with Robinson the **major (89%)** contributor [05:11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18701s), [08:06:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=29166s). One caveat the defense preserved: that screwdriver swab was a **partial, degraded profile** [05:40:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=20456s). See [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]] and [[The Casing Match and DNA]].
