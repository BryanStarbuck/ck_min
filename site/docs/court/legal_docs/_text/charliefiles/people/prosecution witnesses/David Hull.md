# David Hull

![[david-hull.png|260]]

**Sgt./Agent David Hull** of the **Utah State Bureau of Investigation (SBI)** was the **lead case agent** in the investigation. (His name is uncertain in the Day-1 hearing audio — "Hull/Hall/Holt" — but the record resolves it as **Hull**.)

## Why he matters
- An experienced homicide investigator who described the multi-agency response as "organized chaos"; he was the **second witness** at the Day-1 preliminary hearing.
- He testified to the **multi-source identity chain** — surrender tip → DMV workup → the co-registered silver Dodge Challenger → ~16 hours of UVU surveillance placing Robinson on campus that day. See [[The Surveillance and the Gait]] and [[They Have the Wrong Person]].
- Because the medical examiner did not testify, **Hull summarized the ME's findings** (gunshot wound to the neck; homicide) as reliable hearsay — a point the defense flagged as a trial-admissibility problem. See [[The Medical Examiner and the Wound]] and [[2026-07-06 - Preliminary Hearing Day 1|Day 1 Legal Review]].

Hull was the State's central narrative witness. He testified across the end of **Day 1** (video `6fq2kCMVMXI`) and the start of **Day 2** (video `y6ofpz6ReuE`), where his direct was completed and Nester cross-examined him.

## What he testified to (for the State)
- **The medical examiner's findings.** With the ME not called, Hull read in the report: manner of death **homicide**, immediate cause a **gunshot wound of the neck** ([06:11:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=22311s)). See [[The Medical Examiner and the Wound]].
- **The surrender tip.** On the evening of the 11th, Washington County relayed that an individual wanted to turn himself in for the UVU shooting; the name was **Tyler Robinson** ([06:31:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=23519s)).
- **The DMV workup and the car.** A records "workup" produced Robinson's driver-license photo, date of birth, and address, plus an associated **silver Dodge Challenger co-registered to Robinson and his mother** ([06:32:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=23554s), [06:33:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=23637s)).
- **The campus movements.** Using that image and vehicle, the team tracked the person he believes is Robinson through UVU surveillance — **on campus about four times** that day, roughly **16 hours** of UVU video in total ([06:34:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=23684s), [06:37:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fq2kCMVMXI&t=23839s)). See [[Robinson on Campus]] and [[The Surveillance and the Gait]].
- **The gait / limp identification.** On Day 2, describing the second return to campus, Hull said it was the same person by the shoes and — notably — that he was **"walking with a gait or a limp"** ([00:51:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=3085s), [00:51:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=3109s)). See [[The Surveillance and the Gait]].

## What the defense drew out on cross
Nester used Hull to keep the investigation's limits and Robinson's own conduct in view.
- **The break came from a surrender, not the forensics.** As of the moment Robinson **voluntarily surrendered**, the team had "a person of interest" but "did not specifically know who the shooter was"; the **roof video itself was not distinguishable** — no face, no clothing ([01:51:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6719s), [01:52:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=6764s)). See [[They Have the Wrong Person]] and [[Gaps in the Investigation]].
- **A peaceful, self-arranged surrender.** No police went to arrest Robinson; he was driven in with his parents and **walked into the Washington County Sheriff's Office** — the surrender facilitated by someone known to the family (Nester noted a former Boy Scout leader). That name was the first Hull had connecting anyone to the case ([02:10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7812s), [02:11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7878s)).
- **A flood of public tips.** Before the surrender the team received "lots and lots" of tips — including about online threats to Kirk and multiple **rival "suspects"** the public thought they recognized ([02:09:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7791s)). See [[Gaps in the Investigation]].
- **Hearsay ME.** The homicide/cause-of-death evidence came in only through Hull's summary of a report he did not author — usable at a probable-cause hearing, but a trial-admissibility flag. See [[The Medical Examiner and the Wound]].

As the record now stands, Hull established the State's identity chain for probable cause; the defense's cross leaves open how much of it rests on the surrender and on video the State's own agent called indistinguishable.
