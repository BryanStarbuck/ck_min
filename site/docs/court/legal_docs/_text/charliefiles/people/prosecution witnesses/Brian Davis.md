# Brian Davis

![[brian-davis.png|260]]

**Agent Brian Davis** is a Utah **State Bureau of Investigation (SBI)** case agent and the **affiant on the probable-cause affidavit** in *State of Utah v. Tyler James Robinson* — a **second State case agent** alongside lead agent [[David Hull]]. Named as a scheduled witness on Day 1 and permitted to remain in the courtroom, he **finally testified on [[2026-07-08 - Preliminary Hearing Day 3|Day 3]]** (July 8, 2026), examined on direct by prosecutor **Lauren Hunt**.

## What he testified to (Day 3)
Davis was the State's foundation witness for the **roommate evidence** and several exhibits:
- **His role.** SBI agent on the investigation; the **affiant** who wrote and signed the probable-cause statement; received the early tip via **Sgt. Elsholz (SBI)** and worked with the evidence-response team led by Sgt. [[Jennifer Faumuina]].
- **[[Lance Twiggs]] — the roommate.** Davis confirmed Twiggs is **"the roommate of Tyler Robinson"** [01:01:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3703s) and laid the foundation for the State's Twiggs evidence:
  - **Two recorded interviews** — one the **morning of Sept. 12, 2025** at the Washington County Sheriff's Office, and a **second on April 20, 2026** at the Utah County Attorney's Office, **with Twiggs's attorney present** [01:03:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3802s)–[01:03:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3832s), [01:05:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3959s).
  - A **buccal swab** taken from Twiggs **to collect his DNA** [00:46:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=2788s), [01:02:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3767s).
  - **Use immunity** given to Twiggs before the second interview [01:05:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3909s).
- **The exhibits.** He was the vehicle for moving the **Discord / text-message thread (Exhibit 16)** [01:13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=4396s) and other items into evidence, and for the State's offer of **Twiggs's recorded statement** under **[[Chain of Custody Problems|Rule 1102]]** (reliable hearsay) — the [[2026-06-22 - Twiggs Subpoena Hearing Transcript|1102 issue having already been briefed and decided]] at the June 22 hearing.

## How to weigh his testimony
Davis is a **State case agent laying foundation** — much of what he relayed is **conduit / hearsay** (the roommate's statements, the recorded interviews, the digital exhibits), permissible at a **preliminary hearing** under Rule 1102 but a **trial** confrontation question the defense pressed hard. He is the State's witness; read his account as the prosecution's foundation, not a neutral finding. See [[2026-07-08 - Preliminary Hearing Day 3|Day 3 Legal Review]], [[Lance Twiggs]], and [[The Discord and Text Messages]].

## Day 4 (July 9) — completed direct; excused with no cross
Davis returned on Day 4 and **finished his direct**. See [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4|Day 4 Legal Review]] and [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4 Transcript|Day 4 Transcript]].

- **Read the [[Lance Twiggs]] texts into the record (Exhibit 16.1).** Among them: **"I had enough of his hatred, some hate can't be negotiated out"** ([02:43:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=9819s)); the rifle **"wrapped in a towel"** in a bush where he "changed outfits" ([02:46:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10009s)); **"delete this exchange"** ([02:48:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10131s)); **"I'm going to turn myself in willingly"** ([02:49:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10166s)); and **"My lawyer should be Doug Terry"** ([03:17:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=11874s)).
- **Cellebrite / Discord foundation.** Davis laid the digital chain: a **Cellebrite** phone extraction and a **Discord subpoena** (username **"tylerikum"**; Twiggs's phone → **RCFL Salt Lake City** → **SIAC** analyst) ([02:52:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10336s)–[02:58:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=10703s)).
- **Moved the digital exhibits (16.1–16.4)** — 16.1 texts, 16.2 Discord, 16.3 note photo, 16.4 FBI images — into evidence. See [[The Discord and Text Messages]].
- **Identified Robinson in court** ([03:18:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=11925s)) and was **excused with NO cross-examination** ([03:19:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=11992s)) — the defense reserved its fire, consistent with reading the roommate/digital evidence as foundation to be contested at trial, not here.
