# Jennifer Faumuina

![[jennifer-faumuina.png|260]]

**Sergeant Jennifer Faumuina** — **Utah Department of Public Safety, State Bureau of Investigation (SBI)** — was the evidence-response team lead who oversaw the crime scenes and physical evidence on September 10, 2025. She was the State's chain-of-custody witness on **Day 2** of the preliminary hearing (video `y6ofpz6ReuE`).

> Note: the prosecutor's preview mis-described her as a "sergeant with the FBI." Under oath she stated she works for **Utah DPS / SBI** ([05:01:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18064s)) — she is a state officer, not FBI.

## Why she matters
She is the link between the physical items and the lab results — the witness who ties the [[The Rifle|rooftop screwdriver and the towel-wrapped rifle]] to the [[The Casing Match and DNA|FBI DNA report]] the State relied on.

## What she testified to (for the State)
Called for a **limited purpose** — chain of custody and to introduce the DNA report — she was then paused so the defense could take its own witness out of order.
- **The screwdriver.** Collected from the Losee rooftop by the State Crime Lab response team, packaged, and — after discussion that evening — placed in **FBI custody** and sent for testing ([05:02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18145s)).
- **The rifle and towel.** In the wooded area northeast of campus, a **long gun wrapped in a dark towel** — a **Mauser 98**, bolt action — was documented in place, rendered safe, and packaged. The **gun went to the ATF lab; the towel and screwdriver went to the FBI lab** ([05:03:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18194s), [05:04:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18268s)). See [[The Rifle]].
- **The DNA report (Exhibit 31).** She identified the FBI report (signed by examiner **Amanda Bakker**) and, over a cumulative objection, read the key results into the record ([05:05:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18352s)):
  - **Item 7 (towel):** male DNA interpreted as from two individuals, one of whom is Twiggs; the result is **~1.7 octillion times more likely** if Twiggs and T. Robinson are contributors than if Twiggs and an unknown person are ([05:11:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18662s)).
  - **Item 8 (screwdriver handle):** male DNA from two individuals, one Twiggs; **~30 quintillion times more likely** with Twiggs and T. Robinson as contributors ([05:11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18701s)).
  - Twiggs's and Robinson's comparison DNA were obtained by **buccal swab** ([05:12:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18745s)).

See [[The Casing Match and DNA]] and [[Lance Twiggs Was More Involved|Twiggs claim check]].

## What the defense drew out on cross
**As the record stands, the defense has not yet cross-examined her.** Michael Burt **reserved cross** until after the defense's own DNA examiner testified, and the State stipulated; Faumuina stepped down still under oath and was asked to leave the courtroom ([05:13:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18801s)). Expect the defense to probe:
- The **multi-agency custody handoffs** — state crime lab → FBI custody → FBI/ATF labs — the kind of transfer chain the defense has flagged elsewhere. See [[Chain of Custody Problems]].
- The reliance on a report **read in by a custodian** rather than the analyst; the reliability of the underlying results was the whole point of calling [[Amanda Bakker]]. See [[The DNA Didn't Match]].

This remains a probable-cause hearing; her chain-of-custody testimony is on the record, but its weight is untested until the reserved cross is taken.

## Day 4 (July 9) — recalled: physical evidence + searches
Faumuina was **recalled on Day 4, still under oath**, this time as the State's physical-evidence and search witness. She walked the court through the rifle's contents, the townhome inventory, and the ATF tool-mark links — and the **reserved cross was in part taken**. See [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4|Day 4 Legal Review]] and [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4 Transcript|Day 4 Transcript]].

**The wooded rifle.** Inside the bolt-action rifle (wrapped in a dark towel) were **one fired .30-06 cartridge case plus three unfired cartridges, all engraved**, headstamped "Remington .30-06 Springfield" ([04:52:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=17524s)). See [[The Rifle]] and [[The Engraved Casings]].

**The townhome (shared with [[Lance Twiggs]]).** The search recovered a **Dremel tool and a bits container**, two Remington **.30-06** boxes, loose **.30-06** cartridge cases, "a handful of long guns or rifles," a revolver, and burned paper ([05:01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18067s)), plus **two gray Converse shoes** (one size 10.5, and another) ([05:01:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18094s)).
- **Exhibit 23 — five .30-06 casings on top of the safe** in the spare bedroom next to Robinson's, one engraved **"test shot"** ([05:04:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18264s)) — the **same caliber** as the round in the rifle ([05:04:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18284s)).
- **But no weapon to match:** asked "Did you find any .30-06 weapons in that house?" she answered **"No."** ([05:05:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18302s)) — a gap the defense will press. See [[The Rifle]].
- A burned note from the kitchen trash went to the FBI as **Exhibit 24** ([05:09:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=18564s)); a **gray Dodge Challenger** was collected at the parents' home ([05:17:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=19029s)).

**ATF tool-mark links (read in through Faumuina).**
- Exhibit 2 cartridge case **"identified as having been fired in the Exhibit 1 rifle"** ([05:28:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=19737s)); tool marks on the rifle's case/cartridges **"consistent with … a Dremel tool"** ([05:29:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=19794s)).
- The **home engraving cutter (Exhibit 15A) was identified as having made the tool marks** on the rifle's cartridge case, cartridges 3–5, and safe casing 18A ([05:40:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=20401s)). See [[The Engraved Casings]] and [[The Casing Match and DNA]]. Note the ATF examiner who did this work, [[Samantha Karner]], separately testified the **murder bullet fragment was inconclusive** — the casing ID is positive, the bullet is not.

**Cross (Burt).** Faumuina conceded she did not personally do the key handling: the towel-and-rifle were **grabbed together by FBI ERT lead Michelle Kamak** — Faumuina only observed ([06:05:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=21916s)); the **Fulton Library backpack, jacket, and gloves were "deemed unrelated"** and their testing **"pulled"** ([06:02:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=21774s)); and she **did not personally observe the rooftop screwdriver seizure** ([06:08:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=22103s)). These feed the [[Chain of Custody Problems]] thread — the person on the stand is not always the person who handled the item.

**The State rested its case-in-chief** at the close of her testimony ([06:14:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=22472s)).
