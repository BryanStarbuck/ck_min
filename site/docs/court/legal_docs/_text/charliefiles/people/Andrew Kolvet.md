# Andrew Kolvet

**Andrew Kolvet** is a Turning Point USA spokesperson and the **executive producer of *The Charlie Kirk Show***. After the assassination he became a primary public spokesman for the organization, and he sits at the center of two of the claims on this site.

## Why he matters
- **The SD cards:** Kolvet publicly explained that the man filmed removing memory cards from the event cameras was a **TPUSA staffer** (the "head of tech") who grabbed the cards so footage "wouldn't be stolen," and that **all footage went to the FBI**. See [[A Staffer Pulled the SD Cards]] and [[The SD Cards]].
- **The leaked texts:** on the Oct 7, 2025 *Charlie Kirk Show*, Kolvet **confirmed the authenticity** of the leaked messages in which Kirk vented about a donor dispute and "leaving the pro-Israel cause," while characterizing Kirk's actual views as "complicated and nuanced." See [[Kirk Was Leaving the Pro-Israel Cause]].
- **The wound:** he is one of the staff-level sources for the (unofficial) [[The Medical Examiner and the Wound|"absolute miracle" / spine]] account of the wound.

Because he is a **Kirk-affiliated spokesman**, his explanations are sourced but not independent — which is why several related questions remain fair to test. See [[What Is Genuinely Open]].
