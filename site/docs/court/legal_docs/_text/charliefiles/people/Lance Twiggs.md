# Lance Twiggs

![[lance-twiggs.png|260]]

**Lance Twiggs** was Tyler Robinson's roommate and romantic partner (a biological male transitioning genders; Robinson referred to him as "Luna"). Twiggs is the **key text-message witness** in the case.

## Why he matters
- He received Robinson's **incriminating messages** — including the note to "look under my keyboard" and the Discord/text thread about the rifle, the engraving, changing clothes, and surrendering. See [[The Discord and Text Messages]].
- He was granted **limited immunity** for a **recorded statement** to investigators (an FBI interview at the Washington County Sheriff's Office). He **cooperated** with police — which is part of why the "[[Robinson Is a Patsy|patsy / didn't act alone]]" theory finds no traction: the associate on the other end of the messages describes **Robinson** carrying out the plan.
- His testimony became a **litigation fight**: the State moved to admit his **recorded interview as reliable hearsay** rather than call him live. On **June 22, 2026**, Judge Graf **quashed the defense subpoena** to compel his in-person testimony and ruled the hearsay admissible; the defense petitioned the Utah Supreme Court. See [[2026-06-22 - Twiggs Subpoena Hearing Transcript|Twiggs Subpoena Hearing Transcript]]. **That recorded statement was ultimately played in open court on Day 4 (July 9, 2026)** — sworn, immunized, and offered *"in lieu of"* live testimony — so his account is now **in the record**, though **still never cross-examined** (see the Day 4 section below).

## Day 2: Twiggs at the center of the DNA
On Day 2, Twiggs surfaced not as a texter but as a **DNA reference** — and became the defense's sharpest reliability point:
- Sgt. Faumuina identified him as **"Lance Twiggs… a roommate of Mr. Tyler Robinson"** and confirmed his DNA was obtained for comparison [05:12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18728s).
- The FBI examiner, **Amanda Bakker**, treated Twiggs as an **"elimination" (assumed) contributor** on both the towel and the screwdriver — meaning she assumed his DNA was present rather than proving it statistically [06:33:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=23632s), [07:21:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26489s). That choice mattered: submitting him as an elimination is part of how she moved Item 7 from **three contributors to two** [06:33:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=23632s).
- **The uninformative likelihood ratio.** The STRmix run for Twiggs returned a **likelihood ratio of 1 — "uninformative"** — yet he was still **reported as a contributor**, on Bakker's visual judgment plus his submission as an elimination [07:20:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26404s)–[07:22:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=26538s). Bakker also knew the **towel was found in a bush at the university, not in Robinson's home** — undercutting the premise for treating Twiggs's DNA as "expected" [06:38:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=23920s). This is a **trial-stage reliability** point, not a prelim-fatal one, but it is a real one. See [[The Casing Match and DNA]] and [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].

## Day 3: the foundation for the roommate evidence
On Day 3, case agent [[Brian Davis]] (the affidavit affiant) laid the full foundation for the State's Twiggs evidence — sharpening the earlier "recorded statement / immunity" picture into specifics:
> **Day 3 (July 8) — two recorded interviews, immunity, and no live cross.** Davis confirmed Twiggs is **"the roommate of Tyler Robinson"** [01:01:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3703s) and described **two recorded interviews** — the **morning of Sept. 12, 2025** (Washington County Sheriff's Office) and a **second on April 20, 2026** (Utah County Attorney's Office, **with Twiggs's attorney present**) [01:03:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3802s), [01:03:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3832s), [01:05:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3959s) — a **buccal DNA swab** [00:46:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=2788s), [01:02:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3767s), and a grant of **use immunity** [01:05:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szsmK2Ul9XY&t=3909s). The State then moved to present **Twiggs's recorded statement as reliable hearsay under Rule 1102** — not live testimony — the 1102 question having **already been briefed and decided on June 22** (subpoena to compel him live quashed). The defense **preserved a Confrontation-Clause objection** (Twiggs is not cross-examinable) alongside a pretrial-publicity/fair-trial argument. See [[2026-07-08 - Preliminary Hearing Day 3|Day 3 Legal Review]] and [[2026-06-22 - Twiggs Subpoena Hearing Transcript|Twiggs Subpoena Hearing Transcript]].

## Day 4: his immunized, sworn recorded statement was played
Day 4 turned the earlier "awaiting / not live" posture into reality: Twiggs's **sworn, recorded statement was played in open court** — never live, never cross-examined, but now squarely **in the record**. He was placed **under oath** with a false-statement admonition, and the statement was offered "**in lieu of your sworn testimony at a preliminary examination**" [02:11:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=7882s), backed by **federal + state immunity** on the record [02:12:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=7934s). The prosecutor cautioned that the recording carries "**large gaps of … black empty silent space**" [02:10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=7810s).

**What his account added for the State:**
- Robinson **borrowed a Dremel** "**to create messages on bullets**," framed to Twiggs as going "**hunting with his family**," about a month before [02:18:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8315s) — tying Twiggs directly to the engraving story (see [[The Engraved Casings]]).
- He **read the "keyboard" note and photographed it** himself [02:20:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8420s). See [[The Keyboard Note]].
- **An in-person confession (Sept. 11):** he asked Robinson whether what he'd said was true; "**he said it was**" and "**wishes he hadn't done it**" [02:26:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8778s). See [[The Confession Is Coerced or Fabricated]].

**What his account gave the defense (read in Robinson's favor):**
- Twiggs "**never heard him talk about Charlie Kirk before specifically**" and recalled **no LGBTQ / gender-identity discussions** [02:30:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=9052s) — cutting against a settled-motive narrative.
- His account shows **no advance knowledge** — he asked Robinson "you weren't the one who did it right" — and he **hedged his FBI-photo identification**: "**I wouldn't say with 100% certainty**" [02:28:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89ZKQIHaJpM&t=8904s).

The core caveat survives Day 4 unchanged: this is an **immunized** account from an **interested** witness, **partly inaudible**, and **not cross-examined**. It is now evidence the magistrate can weigh, but its reliability is exactly what a trial would test.
