# Mike Mitchell

**Mike Mitchell** is a **retired Washington County sheriff's deputy** and a **family friend** of the Robinsons. He is the intermediary through whom Tyler Robinson's **surrender** was arranged.

## Why he matters
- After Robinson's parents recognized him from released images, the family — fearing a violent arrest — reached Mitchell, who **relayed the situation to law enforcement** and helped coordinate a peaceful surrender: he called the Washington County Sheriff at **8:02 p.m. on Sept. 11**, and Robinson was driven in to surrender ~9 p.m. See [[The Surrender]].
- His involvement is why the "[[Robinson Did Not Turn Himself In|he didn't turn himself in]]" claim is rated **misleading**: it was a genuine, **family-brokered voluntary surrender** through a trusted retired lawman — not a walk-in, but not a capture either.

> **Day 2 (light) corroborated the *type* of facilitator — without naming him.** On cross, Agent Hull confirmed the surrender was facilitated by **"someone known to the family"** with a **law-enforcement connection**, and agreed he had "heard at some point" the person was one of Robinson's **former Boy Scout leaders** [02:10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7812s)–[02:10:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=7826s). Hull did **not** name Mitchell and said he did not know the specific connection — so Day 2 fits the family-friend, retired-lawman profile described here **without independently confirming the name**. See [[The Surrender]] and [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].
