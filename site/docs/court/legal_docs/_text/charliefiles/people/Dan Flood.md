# Dan Flood

**Dan Flood** was **Turning Point USA's own in-house head of security** — distinct from the contracted detail run by **[[Brian Harpole]]** — and one of the men physically closest to Charlie Kirk when he was shot on September 10, 2025. See [[Kirks Security Detail]] and [[Roles and Responsibilities at the Scene]].

## Background and role
- A **former U.S. Marine** (service ~1997–2003). He came up through **[[Greg Shaffer]]**'s Shaffer Security Group protecting Kirk (photographed on the detail at events such as CPAC 2020), and is **Harpole's close friend.**
- Around **2022** he moved **in-house to TPUSA**, becoming its head of security (LinkedIn showed him with TPUSA since **Feb. 2022**, later titled *"Head of Risk Strategy"*). In the same handover, Harpole founded **Integrity Security Solutions**, which took the contracted-protection role. So on 9/10 the security picture had **two leads**: Flood (TPUSA-internal) and Harpole (contracted).
- **On the scene:** Flood was among those who moved immediately to Kirk after the shot and helped carry him to the SUV that took him to Timpanogos Regional Hospital.

## The contract history (kept in its lane)
Flood sits inside the same **Shaffer → Flood/Harpole** falling-out documented under [[Greg Shaffer]] and [[Kirks Security Detail]]: Shaffer says the two **"stabbed [him] in the back"** by taking the TPUSA contract after coming up through his firm. This is a **personal/business dispute**, not case evidence — it matters only because it establishes the detail's **U.S. law-enforcement / Marine lineage** (bearing on [[The Security Detail Was Israeli]]) and because it means the security insiders talking publicly are **adverse** to one another.

## The "removed from the TPUSA website" claim — commentary, low weight
A widely-circulated **YouTube commentary video** ("TriggerSmart") built a "what are they hiding?" narrative around the observation that **Flood's biography page was removed from TPUSA's website** (present ~mid-2025, gone by ~October), while peer executives' pages remained. Two cautions:
- The **underlying observation** (a bio page no longer resolving) is **checkable** via the live site or the Wayback Machine — but the video itself sources its facts to **ChatGPT "deep research"** (the narrator says so on camera) and carries **internally inconsistent dates and headcounts.** It is **commentary, not evidence.**
- The **inference** — that a removed staff page implies Flood was "fired," "in legal trouble," or part of a cover-up — is **pure speculation.** Staff pages are edited for many ordinary reasons. Nothing in the court record or mainstream reporting ties Flood to any wrongdoing. This site does **not** adopt that framing; it is noted here only so the claim can be answered rather than amplified. See [[A Hypothetical Cover-Up]] for why "personnel change = concealment" doesn't follow.

## Weighing his account
Like the other security principals, Flood is an **interested party** (TPUSA's own security head, defending an outcome on his watch). He has been **far less public** than Harpole — which itself became grist for online speculation, but is not evidence of anything. Weigh any statement attributed to him accordingly, and prefer the **sworn hearing record** over commentary.
