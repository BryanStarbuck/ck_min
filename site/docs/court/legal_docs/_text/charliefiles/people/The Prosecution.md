# The Prosecution

The State is represented by the **Utah County Attorney's Office**:

- **Jeffrey S. Gray** — Utah County Attorney.
- Deputies **Grunander, McBride, Lauren Hunt, David Sturgill, Christopher Ballard**.

The State is **seeking the death penalty**, charging Robinson with **aggravated murder** and relying on the "grave risk to others" aggravator — the [[2025-09_charging_information-charges.pdf|Information]] alleges the bullet's trajectory passed close to bystanders, including the questioner directly in front of Kirk. See [[The Established Record]].

## On Day 1
The State's foundation lawyering was disciplined — it cleared nearly every hearsay/authentication objection (citing *State v. Purcell*, *State v. Griffin*, Utah R. Evid. 1102(b), and Art. I §12). Its one loss was the **excluded annotated video**, after which it chose to rebuild a clean, unaltered version rather than fight. See [[The Surveillance Video Was Doctored]] and [[2026-07-06 - Preliminary Hearing Day 1|Day 1 Legal Review]].

## On Day 2
The State's forensic phase was carried by **McBride** (chain of custody and the DNA report; the *Ramirez/Drosbeck/Merrill* "the magistrate does not weigh evidence" line, and the Bakker cross) and **Sturgill** (the surveillance-video foundation and Hull's redirect). Two choices stand out:
- **Proving the DNA through a conduit.** Rather than call FBI examiner **Amanda Bakker** in its case-in-chief, the State put its DNA numbers in through **Sgt. Jennifer Faumuina**, who read the FBI report (Exhibit 31) she **did not author** [05:07:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18430s), [05:11:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=18662s) — permissible reliable hearsay at a prelim, but it left the examiner to be examined **first by the defense**, out of order. It also meant Faumuina's own **cross was reserved** and had not happened when Day 2 adjourned. See [[Chain of Custody Problems]].
- **The motive push (5.1).** McBride moved the TPUSA statement for **motive** and the **victim-targeting enhancement**, telegraphing a theory that Robinson's "gender persuasion" put him on the other side of Kirk's views — to be proven "through other witnesses" and Robinson's own statements [03:27:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=12443s). The court **provisionally admitted** it but **reserved** its state-of-mind relevance and **declined to publish** it [03:42:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=13354s) — a partial win at best.

On cross, McBride rebuilt the DNA record — the lab is **ANAB-accredited**, STRmix **validated** and used in ~120 labs, degradation **normal** [07:51:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=28299s), [07:55:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6ofpz6ReuE&t=28512s). See [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].
