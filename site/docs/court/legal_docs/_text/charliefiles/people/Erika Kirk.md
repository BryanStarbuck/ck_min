# Erika Kirk

![[erika-kirk.jpg|220]]
*Erika Kirk, 2025. Photo via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Erika_Kirk,_2025_(cropped).jpg) — reuse under its Commons license.*

**Erika Kirk** is Charlie Kirk's widow. After his death she **succeeded him as head of Turning Point USA**, and in this case she is the designated **victim representative** — she attended the preliminary hearing, facing Tyler Robinson for the first time.

## Where she intersects the record
- As victim representative, her counsel filed the [[2026-01-16 - Victim Invocation of Speedy Trial Rights|invocation of speedy-trial rights]].
- Like any high-profile figure, she has a **personal security detail** whose recommendations she follows (e.g., canceling an April 2026 University of Georgia appearance over "credible threats"). That is unremarkable, and — importantly — **does not tie her to the UVU security firm**. See [[Erika Kirk and the Security Team]] and [[Kirks Security Detail]].
- She has publicly **condemned right-wing antisemitism.** Claims casting her as an "Israeli intelligence" figure are **unsupported by any records or filings.** See [[AI and Deepfake Evidence]].
