# Joe Kent

**Joe Kent** was confirmed in **July 2025** as **Director of the National Counterterrorism Center (NCTC)** (under the Office of the Director of National Intelligence / DNI Tulsi Gabbard). He is the central figure in the "[[The Investigation Was Limited|the investigation was stopped/limited]]" claim.

## Why he matters
- In **October 2025**, Kent reviewed FBI case files to check for a possible **foreign nexus** to the killing. Per the New York Times (Oct 28, 2025), this **alarmed FBI Director Kash Patel**, who felt Kent was overstepping onto FBI turf and potentially interfering with the prosecution — a documented **inter-agency access dispute**.
- Kent later **resigned** (over an unrelated dispute concerning the U.S.–Iran conflict) and, in **March 2026** on Tucker Carlson, said his team was "told to stop" and "blocked" from pursuing the foreign-nexus angle.
- His credibility is **contested**: he was himself under **FBI investigation for alleged leaks** at the time of those claims, and no independent source corroborates that real leads were suppressed. See [[The Investigation Was Limited]].
