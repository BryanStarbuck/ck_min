# Brian Harpole

**Brian Harpole** led the private security detail for Charlie Kirk's tour stop at UVU on September 10, 2025. He is a **former Dallas–Fort Worth-area police officer** (~14 years) and the founder (2023) of **Rockhouse Integrity Group / Integrity Security Solutions**. He is American and, by his own account, has never been to Israel. See [[Kirks Security Detail]].

## Why he matters
- **On the scene:** he covered Kirk's body within seconds and applied pressure to the neck wound. His account is one of the two staff-level sources for the widely-repeated (but not officially confirmed) [[The Medical Examiner and the Wound|spine / no-exit]] detail.
- **He rebutted the Israeli/Mossad-ties theory on the record.** On the **Shawn Ryan Show (#254, Nov. 17, 2025)** he answered *"No"* when asked about Mossad: *"I've been in 39 countries… never been to Israel… We're Americans, period."* See [[The Security Detail Was Israeli]].

## The defamation lawsuit (Harpole v. Owens)
Harpole is now a **plaintiff**. He sued **Candace Owens** for defamation:
- **Court:** U.S. District Court, **Middle District of Tennessee** — **Case No. 3:26-cv-00556**, filed **April 30, 2026**.
- **Defendants:** Candace Owens; **Candace Owens LLC**; **GeorgeTom, Inc.**; and **Mitchell "Mitch" Snow** (a/k/a "Harry Meyers"), the alleged source.
- **The alleged defamation:** statements Owens aired about Harpole's whereabouts on Sept. 9, 2025, based on an account attributed to Mitch Snow; Harpole says they are false. Owens called Snow's account *"one-hundred percent honest."*
- **Harpole's rebuttal:** his **travel records place him in Dallas** during the alleged meeting window.
- **Claims:** defamation, defamation as to professional unfitness, false light, civil conspiracy to defame, aiding-and-abetting defamation; jury demanded.
- **Damages:** $75,000 formally pleaded (the federal diversity threshold), but the complaint says a reasonable award is *"well into the millions,"* plus an injunction to remove the statements.
- **Status:** Owens (represented by First Amendment attorney **Daniel Horwitz**) has called it *"one big PR move"* and raised opinion / public-figure defenses; a motion to dismiss has been reported and Harpole filed an emergency evidence-preservation motion. **No ruling on the merits** as of the latest reporting *(post-filing motion status is reported, not docket-verified)*.

## Interviews & public statements
- **Shawn Ryan Show #254** (Nov. 17, 2025, ~2h48m) — his most detailed account. Full transcript + summary: **[[2025-11-17 - Brian Harpole on the Shawn Ryan Show]]**. Highlights: he says his team flagged a **student roof-access point** to **UVU's Chief Long** the Monday before the event and got the reply **"I got you covered"** (his central blame point against UVU police); he **rejected** the [[The Security Detail Was Israeli|Mossad]] theory on the record (*"39 countries… never been to Israel… We're Americans. Period"*); and he gave a firsthand **medical account** of the wound (no exit; per the OR doctor, the round struck the spine around **C6** and fragmented — see [[The Medical Examiner and the Wound]]).
- **Salt Lake Tribune** (Nov. 19, 2025) — his first extended print interview (*"I heard that bullet"*).

> **On weighing his account.** Harpole is a **partisan, interested source** — he is defending his own team's conduct and is a **defamation plaintiff**. His "I got you covered" claim is **his side of a blame dispute** the UVU chief has not answered on the record; it should be weighed against the hearing testimony that the **Losee roof was never secured** ([[Gaps in the Investigation]]). Notably, in ~2h48m he **never says security was "told to stand down"** — that framing is a later characterization, not his.
