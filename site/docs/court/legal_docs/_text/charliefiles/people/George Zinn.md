# George Zinn

**George Hodgson Zinn** (born ~1953; **71** at the time), of **Salt Lake City**, is the man who — about **two minutes after Kirk was shot** — **falsely confessed to the shooting** at the scene, yelling **"I shot him, now shoot me"** as the crowd fled. He was detained, quickly cleared (he was **unarmed** and knew nothing about the crime), and later admitted he had claimed it **to divert police and let the real shooter escape.** He matters to this project as a **documented, in-this-very-case example of a voluntary false confession** — a real caution against treating any bare *"I did it"* as proof. See [[The Confession Is Coerced or Fabricated]] and [[Robinson Is a Patsy]].

## What he did at the scene
- Roughly two minutes after the shot, amid the panic, Zinn shouted **"I shot him, now shoot me"** (twice, per the charging report). Asked where the gun was, he answered: *"I am not going to tell you where it is. I shot him, now shoot me."*
- Police, mid-manhunt, took him into custody as an apparent confessor. He was **unarmed** and uncooperative.
- After a medical episode he was taken to a hospital, where — questioned by an **FBI agent and a Utah SBI agent** — he **admitted he was not the shooter**, saying he had falsely claimed it to **draw attention away and allow the actual suspect to flee.** Utah County Sheriff **Michael Smith** said Zinn admitted he *"yelled that he was the shooter to allow the actual suspect to flee."*
- DPS Commissioner **Beau Mason** stressed that Zinn — and the other man briefly detained, **Zachariah Qureshi** — were **"not suspects … people of interest,"** and urged the public not to harass them. There is **no evidence Zinn actually colluded** with the shooter; his diversion appears to have been **unilateral** and uninvited. See [[Roles and Responsibilities at the Scene]].

## Who he is — a pattern of inserting himself
- A long-time Utah **"political gadfly"** — the word former Salt Lake County DA **Sim Gill** used for him: a registered Republican who for decades turned up at political events and public forums to heckle or disrupt the speaker.
- A **criminal record dating to 1989** — dozens of petty convictions (criminal trespass, theft of services, interfering with a police officer, disturbing the peace); arrested for disrupting a 2019 Utah Inland Port board meeting, and charged with criminal trespass again in **August 2025**.
- **A prior false claim tied to a mass-casualty attack.** Days after the **April 2013 Boston Marathon bombing**, Zinn emailed the **Salt Lake City Marathon**'s marketing director: *"I was wondering if you needed anybody to help place bombs near the finish line on Saturday???"* He was charged with a **threat of terrorism** (a 2nd-degree felony), called it a "joke," pleaded to a reduced charge, and (after a probation violation) served roughly a year. The through-line — **attaching himself to a high-profile attack** — is the same impulse that played out at UVU.

## What he was charged with
- **Obstruction of justice**, for the false confession / diversion.
- Separately: when officers sought his phone, he warned that it contained child sexual abuse material; a warrant search found **20+ images**, producing **sexual exploitation of a minor** charges.
- **Plea & sentence (late January 2026):** he pleaded **no contest to third-degree obstruction of justice** and **guilty to two counts of sexual exploitation of a minor**, before Judge **Thomas Low** (Utah 4th District). Exposure: **0–5 years** (obstruction) and **up to 15 years** (exploitation), with the **Board of Pardons and Parole** setting the term. *(These CSAM charges are unrelated to the assassination; they surfaced only because of the phone search that followed his false confession.)*

## Why he's in this vault — the false-confession parallel
Zinn is the case's **built-in proof that a confession is not self-proving.** Read for the defense, he powerfully illustrates a point that runs through [[The Confession Is Coerced or Fabricated]] and [[Robinson Is a Patsy]]: **people do falsely claim responsibility for notorious crimes** — to divert attention, to protect the real actor, for notoriety, to self-punish, or because they are not thinking straight (the psychology of **"voluntary false confessions"**). It happened **in this very case, within minutes**, from a man who did not do it.

**But weigh the parallel honestly — it cuts a specific way:**
- Zinn's confession **collapsed under testing almost immediately**: he was **unarmed**, could not say where the gun was, knew **no non-public details**, and **recanted within hours.** That is what an **uncorroborated** false confession looks like.
- [[Tyler Robinson]]'s statements are the **opposite posture**: they are **corroborated** by the rifle, his DNA, the engraved bullets his messages predicted, the surveillance track, and the surrender ([[The Established Record]]; [[Defense Problems]]). A confession that **survives** corroboration-testing is not in Zinn's category.
- So Zinn proves the **general** defense point — a bare *"I did it"* proves nothing on its own — **and** illustrates the very **test** that separates a false confession from a real one. He is a genuine reasonable-doubt data point *and* a reminder of why **corroboration** is what matters. That double edge is exactly why he is worth documenting.

> **What is *not* established.** Some online accounts claim Zinn "confessed to" or was "linked to" other attacks — e.g. the **2017 congressional baseball shooting** (that was **James Hodgkinson**; the claim appears to be a name mix-up with Zinn's middle name, *Hodgson*), a **9/11 "witness"** role (thinly sourced — he reportedly described watching it on TV from a Denver hotel, not as an eyewitness), or "crisis actor" framings. **None have mainstream support**, and this page does not adopt them. His **documented** record — the 2013 marathon email and decades of event-disruption — makes the point without them.
