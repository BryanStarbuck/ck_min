# Terryl Farnsworth

**Terryl Farnsworth** is the CEO of **Visual Impulse**, Turning Point USA's longtime A/V contractor, which ran the **multi-camera rig** at the UVU event. His sworn **Voluntary Statement is Plaintiff's Exhibit 8.1** in this case.

## Why he matters
- His statement describes the **three-angle camera setup**: one camera on Kirk, one on the questioner, and a wide crowd angle — the footage at the heart of the [[A Staffer Pulled the SD Cards|SD-card]] question.
- Because his rig produced the event footage, his account is a primary reference for what those cameras captured and how the cards were handled — the chain of custody that critics say should be tested under oath. See [[The SD Cards]] and [[What Is Genuinely Open]].

*(Note: a differently-named "Corey Farnsworth" appeared as a courthouse spectator in one report — a different person.)*
