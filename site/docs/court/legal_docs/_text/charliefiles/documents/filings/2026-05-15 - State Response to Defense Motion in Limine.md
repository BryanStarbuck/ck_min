# State Response to Defense Motion in Limine

**Filed by:** Utah County Attorney's Office · **Date:** 2026-05-15 · **Type:** response

## What it is
The State's **response** to the defense's Motion in Limine to close portions of the preliminary hearing and to exclude exhibits that would not be admissible at trial.

## What it argues / orders
Opposes closing the hearing and excluding the exhibits, defending **public access** and the admissibility of the State's evidence for the limited **probable-cause** purpose.

## The other side
The defense's motion (filed May 1) sought to close portions of the hearing and bar certain exhibits.

## Why it matters
Part of the pre-hearing motion practice over **closing the prelim and the exhibits** — the court ultimately kept the hearing **open** (consistent with its media-access rulings) while admitting the exhibits with redactions.
