# Joint Status Report on Redactions to the October 24 Sealed Hearing Transcript

**Filed by:** State of Utah and Tyler James Robinson (jointly) · **Date:** 2025-12-04 · **Type:** joint status report

## What it is
A joint status report by the prosecution and the defense proposing specific redactions to the official transcript of the sealed hearing held on October 24, 2025. It was filed to comply with the court's November 10, 2025 order and sets up the December 11, 2025 hearing.

## What it argues / contains
The parties met and conferred and agree that, apart from the specific passages one side or the other wants redacted, the balance of the transcript may be classified as public. The State proposes eleven redactions, all keyed to courtroom or courthouse security measures and one security assessment, and Mr. Robinson does not object. Mr. Robinson proposes eleven redactions of his own, justified as protecting privacy interests, security measures, and fair-trial rights, and the State does not object. Each proposed redaction is identified by transcript page and line with its justification in parentheses, citing the interests in Utah Rule of Judicial Administration 4-202 et seq. Counsel stated they would be prepared to discuss the proposals at the December 11 hearing.

## The other side
The two media groups that requested notice of motions to classify or close records were served with the report, consistent with the court's November 10 order, but are not parties to the joint proposal.

## Why it matters
It shows the prosecution and defense agreeing on a narrow, security- and privacy-based set of redactions rather than sealing the whole October 24 transcript, marking a step toward making most of that sealed hearing record public.
