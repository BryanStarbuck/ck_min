# Ruling on Contempt Order to Show Cause

**Filed by:** Fourth District Court — Judge Tony F. Graf, Jr. · **Date:** 2026-06-26 · **Type:** ruling

## What it is
Judge Graf's **20-page ruling** on the defense's Motion for an Order to Show Cause (Dkt. 504) seeking to hold Deputy Utah County Attorney **Christopher Ballard** in **civil contempt** for violating the court's **Pre-Trial and Trial Publicity Order** (Dkt. 23, amended Dkt. 242), after the **June 12, 2026 evidentiary hearing** at which Ballard and County Attorney Jeffrey Gray testified under oath.

## What it argues / orders
The court reviewed the motion, the Declaration of Counsel (Dkt. 505), the State's Opposition (Dkt. 515), the reply (Dkt. 520), and the State's Objection to the defense's request to remove the death penalty (Dkt. 535) with the defense response (Dkt. 540), applying Utah statutory law, the Rules of Professional Conduct, and persuasive precedent.

## The other side
The State argued its statements were protected responses to publicity the defense's own filings generated.

## Why it matters
This is the **primary document** behind the widely-reported outcome: Graf **held Ballard in civil contempt** for a March statement to TMZ touting the State's evidence, **but kept the death penalty on the table** — declining the defense's requested sanction.
