# Petition for Permission to Appeal the Interlocutory Orders

**Filed by:** Defense — Kathryn Nester, Richard Novak, Michael Burt (for Defendant/Petitioner) · **Date:** 2026-06-11 · **Type:** appellate petition

## What it is
The defense's **282-page Petition for Permission to Appeal from Interlocutory Orders (Expedited Review Requested)** — the appellate filing seeking to appeal, before the preliminary hearing, the trial court's orders **(1) denying the motion in limine to preclude Rule 1102 hearsay** and **(2) quashing the subpoena to compel out-of-state witness [[Lance Twiggs]]** (and granting the State's motion to quash). The bulk of the length is the **appended record**.

## What it argues / orders
That the hearsay-at-preliminary-hearing and compelled-witness questions are **controlling, unsettled issues** warranting immediate appellate review — asking the appellate court to take up whether a bindover can rest on **hearsay** the defendant never gets to confront.

## The other side
The trial court **refused to stay** the prelim pending this petition ([[2026-06-12 - Ruling Denying Stay Pending Interlocutory Appeal|June 12]]), so the July 6–10 hearing went forward with the hearsay admitted; the petition rides on independently.

## Why it matters
This is the **interlocutory appeal to the Utah appellate courts** — the primary document behind the reported "appeal to the Utah Supreme Court over hearsay." Whether Utah's appellate courts bless **hearsay-only bindovers** is a genuinely open, potentially case-shaping question. See [[What Is Genuinely Open]], [[Confessions and Admissions]], [[The Discord and Text Messages]].
