# The Information and Charges

**Filed by:** Utah County Attorney's Office — Jeffrey S. Gray (County Attorney); Deputy County Attorneys Chad E. Grunander, Ryan McBride, Lauren Hunt, David Sturgill, Christopher D. Ballard; probable-cause statement by Brian Davis (State Bureau of Investigation) · **Date:** 2025-09-16 · **Type:** information

## What it is
The formal charging document that opened *State of Utah v. Tyler James Robinson* in the Fourth Judicial District Court, listing seven counts and the aggravators the State intends to prove.

## What it argues / establishes
Count 1 is aggravated murder, a capital felony, on the theory that Robinson knowingly created a great risk of death to individuals other than Kirk — the basis for seeking the death penalty. Count 2 is felony discharge of a firearm causing serious bodily injury. Counts 3 and 4 are obstruction of justice for hiding the rifle and disposing of his clothing. Counts 5 and 6 are witness tampering for directing his roommate to delete text messages and to stay silent. Count 7 is a violent offense in the presence of a child. The Information adds a victim-targeting enhancement (that Robinson selected Kirk over his political expression) and quotes at length from the recovered note ("I had the opportunity to take out Charlie Kirk and I'm going to take it") and the text exchange with his roommate.

**The seven counts by degree:** Count 1 aggravated murder (**capital**); Count 2 felony discharge causing serious injury (**1st-degree**); Counts 3–4 obstruction of justice (**2nd-degree** each); Counts 5–6 witness tampering (**3rd-degree** each); Count 7 violent offense in the presence of a child (**class A misdemeanor**); plus a **victim-targeting enhancement** (§ 76-3-203.14) and a child-presence aggravating factor. *(A July 7, 2026 report described "10 charges" while enumerating these seven — apparently counting the enhancements; verify against any amended Information.)* For the defense's count-by-count posture, see [[Beating Some Charges Losing Others]].
