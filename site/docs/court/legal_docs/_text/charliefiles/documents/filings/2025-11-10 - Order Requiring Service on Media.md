# Order Requiring Service on Media

**Filed by:** Fourth District Court — Judge Tony F. Graf, Jr. · **Date:** 2025-11-10 · **Type:** order

## What it is
An order requiring the parties to serve all motions to close or reclassify court records on the news organizations that have requested notice, and signaling that a closure hearing will be set.

## What it argues / orders
Citing Rule 4-202.02's presumption that court records are public and Rule 4-202.04's service requirement, the court lists the many outlets that requested notice — including The Salt Lake Tribune, The Associated Press, The New York Times, CNN, Fox News, KSL, Deseret News, and the Utah Headliners chapter of the SPJ. It notes the parties' joint motion to transcribe the October 24, 2025 closed hearing and classify the transcript and audio as private, finding that motion subject to the Rule 4-202.04 process. The court ORDERS the parties to serve that motion on the requesting press and to file proof of service, and directs that all future classification motions be served the same way. Because the press requested notice, the court will set a closure hearing at which the press has a right to be heard.

## The other side
The order responds to notice requests from two media coalitions (Dkts. 150 and 164) and expressly says it is not a ruling on the media's pending Motion for Limited Intervention, which awaits further briefing.

## Why it matters
It embeds the press into the record-sealing process and guarantees a contested closure hearing, a central procedural win in the fight over public access to the case file.
