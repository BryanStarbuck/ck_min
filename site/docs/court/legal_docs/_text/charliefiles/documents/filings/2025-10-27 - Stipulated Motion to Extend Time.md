# Stipulated Motion to Extend Time

**Filed by:** Defense — Kathryn N. Nester, with Richard G. Novak and Michael N. Burt (pro hac vice), by stipulation with the State · **Date:** 2025-10-27 · **Type:** stipulated motion

## What it is
A jointly agreed defense motion asking the court to extend the deadlines for Robinson to respond to the State's Motion to Set Aside the October 20, 2025 evidence-preservation order and to reply on his own Motion to Preserve Evidence.

## What it argues / orders
The motion recounts that the defense filed a Motion to Preserve Evidence on October 8, the court granted it October 20, and the State moved on October 22 to set that order aside while also responding to the defense motion. Robinson's response and reply were due October 29. The parties state they are negotiating a proposed stipulated order and need more time; if they cannot agree, they jointly ask that the deadline be extended to November 5, 2025.

## The other side
The motion is stipulated — the State joins in the request — so there is no opposing filing. The underlying dispute concerns the State's motion to set aside the earlier preservation order. The extension was granted the next day.

## Why it matters
It reflects the parties working toward the stipulated evidence-preservation order that would issue in November, part of the broader fight over how physical evidence is preserved and tested.
