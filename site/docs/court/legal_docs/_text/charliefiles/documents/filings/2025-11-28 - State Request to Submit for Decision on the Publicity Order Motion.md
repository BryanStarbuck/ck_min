# State Request to Submit for Decision on the Publicity Order Motion

**Filed by:** State of Utah — Utah County Attorney (Gray / Grunander / Ballard as applicable) · **Date:** 2025-11-28 · **Type:** request

## What it is
A short procedural filing asking the court to take the State's Motion to Amend or Clarify the Pre-Trial and Trial Publicity Order (Dkt. #23) under advisement now that briefing is complete.

## What it argues
The State notes that the motion is fully briefed: it served the motion November 6, 2025 (Dkt. #167), the defense served its opposition November 20, 2025 (Dkt. #190), and the State served its reply November 28, 2025 (Dkt. #201). On that basis the State submits the matter for decision and requests oral argument. The filing also certifies service on defense counsel and on counsel for the interested news media.
