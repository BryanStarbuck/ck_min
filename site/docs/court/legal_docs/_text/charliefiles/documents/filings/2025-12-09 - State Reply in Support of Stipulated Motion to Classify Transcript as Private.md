# State Reply in Support of Stipulated Motion to Classify Transcript as Private

**Filed by:** State of Utah — Utah County Attorney (Gray / Grunander / Ballard as applicable) · **Date:** 2025-12-09 · **Type:** reply

## What it is
The State's reply supporting the parties' stipulated motion to classify the transcript and audio of the October 24, 2025, hearing as private, but only in narrow part.

## What it argues
The State clarifies that it and the defense agree most of the 82-page transcript should be public; they seek to redact only 108 lines — just over four pages — that discuss court security measures. It argues those portions are properly protected under Utah Rule of Judicial Administration 4-202.02(5)(F) (court security plans) and 4-202.02(5)(J)(iv) (records concerning court-facility security), noting the Judicial Council itself may close meetings to discuss deployment of security personnel or devices without keeping minutes. Applying the balancing test in Rule 4-202.04(6), the State contends the public-access interests — educating the public, holding officials accountable — do not apply to security details, while disclosure would undermine the very measures meant to protect everyone in the courtroom. It concludes there is no reasonable alternative to redaction of those specific lines.

## The other side
Answers the prospective Media Intervenors' opposition, which sought to make the transcript public "with limited, if any, redactions."

## Why it matters
Balances public access to a high-profile capital proceeding against courthouse security, defining how narrowly a court record may be sealed.
