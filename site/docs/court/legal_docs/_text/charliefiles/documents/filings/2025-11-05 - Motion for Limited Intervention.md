# Motion for Limited Intervention

**Filed by:** Media intervenors (Parsons Behle — C. Michael Judd, on behalf of The Salt Lake Tribune, the Associated Press, The New York Times, CNN, Fox News, Law & Crime Network, and Utah local outlets) · **Date:** 2025-11-05 · **Type:** motion

## What it is
A motion asking the court to formally recognize the news media as limited-purpose intervenors and to order that any request to close, seal, or reclassify records or proceedings be served on them, with a chance to respond before any closure order is entered.

## What it argues
The press and public hold a constitutional right of access to criminal proceedings, and representatives of the media "must be given an opportunity to be heard on the question of their exclusion." Citing Globe Newspaper, Richmond Newspapers, the Press-Enterprise cases, and Utah precedent, the media contend that Utah courts routinely grant limited intervention so outlets can contest closures. They note the court has already called the media interest "arguably unprecedented in the State of Utah" and flag that both parties had begun chipping away at access — including the State placing arguments to limit media coverage inside a filing classified as Private.

## The other side
The State does not oppose the requested notice-and-service relief. The defense agrees to serve reclassification motions on the media but opposes granting the media any "more formal status," preferring event-specific protocols.

## Why it matters
This is the media's foundational bid to get a seat at the table before records or hearings are sealed, setting up the transparency fight that runs through the rest of the case.
