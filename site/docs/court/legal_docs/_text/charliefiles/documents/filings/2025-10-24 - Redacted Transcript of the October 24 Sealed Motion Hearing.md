# Redacted Transcript of the October 24 Sealed Motion Hearing

**Filed by:** Fourth District Court (court reporter) · **Date:** 2025-10-24 · **Type:** redacted transcript

## What it is
The redacted official transcript of the sealed motion hearing held October 24, 2025 before Judge Tony Graf, prepared by court reporter Phoebe S. Moorhead. Portions agreed to by the parties have been withheld; the remainder was made public.

## What it argues / contains
The hearing was called at 3:00 p.m. with the prosecution team from the Utah County Attorney's Office (Christopher Ballard, Jeffrey Gray, Chad Grunander, Ryan McBride and others), the defense team (Kathryn Nester, Staci Visser and Richard Novak), and Benjamin Van Noy for the Utah County Sheriff's Office. The central subject was the defense motion to let Mr. Robinson appear at all in-person hearings in civilian clothing and without restraints (shackles). The defense opened by asking that one of the defendant's hands be unshackled so he could take notes; the sheriff's counsel objected, calling the request the heart of what the court was being asked to decide. The transcript also covers a related defense motion to strike the State's opposition as an impermissible pleading by a non-party, and discussion of a briefing schedule for other anticipated motions. Judge Graf indicated he would take the weekend to review the memos and issue an oral ruling the following Monday.

## The other side
The prosecution and the Utah County Sheriff's Office opposed unshackling, arguing restraints are appropriate in pretrial hearings; several passages remain redacted for security, privacy, and fair-trial reasons.

## Why it matters
It is the public window into a hearing that was originally closed and sealed, showing how the restraints-and-clothing dispute was argued and setting up Judge Graf's later ruling.
