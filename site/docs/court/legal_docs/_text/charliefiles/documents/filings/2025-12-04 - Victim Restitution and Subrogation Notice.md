# Victim Restitution and Subrogation Notice

**Filed by:** Victim — Utah Office for Victims of Crime, via the Utah County Attorney's Office (Restitution/Subrogation Coordinator Dallas Fawson) · **Date:** 2025-12-04 · **Type:** notice

## What it is
A restitution and subrogation notice from the Utah Office for Victims of Crime (UOVC) documenting benefits paid to victims and asserting the State's subrogated right to recover those amounts. It references a UOVC claim and lists the victims, with a crime date of September 10, 2025.

## What it argues
Under the Utah Office for Victims of Crime Act (Utah Code Ann. § 63M-7-519), the notice states that the State of Utah is subrogated to all of the claimant/victim's rights to receive or recover benefits for economic loss. The claim remains open through September 18, 2028 for additional crime-related expenses.

## The other side
None. This is an administrative notice rather than a contested motion.

## Why it matters
Establishes the State's early restitution/subrogation claim on behalf of crime victims, an amount that could grow and factor into any future restitution order upon conviction or disposition.
