# Docket Sheet November 6 2025

**Filed by:** Fourth Judicial District Court, Provo · **Date:** 2025-11-06 · **Type:** docket sheet

## What it is
A case docket sheet ("Xchange Short Case History") for State of Utah v. Tyler James Robinson, Case No. 251403576, printed November 6, 2025.

## What it argues / contains
It lists the parties (State of Utah as plaintiff; Tyler James Robinson as defendant), defense and prosecution counsel of record, the seven charged offenses beginning with aggravated murder, and the assigned judge, Tony F. Graf Jr. It then provides a chronological list of filings and events from September 15 through November 5, 2025, including the initial appearances, the State's notice of intent to seek the death penalty, the shackling and civilian-clothing motions, the October 24 closed/sealed hearing, and the stipulated motion to order and classify the transcript.
