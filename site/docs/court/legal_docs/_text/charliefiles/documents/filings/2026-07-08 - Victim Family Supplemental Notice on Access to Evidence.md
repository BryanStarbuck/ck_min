# Victim Family Supplemental Notice on Access to Evidence

**Filed by:** Erika Kirk, through counsel Jeffrey A. Neiman · **Date:** 2026-07-08 · **Type:** notice

## What it is
A supplemental notice by the victim family's counsel concerning the **family's access to evidence presented in open court** during the preliminary hearing.

## What it argues / orders
That the **victim's family traveled to bear witness but was not able to view the exhibits** shown in court, and it requests relief ensuring the family can see the evidence presented in open court.

## The other side
The court balanced this against the defense's fair-trial/publicity objections; by **Day 5** it let the family view the enhanced **Exhibit 12.1** video in the courtroom (with the livestream cut to audio-only).

## Why it matters
The backstory to the Day-4/5 fight (Kirk-family counsel **Jeffrey Neiman**) over letting the family see the exhibits and the enhanced surveillance video. See [[The Surveillance and the Gait]].
