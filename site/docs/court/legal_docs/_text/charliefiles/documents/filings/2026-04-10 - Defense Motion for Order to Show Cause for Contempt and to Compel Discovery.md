# Defense Motion for Order to Show Cause for Contempt and to Compel Discovery

**Filed by:** Defense — Kathryn Nester, Richard Novak, Michael Burt · **Date:** 2026-04-10 · **Type:** motion

## What it is
The defense's **67-page Motion for an Order to Show Cause for Contempt of Court** (Docket 504) — the underlying motion that produced the [[2026-06-26 - Ruling on Contempt Order to Show Cause|June 26 contempt ruling]] — coupled with a motion to **compel the Utah County Attorney's Office to produce discovery** of its media/outside communications.

## What it argues / orders
That prosecutors made **extrajudicial statements to the media** about the case in violation of the court's [[2026-06-26 - Ruling on Contempt Order to Show Cause|Pre-Trial and Trial Publicity Order]] (Dkt 23, amended Dkt 242) — attaching the orders as exhibits — and asks the court to require them to **show cause** why they should not be held in **civil contempt**, and to **compel discovery** of the prosecutors' communications.

## The other side
The State filed its [[2026-04-30 - State Opposition to Motion to Show Cause for Contempt|opposition]]; the court **granted** the show-cause request ([[2026-06-01 - Ruling Granting Order to Show Cause and Denying Compel Discovery|June 1]], while denying the compel-discovery piece) and, after the June 12 hearing, held Ballard in contempt but kept the death penalty.

## Why it matters
The **defense side** of the contempt/publicity fight — previously we had only the State's opposition and the court's rulings. See [[Prosecution Problems]], [[Judge Graf Is Bought Off|Judge Graf claim check]].
