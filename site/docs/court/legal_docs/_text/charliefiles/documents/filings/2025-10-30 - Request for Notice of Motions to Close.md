# Request for Notice of Motions to Close

**Filed by:** Media intervenors (Parr Brown — Jeffrey J. Hunt, David C. Reymann, Carissa A. Uresk, for the Deseret News, KSL entities, KUTV 2News, Fox13, Utah News Dispatch, and the Utah SPJ chapter) · **Date:** 2025-10-30 · **Type:** request

## What it is
An early, standalone request under Utah Rule of Judicial Administration 4-202.04(4) asking to be served with, and heard on, any motion to seal, close, or limit public or press access to any proceeding or public record in the case.

## What it argues
Invoking Rule 4-202.04(4), the News Media ask for notice of and service of any petition or motion to seal, close, or limit access — including limits on electronic media coverage — and for the opportunity to make argument before any closure is ordered. They cite Utah and federal authority (Bullock, Kearns-Tribune, Globe Newspaper) establishing that the media have standing to contest closure and must be permitted to participate in closure hearings "to the maximum extent possible."

## Why it matters
Filed by the Parr Brown media coalition, this is the earliest formal move to lock in the press's right to advance notice before anything is sealed, laying groundwork that the court's later November 13 order would adopt.
