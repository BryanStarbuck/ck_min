# Exhibit List for the February 3 Disqualification Hearing

**Filed by:** Fourth District Court — Judge Tony F. Graf, Jr. (clerk's exhibit list) · **Date:** 2026-02-03 · **Type:** exhibit list

## What it is
The court's **exhibit list** from the **February 3, 2026 disqualification hearing (part two)** — the evidentiary hearing on the defense's motion to disqualify the Utah County Attorney's Office — recording the exhibits offered, received, and referenced.

## What it argues / orders
A clerk's record of the exhibits at that hearing (e.g., a defense **Rule 1102 statement** marked and received), with the parties' attorneys listed (Nester/Visser for the defense; Gray, Grunander, McBride, Ballard for the State).

## Why it matters
The exhibit record of the **Feb 3 disqualification hearing** — cross-reference to the [[2026-02-03 - Disqualification Hearing, Part Two Transcript|Part Two transcript]] and the eventual **Feb 24** denial of the disqualification motion.
