# State Objection to Defense Supplemental Discovery Request

**Filed by:** Utah County Attorney's Office · **Date:** 2026-04-10 · **Type:** objection

## What it is
The State's **objection** to the defense's supplemental request for discovery filed **April 3, 2026**.

## What it argues / orders
Objects to the scope of the defense's discovery demands — particularly requests for the prosecutors' internal and media communications — as improper or not discoverable.

## The other side
The defense's April 3 request sought, among other things, a comprehensive list of the County Attorney's Office contacts (tied to the publicity/contempt dispute).

## Why it matters
Opens the **discovery dispute** that runs through the spring/summer — much of it aimed at the prosecutors' **media contacts** underlying the contempt fight. See [[Prosecution Problems]].
