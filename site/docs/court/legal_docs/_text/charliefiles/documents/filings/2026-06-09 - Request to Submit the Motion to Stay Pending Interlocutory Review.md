# Request to Submit the Motion to Stay Pending Interlocutory Review

**Filed by:** Defense — Kathryn Nester, Richard Novak, Michael Burt · **Date:** 2026-06-09 · **Type:** request to submit

## What it is
A **request to submit for decision** the defense's **Motion to Stay Pending Interlocutory Review** (Dkt 561) — the motion to halt the July 6 prelim pending the Utah appellate court's review of the hearsay ruling.

## What it argues / orders
Asks the court to rule on the stay motion promptly.

## The other side
Graf **denied** the stay on [[2026-06-12 - Ruling Denying Stay Pending Interlocutory Appeal|June 12]].

## Why it matters
Part of the **interlocutory-appeal** track on the hearsay issue. See [[What Is Genuinely Open]].
