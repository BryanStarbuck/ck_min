# Order Granting Extension of Time

**Filed by:** Fourth District Court — Judge Tony F. Graf, Jr. · **Date:** 2025-10-28 · **Type:** order

## What it is
A short order granting the parties' stipulated motion to extend the defense's briefing deadlines on the competing evidence-preservation motions.

## What it argues / orders
The court finds the matter comes before it on a stipulated motion to extend time and GRANTS it, setting a new deadline of November 5, 2025 for the defense to file its response to the State's Motion to Set Aside the October 20, 2025 Order Granting Motion to Preserve Evidence, and its reply to the State's response to the defense's own Motion to Preserve Evidence.

## The other side
None — the underlying motion was stipulated, so there was no opposition to the extension.

## Why it matters
A routine scheduling order, but it kept the evidence-preservation dispute on track toward the stipulated order the court would enter on November 4, 2025 governing how evidence in the case is preserved and tested.
