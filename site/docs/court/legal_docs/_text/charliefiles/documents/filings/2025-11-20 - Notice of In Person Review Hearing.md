# Notice of In Person Review Hearing

**Filed by:** Fourth District Court — Deputy Clerk Treena Hansen, for Judge Tony F. Graf, Jr. · **Date:** 2025-11-20 · **Type:** notice

## What it is
A clerk's notice scheduling an in-person review hearing in the Robinson case.

## What it argues / orders
The notice sets a closed review hearing for December 11, 2025 at 12:00 p.m. in Courtroom 4B on the fourth floor of the Fourth District Court in Provo, before Judge Tony F. Graf, Jr. It states the hearing will be conducted in person and reminds recipients, under UCJA Rule 4-401.02, that proceedings may not be recorded, photographed, or transmitted, with violations punishable as contempt. It also offers interpreter and accessibility services. The certificate of notification shows service on the full set of prosecution, defense, and media-intervenor counsel.
