# Defense Reply Supporting the Motion to Vacate or Continue the Preliminary Hearing

**Filed by:** Defense — Kathryn Nester, Richard Novak, Michael Burt · **Date:** 2026-04-10 · **Type:** reply

## What it is
The defense's **reply in support of its Motion to Vacate or Continue the Preliminary Hearing Pending Discovery** (the ballistics/discovery motion, Doc 466), answering the State's opposition.

## What it argues / orders
That the preliminary hearing should be **continued** until the defense receives outstanding discovery (the ATF casefile, the FBI STRmix DNA analysis, and related ballistics materials) — the fight that publicized the **"inconclusive bullet"** result.

## The other side
The State opposed, arguing the defense had what it needed for a probable-cause hearing; the court **denied** the continuance and the hearing went forward July 6.

## Why it matters
The **defense reply** in the ballistics/continuance fight. See [[The Inconclusive Bullet]], [[2026-03-27 - Motion to Vacate or Continue the Preliminary Hearing|the underlying motion]].
