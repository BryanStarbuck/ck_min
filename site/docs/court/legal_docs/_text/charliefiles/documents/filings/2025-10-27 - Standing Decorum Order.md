# Standing Decorum Order

**Filed by:** Fourth District Court — Judge Tony F. Graf, Jr. · **Date:** 2025-10-27 · **Type:** standing order

## What it is
A six-page standing order, effective October 30, 2025, setting rules of conduct for media representatives and members of the public attending proceedings in the Robinson case in Courtroom 4B.

## What it argues / orders
Entered under the court's inherent authority and UCJA rules 3-414 and 4-401.01, the order governs bags, face coverings, weapons, seating (reserved front rows for the victim's family and for the defendant's family), and spectator behavior. It bars audible comments, gestures, and pins or clothing expressing support for or against anyone in the case. It restricts portable electronic devices and prohibits recording, broadcasting, or photography unless the court authorizes Electronic Media Coverage under the rules. It sets pooling procedures for approved media, and bars recording or photographing Robinson while he enters, exits, or stands, or of his physical restraints. Violations may bring removal or contempt sanctions of up to 30 days in jail and a $1,000 fine per offense.
