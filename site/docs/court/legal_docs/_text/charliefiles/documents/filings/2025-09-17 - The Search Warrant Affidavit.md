# The Search Warrant Affidavit

**Filed by:** State of Utah, sworn by Investigator Justin Bowen (State Bureau of Investigation, Major Crimes Unit); reviewed by David Sturgill (Utah County DA Office) · **Date:** 2025-09-17 · **Type:** affidavit (search warrant No. 3217131)

## What it is
A sworn application for a warrant to search two Apple iCloud accounts tied to roommate Lance Twiggs (lancetwiggs@icloud.com and memesinsteadofdreams@gmail.com) for evidence of aggravated murder, covering August 27 to September 12, 2025.

## What it argues / establishes
Beyond restating the surveillance, gait, rifle, and casing findings, this affidavit adds detail found nowhere else in the charging packet. It records that Dr. Guajardo of the Office of the Medical Examiner certified Kirk's cause of death as homicide by gunshot wound, with Sgt. Barrett attending the autopsy. It describes a possible bulge in the suspect's waistband on stairwell footage, and touch-DNA, fingerprint, and smeared-palm-print collection at the rooftop drop point. It reproduces long verbatim message threads between Robinson and Twiggs, the group Discord surrender post from user "tylor/Ikum," and the full handwritten "Luna" letter ("I had the opportunity to take out Charlie Kirk, and I took it"). It requests sealing until March 17, 2026, and 90-day non-disclosure by Apple.
