# Victim Family Statement for Speedy Bindover

**Filed by:** Erika Kirk and Charlie Kirk's parents, through counsel Jeffrey A. Neiman (Parkinson Benson Potter) · **Date:** ~2026-07-11 (post-hearing) · **Type:** victim statement

> **Date note:** the document's certificate of delivery bears a **July 8** date and refers to a "SUPPLEMENTAL NOTICE," apparently **copied from the family's same-day [[2026-07-08 - Victim Family Supplemental Notice on Access to Evidence|access-to-evidence notice]]**. Its text, however, states the preliminary hearing **"has now concluded"** (which happened July 10), so the operative filing is **post-hearing** — dated here ~July 11.

## What it is
A statement filed by **Erika Kirk** (Charlie Kirk's widow and recognized victim representative) together with **Charlie Kirk's parents**, just after the five-day preliminary hearing concluded, urging the court to **promptly enter its probable-cause (bindover) determination**.

## What it argues / orders
That the evidence at the five-day hearing was **overwhelming** and a prompt ruling is imperative — nearly a year since the death, ten months since arrest, the hearing now concluded. Robinson is entitled to a fair trial but **"not... to cause undue delay,"** and the **victim's family has an independent statutory right to a speedy disposition** under **Utah Code § 77-38-7(2)**. It **urges the court to rule no later than September 1, 2026.**

## The other side
The defense had just secured a **briefing schedule** (State memo July 28, defense Aug 11, reply Aug 18) with oral argument Sept 1, arguing it needed the hearing transcript first; Robinson **waived speedy trial**.

## Why it matters
The victim family is publicly **pressing for speed** against the defense's briefing schedule — directly relevant to whether Graf's ~2-month bindover timeline is "slow" (it is normal — see [[The Death Penalty in Utah]]).
