# Defense Reply Opposing Media on Classifying October 24 Transcript

**Filed by:** Defense — counsel for Tyler James Robinson (Kathryn N. Nester, Richard G. Novak, Michael N. Burt, Staci Visser) · **Date:** 2025-12-09 · **Type:** reply

## What it is
Robinson's reply to the media's memorandum opposing the stipulated motion to classify as private the transcript and audio of the closed October 24, 2025 hearing.

## What it argues
The reply clarifies the procedural history rather than mounting a broad access argument. It states Robinson did not request that the October 24 hearing be closed; the Court closed it on its own to safeguard court-security information and Robinson's rights concerning restraints, which was within the Court's discretion. Because the hearing and its records were sealed before any media entity had requested notice (the first such request came October 30, 2025), the media were not entitled to notice or to be heard on it under Utah Rule of Judicial Administration 4-202.04. The defense explains its motion to classify was meant to expedite obtaining and unsealing the transcript so the parties could review it and decide whether to seek an interlocutory appeal.

## The other side
The media entities filed a memorandum (December 8, 2025) opposing the stipulated motion to classify the transcript as private; the State filed a reply supporting the stipulated motion.

## Why it matters
Bears on access to records of a sealed hearing and the procedural limits of media notice rights, part of the broader fight over transparency in the case.
