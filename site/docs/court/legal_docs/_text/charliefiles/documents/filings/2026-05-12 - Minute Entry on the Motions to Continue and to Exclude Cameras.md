# Minute Entry on the Motions to Continue and to Exclude Cameras

**Filed by:** Fourth District Court — Judge Tony F. Graf, Jr. · **Date:** 2026-05-12 · **Type:** minute entry

## What it is
A **court minute entry** recording the court's handling of discovery status and its oral rulings on the defense's **motion to continue the preliminary hearing** and **motion to exclude cameras** — both **denied**.

## What it argues / orders
The court inquires as to discovery (McBride gives status), then **denies** the motion to continue the preliminary hearing and, on findings, **denies** the motion to exclude electronic media.

## Why it matters
The **docket record** of the court denying the continuance and the camera-exclusion motion (the written camera ruling issued [[2026-05-08 - Ruling Denying Motion to Exclude Cameras|May 8]]).
