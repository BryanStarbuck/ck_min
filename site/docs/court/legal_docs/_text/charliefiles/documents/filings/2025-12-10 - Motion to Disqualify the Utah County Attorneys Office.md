# Motion to Disqualify the Utah County Attorneys Office

**Filed by:** Defense (Nester / Burt / Novak / Visser) · **Date:** 2025-12-10 · **Type:** Motion

## What it is
A defense motion asking the court to disqualify the entire Utah County Attorney's Office (UCAO) from prosecuting the case, based on a claimed conflict of interest.

## What it argues
The defense argues that a supervising prosecutor in the UCAO has a family member who was present at Utah Valley University when Charlie Kirk was shot — placing that prosecutor's relative among the potential witnesses/victims. According to the motion, the relative texted the prosecutor in real time during and after the shooting, and the prosecutor discussed the situation with County Attorney Jeffrey Gray and shared the exchange with the prosecution team. The defense contends this is a "concurrent conflict of interest" under Utah Rules of Professional Conduct 1.7, 1.11, and 3.8, endangering Robinson's due-process rights in a capital case. It faults the UCAO for using no screening protocol after learning of the conflict and for letting the information spread across the assigned team. It asks the court to find a conflict and disqualify the office.

## The other side
The State's later opposition (filed Jan. 5, 2026) contended the relative will not be a witness and that the circumstance does not preclude the office from prosecuting; the defense's reply requested an evidentiary hearing on those points.

## Why it matters
It goes to whether the prosecuting office is impartial — a foundational fairness question in a death-penalty case and a recurring theme in coverage of the prosecution.
