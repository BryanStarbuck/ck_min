# Defense Reply on the Contempt and Compel-Discovery Motion

**Filed by:** Defense — Kathryn Nester, Richard Novak, Michael Burt · **Date:** 2026-05-07 · **Type:** reply

## What it is
The defense's **reply** to the State's opposition to its motion for an order to show cause for contempt and to compel discovery.

## What it argues / orders
Answers the State's defenses — pressing that the prosecutors' media statements were prohibited by the publicity order and that discovery of their communications should be compelled.

## The other side
The State's [[2026-04-30 - State Opposition to Motion to Show Cause for Contempt|opposition]] argued its statements were protected responses to publicity the defense generated.

## Why it matters
Completes the **contempt-motion briefing** the June 26 ruling resolved. See [[Prosecution Problems]].
