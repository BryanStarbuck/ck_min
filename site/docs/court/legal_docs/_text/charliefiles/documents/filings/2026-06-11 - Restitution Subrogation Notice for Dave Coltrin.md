# Restitution Subrogation Notice for Dave Coltrin

**Filed by:** Utah Office for Victims of Crime / Utah County Attorney · **Date:** 2026-06-11 · **Type:** restitution notice

## What it is
A **restitution / subrogation notice** from the Utah Office for Victims of Crime (UOVC) for a **second named victim, Dave Coltrin**, whose losses from the September 10, 2025 UVU shooting were reimbursed — **mental-health therapy ($230.00)** and **medical ($62.50)** — with the State subrogated to his recovery rights (Utah Code § 63M-7-519). Crime location: 800 W University Pkwy, Orem.

## What it argues / orders
Notes the UOVC claim (No. 1113638) **remains open through 9/18/2028** for further crime-related expenses, and the State is subrogated to the victim's recovery.

## The other side
A separate restitution/subrogation notice was filed earlier (Dec 4, 2025).

## Why it matters
Documents a **second identified victim (Coltrin)** beyond Kirk — a person who received victim-services reimbursement for the shooting — and shows restitution/subrogation attaching to the aggravated-murder and felony-discharge counts. See [[Charges and Elements]].
