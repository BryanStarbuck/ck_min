# Defense Notice of Objection to Exhibit 4.1

**Filed by:** Defense — Kathryn Nester, Richard Novak, Michael Burt · **Date:** 2026-01-27 · **Type:** objection

## What it is
The defense's **notice of objection** to the admission and publication of the State's proposed **Exhibit 4.1** in connection with the **evidentiary hearing on the motion to disqualify** the Utah County Attorney's Office, with a memorandum of points and authorities.

## What it argues / orders
Objects to admitting/publishing Exhibit 4.1 during or in connection with the disqualification hearing, on the grounds set out in the memorandum.

## Why it matters
A procedural marker in the **disqualification fight** (Jan–Feb 2026); cross-references the [[2026-02-03 - Disqualification Hearing, Part Two Transcript|Feb 3 hearing]] and the Feb 24 denial of the motion.
