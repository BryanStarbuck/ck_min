# Attorney General Notice Not to Respond to Constitutional Challenge

**Filed by:** Utah Attorney General (Intervenor) — Derek Brown / Jason E. Greene, Gregory W. Marsh, Ginger Jarvis · **Date:** 2026-06-12 · **Type:** notice

## What it is
A notice from the **Utah Attorney General**, appearing as intervenor, that it will **not further respond** to the defense's **constitutional challenge** — the Motion in Limine to preclude Art. I § 12 / Rule 1102 hearsay (Dkt. 525), of which the AG was notified May 8, 2026 under Utah R. Crim. P. 12(i).

## What it argues / orders
Having reviewed the State's opposition (Dkt. 557), the AG **"determines that a further response... is unnecessary"** (Utah R. Crim. P. 12(i)(4)).

## The other side
The County Attorney's Office defended the challenged rule; the court denied the defense motion (see the June 23 ruling).

## Why it matters
Confirms the defense's Rule 1102 motion **raised a constitutional challenge** significant enough to trigger **AG notice** — and that the AG declined to weigh in, leaving the County Attorney to carry it.
