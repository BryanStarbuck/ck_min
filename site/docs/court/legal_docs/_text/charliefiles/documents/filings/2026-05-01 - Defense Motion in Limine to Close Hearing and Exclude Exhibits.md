# Defense Motion in Limine to Close Hearing and Exclude Exhibits

**Filed by:** Defense — Kathryn Nester, Richard Novak, Michael Burt, Staci Visser · **Date:** 2026-05-01 · **Type:** motion in limine

## What it is
The defense's **Motion in Limine** to (1) **close those portions of the preliminary hearing** where sensitive/prejudicial evidence would be shown and (2) **exclude exhibits that would not be admissible at trial**, with a memorandum of points and authorities.

## What it argues / orders
That publicly displaying inflammatory or trial-inadmissible material at the prelim would prejudice the jury pool and Robinson's fair-trial rights, so those portions should be closed and the exhibits excluded.

## The other side
The State opposed (its **May 15** response), defending public access; the court largely **kept the hearing open** and admitted the exhibits with **redactions** to protect the jury pool.

## Why it matters
The pre-hearing fight over **closing the prelim and excluding exhibits** — resolved mostly against the defense, of a piece with the publicity and camera rulings. See [[Prosecution Problems]].
