# Defense Reply Supporting the Motion to Close and Seal Portions of the Hearing

**Filed by:** Defense — Kathryn Nester, Richard Novak, Michael Burt · **Date:** 2026-05-18 · **Type:** reply

## What it is
The defense's **reply / supplement** in support of its May 1 Motion in Limine to **close portions of the preliminary hearing and seal exhibits** (Dkt 513), responding to the State's and the Media's oppositions (Dkt 546 and the State's objection, both May 15).

## What it argues / orders
That publicly displaying prejudicial or trial-inadmissible material at the prelim would taint the jury pool, so those portions should be **closed** and the exhibits **sealed** — over the media's and State's public-access objections.

## The other side
The State and the **media intervenors** opposed, defending public access; the court largely kept the hearing **open** and admitted the exhibits with **redactions**.

## Why it matters
The defense's push to **close/seal** — resolved mostly against it, consistent with the publicity and camera rulings. See [[Prosecution Problems]].
