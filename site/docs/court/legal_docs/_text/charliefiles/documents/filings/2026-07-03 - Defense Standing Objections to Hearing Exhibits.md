# Defense Standing Objections to Hearing Exhibits

**Filed by:** Defense — Kathryn Nester, Richard Novak, Michael Burt, Staci Visser · **Date:** 2026-07-03 · **Type:** objection

## What it is
The defense's **standing (blanket) objections to the State's preliminary-hearing exhibits**, filed days before the hearing so the objections need not be re-argued exhibit by exhibit.

## What it argues / orders
Preserves the defense's objections — **authentication, Confrontation Clause, Rule 1102, foundation, relevance, and pretrial publicity** — to the State's exhibits (the digital confession, the Twiggs statement, the note, the forensic reports).

## The other side
The court **overruled** these standing objections for probable-cause purposes at the hearing while noting they were preserved.

## Why it matters
This is the document behind the court's repeated references to the **"standing objections"** (e.g., Day 4, Novak: "we have no further objections beyond those the court is familiar with") — the mechanism preserving the defense's trial-stage challenges.
