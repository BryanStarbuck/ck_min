# Ruling Denying Stay Pending Interlocutory Appeal

**Filed by:** Fourth District Court — Judge Tony F. Graf, Jr. · **Date:** 2026-06-12 · **Type:** ruling

## What it is
Judge Graf's ruling **DENYING** the defense's Motion to Stay (Dkt. 561) all proceedings — including the **preliminary hearing scheduled for the week of July 6, 2026** — pending the **Utah Supreme Court's** disposition of Robinson's **Petition for Permission to Appeal from an Interlocutory Order** (the hearsay ruling). State opposed (Dkt. 575); defense replied (Dkt. 579).

## What it argues / orders
The court declined to halt the capital case's preliminary hearing while the appellate petition was pending, keeping the July 6 hearing on track.

## The other side
The defense sought the stay to get appellate review of the **Rule 1102 hearsay** question before the hearsay came in at the prelim.

## Why it matters
This kept the **preliminary hearing on schedule** despite the defense's Utah Supreme Court petition; the **hearsay/Rule 1102 challenge remains preserved for appeal** — a live open issue. See [[What Is Genuinely Open]].
