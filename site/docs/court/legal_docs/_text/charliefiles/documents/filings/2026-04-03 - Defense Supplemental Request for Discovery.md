# Defense Supplemental Request for Discovery

**Filed by:** Defense — Kathryn Nester, Richard Novak, Michael Burt · **Date:** 2026-04-03 · **Type:** discovery request

## What it is
The defense's (first) **supplemental request for discovery** to the Utah County Attorney's Office.

## What it argues / orders
Requests specific documents and information — including a **comprehensive list of the UCAO's written, in-person, virtual, and telephonic contacts** (references dating to Sept 16 and Dec 15, 2025) — much of it directed at the prosecutors' outside communications.

## The other side
The State **objected** (April 10), and the dispute continued with a second request (June 19) and the State's July 2 response.

## Why it matters
The **opening move** in the discovery dispute — aimed largely at the prosecutors' media contacts, which feeds the [[Prosecution Problems|publicity/contempt]] issues.
