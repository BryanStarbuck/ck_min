# Ruling Denying Motion to Exclude Cameras

**Filed by:** Fourth District Court — Judge Tony F. Graf, Jr. · **Date:** 2026-05-08 · **Type:** ruling

## What it is
Judge Graf's ruling **DENYING** the defense's Motion (Dkt. 305) to **categorically ban still photographers, TV cameras, and microphones** from the courtroom, and issuing a **Standing Order on Electronic Media Coverage Requests**. An evidentiary hearing was held April 17, 2026 with defense experts **Dr. Bryan Edelman** (trial consultant) and **Dr. Christine Ruva** (cognitive psychologist).

## What it argues / orders
Because the defense **had not shown that a categorical ban** on electronic media coverage for all proceedings is allowed by Utah law, the motion is denied; the court sets a framework for handling media-coverage requests proceeding by proceeding.

## The other side
Edelman's **survey of 200 Utah County residents** found **99% recognize the case** and **64% believe Robinson is guilty** based on media coverage — the defense's evidence of prejudicial publicity.

## Why it matters
The camera/media-access ruling that put cameras in the courtroom for the case. The **64%-believe-guilty** survey is significant for the **fair-trial / change-of-venue** analysis and feeds the pretrial-publicity fight. See [[Prosecution Problems]].
