# Redacted Affidavit of Probable Cause

**Filed by:** State of Utah, sworn by Officer Brian Davis (Utah Attorney General / State Bureau of Investigation) · **Date:** 2025-09-12 · **Type:** affidavit (redacted copy)

## What it is
A redacted release copy of the booking-stage [[2025-09-12_charging_probable-cause-affidavit.pdf|Affidavit of Probable Cause]] supporting Robinson's arrest for aggravated murder, felony discharge of a firearm, and obstruction of justice.

## What it argues / establishes
The substance mirrors the probable-cause affidavit: campus surveillance tracked a suspect with a stiff right-leg gait from his 11:50 a.m. arrival to a prone rooftop shooting position on the Losee Center, then in flight after Kirk was shot at roughly 12:23 p.m. Investigators recovered a Mauser Model 98 .30-06 rifle with a scope, wrapped in a towel; engraved cartridges (one fired, three unfired) bearing meme and political phrases; and a Converse-style shoe impression at the rooftop edge. The document reflects the same account of the family-relayed confession, the roommate's Discord and text messages, and Robinson arriving on campus in a grey Dodge Challenger. As a redacted version, portions of identifying or sensitive detail are withheld from public view relative to the internal filing.
