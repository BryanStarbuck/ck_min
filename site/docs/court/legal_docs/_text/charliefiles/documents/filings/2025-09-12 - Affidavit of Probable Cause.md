# Affidavit of Probable Cause

**Filed by:** State of Utah, sworn by Officer Brian Davis (Utah Attorney General / State Bureau of Investigation) · **Date:** 2025-09-12 · **Type:** affidavit

## What it is
The booking-stage sworn statement filed at Robinson's arrest, laying out the police theory to justify holding him on three initial offenses: aggravated murder, felony discharge of a firearm causing serious bodily injury, and first-degree obstruction of justice.

## What it argues / establishes
It states that on September 10, 2025, Charlie Kirk was shot in the neck at approximately 12:23 p.m. while speaking under a canopy at Utah Valley University, and pronounced dead at Timpanogos Regional Hospital. Campus surveillance tracked a suspect — marked by a stiff, slow, right-leg gait — from 11:50 a.m. arrival, through the Losee Center, to a prone rooftop position, then in flight after the shot (the limp notably absent while sprinting). Investigators recovered a Mauser Model 98 .30-06 bolt-action rifle with a scope, wrapped in a towel in trees along the escape route, plus engraved cartridges (one fired, three unfired) and a Converse-style shoe impression. A family member reported Robinson had confessed; his roommate showed investigators Discord and text messages about retrieving and hiding the rifle, engraving bullets, and changing clothes.
