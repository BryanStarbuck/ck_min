# Ruling Granting Order to Show Cause and Denying Compel Discovery

**Filed by:** Fourth District Court — Judge Tony F. Graf, Jr. · **Date:** 2026-06-01 · **Type:** ruling

## What it is
Judge Graf's ruling **GRANTING** the defense's Motion for an Order to Show Cause (Dkt. 504) — requiring **County Attorney Jeffrey Gray and Deputy County Attorney Christopher Ballard to show cause** why they should not be held in contempt for violating the publicity order — and **DENYING** the defense's request to compel discovery. Oral argument was heard May 19, 2026.

## What it argues / orders
Applying Utah Code § 78B-6-302(2) and *Von Hake v. Thomas*, the court found the defense's **affidavit sufficient** to require a show-cause hearing on the alleged out-of-court contempt.

## The other side
The State opposed both the show-cause order and characterized its statements as protected.

## Why it matters
The **procedural predicate** to the June 26 contempt ruling — it set the June 12 evidentiary show-cause hearing. It also **denied a defense compel-discovery request**, part of the discovery dispute.
