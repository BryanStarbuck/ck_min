# Request to Submit Motion for Limited Intervention

**Filed by:** Media intervenors (Parsons Behle — C. Michael Judd) · **Date:** 2025-12-03 · **Type:** request

## What it is
A short procedural filing notifying the court that briefing on the media's Motion for Limited Intervention is complete and asking the court to take the motion under submission for a decision.

## What it argues
The media recount the briefing sequence: they filed their motion on November 5, 2025; the defendant and the State each responded on November 19, 2025; and the media replied on November 26, 2025. With briefing closed, the matter is ripe for the court's consideration. No party requested oral argument, but the media suggest that if the court wants argument, it could be folded into the closure hearing already set for December 11, 2025 — while offering to accommodate a separate hearing if preferred.
