# Victim Invocation of Speedy Trial Rights

**Filed by:** Victim — Erika Kirk, through counsel Jeffrey A. Neiman and Brennan Moss · **Date:** 2026-01-16 · **Type:** invocation

## What it is
A notice by Erika Kirk, acting as the victim representative, formally asserting her statutory right to a speedy trial under Utah Code § 77-38-7(2).

## What it argues
The filing asserts that while the defendant is entitled to a fair trial, he is not entitled to cause undue delay in the criminal justice process. It invokes the Utah crime-victim statute's guarantee of "a speedy disposition of the charges free from unwarranted delay caused by or at the behest of the defendant," and to a prompt and final conclusion of the case, including of any collateral attacks. It asks the Court, in ensuring the defendant a fair trial, to also balance and protect Erika Kirk's right to a speedy trial.

## The other side
No specific opposing filing is referenced. The invocation implicitly responds to defense litigation and requests that could extend the timeline.

## Why it matters
Places the victim's statutory speedy-trial interest formally on the record, creating a counterweight to defense motions that could delay proceedings in this capital case.
