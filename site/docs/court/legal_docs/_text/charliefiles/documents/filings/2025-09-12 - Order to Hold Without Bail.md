# Order to Hold Without Bail

**Filed by:** District Court Judge Shawn R. Howell (Provo District Court, Utah County); on the affirmation of arresting officer Brian Davis · **Date:** 2025-09-12 · **Type:** order

## What it is
The magistrate's short order, entered the morning of Robinson's arrest, directing that he be held in custody without bail pending further proceedings.

## What it argues / establishes
The order recites that, based on the affirmation of arresting officer Brian Davis, the magistrate found probable cause existed for Robinson's warrantless arrest. It notes that the current offense is a capital felony and that the court finds substantial evidence to support the charge. On those findings — the standard required under Utah law to deny pretrial release in a capital case — the court orders Robinson held without bail. The order references Probable Cause ID 505275 and Submission ID 3307381 and is signed by Judge Shawn R. Howell at 8:40 a.m. on September 12, 2025.
