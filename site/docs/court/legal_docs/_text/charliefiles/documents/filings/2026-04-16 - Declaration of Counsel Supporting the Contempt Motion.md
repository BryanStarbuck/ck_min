# Declaration of Counsel Supporting the Contempt Motion

**Filed by:** Defense counsel · **Date:** 2026-04-16 · **Type:** declaration

## What it is
The **Declaration of Counsel** (Docket 505) supporting the defense's motion for issuance of an order to show cause — the sworn factual predicate (under Utah Code § 78B-6-302(2)) for the contempt motion.

## What it argues / orders
Lays out, under oath, the facts constituting the alleged contempt — that prosecutors made public statements about the victim, the victim's family, and Robinson's guilt or innocence in violation of ethical duties and the court's orders.

## Why it matters
The **affidavit/declaration** the court found sufficient to require a show-cause hearing ([[2026-06-01 - Ruling Granting Order to Show Cause and Denying Compel Discovery|June 1 ruling]]). See [[Prosecution Problems]].
