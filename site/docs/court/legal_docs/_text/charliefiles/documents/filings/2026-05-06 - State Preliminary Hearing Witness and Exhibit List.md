# State Preliminary Hearing Witness and Exhibit List

**Filed by:** Utah County Attorney's Office (Ryan McBride, General Crimes Division Chief) · **Date:** 2026-05-06 · **Type:** witness/exhibit list

## What it is
The State's **witness and exhibit list** for the preliminary hearing, disclosing (with Bates numbers and disclosure dates) the witnesses and exhibits it intended to offer.

## What it argues / orders
Lists the core witnesses — **Officer Bagley** (UVU PD), **Agent Hull**, **Agent Bricker/Mortensen**, **Agent Davis**, **Sgt. Faumuina** (SBI), and **Lance Twiggs** via a **recorded Rule 1102 statement in lieu of testimony** — and key exhibits, including the **screwdriver** and **sniper-perch** photos, **Exhibit 16** (Twiggs's recorded 1102 statement, disclosed 4/28/2026), and **Exhibit 32** (the ATF ballistics report, dated 12/18/2025).

## The other side
The list reserves the right to add or remove witnesses and to use 1102 statements in lieu of live testimony.

## Why it matters
The **roadmap of the State's prelim case** — it confirms the witness lineup that testified across Days 1–5 and that Twiggs would come in via recorded 1102 rather than live. See [[People Index]], [[Hearing Transcripts]].
