# Ruling Denying Motion to Preclude Rule 1102 Hearsay

**Filed by:** Fourth District Court — Judge Tony F. Graf, Jr. · **Date:** 2026-06-23 · **Type:** ruling

## What it is
Judge Graf's ruling **DENYING** the defense's Motion in Limine (Dkt. 525) to preclude the State from using **hearsay** — under **Article I, § 12 of the Utah Constitution** and **Rule 1102** of the Utah Rules of Evidence — to establish probable cause at the preliminary hearing. Oral argument was heard June 12, 2026.

## What it argues / orders
The preliminary hearing's purpose "is limited to determining whether probable cause exists" (Utah Const. art I, § 12); it is **not a trial** and does not determine guilt, so **reliable hearsay is admissible** — citing *State v. Lopez*, 2020 UT 61, and *State v. Virgin*, 2006 UT 29 (distinguishing the burden at a preliminary hearing versus trial).

## The other side
The defense argued that admitting hearsay to establish probable cause offends the state constitution and Confrontation principles — the objection it **preserved for interlocutory appeal** (see the June 12 stay ruling).

## Why it matters
This is the **legal foundation** that let the **Twiggs recorded statement, the Discord/text thread, and the note** come in at the prelim as reliable hearsay — the backbone of the confession case. The defense's Utah Supreme Court challenge to it remains open. See [[The Discord and Text Messages]], [[Confessions and Admissions]], [[What Is Genuinely Open]].
