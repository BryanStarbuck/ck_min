# Request to Submit the Overlength Reply on the Rule 1102 Motion

**Filed by:** Defense — Kathryn Nester, Richard Novak, Michael Burt · **Date:** 2026-06-03 · **Type:** request to submit

## What it is
A procedural **request to submit for decision** the defense's concurrently-filed motion for permission to file an **overlength reply** in support of its Motion in Limine to preclude Art. I § 12 / Rule 1102 hearsay.

## What it argues / orders
Asks the court to act on the overlength-reply motion without waiting for a response (Utah R. Civ. P. 7(l)(A)).

## Why it matters
Housekeeping in the **Rule 1102 hearsay** briefing that led to the June 23 ruling.
