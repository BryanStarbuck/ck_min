# Media Interviews

Long-form media interviews with people connected to the case, transcribed and summarized here with clickable timecodes. These are collected because the participants speak at length to facts the vault tracks — the security setup, the wound, the theories.

> **How to weigh these.** Every page below is an **interested-participant account given in the media — not sworn testimony.** The speakers are describing (and often defending) their own conduct, and two of them are **adverse to each other** (see the [[Kirks Security Detail|Shaffer–Harpole rivalry]]). Treat their claims as one side of a story: valuable for what an insider saw, but to be **weighed against the court record, the filings, and the [[Hearing Transcripts|sworn hearing testimony]]**, not substituted for it.

## The interviews

- **[[2025-11-17 - Brian Harpole on the Shawn Ryan Show]]** — *Shawn Ryan Show #254*, Nov. 17, 2025 (~2h48m). **[[Brian Harpole]]**, the founder of Integrity Security Solutions and **head of Charlie Kirk's contracted detail on 9/10**, gives his most detailed account: the advance planning and physical layout, the moment of the shot, his hands-on wound care, and his point-by-point rebuttals to the online theories (Mossad, an exploding mic). His central blame point — the **"I got you covered"** exchange with UVU's Chief Long over a student roof-access point. *Incumbent contractor; also a [[Brian Harpole|defamation plaintiff]]. Interested source.*

- **[[2025-09-12 - Greg Shaffer News Interview]]** — NTD News, ~9 min, the day the suspect was named. **[[Greg Shaffer]]**, retired FBI special agent and TPUSA / Charlie Kirk's **security director 2015–2022** (the **predecessor** firm), explains how the "prove me wrong" events were staffed, argues a **~200-yard shot is "almost impossible to defend against"** without countersniper teams, and predicts blame will fall on the **"private security company."** *Predecessor with a personal grievance against the current team (see the [[Kirks Security Detail|rivalry]]). Interested source.*
