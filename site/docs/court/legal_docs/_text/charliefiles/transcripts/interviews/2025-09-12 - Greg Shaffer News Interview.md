## Summary
A ~9-minute cable-news interview (NTD News) recorded the day the suspect was named, with **[[Greg Shaffer]]** — retired FBI special agent, principal of the Active Shooter Prevention Project, and **Turning Point USA / Charlie Kirk's security director from 2015 to 2022** (the **predecessor** to the detail on stage at UVU). Shaffer walks through how Kirk's "prove me wrong" campus events were staffed, why a long-range rifle shot is so hard for a private detail to stop, and where he expects the accountability to land.

**Key points he makes:**
- Kirk was a **private citizen** and TPUSA a **private company**, so the events got **no federal or police protection** — all security was **TPUSA-paid and private**.
- Under the tent Kirk had a **~5-man close-perimeter detail**; the campus police (he cites **~6 officers**) were mostly **crowd and traffic control**, "not really there for security at Charlie Kirk."
- A shot from **~200 yards** is **"almost impossible to defend against"** without **countersniper teams** and Secret-Service-level manpower and money.
- On accountability: **"private security is gonna be the first one … somebody's gonna have to take the brunt of the blame … it's gonna be his private security company."** He references *"the private security firms out of Texas."*
- Kirk **"always knew he had a target on his back."**

> This is one participant's account, given in a media interview — **not sworn testimony**. Shaffer is the team's **predecessor** and left the TPUSA contract in 2022 under a dispute with the men who replaced him ([[Greg Shaffer]]); weigh his blame-allocation against that, and against the court record.
