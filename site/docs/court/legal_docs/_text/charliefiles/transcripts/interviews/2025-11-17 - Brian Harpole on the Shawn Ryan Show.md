Charlie Kirk's contracted head of security gives a ~2h48m account of the UVU security operation, the shooting, and his rebuttals to the theories circulating about it. · video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S0fmq1zffGw

## Summary
**What it is:** A two-person interview between host **Shawn Ryan** and guest **[[Brian Harpole]]**, the founder of Integrity Security Solutions and the head of Charlie Kirk's contracted close-protection detail at Utah Valley University on September 10, 2025. Harpole walks through his team's advance planning, the physical security layout, the moment of the shot, his own hands-on wound care, the drive to the hospital, and the autopsy — and answers, point by point, the online claims about his team (Mossad ties, an exploding microphone, a "trapdoor"). It is his first long-form video account, given as he and his team faced public accusations. See [[Kirks Security Detail]].

**What Harpole claims:**
- The **amphitheater location was not his team's choice** — the school dictated it via the permit, and Kirk preferred the open-air, un-ticketed format so opposing views would show up.
- His team's area of responsibility was the **close-protection "bubble" (roughly the first 30 meters)**; rooftops and overwatch were **not** their statutory job and they legally could not post snipers or fly drones in that airspace.
- On the Monday before the event he warned the **UVU police chief ("Chief Long")** by message about a **student roof-access point** near the site and asked for it to be controlled or for one of his men to be posted there; the chief replied **"I got you covered."** He calls for a **FOIA of the chief's phone and communications**.
- He was **less than ~10 meters to Kirk's right**, heard the shot and heard the bullet strike, and was **on top of Kirk within ~5 seconds**, hand in the wound; the team exfilled in **~15–16 seconds** and self-drove to the closest (Level III) hospital.
- **No Mossad / Israeli ties:** *"I've been in 39 countries… never been to Israel… We're Americans, period."*
- **There was an autopsy** and the wound was an **entry with no exit** — the round hit the vertebra, shattered it, traveled down (he recalls "C6") and fragmented; he says an OR doctor and chief of staff relayed this and that he pressed for an autopsy "now," with the Vice President on the phone.
- The **exploding-microphone / bomb theory is false** — no flash, no char, no burn marks when he cut the shirt off.
- **No internal leak or betrayal** that he saw; **Erika Kirk is a victim**, not a participant.
- He nonetheless has **his own open questions** — when the shooter got on the roof, how the rifle traveled and whether it was disassembled, Robinson's phone/tower records and possible help, and **why the school paved over the crime scene so fast** (he urges FOIAs).

**Caveats:** This is **Harpole's account** — a partisan source who is himself a **defamation plaintiff** ([[Brian Harpole|Harpole v. Owens]]) and is defending his own team's conduct; several of his characterizations (e.g. who bears fault for the rooftop, the wound details) are contested or unconfirmed by the court and official record.

> This is one participant's account, given in a media interview — not sworn testimony. Where it bears on contested facts, weigh it against the court record.
