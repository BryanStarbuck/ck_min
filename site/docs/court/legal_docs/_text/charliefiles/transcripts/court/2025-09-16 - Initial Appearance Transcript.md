# Initial Appearance — Transcript (September 16, 2025)

Tyler James Robinson's first court appearance in *State of Utah v. Tyler James Robinson*, held virtually from the Utah County Jail before Judge Tony Graff. Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BsNojXN6iUw

> 🎥 **Watch the source recording** — the timestamps below deep-link into this video.

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BsNojXN6iUw)
## Summary
**What happened:** Robinson appeared by video from the Utah County Jail for his initial appearance before Judge Tony Graff. The court found him indigent and provisionally appointed qualified capital-case counsel, advised him of his right to counsel and against self-incrimination, and read all seven charges — count one, aggravated murder, a capital felony, plus two counts of felony obstruction of justice, two counts of witness tampering, felony discharge of a firearm causing serious bodily injury, and a class A misdemeanor for a violent offense committed in the presence of a child, several carrying victim-targeting and other aggravating enhancements. The State announced it had just filed a notice of intent to seek the death penalty. Robinson stated only his name, entered no plea, and was ordered held in custody without bail. The matter was continued to a September 29, 2025 hearing.

**For the prosecution:** Chad Grunander appeared with the trial team, noted the newly filed death-penalty notice signed by Utah County Attorney Jeff Gray, secured a pretrial protective order in favor of Erika Kirk, and represented that discovery would be turned over to the defense as soon as possible under Rule 16.
**For the defense:** No defense counsel of record yet — Mr. Scortis appeared only to facilitate the appointment of counsel and expressly did not enter an appearance; the proceeding was otherwise procedural, with no plea entered.
