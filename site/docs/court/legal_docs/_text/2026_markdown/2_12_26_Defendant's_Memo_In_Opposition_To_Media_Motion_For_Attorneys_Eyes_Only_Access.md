                                        MEMORANDUM

        Mr. Robinson has never objected to the News Media receiving notice of

closure/classification motions, although Mr. Robinson did initially propose that the parties

simply effectuate service by publication in the public filings of the case. See Dkt. 169 (“Mr.

Robinson will comply with the notice requirements in Rule 4-204”). However, on November 10,

2025, this Court issued its initial Order Requiring Parties to Serve All Closure or

Reclassification Motions on the Media. Dkt. 171. This order requires the parties to both serve all

classification and closure motions on representatives of the press who have requested notice and

to file proof of service. Id. at 2.

        Mr. Robinson complied with that Order immediately and served the earlier-filed Motion

to Classify Transcript and Audio filed November 3, 2025, on the News Media. Dkt. 173 (Notice

of Service). The News Media objected to the classification motion. Dkt. 220. A hearing was held

on December 11, 2025.

        On December 10, 2025, Mr. Robinson filed his Motion to Disqualify and exhibits. At the

same time, Mr. Robinson filed a Motion to Classify Motion to Disqualify as Private and Request

for Closure Hearing. Dkt. 232. The motion to classify was served on the News Media via email,

as stated in the certificate of service for the motion. The classification issues were resolved with

the filing of redacted, public versions of the motion, opposition, and reply.

        On December 29, 2025, the Court issued the governing order regarding notice of motions

for classification. Dkt. 267. Under the December 29th Order, “the parties are ORDERED to

Motion to Exclude Still Photographers, RV Cameras and Microphones from the Courtroom as

Private. Dkt. 310. In accordance with the Court’s order and as noted in the certificate of service,

id. at 5, Mr. Robinson served a copy of the motion to classify on counsel for the News Media by

email the same day the motion was filed. 1 The News Media cannot deny that they received the

motion to classify, as they lodged an objection to it on January 23, 2026 (Dkt. 323).

       The News Media’s real complaint here is not that they did not receive Mr. Robinson’s

classification motion. Rather, the News Media’s complaint is that Mr. Robinson has not provided

them with a copy of the underlying Motion to Exclude, as the News Media wishes to file an

opposition thereto. However, the News Media fails to acknowledge this Court’s contrary order

on December 29, 2025, which explicitly does not require Mr. Robinson to serve motions to limit

electronic media coverage on the News Media, as this Court may suspend or limit electronic

                                          CONCLUSION

        Based upon the foregoing, the Court should deny the News Media’s blanket request for

access to pleadings the parties seek to classify as private during the pendency of classification

litigation.
