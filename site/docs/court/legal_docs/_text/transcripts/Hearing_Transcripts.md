# Hearing Transcripts

Full, speaker-labeled transcripts of the court hearings in *State of Utah v. Tyler James Robinson* that were livestreamed on YouTube — each with a **summary of the prosecution and defense cases** at the top and **clickable timecodes** that jump to the exact second in the hearing video.

> Speaker labels come from automated transcription (Deepgram) plus review; where a specific person's name is uncertain, the label falls back to a role (**THE COURT / PROSECUTION / DEFENSE / WITNESS**). Verify against the video before quoting a specific person by name. Transcripts are working documents, not the official court record.

## Available transcripts (chronological)
- [[2025-09-16 - Initial Appearance Transcript|Initial Appearance — Sept 16, 2025]] — Robinson appears from jail; 7 charges read; death-penalty notice; no plea; held without bail.
- [[2025-12-11 - December 11 Hearing Transcript|December 11 Hearing — Dec 11, 2025]] — first in-person appearance; **procedural** (sealing the Oct 24 record, media intervention, the publicity/gag order, scheduling) — ~4h43m.
- [[2026-01-16 - Disqualification Hearing, Part One Transcript|Disqualification Hearing, Part One — Jan 16, 2026]] — day one of the evidentiary hearing on the defense's motion to **disqualify the Utah County Attorney's Office** (premised on a line prosecutor's adult child having been in the crowd when Kirk was shot); the court declines to refer the matter to the Attorney General on the briefing alone, imposes a decorum sanction, and swears County Attorney **Jeffrey Gray**; continued mid-testimony.
- [[2026-02-03 - Disqualification Hearing, Part Two Transcript|Disqualification Hearing, Part Two — Feb 3, 2026]] — the continuation; testimony of **Jeff Gray**, Chief Deputy **Chad Grunander** (the prosecutor whose adult child was present), and SBI Agent **Dave Hull**; Judge Graf reserves ruling (the motion was denied Feb 24).
- [[2026-06-01 - Camera Access Ruling Transcript|Camera Access Ruling — June 1, 2026]] — the court rules the press/public get access and cameras for the prelim.
- [[2026-06-12 - Contempt Evidentiary Hearing Transcript|Contempt Evidentiary Hearing — June 12, 2026]] — evidentiary hearing on the defense's order-to-show-cause against prosecutors **Ballard** and **Gray** over media statements about the ballistics evidence (the defense asks the death penalty be struck as a sanction); the stay motion is denied and the hearsay-at-prelim challenge is argued — taken under advisement; after a June 22 delay, on **June 26** Graf held **Ballard** in civil contempt but kept the death penalty.
- [[2026-06-22 - Twiggs Subpoena Hearing Transcript|Twiggs Subpoena Hearing — June 22, 2026]] — Judge Graf quashes the defense subpoena to compel [[Lance Twiggs]] and admits his recorded interview as reliable hearsay.
- [[2026-07-06 - Preliminary Hearing Day 1 Transcript|Preliminary Hearing, Day 1 — July 6, 2026]] — the case agent on the stand; the excluded-video ruling; the rooftop-vs-courtyard cross. Full analysis in [[2026-07-06 - Preliminary Hearing Day 1|Day 1 Legal Review]].
- [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2 Transcript|Preliminary Hearing, Day 2 — July 7, 2026]] — Agent Hull finishes and is cross-examined; Exhibit 5.1 (the TPUSA "Orthodox Christian views" statement) only provisionally admitted; Sgt. Jennifer Faumuina lays chain of custody (screwdriver, towel-wrapped rifle) and the DNA report, then is paused so the defense calls FBI DNA examiner **Amanda Bakker** out of order to challenge DNA reliability. Full analysis in [[2026-07-07 - Preliminary Hearing Day 2|Day 2 Legal Review]].
- [[2026-07-08 - Preliminary Hearing Day 3 Transcript|Preliminary Hearing, Day 3 — July 8, 2026]] — SBI case agent **[[Brian Davis|Agent Brian Davis]]** (the affidavit affiant) on direct (prosecutor **Lauren Hunt**), laying the foundation for the **roommate evidence** — [[Lance Twiggs]]'s two recorded interviews, buccal swab, and **use immunity** — then extended argument over admitting, under **Rule 1102**, Twiggs's **recorded statement**, the **Discord / text thread (Exhibit 16)**, and the **[[The Keyboard Note|keyboard note]]** for **motive**; defense **Confrontation-Clause** objections (*Archuleta / Estes / Rideau*) and a media-counsel publication fight. The court allowed limited **probable-cause** use with redactions, **reserved** the rest (defense to brief it), and recessed to Day 4. Full analysis in [[2026-07-08 - Preliminary Hearing Day 3|Day 3 Legal Review]].
- [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4 Transcript|Preliminary Hearing, Day 4 — July 9, 2026]] — the longest session (~8.5h): the court **overrules** the defense's publication objection and the digital exhibits (texts, Discord, the note) are **published**; **[[Lance Twiggs]]'s immunized, sworn recorded statement** is played and **[[Brian Davis|Agent Davis]]** is **excused with no cross**; Sgt. **[[Jennifer Faumuina]]** is recalled on the physical evidence (the **Dremel** engraving tool, boxes of **.30-06**, five "safe" casings incl. one engraved **"test shot,"** ATF tool-mark links to the murder rifle); **the State rests**; the defense opens by calling the State's ATF examiner **[[Samantha Karner]]**, who concedes the murder **bullet is "inconclusive."** Full analysis in [[2026-07-09 - Preliminary Hearing Day 4|Day 4 Legal Review]].
- [[2026-07-10 - Preliminary Hearing Day 5 Transcript|Preliminary Hearing, Day 5 — July 10, 2026]] — the **short final session** (~3h) that **closed the hearing**: the court **denies** the defense's motion to bar the media camera but **sanctions** it (no exhibit capture/broadcast) over the Day-4 livestream breach; the defense calls its last witness, **ATF DNA Section Chief [[Caitlin Oliver]]**, who lays out DNA's **limits** on direct but on cross confirms Robinson as the **major contributor** ("at least a trillion times more likely"); the **defense rests**, the enhanced **"sniper's-perch" video (Exhibit 12.1)** is shown in-courtroom (livestream audio-only), and the court **adjourns to Sept 1**. Full analysis in [[2026-07-10 - Preliminary Hearing Day 5|Day 5 Legal Review]].
