## Summary
**What happened:** Day 5 (~3 hours — the short final session) closed the five-day preliminary hearing before [[Judge Tony Graf]]. Three things happened, in order:

1. **The camera sanction.** The morning opened on the fallout from Day 4: a **gallery-only** side-by-side image of the [[The Discord and Text Messages|Robinson–Twiggs communication]] had been **briefly caught by the media camera** and hit the livestream. Defense counsel **Richard Novak** asked the court to **bar the media camera** for the rest of the hearing and for the Sept. 1 argument [01:19:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=4787s). Media counsel (**Michael Judd**) and prosecutor **Ryan McBride** conceded the image should not have aired but noted the *text* was already public. The court **denied** the motion to exclude cameras but imposed a **narrower sanction**: "**members of the media shall no longer capture or broadcast any exhibits displayed by the parties during today's proceedings**" [01:34:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=5691s) — "**proportional and appropriate**" [01:34:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=5698s).
2. **The defense's last witness — ATF DNA Section Chief [[Caitlin Oliver]].** Over the State's objection that her testimony was cumulative, the defense called the ATF's **DNA** examiner — the forensic-DNA counterpart to Day 4's firearms witness. On direct (**Burt**) she established the **limits** of DNA science: nothing can be stated "with 100% certainty" [01:56:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=6983s); her lab reports **likelihood ratios**, not source attribution — "you **can't assign a source** to a DNA sample" [02:11:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=7907s); DNA "**can't speak to the activity that led to the deposition**" [02:12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=7946s); and "a person's DNA **may be present when they were not involved in the crime**" [02:12:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=7972s). But on **McBride's cross**, like Karner before her, she **confirmed the inculpatory core**: the swabs from the rifle and ammunition support **Robinson as the major contributor**. The defense then **rested** — "**we have no further witness**" [02:52:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=10336s).
3. **The enhanced video, and adjournment.** With both sides done, the State published **Exhibit 12.1** — the **zoomed, marked "sniper's-perch" surveillance video**, shown at the Kirk family's request — and the court switched the **livestream to audio only**, barring any video or still capture of the monitors, while it played [02:55:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=10500s). The court then **adjourned the hearing to September 1, 2026 at 10:00 a.m.** [03:07:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3OUG7n37po&t=11271s) for the parties' **closing / bind-over arguments** (on the Day-4 briefing schedule). **No probable-cause ruling was made.** Full defense-side analysis: [[2026-07-10 - Preliminary Hearing Day 5|Day 5 Summary & Legal Review]].

> This is a **preliminary hearing**, not a trial — the standard is **probable cause**, and **reliable hearsay is admissible** (Utah Const. Art. I §12; Utah R. Evid. 1102). Robinson is **presumed innocent**.
