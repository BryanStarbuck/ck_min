# December 11 Hearing — Transcript (December 11, 2025)
*A procedural hearing — sealing, cameras, media intervention, the publicity order, and scheduling. **No evidence was previewed or tested here** (that comes at the preliminary hearing).*

## Summary
**What happened:** At this December 11, 2025 hearing before Judge Tony Graf, Tyler James Robinson appeared in person for the first time while cameras remained in the courtroom over the defense's objection. The court closed part of the hearing to argue what should stay sealed from the earlier October 24 audio recording and transcript, then heard open argument on the media organizations' motion for limited intervention and the state's motion to clarify the pretrial publicity order. Midway through, the defense reported that the livestream had shown Robinson's shackles and captured counsel-table material in violation of the decorum order; the judge found a violation but declined to remove the cameras, instead relocating the camera and warning that further breaches would end the broadcast. The court granted the state's motion clarifying that "witness" in the publicity order reaches only witnesses on the prosecution or defense teams, designated Erica Kirk as the victim representative, and set future dates — including a three-day preliminary hearing targeted for May 18–21, 2026.

**For the prosecution:** The state (Ballard, Grunander) described the discovery volume — roughly 13,900 investigative files received and nearly 8,000 already turned over — and pressed to calendar a three-day preliminary hearing, arguing the probable-cause standard does not require complete discovery first. **For the defense:** The defense (Nester, Novak, Visser) objected to the cameras and sought sanctions for the decorum-order violation, resisted setting a preliminary-hearing date before discovery is complete and the disqualification motion is resolved, and flagged the state's early death-penalty announcement as triggering disclosure obligations not yet met.
