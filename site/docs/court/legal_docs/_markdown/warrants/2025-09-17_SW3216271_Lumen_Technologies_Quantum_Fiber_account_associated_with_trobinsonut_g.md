On September 10, 2025, Charles James Kirk was at the Utah Valley University (UVU) campus in Orem, Utah, for a political event. A search warrant is being requested in accordance with Utah Code Title 77 Chapters 23b, 23c, or 23f. These chapters stipulate that a government entity must seek a warrant to require a provider to disclose location information, stored or transmitted data, electronic information transmitted by the owner to a remote computer service or through an electronic communication service, the contents of an electronic communication in electronic storage of an electronic communication service, or identifying information for an electronic device. Utah code mandates such a demand can only be made pursuant to a warrant issued under the Utah Rules of Criminal Procedure, making a subpoena unsuitable in this context. 

Before May, 2019 some of this data could be obtained through an investigative subpoena. However, since then Utah Code Chapter 23c of Title 77 was amended to expand the definition of electronic information to potentially encompass the data mentioned in this warrant affidavit. Subsequent amendments to Chapters 23b and 23f further delineated specific data that required a warrant for access. Out of an abundance of caution the State is therefore seeking a warrant.
