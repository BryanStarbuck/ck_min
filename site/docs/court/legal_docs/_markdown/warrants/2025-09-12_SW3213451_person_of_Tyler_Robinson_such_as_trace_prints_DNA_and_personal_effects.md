Affiant believes the property and evidence described above is evidence of the crime or crimes of 76-5-201. Criminal homicide. 

The facts to establish the grounds for issuance of a Search Warrant are: 

Your affiant, Agent Justin Bowen, is employed by the Utah Department of Public Safety State Bureau of Investigation and is currently assigned to the Major Crimes unit. Your affiant has been given the responsibility to investigate homicides, violent crimes, weapons offenses, fraud, domestic violence crimes, sex offenses, offenses related to vehicle crashes and narcotics offenses among others. Your affiant has been through Peace Officer Standards and Training, and graduated August 2018. Your affiant has received several specialized trainings in different areas, including sex crimes investigations, fraud detection and prevention, domestic violence incidents, and homicides. Your affiant is currently certified as a Certified Fraud Examiner. Your affiant has investigated or assisted in investigations involving homicides, firearms offenses, death investigations, assault offenses, property crimes, crash investigations, and impaired driver investigations. 

The following information was obtained by your affiant from police officers, multiple video recordings posted on social media websites, Utah Valley University campus surveillance cameras and those who witnessed the incident. 

On September 10, 2025, Charles James Kirk was at the Utah Valley University (UVU) campus in Orem, Utah, for a political event. Mr. Kirk is a conservative political activitst who was scheduled to speak at an event in the courtyard area on the UVU campus. The courtyard area is to the west of the Losee Center building. According to video recordings, Charles was wearing a white T-shirt and dark pants, sitting under a white canopy in a courtyard of the campus facing to the east. Charles was holding a microphone and speaking to a crowd. While speaking into the microphone, a loud gunshot was heard at approximately 1223 hours, and Charles appeared to have been hit in the neck. Blood was seen coming out of the left side of his neck as he fell over to his left. Charles was transported to the Timpanogos Regional Hospital in Orem. Charles was pronounced deceased at the hospital. Charles’ body was transported to the Office of the Medical Examiner, where an autopsy was performed on the body. According to information obtained from the police officer who attended the autopsy, Sergeant Barrett with the Utah State Bureau of Investigation, Dr. Guarjado with the Office of the Medical Examiner concluded the autopsy and explained that he was going to certify that Charles Kirk’s cause of death was a homicide caused by a gunshot wound. 

Investigators reviewed the Utah Valley University surveillance cameras. While reviewing the surveillance cameras, investigators observed an individual on the rooftop of the Losee Center building on the UVU campus at the time of the shooting. Hereafter, the unidentified individual will be referred to as “Suspect”. Based on surveillance camera footage, Suspect appears to be a white male with dark colored hair, wearing a blue colored hat, sunglasses, a long sleeve black shirt with a picture of an American flag and an eagle printed on the shirt, dark colored jeans, and white/gray shoes with white colored shoelaces (the shoes appear to be consistent with Chuck Taylor/Converse style shoes). Suspect is also carrying a dark colored backpack on his back. Suspect walks with a distinctive gait. Prior to the shooting, Suspect appears to walk with a stiff, right leg and at a relatively slow pace. Suspect’s ability to bend his right leg appears to be restricted. The Investigators were able to track Suspect’s movements on the UVU campus, starting at approximately 1150 hours on 9/10 /2025.

At approximately 1150 hours, a UVU surveillance camera first captured Suspect walking across a grassy area north of Campus Drive at approximately 800 West. Suspect walks south through the parking lot and approaches a pedestrian tunnel that runs underneath Campus Drive. Suspect pauses briefly before entering the tunnel. Before entering tunnel, Suspect pauses at the top of the stairs and pulls out what appears to be a cellphone from his right pocket using his right hand at approximately 1153 hours. Suspect eventually puts what appears to be a cellphone back in his right pants pocket using his right hand. Suspect walks down the stairs and noticeably keeps his right leg mostly straight while walking down the stairs into the tunnel. Suspect walks through the tunnel and into the parking garage area. Suspect walks up the stairs to the second level of the parking garage. While walking up the stairs in the parking garage, Suspect continues to keep his right leg straight. As Suspect approaches the landing on the stairs between the first and second floor, it appears as though there is something bulging out from Suspect’s waistband area as he walks up the stairs. The bulge appears to be underneath Suspect’s black shirt and on the right side of Suspect’s waistband area. 

At approximately 1202 hours, Suspect is observed walking on the north side of the Losee Center building, walking with the same gait as observed earlier. Suspect walks south on the sidewalk on the east side of the Losee Center building. Suspect enters the Losee Center building through a set of doors on the southeast corner of the Losee Center building. At approximately 1215 hours, Suspect is observed walking up the stairs that lead to the common area adjacent to the Losee Center building. Suspect walks to a short concrete wall that separates the common area from the rooftop of the Losee Center building. At approximately 1217 hours, Suspect climbs over the short wall and appears to crouch down on the north side of the wall on the rooftop. At approximately 1222 hours, Suspect stands up and starts running across the rooftop of the Losee Center building. Suspect runs to the west side of the rooftop and appears to scoot along the rooftop area as he gets closer to the edge of the rooftop. At approximately 1222 hours, Suspect lays down in a prone position near the edge of the rooftop and appears to be facing west, in the direction of the courtyard area. 

The rooftop of the Losee Center building overlooks the courtyard area where the event featuring Charlie Kirk was taking place. The Losee Center building is east of the courtyard area. At approximately 1223 hours, Suspect stands up suddenly from the edge of the rooftop and sprints north on the rooftop of the Losee Center building. This sprinting motion is in stark contrast to the slow and restricted gait observed by Suspect in earlier surveillance camera footage. Suspect runs to the northeast corner of the Losee Center building and approaches the edge of the rooftop. Suspect places a dark colored item on the rooftop and proceeds to lower himself off the rooftop. Suspect drops off the rooftop onto the grass below. Suspect runs north near the parking lot to the east of the Losee Center. At approximately 1224 hours, Suspect runs north across the Campus Drive road. 

The area to the north of Campus Drive road where Suspect crossed over is a grassy area with trees on the edge of the UVU campus. Investigators discovered a bolt action rifle wrapped in a dark colored towel. The rifle was determined to be a Mauser Model 98, .3006 caliber bolt action rifle. The rifle had a scope mounted on top of it. Investigators noted inscriptions that had been engraved on casings found with the rifle. Inscriptions on a fired casing read “Notices Buldge OWO what’s this?”. Inscriptions on the three unfired casings read: “hey fascist! CATCH! (up arrow symbol, right arrow symbol, and three down arrow symbols)”. A second unfired casing read “O Bella ciao, Bella ciao, Bella ciao, Ciao, ciao!”. A third unfired casing read “If you read This, you are GAY Lmao”.

Investigators documented a shoe impression on the northeast corner rooftop edge of the Losee Center building. This shoe impression was located in the vicinity where Suspect climbed down from the rooftop. The shoe impression is consistent with the shoe sole characteristics of a Converse/Chuck Taylor shoe. 

While processing the crime scene, evidence technicians were able to identify the locations where Suspect, through physical contact, had left potential biological evidence. Touch DNA transfers when skin cells, from touch or sweat, are shed onto an object during direct or indirect contact, and it can also transfer from one person to another. This type of DNA evidence comes from epithelials cells, or skin cells, that contain cellular DNA and can be recovered from a wide range of surfaces with the use of forensic DNA techniques. This evidence was collected in order to obtain a potential DNA profile for Suspect. Evidence technicians also identified fingerprints and a smeared palm print in the vicinity of the northeast corner rooftop edge of the Losee Center building where Suspect dropped off the rooftop to the ground below. 

On 9/11/2025, Sheriff Smith with the Utah County Sheriff’s Office received a phone call at approximately 2004 hours from Sheriff Brooksby with the Washington County Sheriff’s Office. According to Sheriff Brooksby, he had received a phone call from a former deputy who had previously been employed with the Sheriff’s Office. The former deputy, identified as Mike Mitchell, explained that he had received a phone call from the father of Tyler Robinson. Mike Mitchell explained that Tyler Robinson confessed to his father that he was the shooter at Utah Valley University. Tyler Robinson’s parents worked with law enforcement to arrange a way for Tyler Robinson to turn himself in without a confrontation with law enforcement. Mike Mitchell coordinated with law enforcement to have Tyler Robinson meet with law enforcement at the Washington County Sheriff’s Office. Tyler Robinson arrived at the Washington County Sheriff’s Office accompanied by his parents at approximately 2226 hours. Tyler Robinson’s mother relayed to law enforcement that Tyler Robinson had expressed having suicidal ideations. Sergeant Elsholz with the Utah State Bureau of Investigation provided me with a photograph of Tyler Robinson’s Utah driver license. The driver license was taken from the person of Tyler Robinson by Agent Davis with the Utah State Bureau of Investigation. 

On 9/12/2025, at approximately 0100 hours, FBI Special Agent Lange conducted an interview with the boyfriend of Tyler Robinson at the Washington County Sheriff’s Office. The boyfriend was identified as Lance Twiggs. During the interview, Lance showed investigators text messages between Tyler Robinson and himself. The following is the content of those messages between Tyler Robinson and Lance Twiggs. First message (Tyler): “I am still ok my love, but am stuck in orem for a little while longer yet”. Second message (Tyler): “shouldn’t be long until I can come home, but I gotta grab my rifle still. To be honest I had hoped to keep this secret till I died of old age. I am sorry to involve you”. Third message(Lance): “you weren’t the one who did it right????” Fourth message (Tyler): “I am, I’m sorry”. Fifth message (Lance): “I thought they caught the person?” Sixth message (Tyler): “no, they grabbed some crazy old dude, then interrogated someone in similar clothing”. Seventh message (Tyler): “I had planned to grab my rifle from my drop point shortly after, but most of that side of town got locked down. Its quiet, almost enough to get out, but theres one vehicle lingering”.
