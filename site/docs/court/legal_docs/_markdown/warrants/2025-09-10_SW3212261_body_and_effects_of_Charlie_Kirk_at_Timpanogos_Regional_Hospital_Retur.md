On 9/10/2025 Charles James Kirk was at the Utah Valley University campus for a political event. Charles was wearing a white tee-shirt and dark pants, sitting under a White canopy in a courtyard of the campus facing to the East. Charles was holding a microphone and speaking to a crowd. While speaking into the microphone, a loud gunshot was heard, and Charles appeared to have been hit in the neck. Blood was seen quickly coming out of the left side of his neck as he fell over to his left. Charles was transported to the Timpanogos Regional Hospital in Orem. Charles was pronounced deceased at the hospital. Access to his body and belongings is requested to obtain photographs of the injuries. Charles is a high profile political figure and threats are a common occurrence, therefore permission is also requested to seize and forensically analyze any electronic device belonging to Charles as well as any on his body or with his property that would have been transported with him to the hospital to analyze for threats or other relevant information that would lead to the suspect identity. Search warrant is requested to prevent the loss of evidence that may be contained on his body or his electronic devices. 

WHEREFORE, your affiant prays that a Search Warrant be issued for the seizure of said items in the daytime.
