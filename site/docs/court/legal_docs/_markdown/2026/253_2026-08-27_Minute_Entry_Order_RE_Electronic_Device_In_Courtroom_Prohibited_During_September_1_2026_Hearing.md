IN THE FOURTH JUDICIAL DISTRICT COURT IN AND FOR UTAH COUNTY, STATE OF UTAH 

STATE OF UTAH, MINUTE ENTRY ORDER RE: ELECTRONIC DEVICES IN Plaintiff, COURTROOM PROHIBITED DURING SEPTEMBER 1, 2026 vs. HEARING 

TYLER JAMES ROBINSON, Case No. 251403576 

Defendant. Judge Tony F. Graf, Jr. 

In light of the substantial public interest in this matter, and to prevent interference with the administration ofjustice, disruption of the proceedings, threats to safety or security, or compromise ofthe integrity of the proceedings, it is hereby ORDERED that portable electronic devices shall not be used in the courtroom during the hearing scheduled for September 1, 2026, except as expressly authorized by the Court. See UCJA 4-401.02(3)(B)(iii). Consequently, no portable electronic devices will be permitted in the courtroom during the duration of this specific proceeding. See id. (permitting further restriction of portable electronic devices). For purposes of this order, “portable electronic device . . . means any device that can record or transmit data, images or sounds, or access the internet, including a pager, laptop/notebook/personal computer, handheld PC, PDA, audio or video recorder, wireless device, cellular telephone, or electronic calendar.” Jd. at (1)(B) (emphasis added). 

This prohibition does not apply to Defendant’s or the State’s legal counsel and support staff or the victim representative’s legal counsel. Nor does the prohibition apply to the operator of, and the equipment permitted under any controlling electronic media coverage order. 

Dated this 27" day of August, 2026.
