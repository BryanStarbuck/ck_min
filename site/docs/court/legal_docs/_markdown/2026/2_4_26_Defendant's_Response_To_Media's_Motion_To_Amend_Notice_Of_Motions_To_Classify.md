Defendant, Tyler James Robinson, by and through undersigned counsel, hereby files this 

response to the _News Media’s Motion to Amend Standing Order for Rule 4-202.04 Notice of_ 

_Motions for Classification_ (Dkt. 325), filed January 23, 2026. 

Mr. Robinson does not oppose the News Media’s requested relief to amend the Court’s 

Standing Order (Dkt. 267) to read in pertinent part: 

If members of the media intend to object to a closure motion and request a closure hearing, such objections and requests must be filed within **fourteen (14) days** of receiving notice.
