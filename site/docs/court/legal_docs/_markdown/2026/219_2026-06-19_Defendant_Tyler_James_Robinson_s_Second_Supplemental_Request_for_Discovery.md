Defendant, Tyler James Robinson, by and through undersigned counsel, hereby requests the Utah County Attorney’s Office (UCAO), on behalf of the State of Utah, provide the documents and information identified below in discovery. This request is made pursuant to Utah Rule of Criminal Procedure 16; the Due Process Clauses of the Fifth and Fourteenth Amendments of the United States Constitution and article I, section 7 of the Utah Constitution; the Sixth Amendment of the United States Constitution and article I, section 12 of the Utah State Constitution; and, where the State is seeking the penalty of death in this case, the Eighth Amendment of the United States Constitution and article I, section 9 of the Utah Constitution. 

Mr. Robinson requests that the UCAO provide the following documents and information 

**no later than June 26, 2026** : 

1. For any and all law enforcement officers the UCAO intends to call at the preliminary examination scheduled to begin July 6, 2026: 

   - a. A list of the individual’s law enforcement employment history including: the name of the law enforcement agency they were employed by; the job title(s) held while 

employed with each agency; and the dates (month/year) of each position held with each agency. 

- b. A copy of the individual’s training history while employed by law enforcement including a description of the training and approximate date the individual participated in the training. 

- c. If the individual purports to be an expert based on training and experience to testify to any particular fact or set of facts, a list of any other training or experience not otherwise disclosed pursuant to (1)(a) and (1)(b) that the individual intends to base their opinion
