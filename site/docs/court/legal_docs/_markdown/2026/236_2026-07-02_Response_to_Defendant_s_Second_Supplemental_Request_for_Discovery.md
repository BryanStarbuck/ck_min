The State hereby notifies Defendant that it does not intend to provide the materials requested in Defendant’s second supplementary request for discovery. 

On June 19, 2026, Defendant requested discovery of materials related to the law enforcement witnesses the State intends to call at the preliminary hearing. These materials include employment history, training history, and expert notice if the law enforcement witnesses will offer expert testimony. 

1 

The State does not intend to comply with the request. Preliminary hearings are expressly excluded from expert notice requirements by Utah Code Section 77-17-13(1)(a). The other materials requested are not required by rule 16(a)(1) and the State does not believe good cause under rule 16(a)(4) exists to require the production (and perhaps creation) of the records for the preliminary hearing. Finally, the Utah County Attorney’s Office does not possess these materials. Accordingly, the State notifies Defendant that it does not intend to produce the materials requested.
