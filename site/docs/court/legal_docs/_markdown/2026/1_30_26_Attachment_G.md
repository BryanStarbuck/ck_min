**Subject:** RE: GRAMA Requests - CK and TJR 

A couple of comments: 

- As a general rule, I would deny all GRAMA requests regarding this case as a case still under investigation and pending prosecution. Further I would cite to rule 3.6 of the rules of professional responsibility in that any material our office releases has the potential to prejudice the potential jury pool. 

- On records that could go either way, please err on the side of denial. 

- I am especially concerned about the release of information from the jail phone calls. In my view, these statements are under investigation. Jail phone calls are often the source of confessions and the like. In my experience, as soon as the suspect learns his calls are being listened to (and especially public), he will stop talking on the phone about the case and we will be unable to gather valuable evidence. We ought to treat these as statements under investigation. 

- 911 calls, I understand referring these to other agencies, but I would add that we would deny them as potential evidence in the case which is pending. 

I know others on the team also have input, so please hold off until you hear from the prosecutors. 

#### Ryan

##### **All printed and electronic correspondence, arrest records, witness statements, reports, notes, recordings, internal communications, interviews, body camera footage, or other records related to the shooting of Charlie Kirk on September 10, 2025.** 

Under Utah Code § 63G-2-305(10), records are protected if their release would reasonably be expected to interfere with an investigation, disclose investigative techniques, or deprive a person of a right to a fair proceeding. Based on this statutory authority, we request that you withhold the release of the abovereferenced records until further written notice from this office. 

Please ensure that all staff responsible for handling GRAMA requests are informed of this restriction. If you receive any requests for these records, please notify our office immediately before taking any action.
