IN THE FOURTH JUDICIAL DISTRICT COURT IN AND FOR UTAH COUNTY, STATE OF UTAH 

STATE OF UTAH, 

STANDING ORDER ON REDACTING AND FILING Plaintiff, TRANSCRIPTS 

vs. 

Case No. 251403576 TYLER JAMES ROBINSON, Judge Tony F. Graf, Jr. 

Defendant. 

On February 10, 2026, defense counsel filed a notice of lodging along with a redacted transcript of the December 11, 2025, hearing. The transcript was not filed in accordance with Utah law and the redactor failed to redact portions of the index. To prevent prejudice or private information from becoming public, the court has sealed the transcript. 

To effectuate the proper filing of<sup>transcriptsinthiscaseandtoeliminatepotentiallyprivate</sup> or sealed records becoming public, the court orders the following: 

Transcripts of court proceedings in this case must be filed by the certified court reporter who prepared the transcript in accordance with section 78A-2-408 and court rules. The parties are prohibited from filing transcripts, unless filed as an exhibit to a motion. 

If redaction of a certified court transcript is required, the court reporter who prepared the transcript is required to perform the redaction. The parties are prohibited from redacting certified court transcripts that will be filed in the case. 

Dated this 23rd day of February, 2026.
