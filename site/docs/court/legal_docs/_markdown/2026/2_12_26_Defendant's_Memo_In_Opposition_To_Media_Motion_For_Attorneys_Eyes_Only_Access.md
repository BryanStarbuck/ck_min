Defendant, Tyler James Robinson, by and through undersigned counsel, hereby responds to the _News Media’s Motion for Attorneys Eyes Only Access to Closure Motions_ (Dkt. 335). Mr. Robinson asks the Court to deny the News Media’s motion. 

# **<u>MEMORANDUM</u>** 

Mr. Robinson has never objected to the News Media receiving notice of 

closure/classification motions, although Mr. Robinson did initially propose that the parties simply effectuate service by publication in the public filings of the case. _See_ Dkt. 169 (“Mr. Robinson will comply with the notice requirements in Rule 4-204”). However, on November 10, 2025, this Court issued its initial _Order Requiring Parties to Serve All Closure or_ 

_Reclassification Motions on the Media_ . Dkt. 171. This order requires the parties to both serve all classification and closure motions on representatives of the press who have requested notice and to file proof of service. _Id_ . at 2. 

Mr. Robinson complied with that Order immediately and served the earlier-filed _Motion_ 

_to Classify Transcript and Audio_ filed November 3, 2025, on the News Media. Dkt. 173 (Notice of Service). The News Media objected to the classification motion. Dkt. 220. A hearing was held on December 11, 2025. 

On December 10, 2025, Mr. Robinson filed his Motion to Disqualify and exhibits. At the same time, Mr. Robinson filed a _Motion to Classify Motion to Disqualify as Private and Request for Closure Hearing_ . Dkt. 232. The motion to classify was served on the News Media via email, as stated in the certificate of service for the motion. The classification issues were resolved with the filing of redacted, public versions of the motion, opposition, and reply. 

On December 29, 2025, the Court issued the governing order regarding notice of motions for classification. Dkt. 267. Under the December 29th Order, “the parties are ORDERED to

In accordance with the Court’s order and as noted in the certificate of service, _id_ . at 5, Mr. Robinson served a copy of the motion to classify on counsel for the News Media by email the same day the motion was filed.<sup>1</sup> The News Media cannot deny that they received the motion to classify, as they lodged an objection to it on January 23, 2026 (Dkt. 323). 

The News Media’s real complaint here is _not_ that they did not receive Mr. Robinson’s classification motion. Rather, the News Media’s complaint is that Mr. Robinson has not provided them with a copy of the underlying _Motion to Exclude_ , as the News Media wishes to file an opposition thereto. However, the News Media fails to acknowledge this Court’s contrary order on December 29, 2025, which explicitly does _not_ require Mr. Robinson to serve motions to limit electronic media coverage on the News Media, as this Court may suspend or limit electronic

Moreover, the State has objected to Mr. Robinson’s _Motion to Exclude_ , citing the same First Amendment cases the News Media oft cites and arguing the same public access issues the News Media raises in its own filings. _See_ Dkt. 354. There is no reason to believe that the public’s interest is not being sufficiently addressed in the litigation between the parties. 

The News Media are not traditional litigants. Mr. Robinson urges the Court to make this clear by denying the News Media’s motion. Specifically, the Court should make clear that the News Media may not simply file pleadings on non-classification/closure motions filed by the parties without invitation from the Court to do so. # **<u>CONCLUSION</u>** 

Based upon the foregoing, the Court should deny the News Media’s blanket request for access to pleadings the parties seek to classify as private during the pendency of classification litigation.
