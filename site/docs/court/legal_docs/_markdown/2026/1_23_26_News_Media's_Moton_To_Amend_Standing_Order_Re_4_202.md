# **ARGUMENT** 

The Court’s Order affords the parties the full fourteen (14) days provided under the Rules to respond to any Closure Motions, while giving the News Media just seven (7) days.  To date, the News Media has been the only voice opposing the multiple Closure Motions filed in this case, so it is difficult to understand why they should be afforded less time to respond to Closure Motions than provided under the Rules.  Some of the Closure Motions, including, in particular, those filed by the Defendant, have been quite lengthy and factually complex, such as the most recently filed Motion to Exclude Still Photographers, TV Cameras, and Microphones from the Courtroom dated January 16, 2026, which clocks in at 262 pages (Dkt. 304). 

Requiring the News Media to respond within seven days to such Motions is unreasonable and will impair its ability to provide the Court with legal argument and advocacy to assist in its consideration of these important constitutional issues.  Because both the State and the Defendant have fourteen days to respond to Closure Motions, providing the News Media the same amount of time will simply align all the response times and not cause any delay in the proceedings. Further, some Closure Motions may present issues that are not close calls and for which the Court would not benefit from oral argument, making them appropriate for resolution on the written submissions.  Finally, any additional time is warranted given that the interests at stake are of constitutional magnitude.

In an attempt to resolve this issue without involving the Court, counsel for the News 

Media met and conferred with counsel for the State and Defendant and proposed a compromise of ten (10) days to respond to Closure Motions.  The State agreed; the defense refused.  Now that 

the News Media have been required to incur the time and expense necessary to seek the Court’s assistance on this issue, and to put the litigants on equal footing, the News Media respectfully request that the Court afford the News Media litigants the same amount of time to respond to Closure Motions as it has provided the State and Defense – fourteen days.
