STATE OF UTAH, **REQUEST TO SUBMIT FOR DECISION MOTION FOR** Plaintiff, **LIMITED INTERVENTION** vs. TYLER JAMES ROBINSON, Case No: 251403576 Defendant. Judge Tony F. Graf, Jr. 

On November 5, 2025, Proposed Media Intervenors The Salt Lake Tribune and its reporter Jessica Shreifels; St. George News; The Associated Press; Fox13 News; Millard County Chronicle Progress; KOAL (Castle Country Radio); KUER-FM; Lehi Free Press; The Park Record; CBS Broadcasting; The New York Times; CNN; Fox News; and Law & Crime Network (“Media Intervenors”) filed a Motion for Limited Intervention and Request for Notice of Motions to Close, Seal, or Reclassify [Doc. 164]. Defendant Tyler James Robinson filed a Response [Doc. 185] on November 19, 2025, and the State filed its own Response [Doc. 187] that same day. Proposed Media Intervenors filed a Reply [Doc. 199] on November 26, 2025. 

The briefing associated with the Motion to Intervene is now complete and the matter is ready for the Court’s consideration. 

No party has requested oral argument. However, to the extent that the Court believes oral argument may prove useful in resolving this motion, the Proposed Media Intervenors propose that argument be held in conjunction with the closure hearing already set for December 11, 2025
