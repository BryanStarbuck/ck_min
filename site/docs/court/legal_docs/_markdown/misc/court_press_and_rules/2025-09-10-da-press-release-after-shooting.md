## **UVU Shooting Investigation** 

Utah County Attorney Jeff Gray provides the following statement on today’s shooting at Utah Valley University: 

The Utah County Attorney’s Office is working closely with our local, state, and federal law enforcement partners as they investigate the circumstances of today’s tragic shooting at Utah Valley University. I have also been coordinating with Utah Attorney General Derek Brown and officials at the United States Attorney’s Office. As with other active criminal investigations, my prosecutors will assist by reviewing affidavits for search warrants and providing other legal guidance to law enforcement. 

Once the investigation is complete, we will carefully review the evidence and decide on the appropriate charges. The Utah County Attorney’s Office is committed to bringing those responsible for this tragedy to justice. 

We express our deepest concern for Charlie Kirk, his family, friends, and the many others who were impacted by this horrific event.
