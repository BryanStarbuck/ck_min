# **I. INTRODUCTION** 

Plaintiff, the State of Utah, and Defendant, Tyler James Robinson, by and through their respective counsel of record, hereby file this _Joint Status Report_ setting forth each of the parties’ proposed redactions to the official transcript of the sealed hearing which occurred in this matter on October 24, 2025, in compliance with this Court’s order dated November 10, 2025. 

The parties have met and conferred concerning the proposed redactions, bearing in mind 

the interests identified in Utah Rule of Judicial Administration 4-202 et seq. 

The parties agree that with the exception of those portions of the transcript as to which 

one or the other party proposes redaction, the balance of the transcript may be classified as 

public. With respect to each proposed redaction, the justification is identified in parenthesis.
