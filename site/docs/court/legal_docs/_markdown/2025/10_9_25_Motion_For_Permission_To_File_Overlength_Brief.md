Defendant, Tyler James Robinson, by and through undersigned counsel, hereby requests permission to file his overlength _Motion to Appear at All In-Person Proceedings in Civilian Clothing and Without Restraints and Memorandum in Support_ , consisting of 18 pages (not 

1 

including the certificate of service). In accordance with Utah Rules of Civil Procedure 7, good 

cause exists to allow and consider Defendant’s overlength motion based upon the following: 

1. Defendant stands accused of aggravated murder and the State has already notified the Defendant of its intention to seek the death penalty. 

2. The motion raises constitutional concerns integral to Defendant’s rights to due process and a fair trial. 

3. Defendant must convey what is necessary both to fully inform the Court of the applicable legal standards and issues, and to ensure all arguments are adequately preserved. 

4. While Defendant has endeavored to be concise in briefing, the additional length is 

necessary to meet his burdens of persuasion and preservation. 

ACCORDINGLY, based upon this showing of good cause, the Defendant respectfully requests 

that this Court allow Defendant to file an overlength motion. 

A proposed order accompanies this motion.
