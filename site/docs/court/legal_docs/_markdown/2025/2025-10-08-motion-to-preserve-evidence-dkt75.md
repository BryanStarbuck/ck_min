COMES NOW Defendant, Tyler James Robinson, by and through undersigned counsel, and respectfully moves this Honorable Court to enter an Order directing the Utah County Attorney’s Office 

1 

to preserve all evidence in its custody or under its control, or in the control of their agents, including 

but not limited to any law enforcement agency involved in the investigation of this case. 

Further, Mr. Robinson requests the Court to order the Utah County Attorney’s Office and any law enforcement agency involved in the investigation of this case to notify defense counsel prior to any potentially destructive testing and/or examination, pursuant to U.C.A. §77-11c-303(3) and U.C.A. §77-11c-401(5)(a). 

This motion is made so that defense counsel may effectively meet their constitutional duties to represent Mr. Robinson and is made pursuant to U.R.Cr.P. 16, U.C.A. §77-11c-301, U.C.A. §77-11c302, U.C.A. §77-11c-401, the Fifth, Sixth, Eighth and Fourteenth Amendments to the United States Constitution, and Article I, §§ 1, 7, and 12  of the Constitution of the State of Utah. 

DATED this 8th day of October, 2025.
