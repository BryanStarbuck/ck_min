RE: Robinson v. State Appellate Case No. 20251388-SC Trial Court No. 251403576 

Dear Counsel: 

Please be advised that the Petition for Interlocutory Appeal in this case was filed with the Utah Supreme Court on November 17, 2025. The case number is 20251388-SC and should be indicated on any future filings. 

If the Court requires a response to the petition, the parties will be notified, pursuant to Rule 5(f) of the Utah Rules of Appellate Procedure. 

All documents must be filed with the appellate court clerk. Documents submitted by attorneys must be submitted through the appellate e-filing system. Self-represented

parties may file by email, by mail, or in person. Documents must be a searchable PDF of no more than 7 MB. Large PDF documents must be divided into multiple files of no more than 7 MB each. Unless the court orders otherwise, the filing of documents and briefs is timely if the electronic filing is received before midnight on the last day for filing. A document is deemed filed when the electronic filing is received. Sending an electronic filing is an electronic signature, and it carries all of the representations and consequences under Rule 40, Utah Rules of Appellate Procedure. If a document other than a brief is delivered electronically, a paper copy does not need to be delivered. See Rules 21 and 26 of the Utah Rules of Appellate Procedure. All risks associated with filing electronically are borne by the sender. The email address for the Supreme Court is: supremecourt@utcourts.gov. The email address for the Court of Appeals is: courtofappeals@utcourts.gov. The court emails all documents to the email listed on the State Bar's website for attorneys, or to the email provided by self-represented parties.
