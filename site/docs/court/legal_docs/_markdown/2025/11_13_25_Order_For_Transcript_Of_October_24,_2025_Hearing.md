Defendant. 

At the request of the parties, the court held a closed hearing on October 24, 2025. The hearing was transcribed by the certified court reporter, Noteworthy Reporting. 

The parties have filed a joint motion to classify the transcript and audio of that hearing as private. Because the press has requested notice of all motions to close or classify a record, the court is required to hold a closure hearing in accordance with Rule 4-202.04 of the Utah Rules of Judicial Administration. 

To prepare for that hearing, the court orders the following: 

- e Noteworthy Reporting is ordered to file a transcript of the October 24, 2025, hearing with the court. The transcript must be filed as a private document. See URJA 4-202.04(3)(A) (requiring the court to deny access to the record until it issues an order). 

- ¢ The transcript is subject to the following protective order pending further order from the court: The transcript must be viewed only by the attorneys of record in this case. The transcript must not be disseminated to any other person or entity, including the attorneys’ teams. The attorneys must not make any copies, electronic or otherwise, of the transcript. 

1 

- e The parties must review the transcript before the closure hearing to determine if there are interests favoring closure of the entire transcript or if portions of the transcript may be classified as public. The parties must consider the interests listed in rule 4-202 as well as the parties’ right to a fair trial. The parties must also consider whether there are reasonable alternatives to closure, as required by rule 4-202.04(6)(c). 

- e The parties may request redactions of the transcript, if appropriate. 

The court will hold a closure hearing in this case. The closure hearing will remain open to the extent possible, but the court will consider closing portions of the hearing to allow for the discussion of secure information by the parties. Representative of the media will be allowed to participate in the closed portions of the hearing but are ordered not to disclose any part of the closed hearing to their clients or any other person or entity.
