# Case Stage Map — Which Files Track Which Stage

:::caution Legal Disclaimer
**Tyler Robinson is charged, not convicted**, and is **presumed innocent**. Nothing on this page states as fact that any living person committed a crime, destroyed evidence, or acted improperly. Court filings in this case are subject to a **gag order** and **sealing**, so the public record is incomplete by design.
:::

## What This Page Is For

The court section of this site is spread across several directories, and each of them tracks a **different stage** of the same case. This page is the map: given a stage of the proceeding, it says which files hold that stage's record.

## The Directory Map

| Stage of the case | Where the record lives | What that directory holds |
|---|---|---|

## The Three Page Families In This Section

Since the September 1, 2026 run, this section has three kinds of page and they do different jobs. Knowing which you are reading is the fastest way to use it.

| Family | Count | What it does | Read it when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Spine** | 6 | [Overview](/court/ai_attorney/overview), [Method](/court/ai_attorney/method), [Trial Cursor](/court/ai_attorney/trial-cursor), [The Day-One Track](/court/ai_attorney/the-day-one-track), [Two Tracks Compared](/court/ai_attorney/two-tracks-compared), and this map | You want the rules, the posture, or the comparison as a whole |
| **Contention** | 9 | One position the exercise would advance and have to prove, with its chain of reasoning, its full gap-analysis table, and the single result that would defeat it | You want the argument end to end |
| **Instrument** | 37 | One motion, subpoena, expert retention or witness, in the settled eight-section template | You want the actual document a lawyer would file |

A contention page **never** duplicates the body of an instrument page. It states the chain, carries the table, and links out. An instrument usually serves more than one contention, and a contention usually needs several instruments.

### Which stage each family belongs to

| Family | Stage it is anchored to |
|---|---|
| Spine | The case as a whole, and the cursor's current position |
| Contention | The full arc — pretrial discovery through the defense case at trial |
| Instrument | A single, named stage, stated in its own four-row stage table |

## How Each Difference Page Maps To A Stage

Every page in this section carries a **Where This Belongs In The Case** table with the same four rows in the same shape: the stage, when that stage runs, and the status of that step. Read down the status column of any page and you can see immediately whether the step is unavailable, available now and not taken, or simply not yet due.

The status values are used consistently across all thirty-seven instrument pages:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Not visible on the public record** | The step is available now. Nothing public shows it being taken. Filings are gagged and sealed, so this is a statement about visibility, not about counsel. |
| **Still fully open** | The step belongs to a stage the case has not reached. It cannot have been missed. |
| **Not asked of any witness** | The witness testified. The transcripts and press accounts do not show this line being pursued. |
| **Never subpoenaed publicly** | A witness who could answer was not called and no public subpoena exists. |
| **None noticed publicly** | An expert of this kind does not appear on any public notice. Defense expert work is confidential, so this is weak evidence of anything. |
| **Window closed** | Added on the September 1, 2026 run. The step was available in autumn 2025 and the underlying record has since aged out of its retention schedule. This is a fact about storage, not about anybody's conduct. See [the Day-One Track](/court/ai_attorney/the-day-one-track). |

### The verification tier on every claim

Since the September 1, 2026 run, every right-hand assertion in this section also carries a **tier**, and the tier is what licenses the wording.

| Tier | What it means | What it lets a page say |
|---|---|---|
| **T1** | A **certified** transcript we hold covers that witness on that day, and it was searched | "The certified transcript we hold does not show this line being pursued." **Only T1 supports the words "was not asked."** |
| **T2** | A filed document in the archive shows the motion made or not on the docket we hold | "No filing in the archive we hold requests this." |
| **T3** | Press, pool or secondary reporting only | "Not found in the material we hold." Nothing stronger. |
| **T4** | Sealed, gagged, or a stage not reached | "Not visible on the public record," or "not yet due." |

We hold certified transcripts for **preliminary-hearing Day 4 and Day 5 only**. That single fact is why most claims in this section sit at T3, and why obtaining the certified Day 1, 2 and 3 transcripts is the highest-value acquisition available to this exercise.

## How A Reader Should Use The Stage Map

1. Start with the [trial cursor](/court/ai_attorney/trial-cursor) to see where the case actually is.
2. Read only the rows that are **available now** if you want to know what could be happening today.
3. Ignore the "still fully open" rows if you are looking for criticism — those steps are not due.
4. Cross-check any claim about testimony against the [transcripts](/court/Preliminary_Hearing/overview) rather than against this section.
5. Check the **tier** on any claim before you weigh it. A T3 "not found" is a statement about our own visibility and very little else.
6. Read [the Day-One Track](/court/ai_attorney/the-day-one-track) if you want to know which questions can still be answered and which can no longer be answered by anybody.
7. Take the whole thing to the [score card](/court/score_card/overview) and decide for yourself.

## What This Section Cannot See

The public record of this case is deliberately incomplete. A gag order has been in force since December 2025. Forensic material is sealed. Defense expert work is confidential by rule and is disclosed only if the expert will testify. Sealed motions are not listed publicly.

Every one of those mechanisms means that a step described here as invisible may have been taken months ago. That is not a caveat added for politeness. It is the central limitation of the exercise.

A step described here as "not on the public record" may already have been taken privately, may have been considered and rejected for good reason, or may not yet be due at this stage of the case.
