# AI Defense Attorney — What We Would Do Differently

:::caution Legal Disclaimer
**Tyler Robinson is charged, not convicted**, and is **presumed innocent**. Nothing on this page states as fact that any living person committed a crime, destroyed evidence, or acted improperly. Court filings in this case are subject to a **gag order** and **sealing**, so the public record is incomplete by design.
:::

The **AI defense attorney** section is a written simulation. It asks what an idealised defense lawyer would do in **State of Utah v. Tyler Robinson**, Case No. **251403576**, and it compares that against what the real defense has done **on the public record**.

It exists because this site's [cause-of-death review](/Cause_of_Death/overview) concludes that Charlie Kirk was killed by an explosive device — most plausibly a shaped charge concealed in or on the microphone — and not by the .30-06 rifle round of the government's account. That conclusion is a **physical claim**, and physical claims are decided in courtrooms by motions, subpoenas and experts. This section is about those instruments.

The exercise runs a specific counterfactual, and it is worth stating before anything else.

> **Our AI defense attorney was retained on September 12, 2025** — the day of the first judicial order in this case — **and has been counsel of record ever since.** It joined nothing late, inherited no file, and defaulted on nothing. Every demand it makes, it made at the first moment the rules allowed.

That matters far less for the clever motions than for one blunt fact: **in autumn 2025 the evidence was still there.** The soil had not been hauled, the courtyard not resurfaced, the vehicles not released, the camera systems not overwritten, the telematics not aged out. [The Day-One Track](/court/ai_attorney/the-day-one-track) sets that calendar out date by date, with a column for when each window shut. [Two Tracks Compared](/court/ai_attorney/two-tracks-compared) sets the counterfactual against the visible record at the level of the whole case — including the places where the two tracks are doing **the same thing**, which is most of the conventional ground.

Start with the [method](/court/ai_attorney/method) if you want the rules this section follows, and with the [trial cursor](/court/ai_attorney/trial-cursor) if you want to know where the case actually is today.

:::tip Read this first
The case **has not been bound over**. [Bind-over oral argument is being heard today, September 1, 2026](/court/Days_in_Court/2026-09-01-bindover-argument), and no ruling has issued. Roughly half of what follows is not yet due, and every page says so in its own stage table. **A step that is not yet due has not been missed.**
:::

## The Nine Contentions

This is the spine of the section. Each contention is a position an imaginary defense team would **advance and have to prove** — not a finding this site asserts — and each has its own page carrying the chain of reasoning, the full gap-analysis table, and an honest statement of the single result that would defeat it.

|---|---|---|---|

| **T2** | **[Federal handling has kept material out of the case](/court/ai_attorney/federal-handling-and-nondisclosure)** | The scene was altered and material was routed or withheld, including roughly ten inches of earth | Route 2 |

### The Two Routes To An Acquittal

Every contention above says which route it serves, because a defense does not have to prove who did it. It has to stop twelve jurors being sure this defendant did.

* **Route 1 — the mechanism.** If the fatal wound was not caused by a rifle round, the identity of the person on the roof stops mattering and the prosecution fails at an earlier link than the one being fought over. This is the shortest route and it is **T1**.
* **Route 2 — the investigation itself.** If the scene was altered, if the tests that would have settled the mechanism were never ordered, and if every federal decision reached the courtroom secondhand, then the jury is being asked to convict on the output of a process it cannot inspect. **Route 2 requires no alternative perpetrator at all**, and it is the route most readers underrate. Every discovery instrument below feeds it whether or not it ever produces a document — because a refusal is also an answer.

:::warning The gateway
Four contentions — T3, T4, T6, T7, and the federal half of T9 — depend on one procedural step happening first. Without a noticed and supported [alternative-perpetrator defense](/court/ai_attorney/alternative-perpetrator-notice), every intelligence subpoena downstream of it is irrelevant and gets quashed. That notice is filed **after arraignment on an information**, which this case has not reached. Those pages are written in the order a court would require, not the order the internet argues them.
:::

## What The AI Defense Attorney Would Do Differently

Each page below states **one** difference, then what the defense did instead, then which stage it belongs to and which witness or discovery target it runs through.

### Cluster A — The Mechanism Of Death

The alternative account of this killing is that an explosive device, most plausibly concealed in or on the microphone, caused the fatal wound. That is a physical claim and it is testable. Everything in this cluster is aimed at testing it while the items still exist.

* **[Move For Court-Ordered Explosive Residue Testing](/court/ai_attorney/explosive-residue-testing)** — Compel GC-MS / IMS residue testing on every retained item, and force production of the list of tests the government actually ordered.
* **[Demand The Chemical Composition Of The Fragments](/court/ai_attorney/fragments)** — Government witnesses called every fragment from the body a bullet fragment, but no one has testified what they are made of; a demand for their chemistry (metal, brass and lead for a bullet, or plastic and polymer for an exploding microphone) is where the defense could win outright. The full argument is on the linked page.
* **[Subpoena The Soil — Who Ordered The Ten-Inch Dig](/court/ai_attorney/soil-excavation-subpoena)** — Force production of the excavation work order, the hauling manifest, and the landfill destination for the soil taken from under the tent.
* **[Force Disclosure Of The Seized Security-Detail Clothing](/court/ai_attorney/security-team-clothing)** — Demand evidence numbers, storage location and test results for the clothing taken from the protective detail — and the hospital scrubs that replaced it.
* **[Force The Custody File On The Transport SUV](/court/ai_attorney/transport-suv-custody)** — Get the evidence log for the Yukon: when it was seized, when released, to whom, what was swabbed, and what the burnt object in it was.
* **[Retain An Independent Forensic Pathologist And Cross The Medical Examiner](/court/ai_attorney/independent-autopsy-review)** — Get a defense pathologist onto the wound morphology, and put the medical examiner on the stand instead of letting a case agent read her report.
* **[Subpoena The Microphone Itself](/court/ai_attorney/microphone-hardware-subpoena)** — Demand the physical microphone and transmitter, its serial number, its procurement chain and the AV crew's custody of it.
* **[Retain A Blast Expert And An Acoustics Expert](/court/ai_attorney/blast-and-acoustics-experts)** — Notice two experts nobody has: an explosives and shaped-charge specialist, and an acoustician on the recorded sound and its direction.
* **[File A Spoliation Motion Over The Altered Crime Scene](/court/ai_attorney/scene-alteration-spoliation)** — Ask the court for sanctions and an adverse-inference instruction because the scene was destroyed before the defense existed.
* **[Retain A Point-Cloud Expert For The State's Own 3D Scan](/court/ai_attorney/faro-point-cloud-expert)** — 660 GB of raw FARO laser-scan data of the scene reached the defense in July 2026. A registered point cloud measures trajectory geometry and surface damage. Nobody has retained the expert who can read it.

### Cluster B — The Federal Witnesses Nobody Called

Across five days of sworn testimony exactly one FBI witness appeared, and she was a laboratory examiner called by the defense. Every federal decision in this case has reached the courtroom secondhand, through Utah officers repeating what they were told.

* **[Put FBI Case Agents On The Stand](/court/ai_attorney/fbi-agents-on-the-stand)** — One FBI witness testified in five days, and she was a lab examiner. Subpoena the agents who actually directed the scene.
* **[Establish Whether A Bomb Sweep Was Ever Run](/court/ai_attorney/bomb-dogs-and-eod-sweep)** — Get the canine and EOD records: was there a pre-event sweep, a post-event sweep, and what did either find?
* **[Subpoena The Hospital And Transport Record](/court/ai_attorney/hospital-and-transport-record)** — Get the trauma record, the intake documentation and the footage: the only account of the wound written by people with no stake in the case.
* **[Subpoena The Dispatch And CAD Record](/court/ai_attorney/dispatch-and-cad-records)** — Machine-generated, timestamped, written by nobody for litigation. The cheapest way to establish what the investigation was actually doing in the first hours.
* **[Demand The Detention And Release Record](/court/ai_attorney/detention-and-release-records)** — And the arrival-order record. Who was already at the address when local police got there is establishable from four independent machine-generated sources. Records only; nobody is named.

### Cluster C — The Foreign-Nexus Defense

The material citizen investigators care about most is the hardest to get into a courtroom, and it all depends on one procedural step being taken first. This cluster is written in the order a court would require, not the order the internet argues it.

* **[File The Alternative-Perpetrator Notice First](/court/ai_attorney/alternative-perpetrator-notice)** — The gateway motion. Without a noticed third-party defense, every intelligence subpoena downstream of it is irrelevant and gets quashed.
* **[Subpoena The Halted Foreign-Nexus Inquiry](/court/ai_attorney/nctc-halted-inquiry-subpoena)** — The strongest on-record intelligence thread in the case: a named official saying his foreign-nexus inquiry was halted. Subpoena him.
* **[Subpoena The Organization's Own Records](/court/ai_attorney/tpusa-organizational-subpoenas)** — The event was run by a private organisation. Its contracts, security decisions and AV vendor records are reachable without fighting any agency.
* **[Whether To Call The Victim's Widow And Inner Circle](/court/ai_attorney/family-and-organization-witnesses)** — The question people ask most, answered honestly: what a court would actually allow, and why an AI defense attorney would still not do it.
* **[Records Demand On The September 9 Meeting Week](/court/ai_attorney/fort-huachuca-records)** — Ask for the documents — visitor logs, flight manifests, scheduling — and name nobody.
* **[Subpoena The AV Procurement Chain](/court/ai_attorney/av-procurement-chain-subpoena)** — Purchase order to delivery signature, with serial numbers. Ordinary subpoenas to private vendors: the documentary and reachable half of the hardware question.
* **[Audit What Was Collected Against What Was Analysed](/court/ai_attorney/cell-site-and-tower-dump-discovery)** — At least 25,253 CSLI records are in the discovery corpus. Somebody chose which subset to read, and that decision is discoverable.
* **[The Foreign-Carrier Route, And Why It Would Be Refused](/court/ai_attorney/foreign-carrier-records-mlat)** — Written knowing a court would likely deny it — and then showing that the question is answerable from material the State already holds.

### Cluster D — Forensics, Suppression And Process

The conventional capital-defense ground, where the defense team is already working and already winning. These are refinements and accelerations rather than departures.

* **[Move To Exclude Any Revived Lead-Comparison Evidence](/court/ai_attorney/ballistics-daubert-challenge)** — Get ahead of the sealed report: challenge admissibility before any lead-comparison result arrives, not after.
* **[Move To Suppress The Attributed Confession](/court/ai_attorney/confession-suppression-motion)** — If he was in custody with his phone seized at 6:25 PM, who typed at 7:57 PM? Move to suppress and to compel the custody record.
* **[Order An Independent Re-Extraction Of The Devices](/court/ai_attorney/independent-device-reexamination)** — Do not accept the government's extraction report. Re-extract from the original forensic images with a defense examiner.
* **[Convert The Brady Demands Into A Sanctions Motion](/court/ai_attorney/brady-enforcement-and-sanctions)** — Stop renewing requests that go unanswered. Move for sanctions, exclusion, or dismissal, and make the court own the delay.
* **[Reconsider The Secrecy Posture](/court/ai_attorney/gag-order-and-publicity)** — The defense called the ATF result exculpatory and then moved to seal it. Ask whether secrecy still helps this defendant.
* **[Move To Suppress — There Is No Public Warrant For The Scene](/court/ai_attorney/crime-scene-warrant-gap-suppression)** — Twenty-five public warrants and not one names the UVU campus. This motion does not ask the State for a favour; it asks the court to take evidence away.
* **[Compel The State's Expert Notice, Then Move To Exclude](/court/ai_attorney/state-expert-notice-exclusion)** — No State expert notice appears anywhere on an 867-row docket, and every examiner who testified in five days was called by the defense.
* **[Demand The Exhibits, And The Six Missing Numbers](/court/ai_attorney/exhibit-production-and-bates-audit)** — The State published Bates numbers for 34 exhibits and the public holds none of them. Its own list skips 17, 18, 26, 27, 28 and 29.
* **[Move For The Audio The Court Already Ordered Released](/court/ai_attorney/hearing-audio-and-transcript-release)** — A judge ordered the October 2025 sealed-hearing audio unsealed in redacted form. No copy has ever appeared. Audio is also the cheapest route to the missing transcripts.

### Cluster E — Aircraft And Ground Movement

The half of the flight-and-vehicle question that a state court can actually reach, written with the same control-aircraft discipline the site applies to its own flight-data recovery — including the finding that had to be retracted.

* **[Subpoena The Rental Agreements](/court/ai_attorney/rental-vehicle-subpoenas)** — Agreements, telematics, tolls and damage inspections. An ordinary subpoena to a private company, and the reachable half of the vehicle question.
* **[Subpoena The FBO And Ground-Handling Records](/court/ai_attorney/fbo-and-ground-handling-records)** — An ADS-B trace shows where an aircraft was. The signed, timestamped fuel ticket shows what it did on the ground.
* **[Getting The Recovered Flight Data Into Evidence](/court/ai_attorney/adsb-authentication-and-custody)** — Rule 901(b)(9) and 902(13)/(14), the custodian declaration, and why the control test *is* the authentication.

### Cluster F — The Reachable Records

Three subpoenas that need no gateway, fight no agency, and would be granted as a matter of routine. If any part of this section is worth filing tomorrow, it is this cluster.

* **[Subpoena The University For Its Own Property Records](/court/ai_attorney/university-property-records-subpoena)** — Occupancy, keying, booking, access and the utility consumption series, which shows an occupancy pattern with no witness at all.
* **[The Frontage Camera Canvass](/court/ai_attorney/house-frontage-camera-canvass)** — The clearest illustration on the site of what a day-one defense buys, and of what the retention clock takes away.
* **[Demand The Campus Camera Inventory And Retention Record](/court/ai_attorney/campus-camera-inventory-and-retention)** — The State produced footage. From how many cameras, out of how many that exist? Without the denominator the numerator means nothing.
