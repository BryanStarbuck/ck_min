### Strongest counterarguments conceded this run

The section is worse than useless if it only ever confirms itself. These were written to
persuade and they are on the pages.

* **Standing may be fatal to the warrant-gap motion outright.** Under *Rakas v. Illinois*
  a defendant cannot suppress a search of a place he has no expectation of privacy in. A
  university rooftop reached by climbing a wall, and a rifle left in bushes across a public
  street, are close to textbook. A court could deny the motion without ever reaching
  whether a warrant existed.
* **The expert-notice statute says the opposite of what that page wants, in its first
  line.** Utah Code § 77-17-13 applies "at trial or any hearing, **excluding** a
  preliminary hearing held pursuant to Rule 7." Nothing was evaded, and Judge Graf denied
  the defense's constitutional attack on that architecture on 22 June 2026.
* **Proximity is what the innocent explanation predicts.** UVU's own 2019 board minutes
  say it bought the house *because* it is contiguous to campus with excellent access from
  Campus Drive. Closeness is the stated reason the institution owns the building.
* **Retaining a point-cloud expert may build the State's best exhibit.** If the registered
  model shows a clean line of sight at an angle consistent with the State's account, the
  defense will have paid for it and will have to live with it in discovery.
* **The ADS-B material currently fails Rule 902(14) on its own terms.** The tree holds
  25,446 `.meta.json` provenance records and **not one contains a hash of the payload it
  describes**. They record URL, status, byte count and retrieval time — a chain-of-custody
  record one field short of the rule.
* **Three links in the T5 chain rest on archived scanner audio clipped and narrated by
  citizen researchers**, not on any certified transcript, agency release or filed document.
* **A foreign Mobile Country Code proves nothing about anybody.** A count taken without a
  control window at the same towers on an ordinary day is the single most misusable figure
  in this investigation.
* **The parties agreed to the exhibit blackout and the judge approved it** — and the
  defense sought *more* restriction than it got.
