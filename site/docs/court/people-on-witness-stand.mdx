# People on Witness Stand

:::caution Legal Disclaimer
Appearing on this roster means a person **reportedly testified**, had a **recorded/Rule 1102 statement played or admitted**, or is the **subject of an open question** about who was on the roof — **not** an accusation of crime. **Tyler Robinson is charged, not convicted.** All persons are **presumed innocent**. Rosters come from press, pool reporting, and hearing transcripts, and may be incomplete.
:::

This is the **full table of contents** for people tied to the **witness stand** in **State of Utah v. Tyler Robinson** — primarily the **07/06–07/10/2026** [preliminary hearing](./Days_in_Court/overview). For **non-sworn event interviews** at UVU, see Witnesses (different evidentiary weight).

The [Court](./overview) and People Level 2 pages each carry a short **"People on Witness Stand"** list (top 19 + **More Witnesses** → this page).

## A. Live sworn witnesses (preliminary hearing) — complete set of 7

Transcript + press audit (July 2026): **exactly seven people** took the **live** stand and were examined. **No other FBI special agents** took the live stand; **Amanda Bakker** is the **only FBI** live witness. Defense called **three** forensic experts (Bakker, Karner, Oliver). Prosecution called four Utah LE witnesses (Bagley, Hull, Faumuina, Davis).

| # | Person | Agency / role | Called by | Hearing days | What testimony addressed (reported) |
|---|--------|---------------|-----------|--------------|-------------------------------------|
| 1 | **Chris Bagley** | Former UVU / Spanish Fork PD | **Prosecution** | [07/06/2026](./Days_in_Court/2026-07-06-prelim-day-1) | Shot, Losee roof, screwdriver, gravel/"sniper pad," holster, body-cam stop |
| 2 | **David Hull** | SBI / Utah DPS case agent | **Prosecution** | [07/06](./Days_in_Court/2026-07-06-prelim-day-1)–[07/07](./Days_in_Court/2026-07-07-prelim-day-2) | Surveillance montage, ID, autopsy foundation, discarded leads, paving awareness |
| 3 | **Jennifer Faumuina** | SBI sergeant (crime scene / PE) | **Prosecution** | [07/07](./Days_in_Court/2026-07-07-prelim-day-2), [07/09](./Days_in_Court/2026-07-09-prelim-day-4) | Rifle in towel, lab routing, engraved rounds, Dremel/home casings |
| 4 | **Amanda Bakker** | **FBI** DNA examiner (Quantico) | **Defense** (out of order Day 2) | [07/07/2026](./Days_in_Court/2026-07-07-prelim-day-2) | Mixture DNA on towel + screwdriver; "possible contributor"; policy limits |
| 5 | **Brian Davis** | SBI case agent | **Prosecution** | [07/08](./Days_in_Court/2026-07-08-prelim-day-3)–[07/09](./Days_in_Court/2026-07-09-prelim-day-4) | Surrender, booking, interviews, Twiggs foundation, texts/note |
| 6 | **Samantha Karner** | **ATF** firearm/toolmark examiner | **Defense** | [07/09/2026](./Days_in_Court/2026-07-09-prelim-day-4) | Autopsy jacket fragment vs rifle — **inconclusive** |
| 7 | **Caitlin Oliver** | **ATF** DNA section chief | **Defense** | [07/10/2026](./Days_in_Court/2026-07-10-prelim-day-5) | Rifle DNA statistics; transfer / error-rate cross |
