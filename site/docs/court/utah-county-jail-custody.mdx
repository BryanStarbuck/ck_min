# Utah County Jail & Custody

Tyler Robinson has been held at the **Utah County Jail** (Utah County Security Center) in **Spanish
Fork** since shortly after his September 11–12, 2025 arrest. He is **charged, not convicted**.

## Custody timeline (high level)

* **Sept 11, 2025** — Surrender and Miranda encounter; Bates **003996-R2** video at **6:25 PM** per

* **Sept 12, 2025** — Booking and formal aggravated-murder charges per [timeline](/Timeline/Sept_8_to_13).
* **Ongoing** — Capital pretrial detention without bail per first-appearance order.

### Arrest-time dispute (booking sheet vs. database)

Research-file notes flag a discrepancy that citizen investigators treat as significant. The **Inmate
Booking Sheet / PC Statement** reportedly lists an arrest time of **10:00 PM September 11, 2025**,
while the police database records **04:00 September 12, 2025** (Agency Inmate ID **460956**, OTN
**70090584**, Probable Cause ID **189904**). The affidavit places Robinson's arrival at the Washington
County Sheriff's Office — **accompanied by his parents** — at approximately **10:26 PM (2226 hours)**
on September 11. Commentators argue the later 4:00 AM entry reflects when the **Affidavit of Probable
Cause** was reviewed (reportedly by a judge), not the surrender itself. This is **attributed
analysis** of public documents, not a court finding.

## Jailhouse informant thread

Court commentary describes an inmate held in the **same jail** receiving a **transport order** from
Judge Graf at a Robinson preliminary hearing. Commentators infer the inmate may be positioned as a **jailhouse informant** claiming Robinson made
incriminating statements. That inference is **commentary**, not established fact.

We do **not** claim jail staff or prosecutors manufactured confessions or acted illegally.

## Remote appearances and security

Reporting on high-profile capital cases notes **heavy courthouse security** and lawyers declining
comment under gag orders ([gag orders](./gag-orders-sealing)). Robinson appeared **in person** for
some scheduling hearings (e.g., **Oct 30, 2025** per Fox 13 reporting cited in research notes) while
other dates used video.

## Custody optics in public commentary

- Remote appearances and **heavy security escorts** appear in July 2026 hearing clips shared on X — process visuals, not guilt evidence.
- Jailhouse-informant discourse (including transport order threads) remains **speculative**; document orders and testimony when they exist.
- Citizen investigators still flag **missing Washington County lobby CCTV** of the surrender night as a custody-timeline gap that Utah County jail records alone cannot fill — see Merchant & Travel CCTV.
- Conditions, no-contact orders, and mental-health evaluation claims should be sourced to **filings or reputable reporting**, not anonymous jail rumor.
