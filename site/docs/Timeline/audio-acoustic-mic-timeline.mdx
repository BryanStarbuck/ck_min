# Audio, Acoustic & Mic Timeline (Shot Timing & Analysis)

This page runs **two clocks**. The first is measured in **milliseconds** — the arrival-order sequence that acoustic analysts read off the [Canon XA55](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio) broadcast camera at **12:23:30 PM MDT** on September 10, 2025. The second runs in **months** — how the under-shirt mic placement, the RODE transmitter, the shirt-pull footage, and the burnt object recovered from the SUV surfaced publicly between late 2025 and mid-2026 and were assembled into an argument that the sound did not originate on a roof. The government's narrative remains a **rifle gunshot**; most citizen investigators on X have concluded instead that Charlie was killed by an [explosive at his microphone](/Mic/overview). [Tyler Robinson is charged, not convicted](/court/case-overview). Every entry below is labeled by sourcing strength.

## Clock One: The Acoustic Sequence (milliseconds)

The reference recorder is a **Canon XA55** professional camcorder positioned roughly **46 metres** from the tent. Unlike phone recordings, it captured **uncompressed audio on four channels at 48,000 samples per second** — the reason analysts treat it as the day's cleanest acoustic witness. Phones nearer the stage smear the events into one noise; the distance gave the Canon an acoustic buffer.

* **T+0 ms — 12:23:30 PM MDT (reported)** — The shot. This is the master anchor used across the site and disputed against a **12:27 PM** figure on [12:23 vs 12:27 shooting time](./12-23-shooting-time) and at shot time 12:23 vs 12:27.

* **T+0 to ~T+100 ms (claimed)** — Analysts report the **high frequencies arrived first**, spread across more than 100 milliseconds rather than arriving together. Their argument: a supersonic projectile produces crack-before-boom separation, whereas a single blast at one point radiates all frequencies to the microphone simultaneously. See Canon audio detonation analysis.

* **T+114 ms (claimed)** — First distinct low-frequency event, interpreted as **Mach cone energy** from a supersonic round.

* **T+202 ms (claimed)** — Second distinct event, interpreted as **muzzle blast originating roughly 120 metres away** — consistent in bearing with the rooftop firing position in the charging documents and with the rifle described at Mauser 30-06 rifle.

* **T+321 ms (claimed)** — Third and **loudest** low-frequency event, which the analysts place approximately **46 metres from the camera** — i.e. at the stage, under the tent, where Kirk was seated. They argue it is not an echo or reflection because it is the **strongest** low-frequency peak in the entire recording, and echoes attenuate. See Acoustic Two-Event Split, the Canon XA55 third boom, and the tent structure under Charlie.

* **T+321 ms, interpretation (claimed)** — Proponents conclude two events at two locations roughly **185 ms apart**: a rifle at 120 m and a detonation at the stage. Their framing is that a single shooter cannot produce a detonation under a tent 120 m from the rifle, and a device cannot produce a muzzle blast 120 m away. This is an **inference from waveform structure**, not a court finding. Defenders of the official account attribute the peak to reverberation off the Losee Center; the analysts answer that it is the **loudest** low-frequency event in the file, and reflections arrive weaker, not stronger.

* **Same second — local-source confidence (claimed)** — Acoustic analyst @CorneliusActs publicly claims **greater than 99%** probability the source was **local on/near Kirk’s person** and **under 1%** distant rooftop, with residual uncertainty only because original multi-track forensic files were not examined. Citizen analysis, not a court finding.

* **Same second, independent analysis (claimed)** — A separate analyst applied **acoustic triangulation** — the technique used for artillery sound-ranging — to multiple smartphone recordings. Across repeated runs, one location kept resolving: **in the courtyard, within about 2 metres of Charlie Kirk**, not on a roof. The analyst notes this would make the widely reported witness description of a **"firecracker"** sound coherent. See acoustics from the south and the UVU courtyard.

* **Same second — Freqazoid TDoA geometry (claimed)** — A circulated acoustic-forensics write-up associated with **@Freqazoid26** (promoted by @dustinodaffer, ~June 2026) concludes via **TDoA**, impact-to-muzzle timing, and reflections that the origin is the **south courtyard ~50–95 m** (best estimate ~**72.5 m**), treating Losee Center (~130 m) arrivals as **late higher-order echoes**, not the direct blast. This is a **fourth geometry** distinct from pure roof, on-body detonation, and teachers-balcony claims.

* **Same second — third-path multilateration (claimed)** — @troofevades TDOA multilateration on four synchronized phone recordings argues the waveform fits **neither** a pure Losee rooftop gunshot **nor** a single-point on-body detonation.

* **Same second, competing direction claim (claimed)** — A sound-and-acoustics engineer walking through a 3D model argued the sound came **from the south, above the teachers' balcony** — another candidate origin that agrees with neither the rooftop account nor the courtyard triangulation. Held at acoustics — south.

* **Direction from human behavior (claimed)** — Video analysts (@ClydeMax369) argue involuntary **acoustic orienting** in the crowd points **left of Kirk**. Separate witness compilations (@DiligentDenizen) collect reports of sound from the **east / mountains** (elevated) and “firecracker” character — five-plus witnesses said to disagree with a pure Losee direction.

* **Immediately after (reported)** — Witness accounts of what people heard diverge sharply, from a single sharp crack to a pop or firecracker; the spread is catalogued at witness audio conflicts and [witness testimony discrepancies](./witness-testimony-discrepancies).

* **Source-media integrity (claimed)** — File forensics on circulated Canon-derived clips (@troofevades, March 2026) allege the native **XA55 container** for MVI_0625.MP4 was stripped, bitrate cut ~10×, length cut from ~11:15 to a ~32 s clip, and **metadata fields added** (including titles/dates not native to the original). Strengthens the open question: has any lab received **uncompressed multi-channel originals**? See [Canon XA55 Audio](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio).

## Clock Two: How the Mic Evidence Emerged

### The setup, before the event

* **Pre-event, September 10, 2025 (reported)** — The wireless lavalier is placed **under Charlie Kirk's shirt** rather than clipped outside it, with the transmitter pack on his **left side at the belt**. Researchers note the mic already had a **furry windscreen** fitted — a component that only functions in open air — and that other outdoor debates show the same rig worn **on the outside** of his shirt. The placement is attributed to an AV staffer. We assume that every TPUSA employee and every audio-visual contractor working at UVU that day had no knowledge of any explosive that may have been in the mic, even if they handled the mic. See mic under, not over, mic under shirt, and [AV team mic setup](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup).

* **Pre-event (claimed, model unresolved)** — Which **RODE** unit he was wearing is not settled. One update states a **RODE Wireless PRO** (32-bit float, lapel magnet, dock). Another proponent thread identifies a **RODE Wireless ME**. A third strand notes a square object in a bystander's pocket (roughly 1.8–2 inches); we do not claim that he knew anything ahead of time or was involved — only that whatever was in his pocket could continue to be investigated. The model conflict matters because the internal layout differs; see [RODE wireless mic](/Mic/rode-wireless-mic) and Butch Hibbs access.

* **Pre-event / 2026 hardware fork (claimed)** — Beyond the RODE PRO/ME/GO II fight, mid-2026 threads advance a **Sennheiser G4** candidate for the burnt object / burn shadow / front-speaker unit and remote-receive capability (@lindseey_loo: argument that Sennheiser can receive a detonation frequency while RODE only sends audio). **Hardware model remains unsettled**; treat as competing theory, not inventory fact.

### The day of, after the shot

* **12:23:30 PM MDT +frames (claimed)** — Frame-by-frame reviewers describe the shirt being **pinched and yanked over the left shoulder** at the moment of impact — the observation that later became the shirt-pull argument. See shirt pull at the shot and the shirt and necklace move before the blood.

* **12:23:30 PM MDT +frames (claimed)** — @brandisiciliani (July 2026) publishes anonymous-source footage she says shows **black debris falling from inside the shirt** at the hit; @TPV_John and others describe fall-moment shards resembling mic glass plus Yukon-floor battery-fragment claims. Visual interpretation only.

* **~12:24 PM MDT (claimed)** — Debris later described as **shattered black plastic or tempered glass** is said to be present on the floor of the SUV used to transport Kirk. Proponents argue a fragmenting transmitter would scatter small glass and electronic pieces whereas a single rifle round would not. See [the SUV transporting Charlie](/Mic/suv-transporting-charlie) and charred material and fragments in the SUV.

* **Same afternoon (claimed)** — A **burnt object** remains in the rear of that SUV. Its identification is the single most contested physical item on this page and is developed at [burnt object — RODE transmitter](/Mic/burnt-object-rode-transmitter), burnt object transmitter, and the burnt object in the SUV.

### Late 2025 into 2026 — the analyses arrive

* **Late 2025 (claimed)** — The **exploding-mic** hypothesis takes shape publicly: that a shaped charge concealed in or alongside the transmitter, rather than a distant round, produced the neck wound. Its full statement, including the PETN and shaped-charge framing, is at [exploding pendant / shaped charge](/Mic/exploding-pendant-shaped-charge), the exploding microphone theory, explosive in mic likely killed Charlie, and rigged mic explains anomalies. The snapped necklace and the missing residue search sit alongside it at necklace and residue and necklace cavitation residue.

* **December 18, 2025 (claimed)** — @BlakeBednarz high-reach tent-overhead analysis: vibration ring and debris interpreted as **under-shirt detonation**, not a ~140-yard rifle impact alone. Early visual-detonation node later built on by mic-theory proponents.

* **Late 2025 – 2026 (claimed)** — Proponents pair the mic theory with a **DoD contract for miniature explosive devices** signed April 22, 2025 and with the **AES** plant explosion in Tennessee, arguing capability and coincidence (some threads also align alleged ~**Aug 25** delivery/close-out with the ~**Aug 27** UVU stop announcement). See [Mic and the DoD contract](/Mic/DoD_Contract), [AES](/Mic/AES), [the AES factory explosion](/Mic/aes-explosion-factory), [the Heber City → Nashville flight](/Mic/aes-heber-nashville-flight), explosion and killings at the AES factory, AES Tennessee explosion, and [how Stew Peters broke the AES / exploding-mic connection](/Mic/Stew_Peters_Broke_AES_Mic).

* **2026 (claimed)** — A clearer photograph of the burnt object, taken while the vehicle sat at an **auto auction**, is circulated. Annotators say a thicker black section at the object's left edge contains a **melted female audio jack** of the type found on the top of the RODE transmitter, and that a fragment in the centre of the mass carries **writing and a circular symbol** they match to warning markings on a modified battery pack. Competing annotators match dimensions to **Sennheiser G4** instead (see hardware fork above).

* **2026 (reported)** — No chain of custody for the SUV has been published, and the vehicle reportedly reached an auction instead of an evidence lab — so the object was never examined by anyone who could settle its identity. See [mic vs pendant](/Mic/Mic_vs_Pendant) and [Explosive Residue Cover-Up](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/overview).

* **June 21, 2026 (claimed)** — A 4K close-up of the shirt is published and read frame by frame as showing an explosive path: initiation at the **left-side mic pack**, detonation **under the fabric**, the object driven **upward** producing the pinch over the left shoulder, then falling back and tracking **diagonally across the chest** to rest at the lower right. Proponents present this as visual proof of an internal detonation. See [mic explosion video analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis) and the Jon Bray profile.

* **June 2026 — dual-event refinement (claimed)** — As summarized by @BasedSamParker after TriggerSmart debate context (@jonaaronbray / @triggersmarthq): a **real gunshot** (possibly rooftop) for witness audio / cover **plus** a **Mach-cone acoustic trigger** to a body-worn device, with the lethal mechanism described as a high-pressure jet / hydrostatic effect. Competing reads of necklace vs circuit-board “swoosh” remain internal to the proponent camp. Theory attributed to Bray/Parker ecosystem — **not proven**.

* **2026 (objections from defenders of the official account)** — Critics (@alexboge, @paramounttactcl and others) argue a shirt-clipped transmitter could not form a proper shaped-charge jet without a liner, standoff, and rigid fixation (@alexboge); that multi-angle video shows a puff without the tear, flash, or soot expected of a contact explosive; that the mic pack stays still while the back of the shirt moves first; that the shirt stayed intact (@paramounttactcl); and that no physical replication has been produced — the closest public attempt is catalogued at [Demonstration of a Shaped Charge](/Mic/Demo_of_shape_charge). Hearing coverage they cite describes an autopsy entry of a **gunshot wound to the neck with fragments recovered**. Investigators answer that a contact **EFP-style micro-charge** needs no long standoff, that a few grams of PETN under fabric leaves little visible flash, that the **outward shirt expansion** is itself the blast signature ([video analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis)), that a pressure wave under a tucked shirt moves the whole garment rather than only the fabric over the pack, and that the recovered fragment is **class-characteristic only** — it has never been tied to the seized rifle — while the full autopsy remains sealed.

* **2026 (claimed)** — Candace Owens argues on air that the shaped charge was **intended for the chest** and that the neck wound was an accident of Kirk's posture, and separately says she received tips that assemblies of this type could be built at **Fort Huachuca**. Both are attributed claims about unnamed people; see the [Candace Owens timeline](./candace-owens-timeline) and the Fort Huachuca and military timeline. Speculative posts naming living persons as remote-trigger operators circulate on X; **this site does not elevate those as facts** without court filings.

* **2026 (reported)** — On the official side, the **ATF fragment analysis came back class-characteristic only**, unable to individualize the recovered fragment to the recovered rifle. That inconclusiveness is what keeps the acoustic and mic arguments alive; see ATF fragment class only, ATF fragment inconclusive, 30-06 energy mismatch, and no blood spray / energy mismatch.

* **2026 (reported)** — The prosecution case in preliminary hearings continues to present a **single rifle round from an elevated position**, with the ballistics and trajectory record argued at wound trajectory angle and ballistics and wound analysis.

## Why Investigators Conclude It Was the Exploding Mic

Citizen investigators have largely concluded that Charlie Kirk was killed by an explosive at his
microphone, not by the government's .30-06 rifle narrative. The main reasons:

* **The shirt and fabric move first** — [video frame analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis) shows the shirt expanding outward, spherically, before the neck wound appears.
* **The audio separates two blasts** — the [Canon XA55 recording](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio) reportedly shows a louder detonation at the tent distinct from a distant muzzle report.
* **Eyewitness veterans called it an explosion** — [Rob O'Neill and Gen. Blaine Holt](/Mic/rob-oneill-navy-seal-testimony) said it looked like an explosion, not a rifle wound.
* **No exit wound, and an energy mismatch** — a .30-06 at that range would be expected to exit and destroy tissue; see [six forensic physics phenomena](/Mic/six-forensic-physics-phenomena).
* **The mic was routed under the shirt** — [unlike prior events](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup), placing the transmitter against his chest and neck.
* **Physical debris** — a [burnt object in the SUV](/Mic/burnt-object-rode-transmitter) and a [neck fragment that may be charred plastic](/Mic/neck-fragment-charred-plastic).
* **The residue evidence was removed, not tested** — dirt, clothing, the SUV and the necklace were [hauled, cleaned or seized](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/overview) with no public residue test.
* **Means existed** — an [AES contract for miniaturized anti-personnel charges](/Mic/DoD_Contract) sits on the same timeline.

Full evidence hub: **[Microphone — The Exploding Mic Theory](/Mic/overview)**.

## Open Questions

1. Has any independent laboratory been given the original **uncompressed Canon XA55** files with intact metadata, rather than re-encoded copies?
2. What is the documented **chain of custody** for the SUV, its floor debris, and the burnt object from September 10 forward to the auto auction?
3. Which RODE model was actually issued that day, and does an inventory, invoice, or AV run-sheet exist that settles it?
4. Why was the lavalier placed under the shirt when the fitted windscreen implies outside placement, and who made that call?
5. Was the recovered transmitter ever tested for **explosive residue**, and if so, by whom and with what result?
6. Can a controlled acoustic test at the courtyard reproduce the three-peak structure from a single rooftop shot, and has anyone tried?
