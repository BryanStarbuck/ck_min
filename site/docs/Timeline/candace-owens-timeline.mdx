# Candace Owens Public Statements & Releases Timeline

{/* CK_NO_CLAIM_START */}

:::caution Legal Disclaimer — No Claim Of Wrongdoing
Nothing on this page is a finding by this website that any living person named here committed a crime, acted unethically or illegally, or had any knowledge of the assassination of Charlie Kirk before it happened. No one named here has been charged with, accused by any authority of, or found to have committed any offense connected to September 10, 2025.

What appears here about who was where, who travelled on what, or who attended what comes from **comments made by other people on social media and in public commentary**, attributed to whoever made them. This site does not adopt those claims. They are recorded so that readers, journalists and investigators can check them against primary records — a flight manifest, a visitor log, a FOIA release — and establish whether there is any truth in them. Until that is done, nothing here should be read as an accusation.

This page tracks **what Candace Owens said publicly and when she said it** — a release chronology, not an assessment of whether she was right. A large share of what the wider public believes about this case entered circulation through her podcast, so the order in which she disclosed things is itself a timeline worth having. The analytical treatment of her claims lives on her profile page and her podcast coverage page.

:::caution Living people — attribution only
**Candace Owens is a living person. So are Erika Kirk, Andrew Kolvet, Brian Harpole, Ben Shapiro and everyone else named below.** Nothing here states as fact that any of them committed a crime; none has been convicted of anything connected to this case. Statements attributed to Owens are **her published claims**, reported as claims. Where her account is contested — and several are — the counter-account is given alongside it. Fringe theories that name Owens herself are unsupported rumor and are not adopted here. Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**.
:::

## The Timeline

### Before September 10, 2025

* **2024** — In a filmed interview, Mike Huckabee — later U.S. Ambassador to Israel — reportedly remarks to Owens on camera that people could "make you disappear." Owens' supporters recirculated the clip in 2026 as context; it is a resurfaced interview moment, not a documented threat.

* **Sept 9, 2025** — Owens states that **three sources** told her Charlie Kirk was worried "they" were going to kill him. (KM-07) This is the assertion that anchors the whole pre-assassination warning thread and connects to the disputed ["they are going to kill me" text](./they-are-going-to-kill-me-text).

* **Sept 9, 2025 (as she later described it)** — Owens says Kirk told donors, TPUSA members and head of security Dan Flood that he thought he would be "executed tomorrow." (KM-10) Erika Kirk has publicly disputed that any such messages exist, saying she has his phone — the direct conflict documented at Reported Quotes and Warnings.

### September 10–30, 2025 — the first disclosures

* **Sept 10, 2025** — Owens says Kolvet told her at the hospital that she and **Tucker Carlson** **"were on a list."** Single-source, unrecorded recollection — see It Was Supposed To Be You.

* **Days after Sept 10, 2025** — Owens says Kolvet told her, verbatim, **"It was supposed to be you."** Kolvet has not confirmed the words on the record; the ordinary reading — shock and relief rather than foreknowledge — is set out on the same page.

* **Immediately after Sept 10, 2025** — Owens reports that threats arrived for her, Tucker Carlson and Ian Carroll in the days following the killing — Carroll's own posts on the case are catalogued at Ian Carroll — X posts.

* **September 2025** — Owens **publishes screenshots of Kirk's own messages** to donors, including *"Jewish donors really live up to all the stereotypes"* and language about having *"no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause."* These texts had been dismissed as a hoax until she published them; the donor-pressure reading is at Charlie, Israel, Donors and Motive and donor texts 48 hours before. Later clips also circulate a **Christian Zionist donor text** condemning Kirk for platforming Tucker and Candace as "antisemites" — presented as a "final warning" framing; attribute to Owens' published screenshots only. Carlson's own trajectory on the subject is at Tucker Carlson on evolving Israel views.

* **October 6–7, 2025** — Owens' donor-text disclosures are amplified with named group-chat context (public discussion of **Rob McCoy** and **Josh Hammer** as participants when Kirk explained ditching Israel under donor pressure — living persons; no crime claim). Separately, clips circulate of **Andrew Kolvet confirming** the authenticity of the donor messages Owens published. Confirmation of **texts** is not confirmation of any homicide theory.

* **September 2025** — Owens releases **a photograph she says shows Tyler Robinson at a Dairy Queen in Orem at 6:38 PM** on the day of the shooting. The venue is documented at Dairy Queen — Orem, and the timing conflict it creates with the custody narrative is at [custody vs Discord confession](./custody-vs-discord-confession-timeline). **July 2026 authenticity disputes** are logged below and on the [camera sightings timeline](./camera-sightings-tyler-robinson).

### Autumn 2025 — the episode arc

* **Ep. 235 — "They Are Lying About Charlie Kirk"** — Owens' first sustained attack on the official account, arguing early official statements were internally inconsistent.

* **Ep. 245 — "DEAD OR ALIVE: Who Betrayed Charlie Kirk?"** — turns the inquiry inward toward TPUSA and the circle around Kirk.

* **Ep. 256 — "INFILTRATION: Charlie Kirk Was Being Tracked For Years"** — the episode that introduces the multi-year aircraft-overlap argument later expanded at Planes Following Charlie or Erika.

* **Autumn 2025** — Owens introduces the **color-coded plane shorthand**: SU-BTT as "the yellow plane," SU-BND as "the blue plane" — the naming that the entire Egyptian armada thread inherited. Her SU-BTT chronology is on the [SU-BTT movements timeline](./su-btt-plane-movements-timeline).

* **Autumn 2025** — Owens discloses the claim of roughly **twelve cell phones registered on Israeli plans** operating near UVU on September 10 — the foreign phones at UVU thread. A public confirmation wave peaks around **November 5, 2025** (@ProjectConstitu and amplifiers saying Candace "just confirmed"); later analyst **Tony Seruga** publishes a **16 Israeli / 44 foreign** figure — see [cell-tower and phone-data timeline](./cell-tower-phone-data-timeline). **Unverified private analysis throughout.**

* **Autumn–Winter 2025 (clip wave; exact episode TBD)** — Owens states, as widely relayed on X, that **Benjamin Netanyahu offered major funding / elevation of TPUSA**, that **Kirk refused**, and that **Kolvet** was her source for the offer; she also says **Erika Kirk was unaware**. This is **her account of private offers and conversations**, unconfirmed by the named parties. Keep this **distinct** from her separate claim of a **final Netanyahu call about Iran** (below). No crime by any living leader is asserted here.

* **Autumn 2025** — Owens covers the flight **N560TW**, Scottsdale to Provo on September 10 and back — the same-day turnaround claim — one of several tails she raised alongside the Egyptian armada.

* **Autumn 2025** — Owens reports that the **FBI did not want the bomb dogs taken to certain places** — the claim behind Bomb Dogs Standdown and bomb dogs kept away.

* **Nov 17, 2025 — "Operation Mocking-Plane: The Charlie Kirk Plot Thickens"** — Owens argues it is strange Kirk was routed into Salt Lake City, roughly 45 miles from UVU, rather than Provo Municipal seven miles away, where SU-BTT was parked.

* **Ep. 287 — "The Past Is Prologue: Why Charlie Kirk Was Murdered"** — her most comprehensive single treatment: the lone-shooter narrative challenged, the Israeli-phone signal claim, possible accomplices, and TPUSA donor pressure.

* **Dec 2025 (relayed by @itsmorganariel)** — Owens publicly discusses a tip from police officers alleging that during the manhunt a white Toyota RAV4 was stopped for speeding and the driver **flashed an FBI badge**. Relayed tip, not confirmed by any agency — RAV4 flashed badge.

### 2026 — succession, legal pressure, forensics, and litigation

* **January 21, 2026** — Per reporting by **Will Sommer** (@willsommer / The Bulwark), **TPUSA sent Owens a cease-and-desist** asserting violation of a **non-disparagement agreement** over her coverage of the case. A civil demand is **not a ruling** on the truth of her claims — see also [censorship and information-control timeline](./censorship-information-control-timeline) and TPUSA Legal Threats.

* **Feb 2026** — Backlash builds over the trailer for Owens' **"Bride of Charlie"** series examining Erika Kirk and the TPUSA leadership succession. This is media framing about a living widow, not a judicial finding of any kind.

* **2026** — Owens reports a claim that a man named George Zinn told hospital staff he had been **paid to be a decoy**; unverified relayed account, connected to the distraction people thread.

* **2026** — Owens names **VFX LA**, a voice-cloning and deepfake firm, as a company TPUSA personnel reportedly met with after Kirk's death, and questions the authenticity of the video in which Kirk appears to designate Erika Kirk as successor. Kolvet has reportedly acknowledged a meeting with deepfake creators while saying the offer was declined — see Erika CEO succession disputed.

* **2026** — Owens says she is **"99.9% sure Tyler Robinson did not shoot Charlie Kirk"** and disputes that he confessed or turned himself in, citing the absence of a signed confession and the account of a former deputy who allegedly urged him to go to the station. The messages themselves are at Tyler Robinson Discord messages and [Discord evidence in court](/court/discord-evidence-court); counterpoint material is at Discord and Custody Timeline. Robinson is **charged, not convicted**.

* **2026** — Owens states that Kirk's **final phone call was an intervention by Benjamin Netanyahu** pressing for war with Iran. This is her account of a private call, unconfirmed by any participant — see Netanyahu's final call. Keep **distinct** from the earlier **funding / elevate-TPUSA offer** claim.

* **Jun 22, 2026** — Owens airs a segment arguing that early on-camera witnesses described **blood pooling near Kirk's heart** — a wound that, in her account, never existed — and framing that as evidence of a chest-shot narrative prepared in advance. The competing witness accounts are at [Chest-Shot Witnesses](/Mic/Chest_Shot_Witnesses) and Shot in the Chest, Then the Neck.

* **Jun 26, 2026** — Owens releases **photographs of the SUV interior**, watermarked "CANDACE," showing charred material and debris on the floor of the GMC Yukon Denali. Commentators read a fused, blackened object as melted shirt fabric plus a transmitter component; others read it differently. Reported material, not authenticated forensics — [SUV Transporting Charlie](/Mic/suv-transporting-charlie), burnt object and transmitter.

* **Jul 10, 2026** — Owens publicly discusses the **public-figure question** in Brian Harpole's defamation suit against her, saying she had never covered him before his own podcast appearance. Separate July reporting describes **mass judicial recusal** in the Middle District of Tennessee. A civil defamation docket is not a criminal finding against either party, and this site takes no position on the merits.

* **July 14–16, 2026 (recirculation; verify original air date)** — Clips attribute to Owens a claim that during the post-shot **SUV transport**, someone **prioritized updating Ben Shapiro's security** rather than (or before) normal emergency focus. **Attribute only**; living persons; **no crime established**. Related clocks on [medical/death timeline](./medical-death-timeline) and [ten minutes after the shot](./ten-minutes-after-shot).

* **Jul 16, 2026** — An **Axon Body Cam 4** user emails Owens' tips line challenging the account that an officer's body camera died on the rooftop, citing the model's roughly fourteen-hour battery and mandatory dock-and-upload cycle. Anonymous tip — see Officer Bagley's body cam, [missing bodycam and the GRAMA admission](/court/mirandize/bodycam-grama-no-footage), and the wider GRAMA records denials.

* **July 15–16, 2026** — High recirculation of Netanyahu **funding-offer / refusal** clips framed as confirming bombshells; meta-timeline of claim longevity rather than new primary evidence.

* **July 17–19, 2026** — The **Dairy Queen 6:38 PM** still becomes a two-way fight: critics say it **conflicts with** the Cowboy's Smokehouse receipt alibi timeline or is **not from Sept 10**; defenders treat it as proof the charged suspect was still local hours after the shot. Full clothing/authenticity detail on [camera sightings](./camera-sightings-tyler-robinson).

* **Ongoing 2026** — Owens' engagement metrics on X are reported by supporters to have been suppressed; the claim is documented at X — Candace Owens Engagement Bans. Her role in the proposed reform framework is at Trusted Investigators.

## Open Questions

1. Can the **three sources** behind the September 9 claim be identified, even under seal, or corroborated by any second party?
2. Will a **forensic extraction of Charlie Kirk's phone** ever be published to settle the warning-message dispute in either direction?
3. Who took the **SUV interior photographs**, when, and where was the vehicle when they were taken — and is there any forensic follow-up after the Jun 26 release?
4. Has **Andrew Kolvet** ever been asked on the record about either quoted remark **and** the Netanyahu **funding-offer** account for which Owens names him as source?
5. Which of Owens' flight-log claims are **reproducible from raw ADS-B archives**, and which rest on screenshots alone?
6. What, if anything, did the **VFX LA** meeting produce, and does any document confirm what was discussed?
7. What is the full text of the **January 2026 TPUSA cease-and-desist**, and what is the status of the **Harpole** suit after the July public-figure comments?
8. Did Owens publicly answer the **July 2026 DQ vs Cowboy's** drive-time contradiction?
