# July 20, 2025 Military & Foreign-Leaders Meeting (Claims)

This is the **date hub for July 20, 2025** — the day on which two separate claims converge: that the Egyptian Air Force jet **SU-BTT** made its **first-ever trip to the United States**, landing at a **Nebraska Army base**, and that a **military meeting with foreign leaders took place on U.S. soil**. The aircraft leg is detailed on the [Nebraska Army base page](./nebraska-army-base-july-20) and the attendee question on the [foreign leaders meeting page](./foreign-leaders-meeting-july-20); what this page adds is the **surrounding chronology** — everything in the weeks on either side that researchers use to argue July 20 is a hinge date rather than an ordinary Sunday.

:::caution
This website does not assert as fact that any living person named here committed a crime or had advance knowledge of or involvement in the assassination. The items below reflect **public flight-tracking data and public commentary**. Nothing here is a finding by this website, and mainstream reporting has **not** publicly framed these events as conclusively tied to Charlie Kirk's case.
:::

## The Timeline

### June 2025 — the run-up

* **June 13, 2025** — The **twelve-day war** between Israel and Iran begins, the geopolitical backdrop against which every subsequent item on this page is read by commentators — see Israel.

* **June 18, 2025** — Charlie Kirk reportedly goes to the **White House** specifically to argue **against regime change in Iran**, the earliest dated instance of the position break that the donor-pressure motive page treats as the root of the story.

* **June 25, 2025** — Tennessee Governor **Bill Lee** travels to **France** for a Howmet Aerospace investment — only his fourth international trip in office. Researchers connect it to the Tennessee manufacturing thread that later surfaces on the [AES pages](/Mic/AES); the connection is **interpretive**.

* **Late June 2025** — **Google Trends** activity attributed to Israeli and DC IP ranges begins registering on Utah figures and locations, per the [Israel IP search-pattern page](/GoogleSearches/israel-ip-search-patterns). Trends data shows interest, not identity; the attribution is a claim.

### Early July 2025 — the two weeks before

* **July 2, 2025** — Pastor **Rob McCoy** formally resigns as lead pastor of Calvary Church in Thousand Oaks, a retirement reported as pre-planned in January. It is noted only because his family appears elsewhere in the [ten minutes after the shot](./ten-minutes-after-shot) reconstruction.

* **July 7–9, 2025** — **Benjamin Netanyahu** visits the United States, the diplomatic event closest in time to the July 20 claim, tracked on the [Israel events and pressure timeline](./israel-events-pressure-timeline).

* **July 9, 2025** — A search spike from Israeli IP space is claimed for **Mehtab Syed**, then the FBI Salt Lake City special agent in charge; her successor **Robert Bohls** draws a comparable spike on **September 1**. See [FBI and DPS search patterns](/GoogleSearches/fbi-dps-law-enforcement-searches).

* **July 11, 2025** — Kirk is publicly criticized by pro-Israel figures for **platforming anti-Israel speakers**, an escalation in the pressure sequence documented on the [pre-event political pressure timeline](./pre-event-political-pressure).

* **July 13, 2025** — At the TPUSA Florida conference, **Josh Hammer** debates Dave Smith — the public face of the intra-coalition fight covered on the TPUSA organizational timeline.

* **July 14, 2025** — **Tucker Carlson** speaks at the same conference and tells the audience to **"go all the way."** Commentators date the July 20 Trends anchor to **the same week Kirk platformed Carlson for the first time**, an argument developed on the AmFest control page and in Tucker Carlson and Israel.

* **July 14–19, 2025** — Per the search-pattern compilations, Israeli-IP searches hit a run of emergency surgeons at **Timpanogos Regional Hospital** — Rasmussen (July 14), Neeleman (July 18), a Patterson (July 19) — with more following on July 22, 23–24 and 30. The names and dates are catalogued on [Timpanogos medical searches](/GoogleSearches/timpanogos-medical-searches); the reason that hospital matters at all is that Kirk was taken there rather than to the region's Level I trauma center. These are living physicians; nothing here suggests any of them did anything wrong.

* **July 16–17, 2025** — DC-attributed searches for the incoming **Utah DPS Commissioner** appear on July 16; the appointment is announced **July 17**, replacing the prior commissioner. The sequencing claim — searches preceding the public announcement — is the whole of the allegation, and an ordinary explanation (a leak, a rumor, or an announced shortlist) has not been excluded.

* **July 18, 2025** — Commentary places a **"decision" to target Kirk in Utah** around this date. This is **speculation with no supporting public record**, and it is preserved as such on the [July 18 decision page](./july-18-decision).

* **July 18, 2025 — Flight staging (tracker summary)** — Aviation OSINT accounts describe Egyptian Falcon **SU-BTT** arriving **Europe from Cairo** ahead of the U.S. leg (Cairo → Paris staging before the July 20 Omaha hop). Staging only; no meeting claim attaches to this day by itself.

### July 20, 2025 — the date itself

* **July 20, 2025 — Flight tracking (primary)** — The same calendar day, **@Borrowed7Time** (Johnny Gemini) posted that **SU-BTT (#0101D3)** was **en route Paris → Omaha (KOMA / Eppley Airfield)** after the July 18 Cairo arrival — a contemporaneous aviation log that predates any Kirk-assassination narrative and names a **civil airfield**, not a military base ([Nebraska Army base page](./nebraska-army-base-july-20), [SU-BTT movements](./su-btt-plane-movements-timeline)).

* **July 20, 2025** — Later flight-tracking **compilations** record **SU-BTT** making what trackers call its **first-ever trip to the United States**, often with a reported **Omaha → Lincoln** routing into the Nebraska corridor. The **same-day internal Omaha→Lincoln hop** is compilation-dependent; the best same-day primary located so far is Paris→**KOMA**. The specific military installation **varies by source** and is not confirmed in any Kirk-case filing. Full detail on the [Nebraska Army base page](./nebraska-army-base-july-20).

* **July 20, 2025 — Meeting claim (still unsupported by public minutes)** — The investigation file states, in a single line, that **"a military meeting with foreign leaders took place on July 20th on U.S. soil."** No attendee list, agenda, manifest, visitor log or minutes has ever been produced. X/OSINT research has turned up **primary flight tracks**, not **primary meeting posts**. The attendee question is examined on the [foreign leaders meeting page](./foreign-leaders-meeting-july-20).

* **July 20, 2025** — Alleged purposes circulating in commentary include **counter-UAS (anti-drone) coordination**, special-forces or ISR coordination, and Middle East security planning. The counter-UAS thread later reappears at Provo in September, on the counter-UAS page and the Egyptian jets at Provo page. These are **inferences**, not meeting records.

* **July 20, 2025** — This is the date the Google-Trends compilations call their **anchor**: the point at which searches for case figures, UVU campus geography, and the eventual suspect reportedly cluster. Citizen credit trails often run **[Baron Coleman](./baron-coleman-timeline) → viral compilations (e.g. @ProjectConstitu, Oct 13, 2025)** → the [July 20 anchor page](/GoogleSearches/july-20-2025-anchor) and the [UVU campus location searches](/GoogleSearches/uvu-campus-location-searches). Trends data is aggregate and low-volume queries are noisy; the anchor is a pattern claim, not proof of foreknowledge.

* **Around July 20, 2025** — The same compilations claim a **blip** for the eventual suspect's name — and for his roommate Lance Twiggs — two months before the shooting, tracked on [Tyler Robinson and Lance Twiggs searches](/GoogleSearches/tyler-robinson-lance-twiggs-searches). Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, and the search claim establishes nothing about either man.

### Late July and August 2025 — what followed

* **July 30, 2025** — The last of the Timpanogos surgeon search spikes in the two-week frenzy the compilations describe around July 20.

* **July 31, 2025** — An **off-the-record White House meeting** organized by the Faith and Freedom Coalition, the Latino Coalition for Israel and the **Philos Project** convenes on "confronting the woke-right defamation of Israel." Attendees reportedly included **Ralph Reed**, **Luke Moon**, Apostle Mario Bramnick, Yehuda Kaploun and **Yair Netanyahu**. Kirk and TPUSA were **absent** — the omission examined on TPUSA's absence from July 31 and the July 31 meeting page.

* **Aug 2, 2025** — **French legionnaires** reportedly arrive in the United States for a three-week California training rotation ending **Aug 25** — the claim examined on the French Foreign Legion page.

* **Aug 4–6, 2025** — The **Hamptons "intervention"**, at which Kirk was reportedly confronted by pro-Israel figures, and a reported call from **Benjamin Netanyahu**. Detailed on the [Hamptons intervention meeting](./hamptons-intervention-meeting), the Hamptons claim page and Israel–Hamptons.

* **Aug 6, 2025** — Kirk publicly **"lashes out about donors pressuring him"** in a Megyn Kelly appearance — the on-record moment the donor-pressure motive page treats as confirmation the pressure was real.

* **Aug 8, 2025** — Israeli-IP searches are claimed for the ISR aircraft **N1098L**, 32 days before it flew low passes over Utah Valley — see [planes search patterns](/GoogleSearches/planes-search-patterns).

* **Aug 17–24, 2025** — Trackers describe a **second SU-BTT U.S. visit**: Paris → Omaha/Lincoln → **Wilmington, Delaware** → Paris, repeating the same Nebraska corridor. Detailed on the [SU-BTT movements timeline](./su-btt-plane-movements-timeline).

* **Sept 4, 2025** — **SU-BTT** arrives at **Provo, Utah** direct from France and remains through the event window, closing the arc that began on July 20 — see [September 10, 2025](./Sept_10_2025).

* **Nov 2025 — Rebuttal voice** — Commentators (e.g. **@ConservativeDV**) note Egyptian units have **long trained** in Utah/Nebraska corridors and that TPUSA travel overlaps those geographies — an ordinary explanation for airframe presence that severs automatic causation from geography.

* **March 2026 — Control pattern** — Trackers again logged **SU-BTT** through **Paris → Omaha → Lincoln → Wilmington → Cairo**, showing the Nebraska corridor was **not unique** to summer 2025 and weakens any inference that July 20 alone proves a one-off "military meeting" itinerary ([SU-BTT movements](./su-btt-plane-movements-timeline)).

## Open Questions

1. Does any **base visitor log, NOTAM, flight strip or public-affairs notice** for July 20, 2025 exist, and what does it show?
2. Who, by name and from what government, is alleged to have attended — and is there a **single primary source** for any name?
3. Do **CBP entry records** show foreign nationals entering on or around July 20 in connection with the aircraft?
4. What is **SU-BTT**'s complete registration and movement history before July 20, and does it support or refute the "first trip ever" framing?
5. Why does the same **Omaha–Lincoln corridor** recur on the August 17–24 leg, and is there a mundane operational reason such as maintenance or crew basing?
6. Is the **July 20 Google-Trends anchor** reproducible today from the raw Trends interface, or does it depend on screenshots that cannot be re-derived?
