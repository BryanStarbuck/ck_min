# Tyler Robinson: Pre-Shot UVU Campus Movements Timeline

This page covers only the **morning of September 10, 2025**, from the first reported doorbell-camera sighting at **8:07 AM MDT** to the last pre-shot rooftop frame at **12:22 PM MDT**. Everything after the shot — the roof exit, the flight into the woods, the evening sightings, the surrender — sits on [the post-shot escape timeline](./tyler-robinson-post-shot-escape). **Tyler Robinson is charged, not convicted.** Every sighting below is a figure that law enforcement and media have *attributed* to him, and several of those attributions are actively disputed.

## Before the campus: neighborhood doorbell cameras

* **8:07 AM MDT** — A **Ring doorbell camera** on **S 800 W** records a man walking toward campus in a maroon (or "plum") shirt and light-colored shorts with a black hat, per the reconstruction preserved in the master investigation file and cross-checked on the Ring doorbell camera page; a TMZ-sourced description of the same 8:07 frame adds a **backpack** and a black hat with a triangle logo, which the law-enforcement-referenced description of the 8:29 arrival does not mention.

* **8:23 AM MDT** — A **WYZE camera** on **W 880 S** records the figure *driving*, per the WYZE camera thread — meaning the man walking toward campus at 8:07 is in a vehicle sixteen minutes later, a transition no released footage covers.

* **8:29 AM MDT** — **UVU surveillance** records a **gray Dodge Challenger** arriving on campus, the time used in [the probable-cause affidavit](/court/mirandize/probable-cause-affidavit) and repeated on [the affidavit timeline page](/Timeline/Tyler_Robinson/Affidavit_Probable_Cause). Clothing described: plain maroon T-shirt, light shorts, light shoes, black hat with a white logo. **No backpack is mentioned.** July 2026 hearing coverage adds that investigators tracked the vehicle partly by **distinctive wheels**, not color alone — still a **state attribution** to the charged defendant, not a conviction. Vehicle thread: [rental cars and vehicle movements](./rental-cars-vehicle-movements-timeline).

* **Morning campus loops + Chick-fil-A (hearing-presented)** — In the **[July 2026 preliminary hearing](/court/Preliminary_Hearing/overview)**, prosecutors played UVU surveillance they say shows the charged defendant moving about campus **multiple times** that morning — commentators covering the hearing (e.g. **@Liz_Wheeler**, **@BlakeSNeff**, **@JackPosobiec**) described roughly **four visits / multi-loop scouting**, including a stop at the **on-campus Chick-fil-A** before the clothing-change window and roof sequence (**Salt Lake Tribune** and related coverage of the video presentation). Citizen researchers have pressed for any **merchant receipt** or licence-plate-reader corroboration of that stop; none has been released publicly. See merchant and travel CCTV.

* **~9:24 AM MDT (disputed)** — A video circulated by **@FaithDrivUr** in July 2026 characterizes police as saying the suspect arrived on campus at **9:24 AM** and went to the quad, where he made contact with **TPUSA representatives** — the claim tracked on the TPUSA–Tyler Robinson chat page and on the TPUSA internal timeline. This does **not** match the 8:29 AM arrival above; no TPUSA employee has been named, and no primary filing containing a 9:24 AM time has been located. The ordinary explanation — TPUSA staff were tabling and doing setup on the quad all morning, so a brief exchange is unremarkable — is the baseline this site records first. Citizen analysis (**@AngelaBethMoore**) separately notes that several named TPUSA travelers (e.g. Blake Neff, Danny Phillip, Nate Walker in that analysis) landing **SLC ~9:23** could not yet be on campus for an 8:29–9:25 window, so any genuine early-morning contact would involve **different** staff — if the contact happened at all. Naming those travelers is a scheduling observation, not an accusation.

* **9:57 AM MDT** — The **WYZE camera** on **W 880 S** records the figure again, this time **walking**, still in the morning clothing. Between 8:29 and 9:57 there is no published account of when the Challenger left the parking lot, a gap also flagged on [the camera-sightings timeline](./camera-sightings-tyler-robinson).

* **~10:00–11:44 AM MDT — the unaccounted window** — Roughly **105 minutes** with no released camera frame, no published cell-site record, and no witness account. Citizen researchers treat this as the largest hole in the pre-shot chronology, and it is where the clothing change is presumed to have happened — the sequence agent Hull addressed on clothing change and Agent David Hull. Some online accounts claim **cell-phone meta / geofence** holdings exist that could fill the gap; no public cell-tower map has been released — see [the cell tower and phone data timeline](./cell-tower-phone-data-timeline).

* **Disputed: the "9:00 AM doorbell" reading** — Radio host and attorney **Baron Coleman** has publicly argued, from shadow analysis of the released doorbell frames, that the morning doorbell timeline does not sit where officials placed it. Those claims are tracked on [the Baron Coleman timeline](./baron-coleman-timeline) and on the Ring camera page; they are **his stated analysis, not a court finding**.

## The return to campus

* **11:44 AM MDT** — A **Ring camera** on **W 800 S** records a figure now in a **black long-sleeve shirt with an American flag and eagle**, dark jeans, a dark cap, large sunglasses, and a **dark backpack** — a complete change from the morning attire, per the clothing-change record and Tyler on surveillance cameras.

* **11:49 AM MDT** — A **Ring camera** on **S 800 W** records the figure walking toward campus again. The master file notes that a car reportedly belonging to Utah legislator **Phil Lyman** had been parked on that street since about **11:30 AM**, placing an unrelated public figure's vehicle on the approach route — logged as a coincidence to be checked against [the vehicle-movements timeline](./rental-cars-vehicle-movements-timeline), **not** as an allegation about Lyman, who has not been accused of anything by any authority.

* **11:50 AM MDT** — The figure reappears in a **grassy area north of Campus Drive near 800 West**, walking with a pronounced **limp** (stiff right leg, limited bending), then moves south through a parking lot toward a pedestrian tunnel. The affidavit narration at this point records **no backpack** — one of the discrepancies driving the backpack-and-rifle impossibility argument.

* **11:52 AM MDT** — Movements are tracked through stairwells toward the roof of the **Losee Center**, roughly **150 yards** from the courtyard where Charlie Kirk was speaking, per [the September 10 page](./Sept_10_2025).

* **11:53 AM MDT** — The figure pauses at the top of a stairway, pulls out a **cell phone**, puts it back, descends into the **pedestrian tunnel**, and ascends a stairwell in the adjacent **parking garage** — the sequence that produced the contested stairwell stills discussed on the stairs-guy identity page and Tyler Robinson: Stairs Guy.

## The stairwell stills and the identity dispute

* **11:53 AM frames — kit as described by law enforcement on the day** — Converse-style sneakers, a **blue mesh baseball cap**, and a **dark blue, full backpack**, per the day-of description preserved in the master file and mapped on the UVU surveillance page.

* **The backpack-fullness argument** — Citizen video reconstructions argue the man who left the Challenger carried a **light blue pack roughly half empty**, while the stairwell figure carries a **different, near-full pack** — and that swapping shorts for pants would leave a pack *emptier*, not fuller. That reasoning is developed on Stairs Guy and the backpack; it is an **inference from compressed video**, not an established finding.

* **The "enhanced" image problem** — FBI Director Kash Patel described the released stairwell images as **"enhanced,"** even though UVU operates a high-definition campus system. The tension between an enhanced grainy still and an unreleased native HD original runs through Enhanced Suspect Photos, Kash Patel's briefings, enhanced stairwell photos released after custody, and UVU HD footage withheld.

* **Camera-label discrepancy** — **@Sunny61120** (July 9, 2026) alleged that parking-garage frame labels flip between **"level 2 NE"** and **"level 1 NE"** on what appears to be the same stairwell. Clerical labeling slips are common and mundane; this is logged as a question for the UVU surveillance record and the campus security gaps page, not as proof of editing.

* **The rooftop clothing question** — State investigating agent **David Hull** walked the July 2026 preliminary hearing through the campus video, including a rooftop clothing sequence that citizen investigators say received no public follow-up; see the Hull clothing-change page and [Preliminary Hearing Day 2](/court/Preliminary_Hearing/Day_2).

## The approach to the roof

* **12:02 PM MDT** — The figure is seen on the **north side of the Losee Center**, still limping, and enters the building through the **southeast corner doors**, per the affidavit narrative summarized on [the September 10 page](./Sept_10_2025).

* **12:15 PM MDT** — Per agent Hull's July 2026 hearing testimony as reported, the figure **rolls a public railing** onto the **Losee Center** roof, then moves toward a corner prone position — the access route that anchors the perimeter and rooftop failure analysis, UVU campus security gaps and [the UVU security and camera timeline](./uvu-security-surveillance-timeline). Hull reportedly acknowledged the roof angle is **long-distance without clear facial features**; coverage also states [Judge Tony Graf](/court/judge-tony-graf) **limited some zoomed/enhanced** versions while allowing the continuous long-distance track. Hearing exhibits recirculated July 2026 include **never-before-public stairwell** stills used to bridge the garage to the roof — see [Preliminary Hearing Day 2](/court/Preliminary_Hearing/Day_2).

* **12:17 PM MDT** — Climbs over a short wall and crouches on the **north side** of the roof.

* **12:22 PM MDT** — Stands, runs west, scoots near the roof edge — **the limp is no longer visible** — and lies down facing the courtyard, roughly **430 feet (130 m)** from the stage. The disappearing limp is examined on the roof-run limp page. Hearing coverage describes a **stiff right-leg limp** on the late morning return as consistent with something constraining the leg — **state theory**, not a conviction.

* **12:23 PM MDT — end of this page's scope.** The shot, the rifle-and-towel placement, and the roof exit are covered on [the minute-by-minute reconstruction](./shooting-minute-by-minute), [the shooting-time dispute](./12-23-shooting-time), [the roof-jumping timeline](./roof-jumping-timeline), and [the post-shot escape timeline](./tyler-robinson-post-shot-escape). The recovered weapon itself is on the Mauser .30-06 page.

## Conflicts and Disputed Points

| Point | Version A | Version B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campus arrival time | **8:29 AM** — law-enforcement-referenced UVU surveillance, gray Dodge Challenger (distinctive wheels per hearing coverage); multi-loop / Chick-fil-A stop in state's video theory | **9:24 AM** with a quad meeting attached — a narrator's characterization of police statements in a @FaithDrivUr video (page) |
| Backpack at 8:07 AM | Present, per the TMZ-sourced description | Not mentioned in the 8:29 AM law-enforcement clothing description |
| Backpack at the stairwell | Dark blue and **full**, per the day-of law-enforcement description | Light blue and **half empty** when leaving the car, per citizen video analysis (page) |
| One subject or two | Official position: one continuous subject tracked across UVU cameras | Citizen position: the morning figure and the "stairs guy" are **different people** (page) |
| The limp | Present 11:50–12:02, gone by 12:22 — read by some as consistent with something carried in a pant leg | Read by others as evidence *against* the pant-leg theory, since stairwell frames show full leg bend (page) |
| Image quality | Stills were "enhanced" for release | Native HD exports exist and have never been released (page) |

## Open Questions

1. When did the gray Dodge Challenger **leave** the parking lot, and which camera covers that moment? No public document states it.
2. Where was the subject between roughly **10:00 AM and 11:44 AM** — the 105-minute window in which the clothing change is presumed to have occurred — and does any **Chick-fil-A** merchant receipt or campus POS record corroborate the hearing video stop?
3. Do **cell-site records** for the phone visible at 11:53 AM match the camera track, and why has no cell-tower map been released? See [the cell tower and phone data timeline](./cell-tower-phone-data-timeline).
4. Who were the **TPUSA representatives** in the quad-contact claim, and does UVU or TPUSA production footage of that exchange exist — given travel constraints that rule out several named staff before ~10:15 AM?
5. Is there a **continuous camera-to-camera track** from the parking-garage stairwell to the Losee Center roof, or are there cuts between angles that no released exhibit bridges?
6. Did the rifle enter campus with the subject at all — and if so, on which frame is it visible? See the gun discovery sequence.

## Laws (Charlie Kirk)
