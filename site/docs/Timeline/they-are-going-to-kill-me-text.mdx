# "They Are Going to Kill Me" Text (September 9, 2025, Claims)

One alleged message, sent the day before the shooting, has become the most-cited pre-death artifact
in the case: Charlie Kirk reportedly telling confidants **"they are going to
kill me."** This page is a chronology of that claim — what Kirk did publicly on September 8 and 9,
when each alleged recipient and each wording variant entered the public record, who disputes the
messages exist at all, and what would authenticate them. **No court has authenticated these
messages, and no court has found that any living person named in connection with them caused
Kirk's death.**

## The Timeline

### The months before

* **April 3, 2025** — Kirk posts publicly against a new Middle East war, warning that "the war drums
  are beating again in DC" — the earliest widely-cited marker of the friction that motive
  researchers point to. Context on Charlie–Israel Friction and
  Reported Quotes and Warnings.

* **Summer 2025** — Kirk pushes on the Epstein files and declines to cancel Tucker Carlson, both of
  which appear in donor-conflict accounts —
  the Epstein files push and
  Tucker Carlson and Israel.

* **Aug 2025 (reported)** — A meeting in the Hamptons is described by researchers as a donor-pressure
  event. The date is uncertain in the source material — some accounts place it in early August,
  others tie the confrontation to September 9 —
  [the Hamptons intervention meeting](./hamptons-intervention-meeting) and
  Israel and the Hamptons.

* **Before Sept 9 (leaked, later confirmed by TPUSA)** — A WhatsApp chat with donors in which Kirk
  writes that he has "just lost another huge Jewish donor" over refusing to cancel Carlson, that
  "Jewish donors play into all the stereotypes," and that this is *"leaving me no choice but to
  leave the pro-Israel cause."* **TPUSA has publicly confirmed this chat**, which is what
  distinguishes it from the "kill me" messages —
  [Leaked Kirk Texts](/Media/Leaked-Kirk-Texts) and
  Israel Donors and Motive.

### September 9, 2025 — the day itself

* **Sept 9 (reported)** — A confrontation described as an *"intervention"* in which Kirk is
  challenged by pro-Israel figures. Named attendees in citizen compilations include
  Rabbi Pesach Wolicki, Josh Hammer,
  Seth Dillon, Bill Ackman and
  Natasha Hausdorff. Attendance lists differ
  between retellings and none of the named attendees has been accused in any court of anything —
  [the September 9 high-pressure meeting](./high-pressure-meeting-sept-9) and
  Political Pressure and Meetings 2025.

* **Sept 9 — publicly verifiable** — Kirk appears on **Ben Shapiro's** show
  and voices sharper criticism of Israeli policy to Shapiro directly. This is the one anchor of the
  day that requires no private sourcing — [2025 Context Timeline](./Year_2025) and
  Charlie's Quotes on Israel.

* **Sept 9, during or after the meeting (claim)** — The alleged text. The master investigation file
  records it as: *"During this combative meeting, Kirk reportedly texted others saying, 'THEY ARE
  GOING TO KILL ME.'"* The timing is placed inside or immediately after the confrontation in most
  retellings, but no retelling gives a clock time.

* **Sept 9 (claimed sequence — citizen collage)** — Timelines such as **@Villgecrazylady** (Dec
  2025) place an **after-Zoom** text to **Andrew Kolvet**: *"they are going
  to kill me,"* distinct from the TPUSA-confirmed donor WhatsApp thread of the prior days. Those
  posts clarify that Kolvet is **not** alleged to have been *on* the Zoom with Wolicki/Hammer —
  only to have received a text afterward — a distinction that answers meeting-page debunks aimed
  at a different claim.

* **Sept 9 (Flood variant)** — Viral posts (e.g. **@TheSCIF**, **@AdameMedia**, Owens ecosystem)
  claim Charlie also texted security lead **Dan Flood** wording such as *"They
  are going to kill me tomorrow"* (KM-10) or related *"executed tomorrow"* (KM-10) language. **No public screenshot
  or Flood confirmation** has been produced in the posts reviewed. Flood has not been charged with
  anything.

* **Sept 10, 12:23 PM MDT** — Kirk is shot at UVU, roughly 24 hours after the alleged messages —
  [September 10, 2025](./Sept_10_2025) and
  [the minute-by-minute reconstruction](./shooting-minute-by-minute).

### How the claim entered and grew in the record

* **Weeks after the shooting** — **Candace Owens** publishes the donor
  WhatsApp texts, which had been dismissed as a hoax until she released them. That publication is
  what made subsequent private-message claims credible to a large audience —
  Candace Owens' podcast coverage and
  [the Candace Owens timeline](./candace-owens-timeline).

* **On Myron Gaines' podcast and in later interviews (claim)** — Owens reportedly states that a
  **TPUSA staffer** possessed a text from Kirk the day before he died reading
  *"they are going to kill me,"* (KM-07) and that in additional September 9 communications with **three
  people** he said *"I think they're going to kill me."* (KM-09)

* **Dec 9, 2025 — Russell Brand amplification** — **@rustyrockets** posts a large-audience clip of
  Owens restating that people at Turning Point received night-before texts: *"They are going to
  kill me"* — a carry of the claim beyond Owens's core audience.

* **Dec 12, 2025 — public-entry chain** — Amplifiers including **@EvanAKilgore** (~13k likes on the
  main post) and **@TheSCIF** circulate that Owens said **Kolvet told her off the record** about a
  night-before text, and that she went public after **Erika Kirk** publicly
  implied no such messages existed. Dual-recipient packaging (Kolvet **and** Flood) spreads the
  same day via **@itsmorganariel** and **@AdameMedia**.

* **Aggregation phase** — Multiple outlets including Primetimer, Sportskeeda and Times of India
  repeat the quote, at which point it circulates far beyond its original sourcing —
  [Mainstream vs Alternative Media](/Media/Mainstream-vs-Alternative-Media).

* **Variant: "They will kill me"** (KM-08) — Attributed to a message said to have been sent to a recipient
  named **Andrew**. Some threads on X, including [@AdameMedia](/Media/social-media-platforms),
  identify that recipient as **Andrew Kolvet** after a September 9 Zoom.
  Kolvet has not been accused in any court of anything.

* **Variant: "I'm worried that Israel is going to kill me"** (KM-06) — Circulated in second-hand retellings.

* **Variant: "They will kill me if I break away from being pro-Israel"** (KM-03) — Attributed to second-hand
  sources including **Harrison Smith** segments and unnamed friends —
  Political Pressure and Meetings 2025.

* **Variant: "executed tomorrow" (KM-10) / "kill me tomorrow" (KM-10) language** — Attributed in the master file and
  Dec 2025 viral posts to messages involving **Dan Flood** and security staff.
  Flood has not been accused in any court of anything.

* **Variant: "They want me dead" (KM-05) (Turek — dating disputed)** — A thread attributed to
  **Frank Turek** describing a warning plus increased security, circulated by
  [@Villgecrazylady](/Media/social-media-platforms) among others. At least one **2026** rebuttal
  asserts Turek's exchange of that kind was **about six months earlier**, not September 9 — keep
  the viral attribution and the dating pushback together.

* **Denial — Erika Kirk as primary public denier** — Circulating interview clips quote
  **Erika Kirk** saying she has Charlie's cell phone and that he was **not**
  messaging people the day before that he would be murdered / that someone would kill him
  (wording variants: *"I'm gonna be murdered,"* *"someone is going to kill me,"* *"they're going to
  kill me"*). Earlier page summaries referred only to an unnamed "associate"; the denial speaker in
  widely shared clips is Erika. TPUSA has confirmed the **donor** texts but has **not** confirmed
  this specific message, and the alleged September 9 "kill me" messages have **not been released or
  independently verified**.

* **Counter-reword claim (2026 commentary)** — Some posters (e.g. **@_Alisha_114** threads) claim
  **Brian Harpole** and **Andrew Kolvet** later acknowledged *a* fearful
  text but said the wording was **"the left is going to kill me,"** while Owens insists the reported
  wording was **"they/they're."** Without released threads, three versions remain unauthenticated:
  Owens's they/they're; the "left" reword; and total denial. Harpole's own litigation against Owens
  is tracked separately at Brian Harpole v. Candace Owens,
  and bears on how each side's statements should be weighed.

* **Counterweight reading** — Even taken at face value, the messages may be private hyperbole under
  extreme donor stress rather than a specific warning about a specific plot. The official case
  charges no text recipient with anything.

* **Ongoing** — Phone forensics that could settle it are contested territory in the litigation —
  [Phone Extraction](/court/Phone_Extraction) and
  Phone Surveillance Claims. Still missing for authentication:
  full thread screenshots with headers, carrier records for September 9, forensic extraction of the
  handset Erika says she holds, and any sworn statement from a named recipient.

Pre-event warning signs are also why former NCTC director Joe Kent
says the case warranted a deeper federal look: he has cited on the record the online posts
predicting Kirk would die that day as a strong indication that "more than likely there's something
else at play" — the kind of lead he says his center was ordered to stop pursuing.
