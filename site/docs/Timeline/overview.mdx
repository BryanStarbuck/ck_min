**No single claim strains this whole chronology harder than Panguitch.** Staff at Cowboy's Smokehouse
in Panguitch, Utah — **209 road miles and roughly three and a half hours south of Orem** — say the quiet
man who ate alone at their counter from **8:55 to 9:47 PM on September 10, 2025** was
Tyler Robinson. If that identification holds, then every Orem-area entry on the
night-of clock above — the real-time manhunt texts, the post-11 PM sightings, the plan to go back for the
rifle — cannot be his, and the 6:38 PM Orem Dairy Queen sighting would
require a sustained **98–99 mph** for 209 miles. What makes it the most interesting item on this page is
that it is the **most checkable**: a printed receipt, a debit card with a cardholder of record, and drive
time that is pure arithmetic — and by every account given on camera, no official body has publicly moved
to close it. **The identification is claimed by restaurant staff and contested; Robinson is charged, not
convicted.** →
