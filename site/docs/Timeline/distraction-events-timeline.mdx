# Distraction Events Timeline — September 10–11

Between the shot at **12:23:30 PM MDT on September 10, 2025** and the surrender that began around **9:00 PM MDT on September 11**, roughly **33 hours** elapsed. This timeline tracks what occupied those hours instead of the manhunt: one man's shouted false confession, at least two detentions that ended in release, two separate public "suspect in custody" claims that were both wrong, and a set of parallel weapon scares. Times are **Mountain Daylight Time (MDT)**.

The people named here were, with one exception, **released without being charged as the shooter**. They are interesting to document, but we make no claim that any of them knew about or was involved in the killing.

## The Timeline

### September 10 — the first hour

* **12:23:30 PM** — Charlie Kirk is shot in the courtyard at Utah Valley University; the 66 seconds that follow are reconstructed on [the minute-by-minute page](./shooting-minute-by-minute).

* **~12:24 PM** — The figure on the Losee Center roof drops to the grass and moves north; the search has no confirmed direction of travel yet, a gap covered on [the post-shot escape timeline](./tyler-robinson-post-shot-escape).

* **Minutes after the shot** — **George Zinn**, 71, shouts words to the effect of **"I shot him, now shoot me"** and is taken into custody — the **first person detained** in the case. Per the Utah County Sheriff's Office, he later told investigators he made the claim **to draw attention away from the real shooter**. July 2026 prelim coverage summarizes campus-officer testimony that radio traffic reported a **suspect in custody** almost immediately — a **Zinn-driven false positive** on the channel, not a confirmed shooter capture. This is the one on-the-record admission that distraction at the scene was real and not merely theorised.

* **~12:26 PM** — Per police audio summarised in the master file, the **first radio report of a shooting** is logged, three minutes after the shot; the scanner record is on [the dispatch and radio timeline](./dispatch-radio-scanner-timeline).

* **~12:42 PM (+19 minutes)** — A **campus alert states a suspect is in custody.** It was **false**. Researchers tie the alert to the Zinn radio detention. Roughly ten thousand students stayed put on the strength of it; **who authorized the message remains unknown**. The GRAMA thread on campus messaging is documented at UVU GRAMA warnings, the redacted UVU GRAMA records, and pre-shooting warnings.

* **Same hour — Zachariah Qureshi** — **Zachariah Qureshi**, 25, of Provo — a returned missionary who attended as a **supporter**, per DPS/NewsNation profiles — is reportedly detained near the scene. Commentary describes him as having been within roughly **25 feet** of Kirk; family said **mistaken detention**. He was **released and never charged as the shooter** — the documented arc is exonerating. Fox and others named Zinn and Qureshi as **ruled out** the same night. Scanner narrations (Coleman / **@whokilledck**) place a Qureshi-linked **hospital-to-hospital** hunt on the radio before release; hospital staff, via officers on air, denied any such confrontation ([dispatch / radio timeline](./dispatch-radio-scanner-timeline)).

### September 10 — afternoon

* **Early afternoon** — Utah DPS Commissioner **Beau Mason** states publicly that Zinn was taken into custody but **cleared as the shooter the same day** ("Shortly after the shooting, we did have a suspect in custody, George Zinn…"), an account tracked on the Utah DPS page.

* **Afternoon** — A parallel weapon scare runs on the Provo scanner: **Pellet Gun Man**, on a bicycle with an air rifle. Unrelated to the shooting, it nonetheless pulled units and airtime, as did the crowd threads around USA-Chant Man and the Badge Man "fake cop" claim.

* **Afternoon** — An unidentified **all-black-clothed figure** is reported crossing a construction site north of campus, telling an excavator operator he was "just trying to get home safe after a shooting." That figure — described with a black trench coat, black mask, sunglasses, long dark hair and a small backpack — has **never been identified**. See Black Clothing Figure and all-black construction worker.

* **4:21 PM** — **FBI Director Kash Patel publicly announces a suspect is in custody.** It was **incorrect**. The announcement is documented on Kash Patel's briefings and, in the Qureshi context, on the FBI's Qureshi page.

* **~90 minutes later (approx. 5:50 PM)** — The person named in that announcement is **released without further public explanation**. Commentators ask whether the premature announcement **paused the search** and handed the actual shooter a ninety-minute window. That is an **allegation about consequence, not a finding** — **no public CAD log has confirmed a stand-down**; see the suspect manhunt page.

* **Evening / social-media afterlife** — The public narrative begins shifting toward the eventual charged suspect. Separately, after Qureshi's release some social threads continued a **"Muslim shooter"** frame despite same-night clearances — misdirection that is itself part of the distraction window. Coverage patterns for the day are catalogued on [September 10 breaking news](/Media/september-10-breaking-news) and [FBI press conference media](/Media/fbi-press-conference-media).

### September 11 — the second news cycle

* **7:15 AM** — A gray Dodge Challenger is captured on surveillance at the **Maverik station in Cedar City**, roughly 215 miles south, per the charging documents summarised on [the Tyler Robinson timeline](/Timeline/Tyler_Robinson) and Cedar City Maverik.

* **9:58 AM** — The FBI releases its **first photos of a person of interest** — low-resolution stills that drew immediate criticism, discussed on enhanced suspect photos and enhanced photos released after custody.

* **10:44 AM** — The FBI announces a **$100,000 reward**, keeping the "unknown shooter at large" frame alive through the middle of the second day.

* **6:25 PM (disputed)** — Per the court record analysed on [the Mirandizing page](/Timeline/Tyler_Robinson/Mirandizing), Tyler Robinson is Mirandized. Attorney **Baron Coleman** has said publicly that Robinson was in Washington County custody "well before 6:30 PM." If that is right, the "still searching" news cycle continued for hours after the search had ended — the core of the arrest-time discrepancy.

* **7:55 PM** — Per an AP-sourced timeline preserved in the master file, the FBI says it is looking for a **"person of interest"** and releases photos plus rooftop video — again framing the case as unsolved.

* **7:57 PM** — Discord posts attributed to Robinson appear, saying "It was me at UVU yesterday." Whether those posts precede or follow custody is the whole dispute on [custody vs Discord confession](./custody-vs-discord-confession-timeline) and the Discord custody timeline.

* **8:02 PM** — Washington County Sheriff **Nate Brooksby** reportedly receives a call from a retired deputy saying he knows the identity of the person later claimed to be the shooter and is working with the family. Brooksby later resigned; the account and the questions about it are on the Brooksby resignation page and Miranda vs the Brooksby account.

* **~9:00 PM** — Robinson reportedly turns himself in with his parents at the Washington County Sheriff's Office. No surrender footage has been produced — see missing surrender footage and the claim he did not surrender at all.

* **10:00 PM (as stated by the FBI Director)** — Patel states the arrest occurred at 10 PM Thursday, and later describes a **"33-hour manhunt"** — a figure that, worked backwards, lands at about **9:23 PM on September 11**, roughly three hours after the Miranda time in the court record. That arithmetic conflict is the closing item of this timeline and is developed on the surrender and custody page and [the Kash Patel manhunt timeline](./kash-patel-manhunt-timeline).

### Aftermath for the people named here (2026)

* **Jan 29, 2026** — **George Zinn** is sentenced on **obstruction** and **two counts of sexual exploitation of a minor** under Utah's indeterminate scheme (media report maximum range up to **15 years** concurrent). The CSAM counts are court facts about Zinn's separate criminal exposure; they say nothing about what happened on Sept 10.

* **2026 reporting on payment / TPUSA claims** — Candace Owens and others have reported Zinn told hospital staff he was **paid** to act as a decoy and had not been paid yet / did not know by whom; that payment claim remains **unverified** in official charging narratives, and commentary also notes Zinn denials or alternate trauma explanations. A **@DiligentDenizen** interview clip is cited online for Zinn saying he worked for **TPUSA** — attributed claim only; TPUSA is a living organization and the employment claim is **not** established as fact here.

## Conflicts and Disputed Points

| Point in dispute | Version A | Version B | Who asserts each |

| Was a suspect in custody at 12:42 PM Sept 10? | Campus alert said yes; radio had just carried Zinn detention | No shooter was in custody; first detainee was Zinn (false confession) | The UVU alert versus every subsequent official account; radio→alert chain is researcher inference |
| Who was the 4:21 PM "in custody" suspect? | Zachariah Qureshi (25, Provo supporter), per DPS/press and commentary | Never officially named by FBI in a clarifying release | Citizen research + DPS profiles versus the absence of any FBI clarification |
| Effect of the false announcement | Inadvertent error under pressure | Paused the manhunt for ~90 minutes | Officials have not addressed it; **no public CAD stand-down log**; pause theory is allegation, not a finding |
| Was Zinn coordinated? | Officials treat his obstruction as **real but uncoordinated** | Citizen posts frame him as a placed element (payment, CSAM leverage theories) | Utah officials versus X commentators; Zinn himself reportedly **denied being paid**, attributing his actions to a traumatic reaction |
| Payment / TPUSA claim | Zinn reportedly told hospital staff he was paid to be a decoy; interview clip claims TPUSA work | Unverified / contested; Zinn denials and alternate explanations | Candace Owens / @DiligentDenizen reporting; not charged as paid-agent case |
| When did custody actually begin Sept 11? | ~10:00 PM per the FBI Director | Well before 6:30 PM per defense-side accounts | See arrest-time discrepancy |

## Open Questions

1. Who authorised the 12:42 PM campus "suspect in custody" alert, and on what information?
2. Who was the person the FBI Director referred to at 4:21 PM, and what were the grounds for the detention and the release ninety minutes later?
3. Do dispatch logs show units being pulled off the perimeter after either false custody claim, and for how long?
4. How many minutes did the Zinn arrest occupy responders and cameras during the window in which the shooter left the area?
5. Was the all-black figure described by the construction crew ever identified, and does any released footage show him?
6. Why has no surrender footage from the Washington County Sheriff's Office been produced, given the number of cameras normally covering such a lobby?

## Laws (Charlie Kirk)

The complete CAD and dispatch logs for September 10–11 showing unit assignments during and after each false custody claim, the detention and release paperwork for every person held that day, the FBI's internal record behind the 4:21 PM announcement, the UVU alert-authorisation record for the 12:42 PM message, the booking and interview file for George Zinn, and the Washington County Sheriff's Office lobby video for the evening of September 11 are not public yet; the [Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview) could bring these powerful truths out.
