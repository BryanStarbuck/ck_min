# Medical & Death Timeline — Shot to Pronouncement

This timeline covers one narrow window: **12:23:30 PM MDT on September 10, 2025**, when
Charlie Kirk was shot at UVU, through the reported **1:03:30 PM MDT**
pronouncement of death at Timpanogos Regional Hospital
in Orem. Roughly forty minutes. Almost
every minute inside it is contested — the transport method, the hospital chosen, who performed
CPR, and whether a foreign newspaper announced the death before the surgeon walked out of the
operating room. Suspect movement and the campus manhunt belong to
[the minute-by-minute reconstruction](./shooting-minute-by-minute) and
[the ten minutes after the shot](./ten-minutes-after-shot); this page stays with the body.

## The Timeline

### 12:23 PM — The shot and the scene

* **Sept 10, 2025 — 12:23:30 PM MDT (T+00:00)** — The shot. Most compilations on this site anchor
  here, though a competing **12:27 PM** figure circulates in early media timelines — see
  [Shooting Time Disputes](./12-23-shooting-time) for which sources assert which.

* **12:23:48 PM (T+00:18)** — Security begins moving Kirk from the tent, per the movement log
  reproduced on the Medical section's death timeline and
  [the minute-by-minute reconstruction](./shooting-minute-by-minute).

* **12:23:57 PM (T+00:27)** — Terryl Farnsworth is already in position
  on the rock pile filming, a placement citizen researchers flag as unusually fast on
  the rock pile analysis and in
 crime-scene camera actions.

* **12:24:01 PM (T+00:31)** — Kirk is loaded into a black GMC Yukon Denali — a **private SUV, not an
  ambulance**. That vehicle's interior later became evidence in its own right on
  [the SUV transport page](/Mic/suv-transporting-charlie), and the vehicle was reportedly
  destroyed afterward per SUV Destroyed.

* **12:24:03 PM (T+00:33)** — Mikey McCoy is well past the SUV — reportedly
  on the phone to his wife as the shot lands — and
  Blake Neff is not in frame — the seed of the
  call-witness conflict noted below.

* **12:24:12 PM (T+00:42)** — Frank Turek gets into the SUV. Witness accounts
  describe him performing CPR during transport; see
  [Chest Shot Witnesses](/Mic/Chest_Shot_Witnesses) and
  Shot to the Heart for how those descriptions vary.

* **12:24:22 PM (T+00:52)** — Farnsworth is recorded saying *"They just shot Charlie."*

* **12:24:13–12:24:35 PM (T+00:43–01:05)** — The SUV departs for the hospital, per
  the Medical section's death timeline and
  Charlie's Trip to Hospital.

* **12:24:36 PM (T+01:06)** — Farnsworth is recorded saying *"He's dead"* — roughly 66 seconds after
  the shot and about 39 minutes before the reported pronouncement, a gap discussed on
  Witness Account Conflicts.

### 12:25–12:40 PM — Transport

* **~12:25 PM** — Investigators model roughly **one minute** from shot to departure, then a
  **7–10 minute drive**. No ambulance and no sirens appear in the eyewitness accounts summarized on
  Emergency Response,
  Hospital Scene EMS, and
  No On-Site Ambulance and the Scoop-and-Run SUV.

* **Same window (protocol critique, attributed)** — Commentators including **@FlatEarthDaveX**,
  **@jimmy_dore**, and others argue on-site EMTs/medics were
  **bypassed or blocked** in favor of
  private SUV transport with captain's chairs, eliminating a standard ambulance run sheet. Some
  accounts claim an **ambulance later reached the UVU scene unused**; the staging question is
  tracked at Six Campus Officers and No Ambulance Staged.
  These are **protocol
  critiques and attributions**, not adjudicated findings; the 911/McCoy call conflict is tracked
  separately on Hospital Scene EMS and
  McCoy/Neff call conflict.

* **~12:25–12:35 PM** — The destination is **Timpanogos Regional Hospital** in Orem, a **Level IV**
  trauma center about **5.8 miles / 12–15 minutes** away — not **Utah Valley Hospital**, the
  **Level II** center **4.2 miles / 8–12 minutes** away. Utah County protocol for serious gunshot
  wounds reportedly directs transport past Level IV and V facilities to the Level II center. That
  deviation is the core of Hospital Choice & Transport,
  Was a Nearer Level II Trauma Center Bypassed?,
  and the Timpanogos facility page.

* **Open records gap (route cameras)** — As of 2026 commentary, no public **Flock/ALPR or
  traffic-camera** compilation of the Yukon's route from the courtyard to Timpanogos has been
  released (Charlie's Trip to Hospital).

* **Same window (unverified claim)** — Sourcing summarized on
  Hospital People & Bullet Claims reports a bomb scare
  at Intermountain Health Utah Valley Regional at approximately the same time, with staff
  reportedly asked to sign NDAs. This has not been independently confirmed; it is tracked alongside
  Hospital Footage and NDAs.

* **Disputed, same window** — Candace Owens has publicly asked why a
  medical file for Kirk reportedly exists at **Utah Valley Hospital** if he was taken to
  Timpanogos. The claim originates from an unnamed medical staffer and is unverified; it is logged
  on Hospital People & Bullet Claims.

### 12:33–12:45 PM — Arrival and intake

* **~12:33–12:40 PM** — Arrival at Timpanogos. An eyewitness account circulated on
  [social platforms](/Media/social-media-platforms) — amplified again in 2025–2026 by accounts such
  as **@ProjectConstitu** summarizing a hospital-contractor narrative — describes bodyguards meeting
  staff **outside the surgery doors** rather than through the emergency entrance, and describes
  Kirk as already unresponsive / **"DOA"** on arrival. Strong-form claims that he **never entered
  the ER** circulate in anonymous nurse clips; those remain unverified. Recorded on
  Events at Hospital and
  The Empty ER and Hospital Personnel Changes.

* **~12:33–12:43 PM** — Investigators model **5–10 minutes minimum** to unload, admit, and move a
  patient into an operating room. The 911 call record and
  Emergency Response pages track what is and is not public
  about this interval.

* **~12:35 PM (unverified claim)** — A second SUV is described arriving behind the first, reportedly
  driven by chief of staff Mikey McCoy, who — according to a single
  hospital-contractor account — drove to physician parking rather than the ER, picked up an
  **unidentified passenger**, and left without entering. Single-source; re-aired through 2026 with
  the passenger still unidentified. See Events at Hospital.

* **Reported, same afternoon** — The FBI is described as moving quickly to seize interior hospital
  footage while the campus manhunt was still active, per
  Hospital Footage Seizure and
  Hospital Security Cameras Removed. Commentators
  also describe the ER lobby as unusually empty; no official explanation of either point is public.
  Candace Owens and others later framed the hospital interval as part of a
  broader **cover-up of medical evidence** — attributed commentary, not a court finding.

### 12:43–1:03 PM — Operating room and pronouncement

* **~12:43–1:03 PM** — Investigators model **20–30 minutes in the operating room** before a surgeon
  emerged. No hospital record establishing these intervals is public; they are reconstruction, as
  the Medical section states plainly.

* **1:03:30 PM MDT** — The reported pronouncement of death by the
  surgeon who came out of the OR, never publicly named.
  This is the single most-cited hard time in the medical window and appears in the master
  investigation file as **(1:03:30 MT)**. A separate claim that another
  surgeon was blocked from Kirk's room belongs to the
  same interval and is likewise unverified.

* **Clinical detail (unverified in primary public hospital notes)** — Some secondary summaries
  state doctors **briefly restored a pulse** before the reported 1:03:30 PM pronouncement. This
  page treats that as **unconfirmed** until a named medical source or filing appears.

* **Modeled total** — 1 minute to leave, plus 7–10 minutes driving, plus 5–10 minutes to reach the
  OR, plus 20–30 minutes in the OR = **33–50 minutes**, producing an end window of
  **12:56 PM – 1:13 PM MDT**. The 1:03:30 figure sits inside that window, which is why researchers
  treat the model as roughly sound and then ask what happened outside it.

* **The Jerusalem Post conflict** — The master file records that the **Jerusalem Post had already
  announced Kirk's death** before the modeled pronouncement window closed — the conflict is set out
  in full at The OR Timeline Versus the Jerusalem Post.
  High-engagement posts
  on **Sept 11–13, 2025** (e.g. **@RedPillMediaX**, **@jacksonhinkle**, **@AdameMedia**) recirculated
  screenshots asserting JPost was first. If that reporting timestamp holds up, either the death was
  known outside the hospital before the official pronouncement, or the outlet worked from a
  different time source. Neither branch has been publicly reconciled; see
  [Media Timeline Compilation](/Media/Media-Timeline-Compilation),
  [September 10 Breaking News](/Media/september-10-breaking-news), and
  [Media vs Citizens](./media-vs-citizen-investigation-timeline).

### After the pronouncement

* **Afternoon of Sept 10** — The treating surgeon is publicly described as calling the wound an
  *"absolute miracle"* in the sense that the bullet did not exit and therefore did not endanger
  bystanders — a description that sits uneasily with front-neck-exit theories on
  Wound Trajectory Angle,
  Trajectory Wound Mismatch, and
  Is the Front Neck Wound an Entrance or an Exit?.
  No name was attached to
  the quote in the coverage summarized on Doctors; the chest-versus-neck spread
  in first-hour accounts is at
  Chest Wound Accounts Versus the Neck Wound.

* **Sept 10 evening – Sept 11 pre-dawn (claimed)** — According to commentary attributed to attorney
  **[Baron Coleman](./baron-coleman-timeline)** (recirculated mid-2026), Kirk's body left Timpanogos
  around **~8:00 PM** for the Salt Lake City medical examiner (~45 minutes) and was **released by
  about 5:00 AM** — a compressed window citizen investigators call unusually fast. Bridge detail for
  the [autopsy and forensic timeline](./autopsy-forensic-timeline) and
  The Body Flown Out; not independently verified from
  public ME logs on this page.

* **Aug 19 / Aug 25, 2025 (background)** — **Andrew Zenger** was reported appointed CEO of
  Timpanogos Regional Hospital on August 19 and to have started August 25, weeks before the
  shooting. A further citizen claim that he joined the surgical team on August 28 is unverified.
  Tracked on Hospital Leader and
  Medical Staff Changes. Recording this timing alleges no
  wrongdoing by any person named.

* **July 1, 2025 (background)** — **Dr. Deirdre Amaro** began full-time as
  Utah's chief medical examiner, roughly ten weeks before the shooting. Her post-death role is
  covered on Medical Examiner and in the sibling
  [autopsy and forensic timeline](./autopsy-forensic-timeline). Citizen posts refine a claimed
  Israeli-IP Google Trends blip for Amaro to about **June 30 / early July 2025**; Trends does not
  identify individual searchers and does not allege wrongdoing by any living professional.

* **July 2025 (background, claim)** — Google Trends data cited by citizen researchers reportedly
  shows Israeli-IP searches for *Timpanogos Regional Hospital*, for Amaro, and for seven Timpanogos
  surgeons in a two-week window around July 20. Trends data does not identify individual searchers;
  see [Israel IP Search Patterns](/GoogleSearches/israel-ip-search-patterns),
  [Timpanogos medical searches](/GoogleSearches/timpanogos-medical-searches),
  The Timpanogos Surgeon Search-Trends Frenzy, and
  Israel Google Searches — Medical People.

* **Later, date not fixed (disputed)** — The Yukon Denali used for transport was reportedly cut up
  and melted down. If accurate, that removes a blood-spatter and trajectory reference from any
  future forensic reconstruction. See SUV Destroyed,
  The SUV Destroyed and the ADL Floor-Sharing Claim, and
  [the SUV interior photographs](/Mic/suv-transporting-charlie).

## Open Questions

1. What does the hospital's own arrival timestamp say, and does it fall inside the modeled
   12:33–12:40 PM window?
2. Who made the transport decision — the security detail or a dispatcher — and was Utah Valley
   Hospital considered and rejected?
3. What time did the Jerusalem Post publish, in what time zone, and from what source?
4. Which physician pronounced death at 1:03:30 PM, and does that physician's name appear on the
   death certificate?
5. Was CPR performed in the SUV, by whom, and for how long — and was any pulse restored in the OR?
6. Where is the interior hospital footage the FBI reportedly seized, and does it show the arrival
   and the described second SUV?
7. Do Flock/ALPR or traffic cameras document the Yukon route, and who was the alleged second-SUV
   passenger at physician parking?
8. What do ME intake and release logs show for the claimed 8 PM → 5 AM window?

## Laws (Charlie Kirk)

The hospital arrival log and the operating-room entry timestamp, the 911 and dispatch audio behind
the transport decision, the trauma-triage justification for choosing a Level IV center over a
Level II center, the seized interior hospital camera footage, the identity of the pronouncing
physician, and the publication metadata behind the earliest death announcements are things that
the [Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview) may result in powerful truths coming out that
aren't out yet.

## Best understanding of when this happened

Best understanding: shot ~**12:23 PM**; pronouncement later the same afternoon at Timpanogos (exact minute disputed across reports).

**Date range (working model):** SEPT 10 12–4 PM · Shot ~12:23 → pronouncement later PM
