# Other Planes Activity Timeline (N888KG, SAM Flights, Cluster)

:::caution Legal Disclaimer — No Claim Of Wrongdoing
Nothing on this page is a finding by this website that any living person named here committed a crime, acted unethically or illegally, or had any knowledge of the assassination of Charlie Kirk before it happened. No one named here has been charged with, accused by any authority of, or found to have committed any offense connected to September 10, 2025.

What appears here about who was where, who travelled on what, or who attended what comes from **comments made by other people on social media and in public commentary**, attributed to whoever made them. This site does not adopt those claims. This timeline covers **every aircraft layer in the case except two**. The Army-contracted ISR jet has its own [N1098L activity timeline](./n1098l-plane-activity-timeline), and the Egyptian Falcon has its own [SU-BTT movements timeline](./su-btt-plane-movements-timeline). What is left over is still substantial: a private jet that went dark after the shot, two U.S. government **Special Air Mission** flights in and out of an Army intelligence post, a five-jet Egyptian cluster parked at Provo, and a stack of business jets that landed at Provo Municipal in the hours after the shooting. Times are **MDT** unless marked otherwise, and every entry below is a **reported or tracked claim**, not a finding.

## The Timeline

### Spring and summer 2025 — the pattern before the week

* **May 27, 2025** — The Egyptian-registered jet **SU-BTU** (FlightAware's record makes it **SU-BTV**) flies into **Provo Municipal Airport (PVU)**, roughly seven miles from UVU, reportedly carrying a passenger identified in compilations as "Walid" (Walid Mahmoud) — the earliest entry in the Egyptian armada sequence and the reason researchers argue the September stop was not a one-off.

* **June 2, 2025** — The same passenger reportedly **departs Provo**, closing a roughly six-day Egyptian presence at PVU three months before the shooting.

* **July 20, 2025** — Flight-tracking compilations date **SU-BTT**'s first-ever U.S. trip to a Nebraska Army installation, the anchor of the separate [July 20 military meeting timeline](./military-meeting-july-20) and the [Nebraska Army base page](./nebraska-army-base-july-20).

* **Across 2025** — Raw flight logs circulated by citizen investigators claim **five** Egyptian Air Force jets touched Provo during the year: **SU-BTT**, **SU-BND**, **SU-BTU**, **SU-BTV** and **SU-BGM**. Screenshot tables (e.g. @ProjectConstitu / @WeAre_TheElite) list additional Provo touch dates across **February–October 2025** beyond the May–September bullets below. Skeptics reply that VIP government jets routinely rotate through maintenance-capable fields and that the "armada" framing is a tally, not a finding.

* **August 22–27, 2025 (ADS-B claims)** — Commentators (@furbiddentruths and others) report Kirk's aircraft **N102DZ** sat on the ground in **Bozeman, Montana** between a late-August **Defense Intelligence Agency** leadership change (search-trend context on [DIA search patterns](/GoogleSearches/dia-search-patterns)) and TPUSA's public announcement of the fall campus tour with UVU as the first stop. Correlation only; no public flight purpose is disclosed.

### September 4–9 — the pre-event week

* **Sept 4, 2025** — **SU-BTT** arrives at Provo direct from France and **stays through the event window**, the fact that generated the Egyptian jets at Provo page. Citizen compilations also place **SU-BND** in the same Provo window, with one jet departing before the shot and one after in some accounts.

* **Sept 4–10, 2025** — Sources described as connected to **Duncan Aviation** claim **4–6 subcontractors with Egyptian passports** boarded, that their badges read **"U.S. Department of Defense Liaison,"** and that **counter-UAS (anti-drone) equipment** was tested at Provo across this window — the allegation catalogued on the counter-UAS at Provo page. No manifest confirming any of it has been released.

* **Sept 4–10, 2025 (unconfirmed)** — The same threads name a passenger, **"Maj. Ahmed Khalil,"** allege **no-scan priority** Provo tower handling, and cite a UVU-area maintenance eyewitness of Egyptian-crew drone/tech testing on **Sept 8**. The posters themselves label the passenger name unconfirmed; it does not appear in released FAA or court records.

* **Sept 4–10, 2025** — The same threads place the visiting crew at a hotel in **Orem**, per the Egyptian crew hotel page, and allege four rental vehicles were issued through Duncan Aviation to unidentified passengers — the plates tracked on the [rental cars and vehicle movements timeline](./rental-cars-vehicle-movements-timeline).

* **Sept 7, 2025** — The Air Force VIP jet **tail 99-0404** flies **Joint Base Andrews to Colorado Springs**, the first leg of the route reconstructed on the SAM 99-0404 page.

* **Sept 8, 2025** — **99-0404** flies **Colorado Springs to Tucson**, then departs Tucson roughly five hours later, after which trackers say the **transponder appears to go off** — a gap the SAM-702 Fort Huachuca claim page treats as the most contested link in the chain.

* **Sept 8, 2025** — The investigation file places roughly **12 lieutenant colonels** at **Fort Huachuca**, Arizona — the U.S. Army Intelligence Center — the gathering described on the Fort Huachuca Sept 9 page and in the Fort Huachuca military timeline.

* **Sept 9, 2025 — 00:49** — Per the file's own timeline entry, **SAM702** departs. Candace Owens separately describes the **SAM-702** VIP flight as leaving Fort Huachuca on the **morning** of September 9 with **four passengers**. Which clock reading is correct has never been reconciled.

* **Sept 9, 2025 — 12:31 PM** — **99-0404** flies **Fort Huachuca to El Paso**, then El Paso back to **Joint Base Andrews**, closing the loop.

* **Early September 2025** — The callsign **SAM650** appears in the same compilations on a **Wilmington → Andrews → Nashville** itinerary with what posters call "Colorado Springs syncs." No tail number has ever been attached to it, which is the single largest gap on that page.

* **Sept 9–13, 2025 (claims)** — Compilations (e.g. @ProjectConstitu, crediting @nihilox) track NetJets callsign **EJA685 (N685QS)** near the **El Paso / Biggs** window associated with the SAM chain and a short **Salt Lake City → Las Vegas** hop after the SU-BTT departure narrative — offered as a possible passenger-swap leg. No passenger manifest supports any swap; living NetJets operators are not accused of wrongdoing.

* **Sept 9, 2025** — **Google Trends** shows the tail number **n888kg** being searched **in Utah** — the day before the shooting, and before that aircraft had done anything newsworthy. The [planes search-pattern page](/GoogleSearches/planes-search-patterns) records it; nothing establishes who searched, or why. Separate Fort Huachuca **Google image-search** prep claims (high-level names searched before the SAM window) live primarily on the Fort Huachuca military timeline.

### September 10 — morning

* **~7:00 AM (skeptic clock)** — Some posters assert an Egyptian-registered jet left Provo about **five hours before** the shot (@AuburnJPJ and others), arguing the Egyptian cluster proves presence and maintenance rotations, not operational timing around 12:23. Track this early-departure rebuttal beside the mid-morning power-up claims below.

* **~9:05 AM** — One of the parked **Egyptian jets** at Provo Municipal reportedly **powers up**, then drops off tracking — the start of the anomaly described on the Sept 10 flight timeline. The ADS-B record does not bear it out: SU-BTT had taken off at 7:14 AM, and SU-BND was heard only while parked, 10:05–11:34 AM and 1:40–2:29 PM (SU-BND).

* **~9:30 AM** — Charlie Kirk's aircraft **N102DZ** is reported to land at **Salt Lake City International**, roughly 45 miles north of UVU, rather than at Provo seven miles away. Candace Owens flagged that diversion in her November 17, 2025 "Operation Mocking-Plane" episode.

* **Morning** — The contract survey aircraft **N59906**, a Piper Navajo operated by MARC Inc., reportedly flies **four mapping passes** beginning and ending at Provo at roughly **19,000 feet MSL**; the recovered ADS-B shows five, 11:01–11:35 AM, and the aircraft back on the ground at Provo by 12:06 PM. Early posts labelled these "low-altitude"; the N59906 page corrects that — it is a standard high-altitude mapping band, not a treetop pass.

* **~10:06 AM** — **N560TW** lands at **Provo** from Scottsdale. Some posts instead give **~10:26 AM**; the N560TW turnaround page preserves both readings.

* **~10:27 AM** — **N560TW** departs Provo for **Santa Barbara** after about twenty minutes on the ground (11:37 AM is its Santa Barbara departure, Pacific time) — the turnaround that first drew Candace Owens' attention. Some posts treat the tail as almost exclusively TPUSA-associated travel; that ownership/use pattern is commentary, not a court finding.

* **~1:43 PM** — **N560TW** is back on the ground at **Scottsdale** (12:43 PM Arizona time), about eighty minutes after the shot at UVU.

* **Afternoon (disputed tarmac narrative)** — Citizen ADS-B readings (@hurtfeelingzday and others) claim **N560TW** left a **Santa Barbara** gate roughly **fourteen minutes** after the UVU shot — too soon, they argue, for a cold aircraft to be staged for a Provo recovery — and that Andrew Kolvet's tarmac-hug account fits **Scottsdale**, not Provo. That conflicts with other Provo-arrival narratives for **N872RA** / **N102DZ**. Attribute flight-app disputes only; no wrongdoing is alleged.

### September 10 — afternoon, the Provo arrival stack

* **12:27 PM MST** — **N40JD**, a six-seat Beechcraft Premier 1, **departs Scottsdale**. The clock collision with the disputed **12:27 PM** shot time is a time-zone coincidence, not evidence — see [Shooting Time Disputes](./12-23-shooting-time).

* **~1:11–1:20 PM** — **N888KG**, a Bombardier Challenger 300 registered to an LLC in **Lehi, Utah**, **departs Provo** heading southeast — roughly 45 minutes to an hour after the shot. The entity is profiled on the N888KG LLC page, which alleges no wrongdoing by its owners.

* **~1:29 PM** — The Egyptian jet that powered up at 9:05 AM is described as **back on tracking and idling on the tarmac** to this point, having reactivated roughly **17 minutes after the shot**, per the Sept 10 flight timeline; see the 9:05 AM entry for what the ADS-B record shows.

* **~1:30 PM** — **N888KG** goes unheard by the free ADS-B archives over south-central Utah, about 145 km north of the Arizona line, for 76 minutes. Trackers reported the signal lost at ~1:43 PM near the northern Arizona border.

* **~1:48 PM** — **N888KG** is reported to land at **Page Municipal Airport (PGA)**, Arizona.

* **~2:29 PM** — **N40JD** lands at **Provo**, per the flight-record screenshots circulated by investigators. A competing figure of **~4:40 PM** appears elsewhere on this site — see the conflicts table.

* **~2:30 PM** — **N888KG** departs Page for the return leg. The owner is reported to have stated that **no one disembarked** at Page and that **seven company employees boarded** there for the trip home.

* **~3:06 PM** — **N888KG** lands back at **Provo**. No public flight plan is associated with the dark leg, which trackers label a "ghost flight."

* **~3:31 PM** — **N102DZ**, Kirk's own aircraft, lands at **Provo** after a Scottsdale departure. Candace Owens argues this is the only tail whose timing lets Erika Kirk reach the hospital by the **4:00 PM** that Frank Turek stated on the record.

* **~4:36 PM** — **N872RA**, a Hawker Beechcraft that left **Santa Barbara at ~2:19 PM**, lands at **Provo**. This is the Santa Barbara inbound the tarmac-hug account depends on, and the reason the "4:40 PM Kolvet plane" label is contested below. Andrew Kolvet is a living person and nothing here alleges wrongdoing by him.

* **Afternoon** — Aircraft are described as clustering on the **Duncan Aviation** ramp at Provo after the 3:30 PM landings, with **SU-BND** reported still parked and running.

* **Late evening Sept 10 – ~2:00 AM Sept 11 (claims)** — @PBI_CarrieQuinn and others flag air ambulance **N315NG** departing Provo late on the 10th and landing the Phoenix/Scottsdale area around **2:00 AM** on the 11th. Separate fringe body-preservation theories attach to this tail; ADS-B presence and purpose remain unconfirmed in official releases. Living persons named in those threads are not accused of wrongdoing here.

### September 11 and after

* **Sept 11, 2025** — **SU-BTT** departs the United States for Egypt, per the [SU-BTT movements timeline](./su-btt-plane-movements-timeline).

* **Sept 15, 2025** — The [scene-changes timeline](./scene-changes-paving-timeline) records ground-level alterations at UVU; some posts also date an **N59906** survey flight to this day rather than September 10; the recovered ADS-B puts the campus passes on September 10.

* **Late 2025** — Compilations claim two Egyptian jets appear near Erika Kirk's travel **73 documented times**, the tally examined on the following Charlie and Erika page. Critics including Liz Wheeler argue such overlap counts rely on loose matching criteria and on **UTC-versus-local time-zone errors**. Of the 85 claimed rows this site reconstructs, ADS-B proves 25 at the named field.

* **Nov 17, 2025** — Candace Owens publishes "Operation Mocking-Plane," pulling the airport-diversion and flight-overlap arguments into wider circulation — tracked on the [Candace Owens timeline](./candace-owens-timeline).

* **2026** — The transport aircraft used after the funeral is documented on the casket flight page. No flight record for any aircraft on this page has been produced through the court process tracked on the [discovery and evidence timeline](./discovery-brady-evidence-timeline).

## Open Questions

1. Who filed, and who paid for, the **N888KG** leg that carried no public flight plan, and do FAA strips confirm the owner's account of the Page stop?
2. What **tail number** flew under the **SAM650** callsign, and what was its manifest?
3. Do Fort Huachuca **flight strips and visitor logs** confirm or refute the **SAM-702** passenger list, and who booked the trip?
4. What did the **N59906** survey cameras record over the UVU campus, who tasked the flight, and on which date did it actually fly?
5. Do **Duncan Aviation** ground-handling, fuel and rental-car records exist for the Egyptian aircraft across **September 4–10**, and do they name the passengers?
6. Which aircraft carried **Erika Kirk** into Utah on September 10 — the ~3:31 PM N102DZ or the ~4:36 PM N872RA?
7. Was **n888kg** searched in Utah on **September 9** by a person with prior knowledge, or is the spike an artifact of how Trends aggregates rare queries?
8. Who flew **EJA685 / N685QS** near the El Paso and Salt Lake windows, and do any manifests support a passenger-swap claim?
9. Does ADS-B archive confirm **N315NG**'s late Sept 10 Provo departure, and what was its filed purpose?
10. Does any primary source confirm the unconfirmed Egyptian passenger names and **no-scan** tower claim?
