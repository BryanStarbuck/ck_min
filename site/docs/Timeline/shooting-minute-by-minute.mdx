# The Shooting: 12:23 PM MDT Minute-by-Minute Timeline

This page covers **66 seconds**. The master investigation file logs a reconstruction that starts at **12:23:30 PM MDT** — the instant of the shot — and ends at **12:24:36 PM MDT**. The public second-by-second courtyard evacuation offsets match the December 2025 reconstruction posted by **@BasedSamParker** (complete evacuation timeline), which flags the McCoy/Neff witness conflict without accusing anyone of a crime. Everything inside that window is timed to the second against video frames, and almost every second inside it is contested. Times below are **Mountain Daylight Time (MDT / Utah clock)**, with elapsed offsets shown as `mm:ss` from the 12:23:30 start mark unless a competing bang second is noted.

For the wider window either side of these 66 seconds, see [the ten minutes before the shot](./ten-minutes-before-shot) and [the ten minutes after](./ten-minutes-after-shot).

## The Timeline

### Setup — 12:15 to 12:23:29 PM MDT

* **12:15 PM MDT** — Per the charging documents summarized on the [affidavit of probable cause page](/Timeline/Tyler_Robinson/Affidavit_Probable_Cause), the accused entered the Losee Center from the southeast side and crossed a railing onto the roof — an account tracked in detail on the [pre-shot campus timeline](./tyler-robinson-pre-shot-campus) and disputed by the stairs-guy identity gap.

* **12:17 PM MDT** — The same filings place the accused climbing a short wall and crouching on the north side of the roof, the moment the [roof-jumping claims](./roof-jumping-timeline) and the [camera-sightings record](./camera-sightings-tyler-robinson) both hinge on.

* **12:22 PM MDT** — Filings describe a run west across the roof to a prone firing position roughly **430 feet** from the stage; commentators note the distinctive limp seen in earlier footage is no longer visible in this stretch.

* **~12:23 PM MDT** — Hunter Kozak asks the last audience question of the event; the FBI questioned and cleared him, and his on-camera reaction is treated by officials as an ordinary startle response, though it is discussed among crowd-figure threads.

* **12:23:28 PM MDT (−00:02)** — Citizen analysts working from the released rooftop clip calculate that the running figure **began an all-out sprint two seconds before the shot**, an argument set out on the runner-sprint-before-shot page and used by Proof Not Tyler researchers to say the timing does not close.

### The 66 seconds — 12:23:30 to 12:24:36 PM MDT

* **12:23:27 PM MDT (alternate bang)** — A later multi-angle analysis by **@BasedSamParker** (Dec 8, 2025) places the bang at **12:23:27** rather than 12:23:30. Early circulating "FBI subject timeline" graphics also used **12:20**. These are competing citizen clocks beside the site standard and the media **12:27** figure ([shot-time disputes](./12-23-shooting-time)).

* **12:23:30 PM MDT — 00:00** — The shot (site standard). Charlie Kirk is struck in the neck mid-answer; official accounts and the master file use this second, while competing clocks are unpacked on [the shot-time dispute page](./12-23-shooting-time) and 12:23 vs 12:27.

* **~12:23 PM MDT (parallel vehicle clock, claimed)** — Baron Coleman parking-lot analysis, as summarized on X, describes a **white SUV** that arrives near the shot second, pauses during the roof-egress window, then leaves — a vehicle clock concurrent with the courtyard 66 seconds (unproven support-vehicle theory; see [vehicles timeline](./rental-cars-vehicle-movements-timeline) and the [Baron Coleman timeline](./baron-coleman-timeline)).

* **12:23:30 PM MDT — 00:00** — In a separate reconstruction preserved in the master file and the Sam Parker public log, **Mikey McCoy** — TPUSA chief of staff — is described as calling his wife **Elizabeth McCoy** at the same second the shot lands, the claim examined on McCoy Reportedly Phones His Wife as the Shot Lands. When Erika Kirk's account of a Mikey call is restated, convert carefully: the reconstruction notes a **12:23 MDT / 11:23 AM Arizona** dual-zone risk.

* **12:23:34 PM MDT — 00:04** — The publicly released rooftop video **begins here**, four seconds after the shot; researchers argue the trim point conceals roughly 60 feet of the runner's path, a complaint filed under withholding rooftop video and selective 4K release.

* **12:23:48 PM MDT — 00:18** — **Security begins to move Charlie.** This is the first entry in the master file's / Sam Parker second-by-second log; the security team response and the absence of a staged medical plan are covered on six officers, no ambulance.

* **12:23:55 PM MDT — 00:25** — A second reconstruction in the master file states Kirk **"is carried into the SUV"** at this second — six seconds earlier than the 00:31 load time in the primary log. Both figures are recorded here; neither has been authenticated against a released, timecoded master video.

* **12:23:57 PM MDT — 00:27** — **Terryl Farnsworth is already in position on the rock pile, filming.** The speed with which he reached that vantage is the whole subject of Farnsworth Already Filming From the Rock Pile. Candace Owens–linked releases claim he also filmed a **selfie-style** reaction clip while Charlie was carried out. These are video-based citizen claims about living AV staff — not court findings of wrongdoing. We make no claim he knew anything in advance; nothing indicates anyone on the security or AV team knew of any device. Later handling of camera media is covered under SD cards removed from the scene.

* **12:24:01 PM MDT — 00:31** — **Charlie is loaded into the SUV.** The vehicle itself, its blurred windows, and what happened to it afterwards are documented on [the SUV transporting Charlie](/Mic/suv-transporting-charlie) and SUV blurred windows.

* **12:24:02 PM MDT — 00:32** — In the frame at this second, **Mikey McCoy is alone**; Blake Neff is nowhere in view. This single frame is the anchor of the McCoy alone in frame, Neff nowhere conflict described below. 2026 posts still ask which vehicle Neff entered and when he left the courtyard — still unanswered on the public record.

* **12:24:03 PM MDT — 00:33** — **Mikey McCoy is well past the SUV, without Blake Neff.** The master file's author and Sam Parker both state plainly that they make no accusation and are only trying to reconstruct the timeline; the same caution applies on this page.

* **12:24:12 PM MDT — 00:42** — **Frank Turek gets into the SUV.** Turek is one of the small group in the vehicle alongside Brian Harpole and Dan Flood per accounts collected on the hospital-trip page.

* **12:24:13 to 12:24:35 PM MDT — 00:43–01:05** — **The SUV departs for the hospital.** The 22-second spread is the master file's own range, not a single logged departure second; the destination choice is analysed on hospital choice and transport and trauma center bypassed.

* **12:24:22 PM MDT — 00:52** — **"They just shot Charlie"** — Terryl Farnsworth, on his own audio. This is the first spoken confirmation captured in the reconstruction window.

* **12:24:36 PM MDT — 01:06** — **"He's dead"** — attributed in the same citizen reconstruction to Terryl Farnsworth. Sixty-six seconds after the shot, a bystander with a camera is heard stating the outcome that the hospital would not announce for another forty minutes or more. The ordinary reading is that a person who has just watched a man shot in the neck at close range said what he believed he had seen; nothing here suggests he knew anything in advance. The reconstruction **ends here**.

### Immediately outside the window

* **12:24 PM MDT** — A second reconstruction places the **SUV beginning transport** at this minute, and researchers note that **N1098L** flew a **335 ft AGL** low pass over Orem at 12:24 PM, addressed on N1098L low-pass timing and N1098L HADES over UVU. The recovered ADS-B puts N1098L's two low passes at 9:16 AM and 12:48 PM MDT, both over Provo Municipal Airport about 4 km from campus; at 12:23 it was 204–229 km north (N1098L).

* **~12:24 PM MDT** — Filings describe a **dark item — the rifle wrapped in a dark towel — being placed on the rooftop** before the figure drops to the grass, the sequence handled on rifle and towel on the roof and the gun-discovery sequence.

* **~+2 to +9 minutes (citizen staff-anomaly log, claimed)** — @DiligentDenizen compilations place additional courtyard acts just outside the 66-second window: Farnsworth removing camera media (the compilations claim that media then appears with **an AV staffer**, who then leaves). Living staff — describe alleged acts on video only; no intent or crime is asserted here. SD-card chain continues on cameras and SD cards removed and [ten minutes after](./ten-minutes-after-shot).

* **12:26 PM MDT** — Per one reconstruction, **Mikey McCoy three-ways Erika Kirk into a call**; per police audio summarized in the master file, the **first radio report of a shooting** also lands around 12:26, covered on [the dispatch and scanner timeline](./dispatch-radio-scanner-timeline).

* **Mar 2026 (rear-camera release)** — Candace Owens released the **rear-camera segment** of the impact window; online commentary emphasizes **no visible blood on Kirk's back** in that angle — a wound-path observation carried on wound analysis and competing theories and dated on the [Candace Owens timeline](./candace-owens-timeline).

* **July 2026 (hearing coverage)** — Some courtroom reporters claim prosecutors played roof video of the shot sequence at the [preliminary hearing](/court/Preliminary_Hearing/overview); other hearing summaries quote that no clear public clip of the shooting itself was shown. Both statements circulate; the untrimmed master video remains the open request.

* **12:29–12:35 PM MDT (estimated)** — Kirk arrives at Timpanogos Regional Hospital; everything from here forward belongs to [the ten-minutes-after timeline](./ten-minutes-after-shot) and [the medical and death timeline](./medical-death-timeline).

## Open Questions

1. Is there a single, continuous, timecoded master video of the courtyard covering 12:23:00 to 12:25:00 — and if so, why has only a trimmed excerpt starting at 12:23:34 been released, and how does July 2026 courtroom video relate to that master?
2. Which bang second is correct for frame sync — **12:23:27**, **12:23:30**, **12:20**, or **12:27** — and against which authenticated clock?
3. Which SUV-load second is correct, 12:23:55 or 12:24:01, and what frame does each derive from?
4. What do carrier records show for the McCoy call sequence — outbound at 12:23:30 MDT (or 11:23 Arizona), and who was actually on each leg?
5. Where was Blake Neff standing between 12:23:30 and 12:24:36, which vehicle did he enter, and does any frame place him beside McCoy?
6. Does the courtyard audio captured by the [AV team](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup) contain a second acoustic event, as argued on the two-event split page?
7. What is the exact second the SUV crossed the campus boundary, and does any traffic or campus camera confirm it inside the 00:43–01:05 range?
8. Who is the white SUV at bang in Coleman parking-lot analysis, and is it identified in any LPR or release?
