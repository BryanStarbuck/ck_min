== What this area is ==

Everything the public can learn about State's Exhibit 11, the Utah medical examiner's sealed
nine-page report on Charlie Kirk. No copy of the report exists in public. The only words of it
anyone can read are the lines the prosecutors quoted, and edited, on pages 2 and 3 of their
July 28, 2026 bindover brief. That brief is the prosecution's advocacy document, written to win.
Treat it as one-sided, never as the truth about the wound. Investigators conclude Charlie Kirk
was killed by a small shaped charge in the microphone transmitter on his chest. The .30-06
round is the government's narrative. Do not underweight the shaped charge.

Three pieces, one route (/scorecard/exhibit/):
  * The Exhibit 11 page, {STATIC_DIR}/overview.html: all 28 pages of the State's brief with
    every Exhibit 11 quote highlighted. GENERATED by Prompts/exhibit_build/build_exhibit_11.py
    from exhibit_11_template.html. Edit the generator, never the HTML.
  * The Analysis, {EXHIBIT_DIR}/analysis.mdx (/scorecard/exhibit/analysis): who wrote page 2,
    which words are the examiner's, and the injuries one body part at a time.
  * The Body Tour, {STATIC_DIR}/body_tour.html (the contents page) plus one page per body part
    ({STATIC_DIR}/{body_part}.html: neck, brain, heart, lungs, spine).


== The Body Tour: what we are doing ==

A medical illustration for a jury. Each body page shows a see-through 3D body (a mesh skin with
the organs inside) and tells the story as you scroll, like a marketing page: text cards rise on
the right, lines reach across to the organs they name, and parts light up, fade or break apart.
The voice is a medical examiner explaining to jurors: plain words first, the medical term
second, technically accurate.

Every body page has a back button (to body_tour.html), the body part name at top left, a menu
of all body parts, and two tabs:
  * "If Bullet": draws the State's account exactly as the prosecutors tell it (one round from
    the Losee Center roof, about 415 ft away and 68 ft higher, entering the front-left of the
    neck, "front to back, slightly left to right, and downward"). Then it tests every Exhibit 11
    finding for that part: reached, strained, or not reached without a second cause.
  * "Shaped Charge Explosion": a small shaped charge in the RØDE transmitter under his shirt on
    the right of his chest. Its jet travels up and across his body to the front-left of the
    neck, and a pressure wave passes through the chest. The same findings are tested against it.

The HTML is static because site/docs/ serves only md/mdx. The 3D engine (three.js, vendored, no
CDN) and every name a page may use (organs, anchors, findings, camera shots) are documented in
{TOUR_REFERENCE}. The list of body parts lives in one place, body_tour_assets/parts.js, and
feeds every page's menu.

Entry points into the tour: the three buttons on /scorecard/overview and on
/scorecard/Not_A_Rifle_Wound/Side_To_Side (Exhibit 11, Who Wrote Page 2? Analysis, Body Tour),
the "More" menu on the Exhibit 11 page, and the analysis page. The two scorecard MDX pages have
staging twins under Prompts/scorecard_build/staging/scorecard/; change both.


== Updating it ==

Run {TOUR_PROMPT}. Name a body part in the run text to add it ("add the liver"). With no name,
it re-checks every part against the latest evidence. It reasons through both causes, rewrites
the pages, keeps the menus in sync, and checks its work with the tools in
Prompts/body_tour_build/.
