## Charlie Kirk: The Threat He Said Actually Got His Attention

Speaking on stage before September 10, 2025, Charlie Kirk drew a line between two kinds of people who hated him. The stranger on a campus who wanted him dead, he said, did not bother him. The ones who did were the "close proximity" people — the ones he already knew, who had some sort of relationship with him, and who he says had betrayed or deceived him. Clip surfaced by [@NihiloX](https://x.com/NihiloX/status/2089076950174158960) on X, quoting [@unhealthytruth](https://x.com/unhealthytruth/status/2088853084998865178).
