If Bullet The government's narrative

One .30-06 rifle round, fired from the Losee Center roof about 415 feet away and 68 feet higher, entering the front-left of his neck at a shallow downward angle: about 9 degrees by the sworn figures, about 6 in published estimates. Drawn exactly as the prosecutors describe it: "front to back, slightly left to right, and downward".

Shaped Charge Explosion The investigators' conclusion

A small shaped charge in the RØDE microphone transmitter he wore under his shirt on the upper right chest, about two inches below the neckline: a narrow jet driven up and across his body to the neck, about 45 degrees upward, and a pressure wave through his chest.

Where these words come from

State's Exhibit 11 is the Utah Office of the Medical Examiner's nine-page report. It was admitted at the preliminary hearing on July 6, 2026, through an agent who was not at the autopsy, and the judge barred it from publication. Three weeks later, the prosecutors quoted pieces of it in their bindover brief. Those quotes, a selection chosen and edited by the side trying to win the case, are every word of the report the public has. The examiner has never testified.

Inside the quotation marks the words are the examiner's, with the prosecutors' edits in square brackets and their cuts marked "…". The word "bullet", and the direction "front to back, slightly left to right, and downward", are the prosecutors' own words, written outside the quotation marks.
