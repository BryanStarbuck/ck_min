4. AUTO-GENERATED CAPTIONS EXIST ON EVERY HEARING VIDEO — AND ARE THE CHEAPEST
   ROUTE TO THE ONE PROCEEDING WITH NO TRANSCRIPT
==============================================================================
An earlier note in this investigation recorded that yt-dlp reported "has no
automatic captions" for these videos and that the only track was live_chat.
THAT IS WRONG, and it matters, because it closed off a route that is open.

Fetching the watch page directly on 2026-08-31 and reading the
playerCaptionsTracklistRenderer shows a real ASR track on every one of the 11
primary recordings, for example on Ws3jBiVuh5U (the 4h35m contempt evidentiary
hearing, the proceeding we have no transcript of):

    "captionTracks":[{"baseUrl":"https://www.youtube.com/api/timedtext?v=
      Ws3jBiVuh5U&...&kind=asr&lang=en",
      "name":{"simpleText":"English (auto-generated)"},
      "vssId":"a.en","languageCode":"en","kind":"asr","isTranslatable":true}]

The same structure is present on 6Z76La2OYKQ and on the other nine. yt-dlp's
denial was a symptom of it being blocked ("The page needs to be reloaded"), not
a statement about the video.

WHY THE CAPTIONS ARE NOT IN THIS DIRECTORY: on 2026-08-31 the
https://www.youtube.com/api/timedtext endpoint returned HTTP 429 to this machine
on every attempt, across every format (default, &fmt=json3, &fmt=srv3, &fmt=vtt),
and was still 429 after a 90-second pause. That is per-IP rate limiting, almost
certainly earned by the yt-dlp probing done minutes earlier in the same run. It
is a temporary block on this address, NOT a removal and NOT an absence.

HOW TO GET THEM ON A LATER RUN, from a cold IP:
  1. curl the watch page with a real browser User-Agent.
  2. Regex out  "captionTracks":(\[.*?\])  and JSON-decode it (the blob is
     unicode-escaped, so .encode().decode('unicode_escape') first).
  3. Take the en track's baseUrl and append &fmt=json3.
  4. curl that URL. Each cue is events[].segs[].utf8 with tStartMs.
  Do ONE video at a time and leave minutes between them. Do not run yt-dlp
  against the same channel first — that is what triggered the 429.

WHAT THE CAPTIONS ARE AND ARE NOT: machine ASR. No speaker labels, and names and
legal terms are routinely misheard. They are a SEARCH INDEX for locating a
passage in the video. Never quote them as the record — quote the video itself or
a certified transcript.
