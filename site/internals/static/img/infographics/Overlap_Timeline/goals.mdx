# Infographic Goal — Overlap_Timeline

**Rewritten 2026-08-26.** The first version of this graphic filled its bars from
a hostile line-by-line audit of the claim — an audit done by *reading
flight-tracking websites*. Since then this investigation went and recovered the
underlying ADS-B position traces, kept the bytes in the repo, and re-tested every
row against them. The numbers moved a long way, and in a direction nobody
predicted. Everything below is the plan for the graphic as it now stands. The
superseded numbers are named where they are superseded, not quietly dropped.

**Two artefacts, one design, and only one of them may be published.** The SVG is
generated by `generate_overlap_timeline.py` straight out of `overlaps.csv`, so
every bar is plotted from the register and it moves when the data moves. **That
is the file the site serves, and it is the only one that may be.**

The JPG is the same design rendered by NanoBanana from
`nana_banana_pro_prompt.txt`. It sets the type and the composition beautifully
and it renders every quoted string correctly — but **a generative model draws a
plausible chart, it does not plot a real one.** The 26 August 2026 render
invented its own monthly distribution: amber bars in months that have none, and
a lower trace drawn mostly solid where the real one is mostly dotted. Every
string was right and every bar was wrong.

So the JPG is a **design reference only**. Do not swap it onto a page, and do not
read a number off it. On a site whose entire argument is that the record was not
checked carefully enough, publishing a hand-waved chart would be the single
worst available own goal.

## Audience

A reader who has already heard the headline — "Egyptian jets followed the Kirks
73 times" — from X, from a Candace Owens broadcast, or from a friend forwarding a
screenshot. They arrive with one of two prior beliefs and the graphic has to work
for both:

* The believer, who has heard 73 and treats it as a proven count of shadowing.
* The skeptic, who has heard the rebuttals — that "overlap" meant within 100
  miles and plus-or-minus three days, and that a line-by-line audit found most of
  the sheet wrong — and has written the whole angle off.

Both of them are scanning. Neither has read the 80-row table. Neither will.

There is now a third reader, and the graphic is really aimed at them: the person
who wants to know **whether anything survives once you stop arguing about
screenshots and go get the primary position data.**

## What they should learn

The sentence the reader should be able to say out loud after four seconds:

> "They went and got the raw flight data back. Most of the three-year claim still
> can't be tested — but where it can be, the Charlie Kirk side holds up, and
> nothing on it is refuted."

Not "it is all real." Not "it is all fake." **The asymmetry between the two
ledgers, measured against data we hold, is the finding.**

## Conceptual framing

**A three-year seismograph with two traces, read against primary data we own.**

Time runs left to right. Two horizontal bands share one time axis: Charlie Kirk
and TPUSA above the line, Erika Kirk below it, mirrored, like a two-channel
recording. Each month is one bar. Bar height is how many claimed overlaps the
compilers logged in that month.

Then the second layer, which is what makes this an argument rather than a chart:
**every bar is filled according to what a recovered ADS-B trace says about that
exact aircraft on that exact date.** Solid means a trace we pulled and kept puts
the jet within 15 km of the field the claim names. Hollow means a trace we hold
puts it somewhere else. Dotted means no surviving archive can answer.

The reader sees a long, mostly-dotted Erika trace running the full three years —
and a shorter Charlie trace that is *more than half solid*, with no hollow bars
in it at all, waking up hard in the last five months.

The picture argues what the data argues: the wide claim is mostly untestable, the
narrow one is corroborated, and the September 2025 cluster is the densest solid
run on the chart.

## What we are educating on

1. That there is a claimed count, that it is a researcher tally read off public
   flight-tracking history, and that it spans October 2022 to October 2025.
2. That the count is overwhelmingly an *Erika* count — 56 of the 80 dated rows —
   and that the Erika side is the side with no published itinerary behind it.
3. That this site did not settle it by arguing about the tally. It pulled the raw
   traces out of two independent volunteer archives, stored them, and measured
   each claim point-by-point against the field it names.
4. That the result splits three ways and only one of the three is a refutation:
   **25 corroborated, 3 refuted, 1 right-area-wrong-field, 47 the archives cannot
   answer, 4 that name no aircraft at all.**
5. That the corroboration rate is wildly different on the two sides — **13 of 24
   on the Charlie/TPUSA side and not one refuted, against 12 of 56 on the Erika
   side with all 3 refutations there.**
6. That six of those dates are the strongest evidence class there is: the jet
   transmitting from **on the ground** at the named field.
7. That September 2025 is the densest solid cluster on the chart, and it sits in
   the week of the assassination.

## Perspective — what the reader currently cannot see

A number cannot show distribution, and an argument about a spreadsheet cannot
show provenance. "73" hides the four facts that matter most:

* **Where in time they fall.** 73 sounds continuous. It is not — 15 of the 46
  months in the window have zero, and the tail of 2023 has a nine-month gap.
* **Whose side they are on.** 73 sounds like one pattern. It is two very
  different ledgers with very different evidentiary strength stacked into one
  figure.
* **Which ones can even be tested.** 47 of 80 cannot, and that is a property of
  archive retention, not of the claim.
* **What happens to the ones that can.** Of the 29 rows primary data can decide,
  **25 come back corroborated and 3 come back refuted.** That runs the opposite
  way from what has been published about this sheet, and the graphic has to make
  it visible rather than assert it.

## Polarity and scope

Both directions, deliberately, and the tension between them is the graphic.

* **Wide:** the full 46-month axis, edge to edge, showing three years of rhythm
  and the dead months inside it. This is the zoom-out that makes "73" look thin
  and mostly untestable.
* **Tight:** a call-out lens at frame right on the first ten days of September
  2025, showing what the recovered traces actually record at Provo, to the
  minute. This is the zoom-in that makes the remainder look solid.

Expressed visually by literal scale change: the main timeline is one continuous
low band across the full width; the September call-out is a boxed, magnified
excerpt with thin leader lines back to its position on the main axis. The reader
should feel the eye pulled from "three years of untested claims" to "four days
that are on the record to the minute."

## Sizing — what is bigger and what is smaller

Driven by the order-of-understanding list below. Larger equals more important.

1. **The two-trace timeline** is the largest object on the canvas by a wide
   margin. It is the whole point; everything else annotates it.
2. **The title** is second — it has to survive being seen as a thumbnail, and it
   has to carry the word *recovered*, because the recovery is the news.
3. **The September call-out lens** is third, and it is the only element allowed
   to compete visually with the main timeline. It is what the argument narrows to.
4. **The stat block** (80 / corroborated / refuted / cannot answer) is fourth —
   legible but quiet.
5. **The legend** is fifth and deliberately small; the fill styles should be
   self-evident enough that most readers never look at it.
6. **The source line** is smallest. Present, honest, not competing.

The Erika trace is physically longer and busier than the Charlie trace, and that
asymmetry is left alone rather than balanced — the imbalance IS the information.
So is the second asymmetry now visible inside it: the Erika trace is mostly
dotted, the Charlie trace is mostly solid.

## Framing and placement

**Unframed / bleeding:** the main timeline band and its time axis. It runs nearly
edge to edge, deliberately breaching the margins on both sides, so the three-year
span reads as long. The background is unframed and continuous.

**Framed / boxed:** the September call-out lens (a hard-edged rectangle with a
visible border, thin leader lines back to Sep 2025 on the main axis), the stat
block (a light card, top right), and the legend (a small card). Framing marks
these as commentary about the timeline rather than part of it.

**Vertical order, top to bottom:**

* Title, upper left, on its own line.
* Two subline rows directly beneath the title, smaller.
* Stat block, upper right, at title height — so the eye crossing the top of the
  image collects the headline and the numbers in one sweep.
* The diamond key, centred above the upper band.
* The **Charlie Kirk / TPUSA** band, bars growing UPWARD from the centre axis.
* The **centre time axis**, with year ticks 2022 / 2023 / 2024 / 2025 and light
  month gridlines.
* The **Erika Kirk** band, bars growing DOWNWARD from the centre axis, mirrored.
* The September call-out lens, lower right.
* Legend, lower left. Source line, bottom edge, full width.

**Horizontal:**

* Frame left is the beginning of time and the weakest evidence — 2022, entirely
  dotted, because no free archive reaches back that far.
* Frame right is the end and the strongest — September 2025, entirely solid.
* Left-to-right therefore doubles as untested-to-tested. That is not an accident
  and the design should lean on it.
* Band labels ("CHARLIE KIRK + TPUSA — 24 claimed", "ERIKA KIRK — 56 claimed")
  sit at frame left, inside their bands, so the reader knows which trace is which
  before reading a single bar.
* The 5 undated never-published rows hang off frame right, past the axis end,
  ghosted — literally outside of time.

## Order of understanding

Numbered by priority, most important first. Number 1 introduces the concept and
the issue; number 2 is the first thing the reader should actually understand.

1. **The concept and the issue.** Foreign-government jets are claimed to have
   kept landing where the Kirks and TPUSA were, for three years — and rather than
   argue about the tally, somebody went and got the raw flight data back.
   Delivered by the title, the sublines, and the shape of the two traces.
2. **This is three years, not a moment.** The axis runs Jan 2022 to Oct 2025 and
   there are 46 months on it, with real gaps. Delivered by the width and the ticks.
3. **There are two ledgers, not one, and they are wildly unequal.** 56 Erika, 24
   Charlie/TPUSA. Delivered by the mirrored bands and the band labels.
4. **Most of the claim cannot be tested at all.** 47 of 80. Delivered by the
   dotted fill dominating the Erika trace, and stated plainly in the stat block
   rather than buried.
5. **Where it can be tested, it mostly holds — and the failures are all on one
   side.** 25 solid against 3 hollow, and every hollow bar is below the axis.
   Delivered by the fills and the stacked bar.
6. **The strongest evidence is the jet sitting on the ground at the named field.**
   Six diamonds. Delivered by the markers and the key above the upper band.
7. **September 2025 is the point of the whole thing.** Delivered by the call-out
   lens, timed to the minute off the recovered traces.
8. **Five of the claimed overlaps were never published at all.** Delivered by the
   ghosted detached block off frame right — the last thing a thorough reader finds.

## Counterargument carried in the image itself

Required by `site/docs/Planes/following/CLAUDE.md` — every page carries its
counterargument, and so does every graphic that goes on one. In this design the
counterargument is not a footnote, it is the primary visual encoding, and it now
runs in two directions at once:

* **Against the claim:** the three hollow bars are drawn at the same scale as the
  corroborations, and the 47 dotted bars — most of the chart — are drawn at that
  same scale too. A reader who thinks the whole thing is thin can see exactly how
  thin, and where.
* **Against this site:** the dotted bars are the honest admission that we mostly
  do not know. Nothing here converts an absence into a finding, and the source
  line says so in as many words.

The source line additionally states the corroboration radius (15 km), names the
two archives, states that control aircraft were queried on the same dates, and
states that the Erika side has no published itinerary.

## What this graphic must NOT do

* Must not merge threads. This is the 3-year following pattern only. No N1098L,
  no drones, no HADES, no N888KG Wendover, no day-of ballistics.
* Must not show a single averaged or reconciled tally. 73 and 80 both appear.
* Must not present a dotted bar as a refutation, or a hollow bar as a coverage
  gap. Those two errors are the whole reason the fill vocabulary has five classes.
* Must not depict Tyler Robinson, any suspect, any weapon, or the assassination
  itself. No blood, no crosshairs, no gore, no crime-scene imagery.
* Must not name or depict aircraft owners, crew, passengers or ground staff.
* Must not assert who tasked an aircraft.
* Must not imply that a corroborated trace places any person aboard. It does not,
  it never can, and the lens says so in its own footer.
