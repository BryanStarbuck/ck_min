## What you are building

https://whoassassinatedcharliekirk.com/Planes/following/overview is full of tables whose
cells are just a COUNT ("25", "61", "At the claimed airport", "No archive coverage").
An investigator reading that page wants to click the number and see EXACTLY WHICH ROWS
it is made of — which following aircraft, which airport, which date, when it landed and
when it left, who it is claimed to have been following, and what every verdict on that
row was.

You write ONE `.mdx` page per slice. The page IS the answer to "show me the specifics".

## Writing rules — these are the site's, not suggestions

* Plain, declarative, unhedged about what the data says; strictly hedged about what it means.
* NEVER assert that any living person committed a crime. Attribute every claim
  ("the compilers say", "the sheet records", "the audit found").
* NEVER call an archive gap a deletion or suppression. `NOT_HEARD` and
  `NO_ARCHIVE_COVERAGE` are archive limits. Say so every time they appear.
* Publish the result that weakens the claim as prominently as the one that supports it.
* Every `<div>` and `</div>` at column 0 — an indented closing tag breaks the MDX build.
* No `{` or `}` in prose outside JSX; escape a literal brace as `&#123;`.
* Do not invent an image. Do not add an image.
* Keep every link exactly as the tables give it.

JSON count differs from the number printed on the overview page.
